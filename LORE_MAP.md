# Aerathon Lore Map

This is an authoring and retrieval guide, not an in-world record or an additional source of canon. It lives outside the published content directory and is not included in the website's normal build. Source files remain visible to anyone with repository access.

## Start a lore task

1. Read the vault's [AGENTS.md](content/Aerathon%20-%20Eternal%20Labyrinths/AGENTS.md) and the relevant parts of [WIKI_STYLE_GUIDE.md](WIKI_STYLE_GUIDE.md).
2. Choose the archive layer and section below. Open its index, then follow the relevant subsection indexes and dedicated subject records. The descriptions identify scope; they do not guarantee that every topic has a completed record.
3. Search the entire vault for the subject's name, aliases, and connected people, places, institutions, dates, and rules. Folder placement alone does not capture all dependencies.
4. Compare the sources' time periods, revision statements, narrators, and certainty. A filing date is not necessarily a current review, and a newer rumor does not automatically supersede an established reference.
5. Before editing, identify the connected records that would be affected. After editing, check those records and maintain the nearest browse index.
6. Preserve unresolved uncertainty. Record consequential incompatible claims with source paths and headings in [WIKI_REVIEW.md](WIKI_REVIEW.md); do not invent a reconciliation during an audit. When new lore or a retcon is authorized, follow the creative authority in `AGENTS.md` and update affected records.

Use the repository's continuity-check skill for a focused canon review. Formatting and link checks catch structural faults; they do not establish that lore claims agree.

## Section directory

For campaign developments and inspiration, consult [the private campaign workflow](CAMPAIGN_GUIDE.md) and the local `private/campaign/START HERE.md` when available. Search ignored notes explicitly. Use authorized public adaptations without exposing private sources, and keep future plans separate from played events.

The linked public indexes remain the maintained directories for deeper folders. Read their filing boundaries before choosing where a new record belongs.

## Cross-section reading routes

These are search routes, not assertions that particular subjects are related. Follow the actual links and evidence for the task.

| Proposed change                               | Read together before changing facts                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dates, ages, or historical sequence           | Annals: Eras & Timelines, including its calendar and consolidated chronology; the affected event or biography; any Atlas revision or Ledger notice relying on that sequence.                                                          |
| A settlement, region, or border               | Atlas: Regions, Realms & Settlements and Governments & Geopolitics; relevant Annals events; any current Travel Advisories or Political Climate Watch notices.                                                                         |
| A people, lineage, or cultural practice       | Atlas: Peoples, Lineages & Cultures, Religion & Worship, and Society, Economy & Everyday Life as relevant; Annals: Ancient Peoples & Civilizations. Preserve the distinction between lineage, culture, homeland, and faith.           |
| Magic, divine limits, or artifacts            | Annals: Fundamentals of Recorded Reality and relevant Relics, Ruins, & Archaeological Records; Atlas: Magic, Technology & Infrastructure and Religion & Worship; affected delving procedures.                                         |
| Delving rules, ranks, guilds, or broadcasts   | Atlas: Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle; Governments & Geopolitics for jurisdiction; Magic, Technology & Infrastructure for technical systems; Ledger notices that apply those rules.                                                  |
| An organization or political office           | Atlas: Organizations, Companies & Associations, Governments & Geopolitics, Religion & Worship, or Guilds & Charters according to primary mandate; personal biographies and historical predecessors; current Faction Activity Reports. |
| A person's role or relationships              | The dedicated biography, connected people, organization or office, home settlement, and relevant events. Consult Notable Figures and the Delver Registry filing boundaries before creating a second profile.                          |
| A creature or field hazard                    | Atlas: Bestiary & Field Phenomena; affected geography, delving safety records, and any mission or travel notices.                                                                                                                     |
| A new report, rumor, mission, or event notice | Ledger: Reading the Monthly Ledger and the relevant notice category; the stable Atlas subject record and applicable calendar or ranking standard. A proposed event does not establish a confirmed schedule.                           |

## Keeping this map useful

- Keep factual lore in dedicated source records. Add pointers here rather than copying biographies, dates, ranks, or rules into another summary that can drift.
- Update this directory when a major section is added, renamed, moved, or changes scope. Maintain deeper inventories in the existing public indexes.
- Add a local AGENTS.md only when a subtree needs additional guidance: scope, starting records, related sections, filing boundaries, and specific continuity checks. Link to sources and inherit broader rules instead of repeating them.
- The current quartz.config.yaml excludes every AGENTS.md from publication. An ordinary README.md inside content is not covered by that exclusion. Keep general authoring maps at the repository root, or use the already-excluded AGENTS.md convention for local guidance.
- Do not place campaign secrets in these maps. Exclusion from the website does not make a tracked repository file private.
- For deeper discovery, list files under the relevant section and search names across the whole vault. An empty shelf or a planned category is not evidence that its unwritten lore already exists.
