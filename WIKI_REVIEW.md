# Aerathon Wiki — Canon Decisions Requiring Review

This file records only conflicts or missing facts that cannot be resolved safely from the repository. Editorial, formatting, navigation, and link issues are fixed directly rather than logged here.

## Present Year: 3388 A.D. or the 3730s

### Files

- `content/Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/I. Annals & Antiquities/02. Eras & Timelines/∅ Chronological Timeline of Aerathon.md`
- All 45 published creature dossiers under `content/Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/Bestiary/`
- `content/Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/Cities, Isles & Towns/Major Cities/Vinyot/Malarthain Stronghold.md`
- The following ten pages under `content/Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/Geographic Regions/I. The Material Realms/Notable Distinct Regions/`:
  - `Dunes of the Sun Bull.md`
  - `Monster Meadows.md`
  - `Nattefrost.md`
  - `Symphony Canyon.md`
  - `Tempestrise.md`
  - `The Blackwild.md`
  - `The Kasho'ta Caves.md`
  - `Throne of the Easterlilies.md`
  - `Uriah's Wood.md`
  - `Verges of Ash.md`

### Conflict

The Grade-I chronological authority explicitly identifies **3388 A.D.** as the present year and the current consolidated revision. It also identifies 3388 as the latest event on the master timeline.

The Living Atlas instead contains a coordinated later chronology. Every published Bestiary dossier is filed between **3730 and 3734 A.D.** The ten listed region pages describe events, current operations, and “present” conditions between **3410 and 3728 A.D.** Malarthain’s Under-Crucible is described as publicly resolved in **3509 A.D.** These are not isolated typographical errors; several entries depend on one another and consistently treat the 3700s as recorded history.

### Evidence

- The chronological authority says “The present year is 3388 A.D.” and marks 3388 as a Grade-I anchor.
- The Bestiary filing dates form an orderly sequence by category: 3730, 3731, 3732, 3733, and 3734.
- Related geographic and creature records cross-reference the same later events, including the 3725 Symphony recording, the 3728 Tempestrise monitoring cycle, and Bestiary sightings from the same period.
- The Malarthain page deliberately distinguishes its 3388 party-era status from a later public resolution in 3509, indicating that at least some future-dated material was written intentionally.

### Recommended resolution

Choose which date represents the wiki’s reader-facing present:

1. **Keep 3388 A.D. as the present.** Recast the later Living Atlas dates as forecasts, sealed future material, or backdated records. This preserves the dedicated Grade-I timeline but requires substantial canon decisions about the exact replacement dates.
2. **Advance the present to 3734 A.D. or later.** Extend the master timeline and update all “present year,” era-duration, revision, and current-status references across the Annals. This preserves the internally coordinated Living Atlas chronology but changes the campaign’s temporal anchor.
3. **Establish two explicit timeframes.** Keep 3388 as the campaign/party era and label the 3730s records as a later archival frame. This matches Malarthain’s existing two-era presentation, but the relationship between those frames must become explicit in the chronology and navigation.

### Reason

The repository strongly supports both a 3388 campaign present and an intentionally constructed 3730s archival present. Selecting or reconciling those frames materially changes canon, so the dates were preserved pending an author decision.

## Monster Meadows: Classified Slayer Placeholder

### File

- `content/Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/Geographic Regions/I. The Material Realms/Notable Distinct Regions/Monster Meadows.md`

### Conflict

The 3688 Cleansing Crusade and its associated Red Horn Fragment name the expedition leader as **`[Parent’s Name — classified]`** and **`[Parent’s Name]`**. The brackets read as an unresolved authoring placeholder, but “classified” could also indicate intentional in-world redaction.

### Evidence

No named parent, slayer, crusade leader, or matching horn owner elsewhere in the repository provides a safe replacement. The two references clearly describe the same person.

### Recommended resolution

Either provide the canonical name, or confirm that the identity is deliberately classified. If it is an in-world redaction, replace the placeholder syntax with a stable phrase such as “the classified expedition leader” in both locations.

### Reason

Choosing a name would invent canon; removing the classification could erase an intentional mystery.
