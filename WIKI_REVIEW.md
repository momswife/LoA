## Monster Meadows: Classified Slayer Placeholder

### File

- `content/Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/3. Notable Distinct Regions/02. Monster Meadows.md`

### Conflict

The 3342 Cleansing Crusade and its associated Red Horn Fragment name the expedition leader as **`[Parent’s Name — classified]`** and **`[Parent’s Name]`**. The brackets read as an unresolved authoring placeholder, but “classified” could also indicate intentional in-world redaction.

### Evidence

No named parent, slayer, crusade leader, or matching horn owner elsewhere in the repository provides a safe replacement. The two references clearly describe the same person.

### Recommended resolution

Either provide the canonical name, or confirm that the identity is deliberately classified. If it is an in-world redaction, replace the placeholder syntax with a stable phrase such as “the classified expedition leader” in both locations.

### Reason

Choosing a name would invent canon; removing the classification could erase an intentional mystery.

## Glasrún Pact: Founding Date and Attribution

### Files

- `content/Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/2. Sub-Regions/02. Glasrún/∅ Glasrún.md`
- `content/Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/II. Allemance/1. Settlements/06. Dermad.md`
- `content/Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/II. Allemance/1. Settlements/10. Gocaire.md`

### Conflict

The former standalone Glasrún record dated the original relinquishment to **367 A.D.** and attributed it to an unidentified first ruler. Gocaire's maintained chronology dates the **Forest Abandonment** to **571 A.D.**, while Dermad attributes the mistaken relinquishment to **Aurelian Vossryn, First Warden of Glasrún**.

### Evidence

All three accounts agree on the durable result: noble claim and responsibility north of the Queensriver were relinquished, establishing the protected autonomy later recognized as the Glasrún Pact. They do not agree on whether 367 and 571 describe separate stages or on who executed the decisive act.

### Recommended resolution

Confirm whether the Pact developed in stages, identify the correct officeholder or ruler for each stage, and preserve 571 as a later formalization only if that distinction is canonical.

### Reason

Selecting one date or attribution would silently override another maintained regional record. The new subregional overview therefore records the disagreement without resolving it.
