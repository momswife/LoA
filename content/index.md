## Welcome, Delver

The Lorevault is Aerathon's living archive: a record of its history, peoples, powers, and the events reshaping the world. Begin with one of the archive divisions above, search for a specific record, or follow the current expedition into Malarthain Stronghold.

Records may reflect conflicting accounts, incomplete evidence, or information still in motion. In the Lorevault, contradiction is not always an error—it may be the most important clue.

## I. Annals & Antiquities

Recovered histories, ancient cultures, lost nations, magical traditions, and relics from the ages before the Labyrinths transformed Aerathon.

## II. The Living Atlas

A current guide to Aerathon's settlements, governments, factions, peoples, faiths, technologies, and recognized threats.

## III. Monthly Ledger

Active reports, public notices, rumors, contracts, political developments, and other events that may change before the record is complete.
