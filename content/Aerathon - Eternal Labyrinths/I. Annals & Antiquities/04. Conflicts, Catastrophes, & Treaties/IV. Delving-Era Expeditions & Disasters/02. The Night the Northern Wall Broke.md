# The Night the Northern Wall Broke

> [!warning] Historical record
> Frostguarde and its approaches remain under the northern plague cordon. This account does not establish present services, safe access, or surviving inhabitants. See [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/4. Nattefrost/1. Settlements/01. Frostguarde City|the city’s records status]].

## I. Scope and Uncertainty

The Night the Northern Wall Broke is the archival title for an incomplete account of an organized undead assault on [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/4. Nattefrost/1. Settlements/01. Frostguarde City|Frostguarde City]]. The calendar date and its precise relationship to the establishment of the northern plague cordon remain unresolved. This reconstruction does not certify present contact with anyone inside the closed region.

Illness reports, the assault, and a later protective intervention occur in the account, but their proximity does not establish one cause. The record neither identifies the plague’s origin nor declares the northern emergency ended.

## II. Warning and Assembly

An apparently ordinary evening included reports of fatigue, forgetfulness, abnormal frostbite, and people failing to wake. Guards shifted toward the northern wall without a public display of alarm. [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/03. Religion & Worship/IV. Faith Infrastructure, Politics & Dissent/04. The Concordant Spire|The Concordant Spire]] continued marking the hours.

The Northcall then drew volunteers toward [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/4. Nattefrost/2. Life, Customs & Identity/01. The Northcall and Emberward Assembly|The Northcall and Emberward Assembly]]. [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/I. Annals & Antiquities/05. Figures, Institutions, Houses & Lineages/I. Figures, Sovereigns & Founders/07. Halric Stonewatch|Halric Stonewatch]] reported silent watch posts, missing riders, and an overrun position. Defenders were assigned to the wall, supply movement, and observation.

## III. Failure of the Northern Line

The approaching dead moved under the apparent direction of a tall skeletal figure. The figure is described as lich-like; neither its identity nor its exact nature is established. Wounded defenders were taken into the horde rather than immediately killed in the account.

Cold spread across equipment and masonry. Pressure damaged the parapet, and defenders withdrew as larger stitched and skeletal creatures forced their way through. Some lesser dead appeared to change as the assault progressed. Descriptions of the larger creatures anchoring the swarm remain observations, not a demonstrated command mechanism.

Halric formed a delaying line while a dispatch sought aid from an S-Rank delver. The inner wards were expected to close. The account supplies neither a full evacuation record nor a confirmed final casualty count.

## IV. The Broken Route

The dispatch’s stated destination was [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/06. Magic, Technology & Infrastructure/III. Arcane Research & Observation/01. The Cryostudies Annex|The Cryostudies Annex]]. A later passage describes damage inside [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/4. Nattefrost/1. Settlements/01c. Archives of Frostguarde|Archives of Frostguarde]], while another places a descent beneath the oldest bastion. The account does not securely connect those places.

One street is described as cleared before the narrative resumes there. Who cleared it, by what means, and how long the opening lasted are unrecorded. No complete route or sequence of individual rescues can be recovered from that gap.

## V. The Reprieve Attributed to Elowen

A later passage attributes an intervention to [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/IV. Delver Registry/1. Active/08. Mother Elowen|Mother Elowen of the Pale Hour]]. Light moved through the foundations, old wards rekindled, and undead were described as arrested or deprived of animation. Elowen bore a visible bodily cost. The account represents the protection as time purchased for the city, not a final victory.

The account calls the working **Wish**. That name does not establish its exact duration, repeatability, or spell mechanics. Nor does it prove that the plague was cured, the dead restored to life, or Elowen and the defenders subsequently escaped. The circumstances of her recovery and the earlier report that she was injured remain incomplete.

## VI. What Remains Unknown

The city’s present condition remains governed by the northern cordon record. The attacking commander’s identity, the illness’s cause, the protection’s duration, the evacuation’s outcome, and Halric’s fate are unresolved.

The historical value of the account lies in the choices it preserves under pressure: an assembly held together, a wall relinquished, and aid sought while others delayed pursuit. Those choices deserve examination without a conclusion the record cannot supply.
