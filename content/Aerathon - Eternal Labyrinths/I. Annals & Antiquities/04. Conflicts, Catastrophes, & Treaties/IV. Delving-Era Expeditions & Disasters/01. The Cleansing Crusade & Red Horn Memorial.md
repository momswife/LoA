# The Cleansing Crusade & Red Horn Memorial

**Filed Division:** _Annals & Antiquities — Conflicts, Catastrophes, & Treaties_

## I. Scope and Evidence

The **Cleansing Crusade** was the expedition led by **Anya Lykoi** into **Monster Meadows** in **3342 A.D.**, with the declared purpose of purging the Rift. It ended in an eruption of aberrant light and **eighty-seven confirmed deaths**. Anya survived. The **Red Horn Marker**, a memorial cairn at the eastern watchtower, preserves the loss in the landscape where the expedition failed.

This account draws on the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/2. Allemance/3. Geography & Landmarks/10. Monster Meadows|Monster Meadows dossier]] and [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/10. Notable Figures/II. Military & Civic Defense/01. Anya Lykoi|Anya's current biography]]. Those records establish the purpose, leader, outcome, and memorial. They do not provide a complete participant roll, a reliable sequence of orders, or the circumstances of Anya's escape. The casualty figure therefore cannot be used to reconstruct the expedition's total strength.

## II. The Attempt to End the Threat

The expedition's purpose was to remove a danger whose consequences reached people living outside the Rift. Anya's reasons for delving were protective: she entered labyrinths when they threatened surrounding communities. The Crusade attempted to make that protection decisive by confronting the source.

Its name preserves the confidence of the undertaking. To call an expedition a cleansing is already to imagine a condition that will exist after it succeeds. The Meadows did not yield that conclusion. An eruption of aberrant light brought the operation to catastrophe, leaving the surrounding country with its original danger and the additional burden of the dead.

The surviving record does not establish that the expedition caused the eruption, nor does it identify a particular maneuver, relic, or spell as the trigger. Assigning such a cause would turn a known failure into an invented explanation.

## III. What Is Established

| Matter | Supported account |
| --- | --- |
| **Date** | 3342 A.D. |
| **Leader** | Anya Lykoi, the battle master later known as the Hearthward Compact's Field Chair. |
| **Purpose** | An expedition to purge the Monster Meadows Rift. |
| **Catastrophe** | An eruption of aberrant light overwhelmed the operation. |
| **Losses** | Eighty-seven confirmed dead. |
| **Survival** | Anya survived; the mechanism and sequence of her escape are not recorded here. |
| **Commemoration** | The Red Horn Marker was raised at the eastern watchtower. |

Tiberius's later marriage and partnership with Anya do not establish that he participated in the Crusade. The record makes no such claim.

## IV. The Red Horn Marker

The cairn is a memorial within a working watch landscape. Its eastern tower still belongs to the perimeter's warning system; remembrance takes place beside a duty that continues. A visitor looking from the Marker toward the Meadows sees the reason people went inward and the reason others must remain on watch.

Pilgrims visit yearly. Some believe the fallen company's spirits help ward the Rift against a total awakening. The Ministry records that belief as a practice of remembrance rather than a verified containment effect. The cairn does not certify that the danger has ended, and the dead are not a substitute for the living watch.

The memorial's **Red Horn Fragment** is a separate object: a splinter of Anya's Crusade horn, reported to vibrate when storms approach and held among the Meadows artifacts in Louvain Annex Vault #47. The Marker at the tower should not be confused with the fragment's storage location.

A preserved field-log entry reports five descending tones from the Marker, which local listeners identified as Anya's battle call. The account supplies neither a calendar date nor a verified cause. It records a sound and an interpretation; it does not make Anya one of the dead.

## V. Survival and Responsibility

Anya's survival is part of the Crusade's history, but it does not absorb the history of everyone who failed to return. Her later reputation cannot settle what the lost expedition meant to the people who knew its members. Her own discomfort with heroic accounts of the disaster reflects that distinction: the eighty-seven are not supporting figures in a story whose only purpose is to explain her success.

The loss influenced her later commitment to defending the inhabited perimeter. That change gave protection a continuing form: a patrol relieved before it failed, an evacuation route kept open, and a recruit taught to withdraw before courage became abandonment of the people behind them.

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/09. Organizations, Companies & Associations/I. Civic Defense & Emergency Service/01. The Hearthward Compact|Hearthward Compact]] now carries that work under Anya and Tiberius's joint leadership. The Crusade is part of the background to that commitment; no founding date for the Compact is established by the expedition date.

## VI. The Memorial in the Age of the Circuit

The **3377 Pulse Ascension** belongs to a later phase of the Rift's history. Since then the Rift has remained open, with recurring active intervals managed through the wardens' patrols and the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/V. Broadcasts, Audiences & Sponsorship/05. The Meadow Circuit|Meadow Circuit's]] tournaments. Those controlled engagements do not reenact the Crusade or complete its original objective.

The contrast is part of the Meadows' present character. The same region contains cheering spectators, veterans returning to paid competition, and a cairn raised for an expedition that did not come home. The **Empty Chair Muster**, with its remembrance, shared meals, and relief fundraising, gives the Circuit room for that quieter inheritance alongside its contests.
