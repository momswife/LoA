# Delving-Era Expeditions & Disasters

This shelf records completed events from the Age of Delving and the Age of Spectacle whose consequences warrant historical treatment. The event's evidence, sequence, losses, and public memory belong here; present-day conditions remain in the Living Atlas and developing notices in the Monthly Ledger.
