## **I. Site Description**

The **Sunwound Crater** is a vast circular depression in Arneria containing terraces, civic foundations, waterworks, inscriptions, and active Orichalcum lines beneath its visible basin.

The name suggests that one catastrophic impact created the entire site.

Excavation does not support so simple an origin.

The crater rim contains several episodes of melting, collapse, construction, and later erosion. Some terraces predate the most obvious vitrification. Others were built into already damaged slopes. The central basin has been reshaped by water, heat, magical instability, settlement, scavenging, and modern stabilization.

Sunwound is not one wound.

It is a landscape repeatedly built around damage.

---

## **II. The Buried Terraces**

The deepest accessible terraces belong among the strongest known examples of an Orichalcum **Foundation Settlement**.

Their streets follow conductive lines beneath the stone. Civic halls surround mechanisms regulating water, light, warmth, and boundary response. Several structures alter subtly according to the people present, suggesting that access and function were relational rather than controlled by one key or ruler.

Brethren tools appear beside Beastfolk migration marks and mortuary deposits. Joined divine signs occur on thresholds later carving traditions would assign separately to Dawn and Old Court powers.

No palace or universal central temple has been identified.

The settlement appears coordinated without evidence of one sovereign center.

This finding helped overturn the obsolete claim that every Orichalcum site belonged to one empire.

## **III. The Dawning Measurement**

Official Aerathonian years begin at **0 A.D. — After Dawning**.

At Sunwound, several independent lines of evidence converge around a major stabilization of the Three Weaves:

- long-decay Orichalcum resonance enters a consistent phase;
- vitrified materials stop recording contradictory seasonal states;
- astronomical alignments become sequential rather than overlapping;
- and later settlement layers resume ordinary causal order.

The Louvain Chronometry Commission used this boundary to calibrate the modern calendar after Collapse-era timekeeping failed.

The evidence establishes a measurable transition at Sunwound.

It does not prove that every place in Aerathon stabilized simultaneously, that the gods intentionally created a Dawning, or that all Orichalcum civilizations ended on that day.

Year zero is a useful anchor.

It is not a universal eyewitness.

## **IV. Auric Line Tablets**

Tablets recovered from sealed terrace contexts preserve some of the most complete known [[The Orichalcum Civilizations#X. Auric Line and the Recording of Intent|Auric Line]].

They shimmer, alter emphasis with angle, and respond to the identity and intent of a reader. Popular accounts say they glow when lies are spoken nearby. Controlled observation shows a broader response to contradiction among inscription, speaker, listener, and active obligation.

Several tablets appear to regulate civic works rather than record narrative history.

A sign translated as “sun” may also encode exposure, season, witness, heat debt, and responsibility for opening a channel. Reading it as a simple noun can activate the wrong relationship.

For this reason, no intact tablet is read alone or removed from its architectural context without necessity.

The dedicated textual assemblage is recorded under [[The Sunwheel Tablets]].

## **V. What Made the Crater?**

Current theories include:

### **Celestial Impact**

Some glass and pressure structures resemble impact effects, but no secure single impactor has been identified.

### **Divine Weapon or Failed Making**

Joined divine marks and extreme Weave disturbance support a sacred event, though they do not identify intent or combat.

### **Horizon Mechanism Failure**

The settlement may have redirected light, weather, or celestial energy beyond safe limits.

### **Progressive Collapse**

Several disasters may have overlapped until later observers interpreted the basin as one event.

### **Intentional Containment**

The crater’s inner rings may have been shaped to hold, vent, or isolate an earlier rupture.

No theory explains every layer.

The common name should not be treated as a conclusion.

## **VI. Excavation Hazards**

Sunwound remains active.

Known hazards include:

- surfaces remembering an earlier load path;
- inscriptions enforcing unfinished maintenance;
- brief disagreements in local time;
- light continuing around an obstacle that has moved;
- temperature changes linked to ancient seasonal schedules;
- and chambers that recognize excavators as occupants, trespassers, or missing workers.

The greatest danger is not always violence.

An active structure may assign a person an obligation whose original society no longer exists. Removing the person without closing that relationship can transfer the duty elsewhere.

Excavation plans therefore include legal and ritual termination procedures as well as engineering containment.

---

## **VII. Communities and Custody**

Arnerian communities surrounding Sunwound possess long histories of route, burial, salvage, warning, and seasonal avoidance connected to the basin.

They are not archaeological scenery.

Local knowledge identified stable paths before Ministry surveys and preserved names for chambers absent from official maps. Some communities claim descent from people who survived the Foundation Settlement; others reject descent language while maintaining obligation to the landscape.

Material study does not require resolving every ancestry claim.

It requires recognizing that continued relationship can constitute evidence and custody.

No artifact may be removed solely because an outside institution can conserve it more visibly.

---

## **VIII. Calendar Dependence and False Precision**

Sunwound calibrates modern dating through comparative decay.

That role can create false confidence.

An object dated “relative to Sunwound stabilization” still depends upon assumptions about exposure, material memory, and local time. Dates near 0 A.D. should not be read as exact anniversaries unless supported by independent evidence.

The site makes long chronology possible.

It does not make ancient chronology simple.

The official calendar is a public coordination system, not proof that history happened at one speed everywhere.

---

## **IX. Ministry Conclusions**

1. Sunwound contains a securely attested pre-Dawning Foundation Settlement with mixed cultural evidence.
2. Its crater form developed through several episodes rather than one conclusively identified event.
3. The site preserves the strongest measurable anchor for the Dawning boundary at 0 A.D.
4. That measurement does not prove simultaneous stabilization across Aerathon.
5. Auric Line tablets at the site record relational intent and can remain functionally active.
6. No current crater-origin theory explains every material layer.
7. Excavation can activate inherited obligations as well as physical hazards.
8. Surrounding communities possess evidentiary and custodial relationships to the site.
9. Sunwound supports chronology without justifying false precision.
