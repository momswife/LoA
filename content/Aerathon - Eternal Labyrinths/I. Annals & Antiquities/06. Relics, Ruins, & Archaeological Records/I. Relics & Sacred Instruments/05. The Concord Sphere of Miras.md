# The Concord Sphere of Miras

### _The Convergence Instrument That Made Contradiction Visible without Making Judgment Impartial_

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — Annals & Antiquities  
**Responsible Office:** Relics Registrar  
**Supporting Offices:** Convergence Antiquities Desk, Arcane Breach Division, Forensic Thaumaturgy Laboratory  
**Primary Compiler:** Inspector Jao “Greyglass” Vell, Forensic Thaumaturgy  
**Contributing Scholars:** Curator Rook-of-Rings, Archivist Yelna Stonevoice, Lieutenant Salvia Korrin  
**Current Revision:** 3388 A.D., Age of Spectacle  
**Material Class:** _Active Judicial Instrument / Multi-Perspectival Resonance Device_  
**Evidence Grade:** _Grade-I Convergence Provenance; Grade-II Original Procedure_  
**Primary Evidence:** nested sphere fabric, Miras docket impressions, Harmonium Court Rolls, recovery context, controlled claim trials, and Thesis-War damage  
**Historical Range:** Late Convergence period through the [[The Thesis Wars|Thesis Wars]]; recovered in damaged condition from the Miras Forum

---

## I. Object Description

The **Concord Sphere of Miras** is a nested instrument of translucent mineral shells, suspended metallic paths, moving inscriptions, and an apparently empty center.

It measures slightly wider than an adult’s outstretched hands when dormant. During activation, its inner shells can appear farther apart than the outer diameter should permit.

No fixed front exists.

Observed from different positions, the object presents different arrangements without ordinary motion between them. A line appearing continuous to one witness may be broken to another. Two observers exchanging positions exchange some—but not all—of what they see.

The Sphere was designed to prevent one perspective from appearing naturally central.

Its later use did not always preserve that restraint.

---

## II. Miras and Convergence Judgment

Miras was a major forum-city of [[The Convergence Courts]]. Its surviving dockets concern disputes involving several jurisdictions at once: blood, law, light, thought, flame, earth, water, and breath.

The Sphere appears to have been used when no single Court could lawfully judge the whole claim.

Participants placed testimony, objects, blood marks, written law, memory impressions, or other evidence at separate receiving points. The Sphere then displayed:

- areas of overlap;
- incompatible assumptions;
- evidence dependent upon one witness;
- consequences carried outside the stated claim;
- and relationships the participants had omitted.

The result was called a **concordance**.

It was not originally a verdict.

It was a map of what a verdict would have to answer.

---

## III. Demonstrated Function

Controlled trials confirm that the Sphere responds to structured claims.

It does not react reliably to isolated statements such as “this is true.” It requires relationship: who speaks, what they seek, who bears consequence, what evidence is offered, and which authority is invoked.

During activation, colored paths form among the shells. Paths brighten where claims share evidence, diverge where definitions conflict, and disappear where a necessary relationship has not been represented.

The device can reveal that two parties use the same word differently.

It can expose that an apparently private dispute depends upon a third person’s labor or land.

It cannot independently determine whether a witness lies, whether a law is just, or which consequence should be preferred.

It clarifies the structure of judgment.

It does not absolve the judge from choosing.

---

## IV. The Empty Center

Miras texts describe the Sphere’s center as **the place no claimant may own**.

No throne, divine emblem, or magistrate’s seal was meant to occupy it. The empty center represented the unresolved civic space created when legitimate claims met.

Later Court administrations placed a removable judicial crystal within the center.

Material scoring proves repeated insertion.

The crystal allowed a presiding magistrate to stabilize one perspective and produce a cleaner output. It also made that perspective appear to arise from the instrument itself.

Most surviving verdict-era images show the added crystal and call it original.

The earliest socket is an abrasion, not a designed mount.

The Courts changed an instrument for displaying contradiction into an instrument that displayed their resolution as natural.

---

## V. Harm and Exclusion

The Sphere depended upon evidence that could be presented in forms it recognized.

Written claims, licensed memories, attuned objects, and Court-certified witnesses produced stable paths. Oral traditions, collective identity, ecological need, embodied custom, and evidence refusing extraction often appeared diffuse or incomplete.

Court scholars treated weak display as weak claim.

The instrument may instead have been poorly calibrated to the form of evidence offered.

Several Beastfolk accounts describe the Sphere “cutting a road into pieces because no single traveler owned it.” Household testimony records children and dependents rendered invisible when only legal sponsors could place a claim.

An instrument can expose bias in argument while carrying bias in its inputs.

---

## VI. Thesis-War Damage

The Sphere carries a radial fracture passing through five inner shells without breaking the outer surface.

The damage is consistent with jurisdictional magic rather than impact. Miras records describe a final proceeding in which rival Courts attempted to make the Sphere recognize mutually exclusive definitions of civic personhood.

Afterward, some residents ceased appearing in local licenses even though household and burial records confirm their presence.

Whether the Sphere caused the erasure, recorded it, or was damaged by the same attack remains unresolved.

The object now produces occasional **absent paths**: visible relationships to a participant whom no observer can identify.

Activation is terminated when this occurs.

No absent path is treated as proof of a ghost, erased citizen, or missing Ninth Court without independent evidence.

---

## VII. Recovery and Custody

The Sphere was recovered from a collapsed subforum at Miras with docket seals, judicial crystal fragments, and burned Harmonium rolls.

Its excavation context is secure.

It later entered controlled containment because ordinary display caused visitors to organize spontaneous disputes around it. Modern custody is maintained within a secured prism facility; precise chamber information is restricted because the object can activate through sufficiently structured public argument.

The Sphere is not used to decide modern cases.

Research trials employ fictional claims, informed participants, and independent exit procedures. No criminal, family, citizenship, inheritance, or sovereignty dispute may be submitted to it.

Ancient procedure is evidence, not jurisdiction.

---

## VIII. Replicas and Civic Use

Nonactive replicas of the Sphere are used in education to illustrate competing evidence and unrepresented consequence.

These replicas are often more useful than the original.

They make no claim to reveal hidden truth. Participants must explain why they drew each connection. Disagreement remains visible and reviewable.

Several courts and mediators use “leave the center empty” as a procedural reminder that no institution should begin by treating its own perspective as neutral.

This is cultural inheritance rather than institutional succession from Miras.

---

## IX. Ministry Conclusions

1. The Concord Sphere is a securely provenanced Convergence judicial instrument.
2. It maps relationships, omissions, incompatible definitions, and distributed consequence.
3. It does not independently determine truth, justice, or lawful priority.
4. The empty center was an original restraint against one claimant owning the frame of judgment.
5. Later magistrates added a central crystal that made selected interpretation appear intrinsic to the object.
6. The Sphere privileged evidence compatible with Court procedure and could diminish other traditions.
7. Its internal fracture is associated with a Thesis-War conflict over civic personhood.
8. Modern activation is restricted and possesses no legal authority.
