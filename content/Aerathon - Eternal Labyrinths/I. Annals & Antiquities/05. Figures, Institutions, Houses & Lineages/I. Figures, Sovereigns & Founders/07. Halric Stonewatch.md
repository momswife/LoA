# Halric Stonewatch

> [!warning] Historical record
> Frostguarde and its approaches remain under the northern plague cordon. This account does not establish present services, safe access, or surviving inhabitants. See [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/4. Nattefrost/1. Settlements/01. Frostguarde City|the city’s records status]].

## I. Command at Frostguarde

Halric Stonewatch is recorded as head of Frostguarde’s North Barracks and a senior Ministry field commander for the northern frontier. His historical profile concerns command and emergency service; it establishes no personal delver rank, noble title, or relationship to the Stonewarden family.

His command coat and worn Ministry insignia expressed an office used in the field. In the northern-assault account, his authority appears through practical assignments: who would reinforce the wall, who would move supplies, and who could observe the approach without acting alone.

## II. The Northcall Briefing

At [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/4. Nattefrost/2. Life, Customs & Identity/01. The Northcall and Emberward Assembly|The Northcall and Emberward Assembly]], Halric reported that three outer posts had failed to answer, dispatched riders had not returned, and scouts had found the first post overrun. The archive retains those statements as the substance of his briefing, not as a complete independently verified casualty report.

He called for useful volunteers and discouraged reckless displays. His troops belonged to a city organized around civil defense and evacuation, not an army built to conquer the Nattefrost.

## III. The Delaying Line

When the wall failed, Halric gathered shield-bearers and battlemages at an avenue leading into the city. The account places him holding that line while a sealed dispatch was sent toward [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/06. Magic, Technology & Infrastructure/III. Arcane Research & Observation/01. The Cryostudies Annex|The Cryostudies Annex]]. The assignment sought help before the inner wards closed; the message’s contents are not established.

The narrative ends his part amid the fighting. It does not confirm his death, escape, capture, or survival. Calling the action a delaying stand describes its purpose without turning it into a completed martyrdom.

## IV. Historical Assessment

Halric’s importance in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/I. Annals & Antiquities/04. Conflicts, Catastrophes, & Treaties/IV. Delving-Era Expeditions & Disasters/02. The Night the Northern Wall Broke|The Night the Northern Wall Broke]] is his effort to preserve choices for others after the wall could no longer be held. Whether that effort secured an evacuation remains unknown. His current whereabouts cannot be inferred from the existence of this record.
