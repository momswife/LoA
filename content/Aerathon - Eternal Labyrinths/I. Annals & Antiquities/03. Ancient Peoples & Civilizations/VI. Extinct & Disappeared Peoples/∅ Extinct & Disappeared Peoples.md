# Extinct & Disappeared Peoples

This shelf gathers historical records concerning sapient peoples no longer represented by a known living community in modern Aerathon.

These subjects are filed among peoples rather than in the Bestiary. Unusual bodies, magical adaptations, uncertain origins, or extinction do not make a sapient culture a monster or field specimen.

## I. Scope and Terminology

**Extinct** and **disappeared** are not interchangeable conclusions.

An extinct classification reflects the best-supported finding that no living population remains. A disappeared classification means that a people vanished from the available record without evidence sufficient to determine whether every community died, migrated, transformed, concealed itself, or became known by another name.

Both conclusions remain subject to revision. Labyrinth recurrence, altered geography, incomplete excavation, inherited identity, and conflicting oral histories can all complicate what appears to be a final disappearance.

## III. Evidentiary Limits

Each record combines material remains, later testimony, environmental evidence, and Ministry reconstruction. Physical absence alone does not establish extinction, and a surviving artifact does not speak for the people who made it.

Where evidence concerns anatomy, magic, faith, or cultural practice, the record distinguishes observed remains from later inference. Where a modern community claims descent, continuity, custody, or living knowledge, that claim must be evaluated as evidence rather than treated as an obstacle to classification.
