> _What survives is not always what happened. It is what someone considered worth preserving._

The Annals preserve Aerathon's deep memory: vanished civilizations, disputed chronologies, foundational magic, historic institutions, and the remains of ages reconstructed from incomplete evidence.

These records are the archive's best-supported interpretations, not unquestionable truth. Later discoveries may amend—or overturn—them.
