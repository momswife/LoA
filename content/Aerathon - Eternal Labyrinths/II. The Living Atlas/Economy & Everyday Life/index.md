# Economy & Everyday Life

Aerathon is often described through gods, Labyrinths, and famous delvers. Most days, however, are measured in meals, shifts, fares, lessons, repairs, rent, weather, and the favors people owe one another.

These records explain those ordinary systems without pretending that one region, social class, lineage, or guild experience represents the whole continent. Conditions differ sharply between a Louvain academy, a Vinyot company district, an Oric House, an Arnerian caravan, and a village beyond the nearest scryline.
