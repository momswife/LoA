# Trade Guilds & Merchant Associations

This shelf is for named organizations that coordinate commerce: merchant leagues, craft guilds, shipping combines, trade consortiums, commercial houses, and similar bodies. General economic systems remain in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/04. Society, Economy & Everyday Life/index|Society, Economy & Everyday Life]].

The word _guild_ does not by itself place an organization under Delving. Only organizations principally chartered for Labyrinth work belong in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/III. Guilds & Charters/index|Guilds & Charters]].

> _No individual organizations are currently filed on this shelf._
