# Mercenary Companies & Contract Forces

This shelf records private armed companies, contract defenders, caravan escorts, recovery teams, and comparable forces whose defining mandate is compensated security or military service rather than Labyrinth exploration.

A company that principally conducts licensed delves belongs in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/III. Guilds & Charters/index|Guilds & Charters]], even when it also accepts mercenary work.

> _No individual organizations are currently filed on this shelf._
