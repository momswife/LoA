# The Hearthward Compact

**Filed Division:** _Living Atlas — Organizations, Companies & Associations_

## I. The People Who Stayed Above

The **Hearthward Compact** guards the ordinary world against what comes out of the extraordinary one. Stationed around [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/2. Allemance/3. Geography & Landmarks/10. Monster Meadows|Monster Meadows]], its members intercept escaped labyrinth creatures, defend the roads and farms beyond the quarantine perimeter, and help evacuate people who never volunteered to live beside a battlefield.

Most are former delvers, aging warriors, or veterans of military service. Some despise delving and the fortunes made from it. Others remember it fondly enough to fear what an inexperienced party might bring home. Their agreement is practical: whatever their opinions of the people going underground, those remaining above deserve protection.

At the center of the fellowship stand **Anya Lykoi and Tiberius**, the celebrated battle master and bladesinging wizard, **married partners and joint leaders of the Compact**. They are among Allemance's most notable defenders, known throughout the region both for their individual mastery and for running the Hearthward exceptionally well. Their stewardship has made a disparate gathering of veterans into a dependable defense force, a respected training institution, and a major public attraction. For many recruits, the prospect of serving under Anya and Tiber is reason enough to come to the Meadows.

Locals call them **the Last Fence**. The name suits their mismatched appearance: a battered army breastplate beside an old guild cloak, an expensive enchanted blade carried in a repaired scabbard, a famous champion arguing over whose turn it is to mend the wagon. Their common mark is an open gate crossed by a horizontal spear. The gate stays open toward home.

At their watchfires, stories of great victories compete with complaints about damp boots and aching joints. Younger visitors sometimes mistake the informality for indiscipline. The mistake rarely survives the first alarm. Old arguments stop halfway through a sentence; everyone already knows which road, litter, or shield belongs to them.

## II. Mandate and Place

The Compact's standing work is **surface protection**: interception, escort, rescue, evacuation, and holding a defensible boundary until the appropriate specialists arrive. It also answers nearby incidents involving creatures escaped from guild custody or returning expeditions. It does not claim a general right to police guilds, seize relics, or enter restricted labyrinths.

The **MDO entrusts local protection to the Compact's wardens**, recognizing that those who keep the watch are best placed to judge its immediate needs. Anya, Tiber, and their appointed captains decide patrol priorities, defensive deployments, and the response to ordinary escapes without seeking Ministry permission for each action. The Ministry leaves recruitment, internal appointments, and duty qualifications to the Compact as well. This trust allows the fellowship to remain independently led while carrying an acknowledged responsibility for the communities around the Meadows.

At Monster Meadows, three responsibilities meet:

| Body | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Rift Watch** | Observation of the Meadows, the twelve watchtowers, and the established warning system. |
| **Hearthward Compact** | Patrols and civilian defense on the inhabited side of the perimeter; supporting evacuation and intercepting outward threats. |
| **MDO Arcane Breach Division** | Specialist assessment, stabilization, and sealing under [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/II. Field Operations & Safety/02. Breach Containment|Breach Containment]] protocols. |

The Compact's stations and competition grounds stand outside the restricted inner Meadows. An arena ticket grants no access to the quarantine zone. The tranquil herds within the Meadows are not a stock of animals to be driven out for entertainment; patrols distinguish a creature's origin from its behavior. Contact suggesting speech, negotiation, or uncertain sapience is referred for assessment rather than treated as a sporting opportunity.

Since the 3377 Pulse Ascension, the Rift has remained open, with active openings recurring at regular intervals. The Compact manages the emerging monsters through patrols, interception, and the Meadow Circuit's held tournaments. These controlled engagements are a working part of home defense as well as a source of income and recognition. Major awakenings remain exceptional disasters beyond that regular rhythm and require emergency response.

Anya's commitment to the inhabited perimeter carries the weight of the **3342 Cleansing Crusade**. She survived the failed attempt to purge the Rift; eighty-seven members of the expedition did not. The Red Horn Marker remembers those losses. Her present work begins with a different question from the one that led the expedition inward: how many people can be brought home?

## III. Leadership and Organization

### The Two Chairs

Anya and Tiber's marriage is a familiar part of the Compact's public identity. Within the fellowship, their partnership is felt in the daily work: shared responsibility for its people, confidence in one another's judgment, and the willingness to settle difficult decisions together. Each commands respect in their own right. Their success rests on bringing those strengths into the same organization and giving capable wardens room to lead.

Their shared surname, **Lykoi**, was Anya's before their marriage. Tiberius deliberately took her name following hardship in his own family. Their individual biographies discuss the significance of that choice; the family circumstances remain private.

[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/10. Notable Figures/II. Military & Civic Defense/01. Anya Lykoi|Anya Lykoi]], the famed black-haired Chikitu battle master fighter, holds the **Field Chair**. She directs patrol readiness, veteran instruction, and the conduct of a defense once people are in danger. Her teaching centers on the unglamorous decisions that keep a line intact: where to stand, when to exchange places, and which apparent opening will leave someone else exposed. In her drills, the best fighter may spend most of the exercise guarding a cart.

[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/10. Notable Figures/II. Military & Civic Defense/02. Tiberius Lykoi|Tiberius]], usually called **Tiber**, is the renowned tiefling bladesinging wizard who holds the **Ward Chair**. He oversees magical defense, works with the artificers maintaining the holo-stations, and sets the Compact's standards for controlled arena conditions. His lessons connect movement to attention: a graceful escape is poor work if it leaves a companion inside the danger being escaped.

Together they lead the fellowship. Anya decides field deployment; Tiber decides whether the Compact's magical systems are fit to operate. Either can halt a public event. Neither can countermand a Ministry quarantine or convert a Rift Watch retreat signal into a challenge to stand for the cameras.

### A Reputation Earned in Stewardship

Both have delved when labyrinths threatened surrounding communities. Tiber is fascinated by their inner workings, while Anya appreciates the chance to sharpen her abilities in an unpredictable environment. Their shared purpose is protection. Both strongly dislike the superficial fame-and-glory culture of televised delving, even as their own work has made them widely known.

Their standing reaches beyond the tournament audience. Veteran fighters seek Anya's instruction, artificers and aspiring bladesingers value Tiber's judgment, and local households know both names as readily as those of the region's celebrated delvers. A joint appearance at the Circuit is an occasion in itself. Their fame draws visitors and sponsors, while their continued attention to patrols, training, and care for members gives that fame substance.

The MDO's confidence in the Compact rests heavily on the couple's record of leadership. They have kept its defensive purpose clear while making its tournaments and training facilities prosper, maintained cooperation among fiercely independent veterans, and entrusted daily command to people able to carry it. Their wardens can take pride in an organization that is well supplied, well taught, and worth remaining with after the applause ends.

### Working Branches

The Compact organizes around duties rather than former military rank or delving fame.

| Branch | Work | Reports to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fence Companies** | Patrol assigned approaches, escort travelers, and hold interception positions. Each contains mixed skills rather than a single weapon specialty. | Field Chair through company captains. |
| **Road Reserve** | Reinforce threatened sectors, recover stranded patrols, and keep evacuation routes usable. | Field Chair through the duty captain. |
| **Hearth Service** | Triage, transport, provisions, equipment repair, and civilian shelter coordination. | A service steward working with the duty captain. |
| **Lantern Works** | Artificers, ward-tenders, and scenario keepers who maintain holo-stations and arena protections. | Ward Chair through the workshop lead. |
| **Trial Office** | Book training, maintain exercise records, and arrange entrance examinations with MDO examiners. | The two chairs for administration; the MDO official for licensing decisions. |
| **Ring Office** | Arrange tournaments, purses, concessions, and broadcast commitments. | The two chairs; no command over patrols or emergency closures. |

The company captains and branch leads meet with the chairs to settle rosters, repair priorities, and expenditure. On an ordinary watch, a named **duty captain** coordinates the shift. In an emergency, that captain places the Road Reserve, the Hearth Service clears civilian routes, and the Lantern Works checks defensive systems while the Ministry handles breach stabilization. The Ring Office clears spectators and relinquishes transport and space needed for relief.

Tournament champions hold no command merely because they win. A famous guest taking a watch answers to its captain, including when that captain spent an entire military career as an uncelebrated sergeant.

## IV. Joining and Remaining Useful

The Compact chooses its own wardens. Its leadership sets admission standards, accepts or refuses applicants, and decides who is ready for a watch, an instructional post, or command. MDO officials do not select its members or approve its routine recruitment decisions. The Ministry's licensing role applies separately to applicants entering the delving profession through the holo-station entrance trial; membership in the Compact is the wardens' decision.

Membership begins with an assessment of what a recruit can reliably do now. Old titles establish experience; they do not establish current endurance. Recruits train in a [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/I. Orientation, Licensing & Classification/01a. Holo-Stations & the Entrance Trial|holo-station]], accompany an experienced watch, and qualify for a particular duty before taking it independently.

The Compact makes room for people who can no longer sustain a full expedition. A retired shield fighter may teach spacing, an injured scout may interpret tracks at a watch desk, and a former quartermaster may be more valuable keeping evacuation carts supplied than entering an arena. Rest and reassignment are ordinary parts of service. Members do not have to prove that their old wounds have stopped hurting.

Off-duty delvers can take temporary watches or train without abandoning their guilds. Permanent members are expected to honor the watch roster before accepting a performance booking. Those who dislike delving work beside those who miss it; arguments are tolerated, abandoning a partner is not.

## V. Money, Recognition, and the Ring

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/V. Broadcasts, Audiences & Sponsorship/05. The Meadow Circuit|Meadow Circuit]] and paid holo-station sessions bring the Compact substantial income and regional recognition. Admissions, broadcast agreements, sponsorship, concessions, and training fees help pay for patrol wages, artificer maintenance, medical care, and reserve supplies. Retired and off-duty delvers are a major audience and a large part of the competitive field.

The arrangement is also the Compact's most persistent argument. Its members protect communities from the consequences of a spectacle industry while running one of Allemance's largest broadcast attractions outside live delving. A sponsor sees a veteran with a recognizable face; a duty captain sees a tired watch member whose next shift begins before dawn.

For Anya and Tiber, the broadcasts are justified by the defense, training, and provision they support. Their personal distaste for performative delving remains. The success of the Circuit gives them useful resources and a continuing responsibility to keep audience demand from directing the watch.

Operational stores and scheduled watch strength are allocated before tournament purses. The Ring Office cannot keep an unsafe match running to satisfy a contract. Even so, closures cost money, and quiet weeks sell fewer live-monster bouts. The temptation to exaggerate a threat or turn necessary defense into a performance is real enough to be discussed openly within the fellowship.

## VI. Life Along the Fence

The homeward side of the watch is busy even when the Meadows are quiet. Equipment dries beside cooking fires. Artificers carry cracked lenses between workshops. A recruit practices the same shield exchange while a retired delver explains, at length, how their old party used to do it incorrectly.

On tournament days, the road fills with vendors, visiting teams, and children trying to recognize fighters beneath ordinary traveling clothes. Beyond the temporary stands, a patrol still has to walk its route. One of the Compact's most respected customs is the **empty place**: competitors leave space at their table for whoever drew the watch while everyone else enjoyed the event. Food is kept warm; winnings and stories can wait until the relief arrives.

The retreat horn changes the place completely. Stalls fold, applause breaks into shouted directions, and competitors become escorts. The veterans judge an event's real success by how quickly that change happens. A crowd that knows where to go is part of the defense they have been building all along.
