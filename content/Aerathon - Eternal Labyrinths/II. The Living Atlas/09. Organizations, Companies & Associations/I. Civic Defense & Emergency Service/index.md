# Civic Defense & Emergency Service

This shelf is for reserve formations, volunteer watches, emergency corps, relief organizations, and other bodies whose primary work is protecting communities at home. An organization may reject delving as a profession and still belong here if it mobilizes against breaches, invasions, disasters, or comparable threats when ordinary defenses are overwhelmed.

Organizations acting as formal arms of a government remain with [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/05. Governments & Geopolitics/index|Governments & Geopolitics]]. Licensed expeditionary bodies remain with [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/III. Guilds & Charters/index|Guilds & Charters]].
