# Organizations, Companies & Associations

This division records enduring organizations that shape modern Aerathon without fitting the mandates of a government, continental authority, religious institution, or licensed delving guild. It is the home for civic-defense organizations, mercenary companies, merchant associations, professional societies, reform leagues, criminal networks, and comparable organized interests.

## Filing Boundaries

- File a body by its primary mandate, not merely by its allies, opponents, funding, or public reputation.
- Governments and bodies exercising public jurisdiction belong in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/05. Governments & Geopolitics/index|Governments & Geopolitics]].
- Crowns, noble houses, claimants, and inherited offices with recognized present-day constitutional or political standing belong in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/05. Governments & Geopolitics/IV. Crowns, Noble Houses & Inherited Offices/index|Crowns, Noble Houses & Inherited Offices]]. Heritage societies and restorationist movements without such standing remain organizations and are filed by their actual activity.
- Religious orders whose identity and authority are principally sacred belong in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/03. Religion & Worship/index|Religion & Worship]].
- Licensed organizations organized around Labyrinth exploration belong in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/III. Guilds & Charters/index|Guilds & Charters]].
- Networks and technical systems such as the Grand Scrying Network belong in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/06. Magic, Technology & Infrastructure/index|Magic, Technology & Infrastructure]]; the organizations that own, regulate, or contest them receive their own filings where appropriate.
- Developing operations, rumors, and recent changes belong in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/III. Monthly Ledger/03. Faction Activity Reports/index|Faction Activity Reports]], with links back to the stable organization record.

When a body crosses several shelves, keep one canonical profile under its dominant purpose and link its secondary roles rather than duplicating the record.
