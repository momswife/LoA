# Mutual-Aid, Advocacy & Reform Movements

This shelf is for enduring groups built around mutual support, public advocacy, social reform, civic campaigning, or a shared political ideal without themselves exercising governmental authority.

Broad social conditions and practices remain in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/04. Society, Economy & Everyday Life/index|Society, Economy & Everyday Life]]. Electoral bodies, ruling coalitions, and organizations exercising public jurisdiction may instead belong in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/05. Governments & Geopolitics/index|Governments & Geopolitics]].

> _No individual organizations are currently filed on this shelf._
