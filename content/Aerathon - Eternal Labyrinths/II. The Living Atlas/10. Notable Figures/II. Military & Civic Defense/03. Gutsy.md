# Gutsy

## Profile

**Gutsy** is a [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/3. Beastfolk/10. Murine|murine]] agent of the Ministry of Delving Operations and one of the fourteen people recorded as escaping [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/7. Lisvenn/1. Settlements/01. Gonlev|Gonlev]] during the northern undead assault.

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/III. Monthly Ledger/04. Headlines & Rumors/02. Gonlev Survivor Bulletin|survivor bulletin]] places Gutsy aboard the departing [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/III. Monthly Ledger/04. Headlines & Rumors/03. Ghost Train at Gonlev|ghost train]] with Sickle Grymes and twelve grandi felines. The record establishes a shared escape; it does not assign Gutsy responsibility for summoning, operating, or directing the train.

## Recruitment Assignment

Gutsy leads the effort to locate **A-Rank or S-Rank operatives** to assist with the northern crisis. The work connects the survivors' immediate need for help with the Ministry's search for people capable of responding.

The assignment does not establish Gutsy's own delver rank, combat discipline, or authority over every part of the emergency. Assistance being sought should not be mistaken for a relief force already assembled.

## Current Record

Gutsy's destination after leaving Gonlev and present location are not recorded. The recruitment assignment remains the stated role in the northern response; a completed deployment or resolution has not been filed.
