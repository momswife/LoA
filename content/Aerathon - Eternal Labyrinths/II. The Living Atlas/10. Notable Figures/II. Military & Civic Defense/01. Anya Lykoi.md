# Anya Lykoi

**Filed Division:** _Living Atlas — Notable Figures_

## I. Profile

**Anya Lykoi** is a black-haired [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/3. Beastfolk/06. Feline|Chikitu]] battle master fighter and one of Allemance's most respected defenders. She holds the **Field Chair** of the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/09. Organizations, Companies & Associations/I. Civic Defense & Emergency Service/01. The Hearthward Compact|Hearthward Compact]], which she leads alongside her husband, the bladesinging wizard [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/10. Notable Figures/II. Military & Civic Defense/02. Tiberius Lykoi|Tiberius]]. Her reputation rests on martial mastery and on the capable stewardship of a force trusted to keep danger from the surrounding communities.

Anya has delved, particularly where a labyrinth threatened people living nearby. Protection was the purpose of those descents. The fame and glory surrounding the profession held little attraction for her, and she strongly dislikes the superficiality of televised delving: the habit of judging a dangerous undertaking by how impressive it looks to an audience.

She nevertheless appreciates what a labyrinth demands of a fighter. Unpredictable ground, unfamiliar opponents, and a situation that refuses to follow yesterday's lesson offer opportunities to sharpen her abilities. For Anya, the satisfaction lies in becoming more capable of meeting the next danger.

## II. The Crusade and the Perimeter

In **3342**, Anya led the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/I. Annals & Antiquities/04. Conflicts, Catastrophes, & Treaties/IV. Delving-Era Expeditions & Disasters/01. The Cleansing Crusade & Red Horn Memorial|Cleansing Crusade]], an expedition intended to purge the Rift at [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/2. Allemance/3. Geography & Landmarks/10. Monster Meadows|Monster Meadows]]. The attempt ended catastrophically in an eruption of aberrant light. Anya survived; eighty-seven members of the expedition died. The **Red Horn Marker** at the eastern watchtower commemorates the losses.

The failure remains central to her later commitment to defending the inhabited perimeter. Her purpose of protecting people endured, while the work took a different shape: guarding approaches, preparing withdrawals, and keeping a force ready to respond when creatures emerge. The Meadows could not be made harmless by a single act of courage. Its neighbors still needed someone willing to return to the watch the following day.

This history gives weight to her instruction. The possibility of retreat is part of a plan from its beginning. Holding a position matters because of what it protects and whom it allows to escape; a fighter who forgets that purpose can win an exchange and lose the defense.

The Crusade also complicates her public reputation. Praise for her present leadership cannot undo the consequences of a decision she once believed would protect the same country. She has little patience with accounts that turn those deaths into scenery for her own survival. The memorial belongs to the people who did not return.

Her response to responsibility can become demanding even by the standards of the Compact. After a difficult watch, she is inclined to keep reviewing decisions when everyone else is ready to stop. This makes her thorough, but it can also make rest feel like unfinished work. Learning to trust a captain's account and leave a task in competent hands is part of her leadership, not a lesson she has finished learning.

## III. Battle Master and Teacher

Anya's teaching emphasizes judgment under changing conditions. She pays attention to spacing, timing, exchanges between tired fighters, and the consequences of pursuing an apparent opening. A recruit may arrive hoping to learn a celebrated maneuver and spend the session learning why a companion needs room to step back.

Her own enjoyment of a difficult fight coexists with the discipline of command. A challenge can be valuable training without becoming a reason to expose someone else. Within the Compact's exercises, skill is demonstrated by keeping the team effective as the situation changes. The strongest fighter may be assigned to guard the cart because that is where strength is needed.

Anya helps shape the practical lessons used in the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/I. Orientation, Licensing & Classification/01a. Holo-Stations & the Entrance Trial|holo-stations]]. Their repeatable encounters let her examine a decision, change the circumstances, and show how a familiar response can become the wrong one. She values the experience a simulation can develop while recognizing that an actual labyrinth will not remain within an instructor's prepared possibilities.

### Temperament Beyond the Command

Anya enjoys skilled opposition. In practice, a partner who finds a genuine gap in her defense earns her attention, and often another round. Her delight in improvement is visible enough to surprise people who know her chiefly as a stern commander. A cleanly solved problem pleases her; an unexpected solution can please her more.

Her instruction is direct, and she tends to express approval specifically. She notices the moment a recruit steadies a companion or abandons a poor angle. Praise becomes useful information about what to carry into the next attempt. Her humor is similarly particular: a grand explanation for a missed step may earn a dry observation about where the speaker's feet actually went.

She is less comfortable accepting care than providing it. Checking another person's equipment, arranging relief, or making sure a late patrol has food comes naturally. Admitting that she needs the same attention takes more effort. Those close to her learn that her competence is not evidence that she has no needs of her own.

## IV. Leadership and Public Reputation

As Field Chair, Anya directs patrol readiness, deployment, and the conduct of a defense. She shares responsibility for the Compact's recruitment and leadership with Tiberius and entrusts duty captains with meaningful authority. The **MDO's confidence in the Compact** includes confidence in the couple's ability to choose their people and make local defensive decisions without awaiting individual Ministry orders.

Her fame brings experienced fighters to the Meadows and helps draw an audience to the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/V. Broadcasts, Audiences & Sponsorship/05. The Meadow Circuit|Meadow Circuit]]. She regards that attention as useful when it supports the watch. The tournaments manage emerging monsters, train defenders, and help pay for the people and equipment that protection requires.

Running a successful broadcast attraction has not changed her distaste for delving performed for glory. It places her close to the very pressures she distrusts. Her answer is expressed through command: a watch remains staffed, an unsafe bout ends, and applause never creates a reason to delay a withdrawal. The audience may remember the champion; Anya remains responsible for everyone still on the ground.

## V. Marriage and Family

### Her Name, Their Family

**Lykoi is Anya's own family name, retained through marriage. Tiberius deliberately took her surname**, a choice shaped by hardship in his family before their shared life. People expecting the wife to have taken the husband's name sometimes assume the reverse. Anya and Tiber make the direction of that choice clear without treating his private history as something an audience is owed.

For Anya, his choice gives their shared name a personal significance beyond public recognition. It joins two people who have chosen to make a home together. She respects his right to explain what he wishes about the past and to leave the rest unspoken. Their marriage does not depend on turning that past into a public story.

### The Person Beside the Warden

Anya and Tiberius are partners in both private life and the leadership of the Compact. She values his fascination with a problem even when her first question is what must be done about it. He values her capacity to act even when he needs her to wait for an answer. Neither has to borrow the other's temperament to share the same purpose.

Their familiarity shows in small exchanges as readily as in command. Anya can recognize when a short explanation is becoming a lecture; Tiber can recognize when her insistence that a task will take only a moment means she is preparing to do someone else's work herself. They can be amused by these habits while still challenging them.

She trusts him to see the person behind the Field Chair: someone who enjoys a hard bout, can be stubborn about admitting exhaustion, and sometimes needs company without a report to review. That trust helps keep their marriage from becoming merely another part of the organization they run. Their shared life gives them reasons to protect an ordinary evening as well as the people gathered around an alarm.

They have a son, **Corvo Lykoi**, a Chikitu who has recently become a delver.
