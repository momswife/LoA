# Ercanhardt Schnofinkel

## I. Career and Standing

**Ercanhardt Schnofinkel** is an auditor of the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/05. Governments & Geopolitics/I. Continental Institutions/1. Ministry of Delving Operations (MDO)/01. Ministry of Delving Operations|Ministry of Delving Operations]], known for extraordinary compliance work: investigations in which several offices can produce plausible records that cannot all describe the same event.

His reputation rests on patient reconstruction. He compares receipt dates with transport logs, the custody of a seal with the tenure of its signatory, and a declared closure with the resources still being drawn from the site. An account may balance perfectly and still describe something that never happened. Ercanhardt has made a career of finding the difference.

## II. Background and Training

Ercanhardt and [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/IV. Delver Registry/1. Active/04. Huldiberaht Schnofinkel|Huldiberaht Schnofinkel]] grew up in the same family, where their rivalry developed around different kinds of achievement. Huldiberaht sought recognition through power and spectacular results. Ercanhardt learned to question who had certified those results, who had paid for them, and which failures had disappeared from the account.

He entered Ministry work through the examination of expedition returns and custody documents. Reconstructing a journey from its surviving paperwork taught him both the value of procedure and the ease with which an influential official could hide behind it. He pursued audit work because it allowed him to demand answers from people who otherwise expected deference.

His early competence brought increasingly difficult cases. It also encouraged a lasting conviction that his own judgment was more reliable than the institutions employing him.

## III. Selected Accomplishments

### Recovery accounts reconciled

In a contested salvage settlement, Ercanhardt established that the same recovery expense had been charged through several offices under different descriptions. He reconstructed the underlying consignment from weight records and escort payments. The resulting correction released funds owed to the working company and forced the departments involved to identify one accountable custodian.

The case strengthened his standing among officers who had seen legitimate claims buried beneath contradictory accounts.

### A warrant tested beyond its seal

During a custody review, Ercanhardt identified an order renewed under an appointment that had already expired. The seal was authentic; the authority it purported to exercise was not current. His finding stopped the renewal from being treated as automatic and required the responsible office to submit the holding to fresh review.

He has since insisted that a document's appearance and its lawful force be examined separately.

### Losses restored to the record

An expedition return classified missing equipment as ordinary wastage while omitting the recovery crew's account of a preventable failure. Ercanhardt compared procurement specifications, issued equipment, and surviving testimony. His amended finding restored the crew's evidence and placed responsibility on the supply decision rather than the people sent to work with it.

These successes explain why people who dislike his manner may still seek his intervention.

## IV. Methods and Reputation

Ercanhardt prefers questions whose answers can be checked. He asks witnesses to separate what they saw from what they were told, requests original documents before summaries, and follows a discrepancy into another department even when the first office considers the matter closed.

Those habits can expose abuse. They can also make an interview coercive when he treats hesitation as concealment or assumes that only he is entitled to interpret the evidence. His reputation for detecting corruption should not be mistaken for an absence of ambition, prejudice, or self-interest.

His authority remains subject to the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/05. Governments & Geopolitics/I. Continental Institutions/1. Ministry of Delving Operations (MDO)/01a. Ministry Authority and Continental Jurisdiction|Ministry's charter and jurisdiction]]. An auditor's finding is evidence for a decision; it does not make the auditor sovereign over the people being investigated.
