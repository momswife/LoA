> _Where the Annals reconstruct the past, the Living Atlas monitors the present._

The Living Atlas documents Aerathon as it exists now: mapped, governed, inhabited, contested, and continually changed by magic, technology, politics, and the Labyrinths.

Unlike historical records, these entries are revised whenever borders shift, governments fail, discoveries spread, or accepted doctrine no longer matches reality.
