## I. Lineage Overview

Orcs are a widespread Brethren people whose history has too often been told only through the armies, empires, and gods that attempted to command them.

They are not the missing half of Half-Orcs. They possess their own households, philosophies, arts, agricultural traditions, burial practices, and civic histories. The full Orc record is necessary because mixed Orcish people cannot be understood honestly while Orcs themselves appear only as ancestry, threat, or vanished parent culture.

---

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

Several Orcish peoples arrived during separate Convergence migrations. Some were bound to war gods or transported by military powers; others crossed as refugees, caravan communities, farmers, hunters, craftspeople, and defectors from armies that later claimed to represent all Orcs.

The **Unbinding Fires** commemorate communities that publicly destroyed hereditary war contracts. Weapons were not always burned: many were reforged into tools, door fittings, instruments, or memorial iron. The lesson is that ending forced purpose does not require erasing the skill used to survive it.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Orcs are Medium Humanoids with varied builds, tusks, pointed ears, dense musculature, and broad ranges of complexion. Tusks may be decorated, capped, repaired, or left unaltered. They are sensitive living anatomy, not handles or trophies.

Orcish stamina is real but routinely exploited. Strength and rapid recovery do not reduce requirements for rest, pain care, protective equipment, or consent.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Orcish kinship includes clans, hearth circles, trade companies, neighborhoods, military-refusal houses, farms, temples, and mixed households. A clan is a network of answerable relationship, not automatically a chain of command.

Many communities practice the **Open Hand Oath**: before accepting service, each participant names what they will do, what they will not do, and how the obligation ends. An oath without an exit is considered a surviving fragment of conquest.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Orcish cultures commonly admire candor, endurance, generosity under scarcity, decisive protection, humor, and refusal of imposed purpose.

Their recurring debate concerns strength: whether public pride repairs historic shame or quietly preserves the demand that every Orc remain useful in crisis. Modern Orc artists and teachers insist that softness, hesitation, scholarship, and ordinary domestic life are equally Orcish.

---

## VI. Orc Peoples in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Court guards, metalworkers, advocates, farmers, and playwrights challenge Allemance’s habit of admiring Orcish strength while distrusting Orcish authority.

### Oria

Mountain clans maintain winter roads, memorial forges, and House alliances without surrendering independent clan law.

### Vinyot

Dock unions, arenas, broadcast crews, and mutual-aid kitchens make Orcish life highly visible and commercially contested.

### Arneria

Caravan hearths and oasis communities preserve migration songs that distinguish chosen travel from forced movement.

### Al'Ar

Maritime Orcs work in fishing, ship repair, volcanic agriculture, and neighborhood defense alongside Beastfolk kinships.

---

## VII. Orc Delvers

Orc delvers are valued as defenders, rescue leaders, scouts, negotiators, and endurance specialists. Their greatest operational risk is sacrificial assignment: the assumption that the Orc should hold the line because they are most likely to survive.

Responsible crews rotate danger, plan extraction before entry, and never convert Relentless Endurance into permission to spend a companion’s body.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits are an initial Aerathon playtest package and require DM approval until table testing is complete.

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Size and Speed

You are Medium. Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Darkvision

You have darkvision out to 120 feet.

### Adrenaline Rush

As a bonus action, you take the Dash action and gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Relentless Endurance

When reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 hit point instead. You regain this trait after a long rest.

---

## X. Recognized Sublineages and Traditions

### Hearth Orcs

Hearth Orc traditions emphasize neighborhood defense, cultivation, food preservation, and the conversion of former military infrastructure into public life.

### Road Orcs

Road Orc communities preserve caravan law, mobile craft, animal care, and the right to refuse borders created after their routes.

### Hold Orcs

Hold Orcs maintain mountain, fortification, and deep-road traditions often shared with Dwarven and Goliath neighbors.

---

## XI. Playing an Orc

Consider:

- Who taught you what Orcishness meant?
- What obligation have you ended correctly?
- How do you use strength when nobody is watching?
- Which clan or hearth recognizes you?
- What do you refuse to prove?

A character may embrace, reinterpret, or reject every tradition described above. Lineage creates context, not personality.

---
