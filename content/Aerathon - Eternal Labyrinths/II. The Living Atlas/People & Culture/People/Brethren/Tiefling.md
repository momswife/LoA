## I. Lineage Overview

Tieflings are fully mortal peoples whose bodies carry infernal, lower-planar, or pact-shaped inheritances.

They may possess horns, tails, unusual eyes, heat, scent, shadow, scales, hooves, claws, or subtler marks. None of these establishes allegiance, morality, religion, magical training, or an active relationship with a fiend.

Tieflings are often called children of old bargains.

They are not parties to contracts made before their birth.

Some reclaim infernal imagery with pride. Some treat their inheritance as ordinary anatomy. Some pursue the history of a bloodline. Others refuse to allow an ancestor’s desperation or ambition to occupy any more of their life.

The defining modern Tiefling struggle is not against evil within.

It is against institutions that mistake inheritance for consent.

---

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

During the Convergence and early Labyrinth wars, mortals made bargains with fiends, archdevils, lower-planar powers, and entities that later records grouped together inaccurately.

Some bargains sought survival. Some sought conquest. Some were imposed. Some were made by rulers who treated descendants as collateral. Some Tiefling lines arose through exposure, transformation, planar settlement, or magic without any identifiable contract.

The descendants of those events are diverse peoples, not a continuing pact organization.

### The Uninherited Clause

Modern Tiefling law rests upon the **Uninherited Clause**:

> No promise can transfer the will of a person not yet capable of consent.

Fiends dispute the principle regularly.

Aerathian courts recognize it regardless.

### Bloodline Archives

Tiefling communities maintain voluntary archives of manifestations, dreams, names, legal claims, and known planar contacts. Access belongs to the descendant, not automatically to scholars or the state.

---

## III. Body, Manifestation, and Privacy

Tiefling bodies vary more visibly than many Brethren lineages.

Horns may grow throughout life, shed, branch, spiral, or require care. Tails vary in length and mobility. Skin may display ordinary Human ranges or red, gold, violet, ash, blue, and other planar tones. Eyes may lack visible sclera or reflect unusual light.

Manifestations can change with age, magic, stress, planar proximity, or no identifiable cause.

This does not make a Tiefling body a public omen.

Touching horns or tail, testing heat, examining teeth, demanding proof of fire resistance, or photographing a manifestation without permission is intrusive.

Medical practice must distinguish inherited anatomy from injury without attempting to normalize appearance.

---

## IV. Society, Family, and Pact Law

Tiefling households include families of every lineage, mutual-aid cabals, legal clinics, temples, performance circles, monasteries, urban neighborhoods, and delver fellowships.

Most Tieflings are born to families with little direct infernal knowledge. Community networks often provide the practical care a local physician or parent was never taught.

### Claim Witnesses

A **claim witness** records contact from an entity asserting ancestral rights. The witness helps distinguish supernatural fact from manipulation, ensures the Tiefling does not face negotiation alone, and prevents secrecy from becoming leverage.

### Chosen Presentation

Tieflings may conceal, decorate, dramatize, or ignore their visible inheritance. None of these choices indicates shame or allegiance.

Masks can be protection, art, profession, ritual, fashion, or simply a mask.

---

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Tiefling communities commonly admire self-possession, wit, boundaries, solidarity, theatrical control, courage, and the ability to name manipulation.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Defiance | Rejecting inherited claims | Letting the claim define every refusal |
| Self-possession | Controlling access to one’s story | Refusing support to avoid appearing vulnerable |
| Wit | Exposing hypocrisy and threat | Turning every fear into performance |
| Privacy | Protecting information from exploitation | Carrying danger alone |
| Solidarity | Sharing legal and practical knowledge | Expecting one response to infernal heritage |
| Flair | Choosing how others see you | Feeling invisible without spectacle |

A recurring Tiefling fear is that an ordinary mistake will be interpreted as the moment their “true nature” appeared.

Some pursue perfect respectability. Some embrace scandal. Some avoid magic. Others study infernal law more deeply than any fiend expects a mortal to manage.

## VI. Tieflings in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemagnian Tieflings navigate fascination, scandal, divination, court performance, and old noble bloodline claims. Some become advisors or entertainers; others build legal careers dismantling ancestral contracts.

### Oria

Oric Tieflings often live in close households, remote settlements, or protective cabals. Mountain communities preserve long memory of alleged curses, making both prejudice and reliable warning difficult to separate.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Tieflings work as illusionists, brokers, performers, spies, advocates, and black-market specialists. Public masks can create power while becoming contractual property.

### Arneria

Arnerian stories often cast Tieflings as clever figures who defeat fiends through language, hospitality, and disciplined refusal. Living Tieflings debate whether those stories empower them or demand constant cleverness.

### Al'Ar

Al'Ari Tieflings participate in monastic, maritime, artistic, and neighborhood traditions. Spiritual language about “tempering the flame” is welcomed by some and rejected by those who hear it as correction of the body.

---

## VII. Tieflings and Delving

Tiefling delvers commonly serve as curse analysts, negotiators, spellcasters, scouts, defenders, legal readers, and leaders in zones shaped by infernal or lower-planar influence.

Their strengths may include:

- Recognizing coercive bargain structures
- Resisting or using inherited energy
- Functioning in cursed environments
- Detecting when language conceals ownership
- Maintaining composure under public suspicion
- Turning threatening presentation into tactical control

The Labyrinths may address a Tiefling by an ancestor’s name, present contracts already signed, or produce entities claiming familial authority.

No crew should treat the Tiefling as automatically responsible for understanding or resolving such encounters.

Inherited familiarity is not professional expertise.

## VIII. Names, Titles, and Address

Examples include:

- Amara Clause-Broken
- Cerys Emberveil
- Dain No-Debt
- Esha Velvet Horn
- Ilyan Ashgold
- Mara Claim-Witness
- Nerezza Open Name
- Soren Glass-Tail
- Veya Uninherited
- Zahir Laughing Flame

Tieflings may use family names, virtue names, infernal names reclaimed from hostile records, chosen names, stage identities, or deliberately ordinary regional names.

Do not ask for a “true name.” In many magical traditions, the question itself is a threat.

## IX. D&D Lineage Framework

Use the campaign’s approved Tiefling ancestry traits for creature type, size, speed, resistance, spellcasting, and other core features.

The Heritage Traits below represent regional upbringing and supplement or replace the campaign’s normal regional choice as directed by the DM.

An active patron, pact, or inherited claimant is a character-story choice requiring player and DM agreement. It is not automatic.

---

## X. Heritage Traits

### Allemance — Court's Curiosity

You gain proficiency in Deception. Additionally, when a creature targets you with a divination spell or ability, you can use your reaction to obscure your aura—imposing disadvantage on any Insight or Arcana check related to it. Once you use this feature, you can’t do so again until you finish a long rest.

### Oria — Mountain Brand

You gain resistance to cold damage. Additionally, when you take fire or cold damage, you can use your reaction to ignore difficult terrain until the end of your next turn.

### Vinyot — Masked and Known

You gain proficiency in Sleight of Hand or Stealth. When you are hidden from a creature and cast a spell, you do not reveal your location until the spell's effects are resolved.

### Arneria — Tongue of the Clever

You can cast _disguise self_ once per long rest. Additionally, you can add half your proficiency bonus, rounded down, to any Charisma-based skill in which you are not already proficient.

### Al'Ar — Island Flame

You gain proficiency in Insight. Additionally, when you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can use your reaction to unleash a burst of fire. Each creature within 5 feet of you takes fire damage equal to your level. Once you use this trait, you must finish a long rest before using it again.

## XI. Playing a Tiefling

Consider:

- What does your planar inheritance mean to you?
- Is any ancestral claimant active?
- Who knows how to care for your body?
- Which stereotype have you learned to perform?
- What would you rather be ordinary about?
- Do you conceal, decorate, or ignore visible traits?
- Who serves as your claim witness?
- What offer are you afraid you might accept?
- Which contract should never have named your family?
- What name is safe to use?

A Tiefling may be pious, nonmagical, shy, cheerful, trusting, conservative, uninterested in infernal history, or free of any active supernatural conflict.

Ancestry is not a pact.

## XII. Registry Advisory

> [!note] MDO Registry Advisory  
> Tiefling lineage does not indicate evil alignment, infernal allegiance, magical expertise, an active pact, dangerous temperament, or inherited debt.
>
> Ancestors cannot provide consent on behalf of descendants. Claims based upon pre-birth contracts are unenforceable under the Uninherited Clause.
>
> Horns, tails, blood, heat, dreams, names, and planar manifestations may not be inspected, sampled, recorded, or tested without informed consent.
