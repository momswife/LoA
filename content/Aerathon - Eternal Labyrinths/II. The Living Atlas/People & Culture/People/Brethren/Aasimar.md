## I. Lineage Overview

Aasimar are mortal Brethren whose bodies carry celestial inheritance, divine proximity, or the lasting effects of blessings placed upon a bloodline, household, or place.

They are the celestial counterpart to Tieflings only in registry convenience. An Aasimar is not automatically holy, good, chosen, beautiful, or obedient to the power that marked them. Celestial inheritance can be comforting, intrusive, politically valuable, and personally irrelevant at the same time.

---

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

Aasimar lines arose through divine covenant, exposure to the Two Heavens, descent from celestial visitors, consecrated rescue, and miracles whose long consequences were never explained to their recipients.

The **Right of the Unchosen Halo** establishes that descendants may accept, reinterpret, conceal, or refuse the purpose others attach to a blessing. A god may have intended a sign. The living person still decides what the sign means.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Aasimar may display luminous eyes, metallic freckles, radiant scars, shadowed halos, unusual warmth, echoing voices, spectral wings during revelation, or no obvious marker at rest.

Celestial manifestation is bodily and private. Radiance can cause pain, sensory overload, unwanted attention, and magical surveillance. Covering a halo is not shame; displaying one is not consent to worship.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Aasimar households are ordinary families, temple lineages, mutual-aid circles, choirs, legal clinics, and communities formed by people fleeing compulsory sainthood.

A **Witness of Refusal** accompanies anyone asked to accept prophecy, relic custody, or divine office. Their task is to ensure that reverence does not silence refusal.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Aasimar communities often admire mercy, discernment, courage, responsibility, hope, and the ability to distinguish service from submission.

Their shadow is exemplary pressure. Aasimar are expected to forgive faster, suffer beautifully, and turn every wound into inspiration. Many build identities around the right to be petty, uncertain, funny, angry, or wonderfully ordinary.

---

## VI. Aasimar Peoples in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Court and temple institutions compete to patronize visible celestial lines, making privacy a central political concern.

### Oria

Aurora traditions interpret some manifestations as environmental resonance rather than divine command.

### Vinyot

Broadcast culture turns revelations into spectacle, sponsorship, and unwanted public theology.

### Arneria

Aasimar serve as oath witnesses and shrine keepers while debating whether prophecy is testimony or instruction.

### Al'Ar

Island monasteries and mixed-faith households treat radiance as one bodily inheritance among many.

---

## VII. Aasimar Delvers

Aasimar delvers are healers, ward readers, negotiators, scouts, and radiant defenders. Labyrinths may greet them as saints, traitors, keys, or unfinished celestial devices.

The party must not let an architecture’s response appoint the Aasimar as leader or sacrifice. Recognition by a ruin is evidence about the ruin, not the person’s destiny.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits are an initial Aerathon playtest package and require DM approval until table testing is complete.

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Size and Speed

You are Small or Medium. Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Celestial Resistance

You have resistance to necrotic and radiant damage.

### Healing Hands

As a Magic action, touch a creature and roll a number of d4s equal to your proficiency bonus. The creature regains hit points equal to the total. You regain this trait after a long rest.

### Light Bearer

You know the _light_ cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it.

### Celestial Revelation

At 3rd level, as a bonus action, manifest spectral wings, inner radiance, or a necrotic shroud for 1 minute. Once on each of your turns you deal extra radiant or necrotic damage equal to your proficiency bonus. The exact form and any flight granted require DM approval. You regain this trait after a long rest.

---

## X. Recognized Sublineages and Traditions

### Dawn-Marked

Dawn-Marked Aasimar manifest warmth, gold light, healing resonance, or solar imagery without implying Dawn Court allegiance.

### Dusk-Marked

Dusk-Marked Aasimar carry funerary, moonlit, or shadowed celestial signs and are often mistaken for fallen members of another lineage.

### Choir-Marked

Choir-Marked Aasimar experience celestial inheritance primarily through voice, vibration, dreams, or the sensation of distant harmony.

---

## XI. Playing an Aasimar

Consider:

- Who interprets your manifestation?
- What divine expectation have you refused?
- When does mercy need a boundary?
- Who knows you without the halo?
- What ordinary ambition belongs only to you?

A character may embrace, reinterpret, or reject every tradition described above. Lineage creates context, not personality.

---

## XII. Registry Advisory

> [!note] MDO Registry Advisory  
> Aasimar lineage does not establish goodness, holiness, prophecy, temple authority, emotional purity, or consent to divine service and public revelation.
>
> Lineage traits describe playable capacities. They do not assign morality, profession, political loyalty, or social role.
