## I. Lineage Overview

**Half-Orc** is a broad modern registry term for people with Orcish and other ancestry, most commonly Human ancestry.

It describes descent imperfectly and culture even less reliably.

Some people identify proudly as Half-Orc. Some use Orc. Some name both parental lineages. Some prefer a household, nation, clan, or regional identity. Others reject a fraction that seems to describe them as an incomplete version of two peoples.

The Ministry retains the page because the term remains widely used in law and character rules, not because it is universally accepted.

Half-Orcs have been portrayed as natural soldiers, embodiments of conflict, or people divided against themselves.

Their lives are not metaphors for duality.

They are people with families, bodies, histories, and the same right as anyone else to decide which inheritances matter.

---

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

Orcish ancestors entered Aerathon through several pre- and post-Convergence migrations. Some arrived under warlords or divine compacts. Others were refugees, mercenaries, laborers, caravan peoples, farmers, and members of settlements later destroyed or absorbed by Brethren kingdoms.

Early empires enslaved and weaponized many Orcish communities, then used the resulting military records as proof that Orcs existed only for war.

Intermarriage, adoption, alliance, coercion, displacement, and ordinary life produced people of mixed Orcish descent across every region.

No single origin story applies to all Half-Orcs.

### Choice and Inheritance

Modern Half-Orc movements distinguish between an inheritance one possesses and a role one is ordered to perform because of it.

Strength may be inherited.

War is taught.

Rage may be felt.

Violence is chosen under conditions that should be named.

---

## III. Body, Expression, and Descent

Half-Orc bodies vary widely. Common traits may include tusks, strong jaws, dense musculature, prominent brows, varied skin tones, pointed ears, Orcish facial structure, and combinations unique to a family.

No visible trait proves the percentage, legitimacy, culture, or circumstances of a person’s ancestry.

Some Half-Orcs are consistently read as Orcish. Some are mistaken for Human or another Brethren lineage. Some move between how communities perceive them.

Body scrutiny is a recurring harm. Questions about parents, conception, tusks, fertility, anger, and “which side is stronger” are intrusive.

Physical power does not make pain less real. Medical workers are specifically warned against undertreating Half-Orc pain or assuming restraint is required.

---

## IV. Society, Family, and Self-Definition

Half-Orc communities are connected through households, Orcish clans, Human families, mixed neighborhoods, military fellowships, labor movements, temples, arts organizations, and delver networks.

There is no universal Half-Orc culture, but several shared political practices have emerged from common treatment.

### The Chosen Entry

Some community records allow a person to choose how each lineage is named, in what order, and whether the registry term appears at all.

### Refusal Houses

**Refusal Houses** began as safe residences for people leaving military, criminal, noble, or family organizations that treated Orcish strength as owned labor.

They now provide legal aid, training, housing, and help reconstructing identity outside assigned roles.

### Anger Without Ownership

Half-Orc communities often create explicit space for anger without treating it as evidence of danger or demanding it be converted into service.

---

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Half-Orc communities commonly admire self-definition, courage, directness, endurance, protective solidarity, and the ability to choose under pressure.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | Defending boundaries and supporting others | Believing worth depends upon usefulness |
| Defiance | Refusing an imposed role | Letting opposition choose every decision |
| Endurance | Surviving sustained exclusion | Remaining where survival is the only reward |
| Directness | Naming conflict honestly | Treating gentleness as dishonesty |
| Solidarity | Protecting people under similar pressure | Demanding one political identity from everyone |
| Choice | Claiming authorship of one’s life | Blaming people for options coercion removed |

A recurring Half-Orc fear is that every choice will be interpreted as proof of someone else’s theory about Orcish blood.

Some pursue visible excellence. Some disappear into privacy. Some reclaim martial traditions. Others reject every weapon they were expected to carry.

---

## VI. Half-Orcs in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemagnian Half-Orcs work in noble service, law, guards, guilds, arts, scholarship, and urban mutual aid. Court culture praises exceptional individuals while leaving ordinary prejudice intact.

### Oria

Oric Half-Orcs carry histories of conquest, forced labor, warbands, adoption, and hard-won House membership. Many are smiths, guides, herders, survivalists, and record keepers.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Half-Orcs are dockmasters, performers, gladiators, organizers, merchants, and former militia. Broadcast audiences often consume visible scars as entertainment.

### Arneria

Arnerian Half-Orcs serve as scouts, ruin guardians, caravan members, historians, and defenders of cultural sites. Protecting a heritage need not mean sharing its ancestry.

### Al'Ar

Al'Ari Half-Orcs lead fishing crews, work ports, perform music, maintain neighborhoods, and join maritime defense. Fierceness and joy are not treated as opposites.

---

## VII. Half-Orcs and Delving

Half-Orc delvers serve in every role, though employers disproportionately recruit them for front-line combat, hauling, intimidation, and sacrificial defense.

Their common strengths include:

- Continuing after physical and social pressure
- Acting decisively during crisis
- Recognizing coercive group dynamics
- Protecting contested exits
- Combining traditions from several communities
- Leading crews underestimated by institutions

The greatest operational danger is role capture: once a Half-Orc succeeds at a dangerous task, the party begins treating it as their permanent responsibility.

Rotate exposure. Ask rather than assume. Do not turn resilience into a budget for harm.

## VIII. Names, Titles, and Address

Examples include:

- Arda Two-Homes
- Brakka Venn
- Deren Unboxed
- Hessa Forgevoice
- Korrin Open-Hand
- Mara Grael
- Nira Without Fraction
- Oren Salt-Tusk
- Tava Red Quill
- Vask Chose-Again

Some people combine Human and Orcish naming structures. Others use only one, adopt a new name, or refuse lineage-marked names entirely.

Use the term the person uses. Do not “correct” Half-Orc to Orc or Orc to Half-Orc as though one were automatically more respectful.

## IX. D&D Lineage Framework

Use the campaign’s approved Half-Orc or mixed-Orc ancestry traits for creature type, size, speed, resilience, and other core features.

The Heritage Traits below represent regional upbringing and supplement or replace the campaign’s normal regional choice as directed by the DM.

---

## X. Heritage Traits

### Allemance — In the Halls and Gutters

You gain proficiency with one artisan’s tool or musical instrument. Additionally, when you roll a Charisma (Performance) or Charisma (Deception) check, you can choose to add your Strength modifier instead of Charisma.

### Oria — Frostwrought Vitality

You gain resistance to cold damage. Whenever you succeed on a saving throw against an effect that would reduce your hit point maximum, you regain 1d6 hit points.

### Vinyot — Blood in the Spotlight

When you roll initiative, you can choose to gain advantage on your first attack roll that combat. Once you use this feature, you must finish a long rest to use it again.

### Arneria — Desert-Kin

You gain proficiency in Survival. If you have not moved more than half your speed on your turn, you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks until the start of your next turn.

### Al'Ar — Tide-Blooded

You can hold your breath for up to 10 minutes. In addition, when you take damage while submerged in water, you can use your reaction to move up to 15 feet in any direction underwater without provoking opportunity attacks.

## XI. Playing a Half-Orc

Consider:

- Which lineage terms do you use?
- Who taught you what Orcish ancestry means?
- What role have others mistaken for instinct?
- When does defiance feel free, and when does it remain reactive?
- How do you express anger safely?
- Which family history is publicly misunderstood?
- What form of strength do you refuse to perform?
- Who recognizes you without explanation?
- What did you choose that surprised everyone?
- What part of your life has nothing to do with ancestry?

A Half-Orc may be physically weak, conflict-avoidant, cheerful, aristocratic, scholarly, nonviolent, estranged from Orcish culture, or deeply rooted in it.

Mixed descent is ancestry.

It is not inner conflict by default.

---

## XII. Registry Advisory

> [!note] MDO Registry Advisory  
> Half-Orc lineage does not indicate aggression, military suitability, criminality, pain tolerance, anger, divided loyalty, or consent to discuss parentage.
>
> Use each person’s chosen lineage terminology. Registry convenience does not override self-designation.
>
> Physical restraint, pain assessment, employment, and threat evaluation must never be escalated solely because Orcish ancestry is visible or suspected.
