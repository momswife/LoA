## I. Lineage Overview

Arboreals are very rare tree-bodied Verdantfolk ranging from flexible sapling-sized travelers to immense citizens who root for decades or centuries.

Not every animate tree is an Arboreal, and not every Arboreal is ancient. Some walk throughout life; some alternate between mobile and rooted phases; others speak through attendants, grafts, dreams, or constructed vessels when their central body cannot travel.

---

## II. Origins and the Standing Witnesses

Arboreal traditions describe awakenings through divine weather, ancient pacts, slow natural emergence, deliberate cultivation, or transplantation from other worlds.

The **Standing Witnesses** were rooted citizens whose testimony ended the legal fiction that immobility meant absence from civic life. Court was brought to their groves, and each identified decades of land theft conducted while officials called them scenery.

Modern records distinguish age from authority. A thousand-year memory can be valuable, incomplete, prejudiced, or wrong.

New Arboreal awakenings are separated by years or generations. Rooted individuals are often missed by census workers, but even corrected estimates leave the lineage exceptionally uncommon.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Arboreal bodies may show bark, leaves, needles, flowers, fruit, cones, vines, moss partnerships, hollows, scars, and grafts. They sense vibration, light, moisture, soil chemistry, wind, and damage through distributed tissues.

Their movement may be slow, powerful, and seasonally variable. Rooting can provide nourishment and rest but may require legal access to soil, water, and safe space.

Carving initials, hanging signs, gathering fruit, breaking branches, or entering hollows without permission is bodily trespass.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Arboreal kinship includes groves, orchards, graft families, seed descendants, adopted travelers, root-neighbors, and mixed settlements.

Two rooted Arboreals may share soil for centuries without being married, politically aligned, or mentally connected. Conversely, a traveling sapling may remain a full voting member of a distant grove.

**Shade law** governs when bodily shelter becomes a public gift. Shade offered once does not establish permanent public access beneath someone’s canopy.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Arboreal cultures often value deliberation, continuity, hospitality, repair, and promises scaled to the time they require. Individuals are no more universally patient than stone buildings are universally peaceful.

Modern debate concerns stewardship. Some Arboreals accept duties toward the ecosystems entwined with them; others reject the expectation that a tree-bodied citizen must serve as unpaid forest guardian. Responsibility follows relationship and agreement, not appearance.

---

## VI. Arboreals in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

A few ancient estate Arboreals challenge titles that transferred the soil around them without their consent.

### Oria

Coniferous groves and geothermal root halls maintain avalanche records and winter refuge.

### Vinyot

The rare urban Arboreal lives in a reinforced plaza or mobile soil carriage while advertisers compete for access to their canopy.

### Arneria

Oasis and deep-root Arboreals participate in water councils where survival depends upon distinguishing use from ownership.

### Al'Ar

Mangrove, palm, and volcanic-forest communities shape stormbreaks and littoral settlements.

---

## VII. Arboreal Delvers

Mobile Arboreals serve as defenders, geomantic readers, climbers, rescuers, and living anchors. Their size, weight, and need for light or soil require route assessment.

Labyrinths sometimes offer false rooting chambers that drain memory through the roots. A rooted rest should always include a watch, an extraction plan, and consent about who may touch the root zone.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits represent a mobile Arboreal suitable for player characters and require DM approval until testing is complete.

### Creature Type, Size, and Speed

You are a Plant, Medium. Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Barkskin

When you are not wearing armor, your base Armor Class is 13 + your Dexterity modifier. You may use a shield and still gain this benefit.

### Rooted Rest

At the end of a short rest during which you touched natural earth or worked soil, you may roll one additional Hit Die and regain the resulting hit points without expending it. Once used, this trait cannot be used again until a long rest.

### Branching Reach

As a bonus action, you extend flexible branches. Until the end of your turn, your reach for interacting with objects is 10 feet, and you have advantage on the next Strength (Athletics) check you make to climb or hold a creature or object. Uses equal your proficiency bonus per long rest.

---

## X. Recognized Growth Traditions

### Canopy Arboreals

Canopy traditions emphasize wide crowns, aerial gardens, shade law, and relationships with Avianfolk and climbing peoples.

### Ironwood Arboreals

Ironwoods cultivate dense bodies, firebreak knowledge, and deliberate long-term construction.

### Mangrove Arboreals

Mangrove communities live across water and land, forming mobile-root settlements whose boundaries change with tide and storm.

---

## XI. Playing an Arboreal

Consider:

- Have you ever been rooted for a long period?
- Who may shelter beneath your canopy?
- Which land remembers you, and what do you owe it?
- What event is visible in your growth rings?
- Are any grafts part of your family history?
- What promise did you make too quickly?

Long life or tree form does not require serenity, wisdom, or ecological vocation.
