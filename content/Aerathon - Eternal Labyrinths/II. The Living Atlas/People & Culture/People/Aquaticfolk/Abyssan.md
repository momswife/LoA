## I. Lineage Overview

Abyssans are a provisional convergent Aquaticfolk lineage including Angler, Lantern, Gulper, and other peoples adapted to water below ordinary sunlight.

They are grouped through shared pressure, darkness, sparse food, bioluminescence, and deep-route history rather than close biological resemblance.

The classification may divide as community review continues.

Surface culture imagines the deep sea through absence:

- No light
- No air
- No warmth
- No visible horizon
- No ordinary road
- No easy rescue

Abyssans do not live inside an absence.

They live among pressure, current, chemical trace, vibration, living light, long stillness, vertical migration, and distances whose scale is hidden by darkness.

Their bodies are routinely described as frightening by people adapted to a bright and low-pressure world.

Abyssans ask:

> Why is the unfamiliar body called monstrous before the unfamiliar environment is called home?

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

In the **Account Below the Last Ray**, the Old Court carries fire downward.

Pressure extinguishes it.

The gods carry a star downward.

The star becomes distant and useless.

The first Abyssan creates a small light from their own body.

It does not illuminate the whole sea.

It illuminates enough to answer another light.

The Old Court asks why they did not make it larger.

The Abyssan replies:

> Because being seen by everyone is not the same as finding one another.

### Deep Routes

Abyssan routes are mapped through:

- Pressure layers
- Thermal vents
- Chemical trails
- Bioluminescent markers
- Current intervals
- Sound
- The movement of deep creatures
- Vertical distance from reliable food

Many routes cannot be represented safely on a flat chart.

Publication may attract hunters, mining crews, relic operations, or creatures that follow light.

### The Monstrous Registries

Earlier MDO and naval files placed numerous Abyssan communities in bestiaries.

Individuals were recorded as:

- Speaking anglers
- False lanterns
- Humanoid gapes
- Trench mimics
- Shattersea-tainted lures

Some records describe actual predators.

Others describe citizens responding to armed intrusion.

The current filing does not assume that every deep humanoid is Abyssan. It requires sapience assessment before bestiary classification where safe communication is possible.

### Shattersea Contact

Some Abyssan communities maintain routes near [[The Shattersea|Shattersea]] breaches.

This does not make them Shattersea-born or corrupted.

Deep adaptation predates many known breaches. Claims that unusual bodies prove planar taint are unsupported.

## III. Body and Deepwater Form

Abyssan bodies vary greatly.

Common features include:

- Bioluminescent organs
- Large or light-sensitive eyes
- Reduced eyes in some communities
- Wide mouths or expandable structures
- Pressure-adapted tissues
- Dark, pale, translucent, or reflective skin
- Fins and tails
- Gills and air-adapted respiration
- Sensitivity to vibration and chemical trace

Most player-character Abyssans are Medium.

### Pressure

Abyssans tolerate deepwater pressure that would injure most peoples.

They are not immune to every crushing force, magical compression, structural collapse, or instantaneous pressure transition.

Rapid ascent can be dangerous despite general adaptation.

### Light

Surface daylight may cause pain, disorientation, or sensory exhaustion.

Tinted lenses, veils, deep hoods, dim interiors, and gradual adaptation are common.

This filing does not impose a universal mechanical sunlight penalty because individual tolerance varies widely.

### Living Lanterns

Bioluminescence may communicate, lure prey, identify kin, mark route, perform art, warn, or provide ordinary light.

It is not always voluntary. Stress responses vary by person.

Covering another person’s light without consent may interfere with speech or safety.

### Feeding

Abyssan bodies are adapted to inconsistent resources.

Surface stories exaggerate this into endless hunger.

Diet and appetite vary by sublineage and individual. Neither establishes moral disposition.

## IV. Society and Kinship

Abyssan communities include lantern fields, trench houses, vent circles, vertical routes, drift families, solitary lights, and settlements built around rare reliable resources.

### Answering Light

A distant light may be answered without approach.

This allows:

- Recognition
- Warning
- Privacy
- Route confirmation
- Mourning
- Proof that another person survives

The right not to approach is important. In a resource-sparse environment, social recognition cannot always imply hospitality.

### The Dark Courtesy

Many communities extinguish or shield light when entering another group’s water until invited to display.

The custom is called **Dark Courtesy**.

It prevents involuntary identification and avoids attracting danger.

Surface officials have misread it as concealment.

### Sparse-Resource Ethics

Abyssan sharing traditions tend to be explicit.

What can be spared?

For how long?

What must remain for return?

Generosity without calculation may kill the giver later. Calculation is therefore not treated as absence of compassion.

### Surface Kin

Abyssans living near the surface may create chosen families with harbor, ship, reef, and night communities.

Some never return deepward.

Others require darkness and pressure access to feel physically and culturally whole.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Abyssan communities commonly admire:

- Deliberate visibility
- Resource honesty
- Patience
- Pressure endurance
- Precise response
- Respect for unknown water
- Small reliable signals
- Recognition without forced approach

These values cast long shadows.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Patience | Waiting through uncertain conditions | Failing to act before opportunity vanishes |
| Resource caution | Preserving future survival | Withholding when aid is possible |
| Controlled visibility | Protecting privacy and route | Concealing danger |
| Endurance | Functioning under pressure | Treating suffering as identity |
| Distance respect | Allowing autonomous relationship | Avoiding intimacy |
| Small signals | Communicating efficiently | Expecting outsiders to notice too little |

A recurring Abyssan fear is forced illumination: being made visible, legible, and available in an environment where visibility has always carried consequence.

### Internal Divisions

Modern Abyssans debate:

- Whether one convergent lineage can represent deepwater diversity
- Whether route lights should be shared with the Ministry
- How bestiary misclassification should be repaired
- Whether surface accommodation encourages dangerous migration
- Whether Shattersea-adjacent communities face discriminatory research
- When Dark Courtesy conflicts with civic identification
- Whether deep resource law can survive commercial extraction

## VI. Abyssans in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemancian Abyssans inhabit deep lakes, western trenches, river caverns, mines with flooded access, and dim urban districts.

They often become night workers, archivists, divers, and keepers of underground water.

### Oria

Oric Abyssans live in deep northern waters, House reservoirs, thermal vents, and pressure research stations.

Their expertise is essential to deep engineering. Bright forge environments require careful access design.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Abyssans work in salvage, finance, deep mining review, night performance, shipping, and rare-material regulation.

The League markets their light while seeking access to routes communities deliberately kept dark.

### Arneria

Arnerian Abyssans inhabit volcanic lakes, deep coastal shelves, sacred caverns, and Shattersea-adjacent waters.

Some temple traditions treat their lights as omens. Abyssan councils resist any theology that turns citizens into public signs.

### Al’Ar

Al’Ari Abyssans live beneath island shelves, in trench routes, deep reef settlements, storm caverns, and night harbor districts.

Mixed-depth councils in Al’Ar are the most developed systems for representing communities that cannot safely attend one shared physical chamber.

---

## VII. Abyssans and Delving

Abyssans serve as deep scouts, pressure specialists, light communicators, patient sentries, Shattersea observers, and navigators.

Their advantages include amphibious breathing, exceptional darkvision, pressure tolerance, living light, and concealment in darkness.

Labyrinths exploit deep adaptation through false pressure, lights that answer incorrectly, empty signals, sudden brightness, and creatures that imitate a lost companion’s pattern.

A common warning states:

> The light answering you may have learned the answer by watching someone die.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

The following mechanics are an initial playtest version.

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Constitution score increases by 2.

### Size

Your size is Medium.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

You have a swimming speed of 40 feet.

### Amphibious

You can breathe air and water.

### Deep Darkvision

You can see in dim light within 120 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light.

You can’t discern color in darkness.

### Pressure-Born

You have resistance to cold damage.

You ignore harmful effects caused solely by ordinary natural deepwater pressure.

### Living Lantern

As a bonus action, you can shed bright light in a radius up to 20 feet and dim light for the same distance beyond it.

You choose the color and radius and may extinguish or alter the light as a bonus action.

### Deep Stillness

While in dim light or darkness, you may use a bonus action to become invisible until the start of your next turn.

The invisibility ends early if you move, make an attack, cast a spell, or emit bright light with Living Lantern.

You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Angler

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Lurelight.** As a bonus action, choose one creature within 30 feet that can see your Living Lantern.

It must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to perceive creatures other than you until the start of your next turn.

The DC equals `8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier`.

You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Lantern

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Intelligence score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Signal Sequence.** You can communicate simple concepts silently through bioluminescent sequence to any creature within 60 feet that understands Abyssan Light.

You learn the `light` cantrip. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability.

### Gulper

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Expansive Maw.** Your bite is a natural weapon that deals `1d6 + your Strength modifier` piercing damage.

When you hit a creature no more than one size larger than you with this attack, you may attempt to grapple it as a bonus action.

You can attempt this bonus-action grapple a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

### Allemance — Deep-Lake Memory

You gain proficiency in Survival.

You have advantage on checks made to navigate flooded caves, deep lakes, and submerged inland structures.

### Oria — Vent Engineer

You gain proficiency with tinker’s tools.

You can identify the direction of the nearest significant natural or mechanical heat source within 300 feet while underwater.

### Vinyot — Licensed Signal

You gain proficiency in Performance or Deception.

You have advantage on checks made to prove that a copied light sequence, signal, or visual identity is false.

### Arneria — Omen Refused

You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened by a creature that can see your Living Lantern.

### Al’Ar — Stormdark

When you take lightning damage, your Living Lantern may remain active without requiring a bonus action and sheds dim light only until the end of your next turn.

During that time, you may use Deep Stillness even while the lantern remains visible.

## XII. Playing an Abyssan

Consider:

- Who recognizes your light?
- When do you choose darkness?
- What resource can you safely share?
- How has a surface person described your body?
- What pressure feels like home?
- Do you accept the Abyssan classification?
- What signal should never be copied?
- How do you experience bright daylight?
- What does distance mean in your relationships?
- What would make you reveal a protected deep route?

An Abyssan may be light-loving, shallow-water raised, socially expansive, warm-seeking, physically delicate, or eager for attention.

Their lineage survives below ordinary light.

It does not belong to darkness as metaphor.
