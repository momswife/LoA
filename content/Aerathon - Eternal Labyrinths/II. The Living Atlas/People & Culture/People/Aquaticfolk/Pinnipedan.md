## I. Lineage Overview

Pinnipedans are Seal, Sea Lion, and Walrus Aquaticfolk shaped by life between water and shore.

They breathe air, swim with exceptional speed, and depend upon land, ice, rock, or stable platforms for ordinary rest, birth, craft, meeting, and portions of community life.

This makes the littoral zone central to Pinnipedan politics.

Surface law often treats a beach as empty until someone builds upon it.

Marine law may treat the same space as a route rather than a residence.

Pinnipedans understand it as both.

A haul-out ground may look like bare rock for part of the year and become a dense neighborhood when migration returns. An ice margin may move continually while retaining the same social function. A dock used by ships during daylight may become a communal sleeping place after the last bell.

Their culture asks:

> Must a home remain occupied every day to remain a home?

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

In the **Story of the Two Safe Places**, the Old Court asks the first Pinnipedan whether sea or land is home.

They answer sea.

A storm drives them ashore.

They answer land.

Hunger draws them back into the water.

The gods ask again.

The Pinnipedan refuses the choice:

> Home is where I may leave without losing the right to return.

### Haul-Out Rights

Ancient Pinnipedan communities maintained recurring shore sites through use, scent, marker stone, song, cleaning, and seasonal agreement.

The sites were rarely occupied continuously.

Expanding ports described them as abandoned and built:

- Warehouses
- Fortifications
- Estates
- Ship ramps
- Tourist platforms
- Waste outlets

Returning communities were then accused of trespass.

Modern charter law recognizes **recurring occupancy**. A site may remain inhabited in law while its people are seasonally absent.

### The Bell-Rock Agreements

Mixed harbor communities developed Bell-Rock Agreements dividing time rather than territory.

A platform might serve commerce through the morning, public gathering at dusk, Pinnipedan rest overnight, and repair at low tide.

These agreements influenced modern amphibious district law in Al’Ar.

They work only when every party possesses power to enforce the schedule.

## III. Body and Littoral Form

Pinnipedans commonly possess:

- Dense bodies
- Smooth fur or thick hide
- Flipper-like hands and feet
- Strong swimming musculature
- Air-breathing lungs
- Insulating tissue
- Sensitive whiskers
- Bodies capable of land movement with varying ease

Most are Medium.

### Breath

Pinnipedans breathe air and can hold their breath for extended periods.

They can drown.

Reliable surface access is required in submerged structures.

### Whisker Sense

Many Pinnipedans detect current, wake, and nearby movement through whiskers.

Cutting, binding, or decorating whiskers without consent interferes with sensation.

### Heat

Cold tolerance does not mean universal comfort in cold.

Warm-water Seal and Sea Lion communities may experience different needs. Thick-bodied Pinnipedans can overheat rapidly on land, especially in armor.

### Haul-Out Rest

Pinnipedans recover effectively when allowed stable communal rest.

Noise, crowd displacement, interrupted breath, and repeated forced movement can prevent recovery even if a nominal resting place exists.

## IV. Society and Kinship

Pinnipedan communities include rookeries, haul houses, shore circles, ice families, harbor neighborhoods, vessel crews, and migrating routes.

### The Kept Place

When leaving seasonally, a community names a **Kept Place**.

Care may be entrusted to:

- A neighboring household
- A mixed harbor council
- A marker keeper
- A surface family
- A rotating watch
- The returning route itself

Keeping does not grant ownership.

The distinction is repeated because history repeatedly ignored it.

### Public Rest

Pinnipedans often rest together in visible groups.

Outsiders mistake public rest for public access.

Sleeping bodies may not be touched, photographed magically, stepped across, moved, or turned into entertainment without permission.

### Noise

Sea Lion and many mixed communities use loud vocal communication.

Seal communities may favor quieter tactile or rhythmic signals.

Walrus gatherings can carry through ice and structure.

No one acoustic style represents the lineage.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Pinnipedan communities commonly admire:

- Return
- Communal rest
- Adaptability between environments
- Defense of shared shore
- Play
- Strong public voice
- Seasonal memory
- Hospitality with clear limits

These values cast long shadows.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Return | Preserving long relationships | Refusing changed conditions |
| Communal rest | Making safety collective | Leaving little room for solitude |
| Play | Building trust and skill | Ignoring someone’s discomfort |
| Loud advocacy | Making displacement visible | Drowning out quieter members |
| Shore defense | Protecting recurring homes | Excluding other necessary users |
| Adaptability | Moving between worlds | Being expected to tolerate inaccessible systems |

A recurring Pinnipedan fear is returning to find that absence was interpreted as surrender.

### Internal Divisions

Modern Pinnipedans debate:

- Whether recurring occupancy should be permanently mapped
- How much tourism haul-out sites may allow
- Whether public rest customs remain safe
- How warm-water communities fit a cold-coast stereotype
- Whether Bell-Rock time-sharing protects or fragments sovereignty
- How fixed borders should treat moving ice
- Whether vessel crews can become haul communities

## VI. Pinnipedans in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemancian Pinnipedans live along western coasts, rivers, islands, and harbor towns.

They maintain ferry landings, flood shelters, fisheries, public baths, and recurring shoreline festivals.

### Oria

Oric Pinnipedans occupy cold ports, ice margins, deep lakes, and House industrial coasts.

Walrus and Seal communities are prominent in ice rescue, structural listening, and winter transport.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Pinnipedans work in shipping, performance, harbor labor, salvage, tourism, insurance, and maritime law.

Their public image is profitable. Their actual rest sites are frequently pressured by the same industries.

### Arneria

Arnerian Pinnipedans inhabit warm coasts, river mouths, temple harbors, and monsoon shores.

Seasonal return may be marked through immersion vows and the repainting of boundary stones.

### Al’Ar

Al’Ar contains extensive amphibious districts shaped by Pinnipedan time-sharing practice.

Sea Lion performers, Seal navigators, and Walrus shipwrights are common, though no profession belongs to one sublineage.

---

## VII. Pinnipedans and Delving

Pinnipedans serve as rescue swimmers, littoral scouts, cold-water specialists, close defenders, acoustic sentries, and expedition morale keepers.

Their advantages include swimming, long breath, cold tolerance, strong recovery, and sublineage movement or support.

Labyrinths exploit shore instinct through false air ledges, platforms that vanish during rest, changing waterlines, and routes that classify the same place differently on return.

A common warning states:

> Test the shore before you trust it to remain shore.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

The following mechanics are an initial playtest version.

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Constitution score increases by 2.

### Size

Your size is Medium.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

You have a swimming speed of 40 feet.

### Long Breath

You can hold your breath for up to 30 minutes.

### Littoral Endurance

You have resistance to cold damage.

You also have advantage on saving throws made to avoid exhaustion caused by swimming, cold water, or exposure while wet.

### Haul-Out Recovery

When you spend one or more Hit Dice at the end of a short rest, you may reroll one of those dice and use the new result.

Once you use this trait, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Seal

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Slip Through.** When you take the Disengage action, you may move through the space of any creature one size larger than you as though it were difficult terrain.

You may take the Disengage action as a bonus action a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Sea Lion

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Carrying Bark.** When a creature within 30 feet that can hear you fails a saving throw, you may use your reaction to add `1d4` to its result, potentially turning failure into success.

You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Walrus

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Powerful Build.** You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.

**Tusks.** Your tusks are natural weapons that deal `1d6 + your Strength modifier` piercing damage.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

### Allemance — Kept Landing

You gain proficiency in Survival.

After spending 10 minutes examining a shore or landing, you know whether it has been occupied by a group within the past seven days and the approximate size of that group.

### Oria — Ice Listener

You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Intelligence checks made to detect weakness, movement, or hollow space through ice.

### Vinyot — Scheduled Access

You gain proficiency in Persuasion.

You have advantage on checks made to negotiate temporary access, shared use, berth time, or recurring occupancy.

### Arneria — Return Vow

You know the `resistance` cantrip.

Once per long rest, when you enter a space you occupied during the previous round, you may gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier.

### Al’Ar — Breakwater Voice

You know the `thunderclap` cantrip.

When you reach 3rd level, you can cast `fog cloud` once per long rest. Charisma is your spellcasting ability.

## XII. Playing a Pinnipedan

Consider:

- Which place remains yours while you are absent?
- Who keeps it?
- Do you rest comfortably in public?
- What does seasonal return mean to you?
- Which environment feels more vulnerable: water or land?
- How loud must you become to be heard?
- What game taught you a serious skill?
- Who may wake you?
- What shore has changed beyond return?
- When does shared space need a firm boundary?

A Pinnipedan may be solitary, quiet, inland-born, poor at swimming, heat-seeking, or uninterested in seasonal travel.

Their lineage lives between environments.

It does not make either one temporary.
