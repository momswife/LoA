## I. Lineage Overview

Batrachians are Frog and Toad Aquaticfolk shaped by amphibious life, metamorphosis, powerful legs, vocal resonance, and wetlands that landbound governments repeatedly mistake for unused ground.

They include quiet burrowers, arboreal climbers, river leapers, poisonous-skinned families, and people whose adult bodies retain features outsiders associate with earlier life stages.

---

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

Batrachian traditions begin in many marshes. The oldest common story describes a divine court asking who owns land that is neither reliably land nor water. The first Chorus answers: those who remain responsible when either description becomes inconvenient.

The **Drowned Boundary Cases** document wetland communities displaced because maps recorded only the dry season.

---

## III. Body and Environmental Needs

Batrachians possess permeable skin, strong jumping legs, broad mouths, varied hands and feet, and respiration combining lungs, gills in some forms, and skin exchange.

Skin contact is medically significant. Oils, perfumes, salts, metals, and casual touch can cause injury. Poisonous or irritating secretions are bodily defense, not public weaponry.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Households include choruses, burrow neighborhoods, tree-platform circles, spawning kin, adoptive pools, and seasonal gatherings.

A chorus is a civic practice as often as music. The **Answered Voice** rule requires public calls for participation to leave audible space for refusal, dissent, and nonvocal response.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Batrachian cultures admire responsiveness, transformation, humor, environmental honesty, patience through seasonal change, and voices used to locate one another.

Their shadow is compulsory visibility. Quiet members may be accused of abandoning the chorus; transformed adults may be expected to narrate private bodily history.

---

## VI. Batrachian Peoples in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Garden marshes and drainage courts employ Batrachian expertise while restricting natural wetland growth.

### Oria

Hot-spring, meltwater, and hibernation traditions support cold-climate communities.

### Vinyot

Music, sport, and rain-broadcast industries commercialize voices and leaps.

### Arneria

Oasis frogs and burrowing toads maintain seasonal water records ignored by permanent maps.

### Al'Ar

Rainforest and volcanic-wetland communities are numerous throughout Al'Ar.

---

## VII. Batrachian Delvers

Batrachian delvers excel at vertical bursts, wet-dry transition, poison awareness, acoustic signaling, and detecting water change.

Their leaps require landing space. Crews must not confuse spectacular movement with safe movement, especially when a Labyrinth alters distance midair.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits are an initial Aerathon playtest package and require DM approval until table testing is complete.

### Creature Type and Size

You are a Humanoid, Small or Medium.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet and your swimming speed is 30 feet.

### Amphibious

You can breathe air and water.

### Standing Leap

Your long jump is up to 25 feet and your high jump up to 15 feet, with or without a running start, limited by available movement.

### Wetland Sense

Gain proficiency in Nature or Perception. You have advantage on checks to detect contamination or sudden water-level change.

### Skin Defense

Choose slippery or warning skin. Slippery grants advantage to escape grapples; warning grants resistance to poison damage.

---

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Frog

Frog Batrachians are commonly powerful leapers and include river, pond, and ground-dwelling communities.

### Toad

Toad Batrachians often possess sturdier bodies, burrowing traditions, and stronger tolerance for seasonal dryness.

### Tree Frog

Tree Frog Batrachians have adhesive digits, climbing adaptations, and canopy-wetland cultures.

### Dart Frog

Dart Frog communities maintain bright warning traditions and careful law around bodily toxins and unwanted harvesting.

---

## XI. Playing a Batrachian

Consider:

- What does your chorus sound like?
- Which transformation remains private?
- What chemical threatens your skin?
- Who drew your wetland incorrectly?
- How do you signal refusal?

A character may embrace, reinterpret, or reject every tradition described above. Environment and body create circumstances, not personality.
