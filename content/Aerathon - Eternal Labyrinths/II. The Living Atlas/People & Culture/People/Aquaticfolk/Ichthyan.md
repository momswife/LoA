## I. Lineage Overview

Ichthyans are bony-fish Aquaticfolk encompassing reef, river, open-water, eel-bodied, and other lineages not represented by Selachians or Abyssans.

They are among the most physiologically diverse peoples in Aerathon. Public registries once compressed thousands of body forms into “fishfolk,” obscuring major differences in salinity, temperature, depth, respiration, social practice, and mobility.

---

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

Ichthyan oral histories describe early shoals receiving will at different waters rather than one creation site. Reef mosaics, river bone-script, and open-water route songs support multiple ancient centers.

The **Uncounted School** remembers communities erased when officials counted bodies from above the surface and declared moving populations ownerless.

---

## III. Body and Environmental Needs

Ichthyans may possess scales, fins, gills, lateral-line senses, barbels, tails, luminous markings, and bodies ranging from compact reef forms to long eel-like frames.

Many are amphibious for limited or indefinite periods; others require regular immersion. Gill privacy is essential. Touching, exposing, or placing substances near gills without consent is dangerous.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Households include schools, dens, route families, reef blocks, river councils, spawning kin, adoption circles, and solitary people whose membership is maintained acoustically or through return.

Schooling is cooperative awareness, not loss of individuality. The **Named Turn** requires every participant in a group maneuver to retain the right to break formation.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Ichthyan cultures admire awareness, responsive coordination, environmental literacy, color and motion language, and collective escape.

Their shadow is consensus pressure. A person who notices danger first may be blamed for disrupting formation; a person who leaves a school may be treated as rejecting all kinship.

---

## VI. Ichthyan Peoples in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Freshwater canals and ornamental ponds create visibility alongside harmful decorative stereotypes.

### Oria

Lake, fjord, and under-ice Ichthyans develop specialized temperature and access law.

### Vinyot

Reef imports, aquarium exploitation, and broadcast schools drive major rights campaigns.

### Arneria

River and oasis peoples negotiate water extraction with distant land authorities.

### Al'Ar

Ichthyans form some of the largest and most internally diverse Al'Ari aquatic communities.

---

## VII. Ichthyan Delvers

Ichthyan delvers serve as current readers, scouts, formation coordinators, hazard detectors, medics, and route specialists. Their lateral-line perception can reveal nearby movement in water.

Labyrinth currents may simulate collective alarm, causing a whole group to turn toward manufactured danger. Crews should preserve independent signals.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits are an initial Aerathon playtest package and require DM approval until table testing is complete.

### Creature Type and Size

You are a Humanoid, Small or Medium.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 25 feet and your swimming speed is 35 feet.

### Amphibious

You can breathe air and water.

### Lateral Sense

While submerged, you know the location of moving creatures within 10 feet that share the water with you, unless behind total cover.

### School Turn

When an ally within 10 feet moves away from a hostile creature, use your reaction to move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. Use this proficiency bonus times per long rest.

### Water Acclimation

Choose freshwater, saltwater, or variable water. You have advantage on saves against environmental stress within that water type.

---

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Reef Ichthyans

Reef peoples favor maneuverability, color language, and dense settlements where sight lines change by the hour.

### River Ichthyans

River peoples preserve seasonal routes, upstream law, and relationships between source, floodplain, and mouth.

### Open-Water Ichthyans

Open-Water peoples are adapted to endurance, distant acoustic reference, and communities that travel without visible landmarks.

### Eel Ichthyans

Eel peoples possess elongated bodies, strong electrochemical awareness in some families, and den-centered traditions.

---

## XI. Playing an Ichthyan

Consider:

- What formation can you leave safely?
- Which water type sustains you?
- How does your body signal stress?
- Who counts your school?
- What danger did you notice first?

A character may embrace, reinterpret, or reject every tradition described above. Environment and body create circumstances, not personality.
