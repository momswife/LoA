## I. Lineage Overview

Testudinans are Sea Turtle Aquaticfolk shaped by long migration, protective shells, nesting shores, route memory, and the need to connect distant water with particular land.

They were formerly classified as Chelonian Beastfolk.

The reclassification followed sustained community petition. Testudinan bodies, history, law, and self-identification are principally marine, even though their dependence upon nesting shore led landbound registrars to treat them as terrestrial people who happened to swim.

Not every chelonian community accepts the change.

Freshwater, terrapin, and terrestrial tortoise peoples may identify differently and are not automatically assigned to Testudinan lineage.

Testudinans are widely associated with memory.

Their shells can preserve inscription. Their routes may span lifetimes. Their return to a distant shore can outlast states that claimed the coast between departures.

The lineage asks:

> If a place is visited only once in a generation, how can law recognize that the return never stopped mattering?

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

In the **Account of the Borrowed Shore**, the Old Court gives the first Testudinan a shell and tells them they now carry home.

The Testudinan travels the sea.

When the time comes to lay the next generation, no shell is sufficient. They must return to warm sand, moon, tide, and a shore shared with countless other lives.

The Testudinan tells the gods:

> Shelter is carried. Home is relationship.

### The Shell-Ledger Houses

The ancient [[Shell-Ledger Houses]] developed one of Aerathon’s most influential legal and archival traditions.

Members inscribed histories, contracts, memories, and obligations upon shell using Salt-Ink. Their Houses served as families, guilds, mediators, and living archives.

Modern Ministry record law owes much to Shell-Ledger practice:

- Authenticity through witnessed inscription
- Records carried with accountable custodians
- The relationship between memory and public law
- Protection against silent alteration
- The understanding that a record can outlive the office that issued it

The Houses also produced severe practices.

Willful erasure could be punished through **Sealing**, filling a person’s shell record so no new inscription could be added. Modern Testudinans condemn compulsory inscription and bodily punishment while continuing to debate how the tradition may be reclaimed.

### Nesting Shore Law

Testudinan routes frequently end at specific shores used across generations.

Ports, resorts, fortifications, and private estates claimed many during seasonal absence.

Modern nesting-shore recognition treats the route, nearshore water, beach, and safe inland margin as one protected system.

## III. Body and Shelled Form

Testudinans commonly possess:

- Protective carapace and plastron
- Flipper-like or webbed limbs
- Beaked mouths
- Air-breathing lungs
- Long breath capacity
- Strong swimming musculature
- Sensitivity to magnetic and environmental route cues
- Bodies capable of slow but reliable land movement

Most are Medium.

### Shell

A Testudinan shell is living body.

It is not clothing, armor that may be removed, public writing space, or a surface others may knock upon.

Shell care may include polishing, oiling, repair, magic, paint, fabric, protective fittings, or deliberate weathering.

### Inscription

Some Testudinans continue shell inscription.

Modern ethical practice requires:

- Adult informed consent
- Reversible surface media where possible
- Independent record copies
- No legal penalty for refusing inscription
- No assumption that visible marks are public text

Reading another person’s shell without permission may constitute bodily and informational intrusion.

### Breath and Cold

Testudinans breathe air.

Long breath does not grant water breathing.

Temperature affects metabolism, movement, rest, and development. Leatherback communities possess unusual cold tolerance, but it should not be generalized.

## IV. Society and Kinship

Testudinan communities include nesting houses, route families, shell guilds, current lines, archive circles, reef settlements, and mixed shore councils.

### Return Without Possession

Traditional law often separates return rights from exclusive ownership.

A Testudinan community may possess the right to cross, nest, protect, and gather at a shore while sharing other uses outside critical periods.

The arrangement requires enforceable limits. “Shared” has often meant everyone else uses the site continually while Testudinans absorb the disruption.

### The Unwritten Plate

Some modern Testudinans leave one portion of the shell deliberately unmarked.

The **Unwritten Plate** represents a part of the self not owed to archive, lineage, government, or descendant.

For some it is political.

For others it is simply aesthetically pleasing.

### Long-Route Kinship

Family may be maintained through scheduled convergence rather than continual residence.

A relative absent for years may still carry a predictable place in the return. Landbound custody systems have repeatedly mistaken this pattern for abandonment.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Testudinan communities commonly admire:

- Reliable return
- Long memory
- Protection
- Deliberate record
- Patience across distance
- Respect for bodily history
- Route stewardship
- Continuity without permanent possession

These values cast long shadows.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Memory | Preserving accountability | Allowing no private reinvention |
| Return | Maintaining distant kinship | Binding descendants to one route |
| Protection | Providing refuge | Withdrawing from needed change |
| Record | Preventing erasure | Turning a body into public property |
| Patience | Acting across generations | Delaying present repair |
| Continuity | Preserving nesting and passage | Treating ancestry as exclusive entitlement |

A recurring Testudinan fear is returning correctly to a place that no longer recognizes them.

### Internal Divisions

Modern Testudinans debate:

- Whether reclassification as Aquaticfolk represents all chelonian communities
- Whether shell inscription can be separated from coercive history
- Whether nesting shores should allow commercial use
- How much route knowledge should be public
- Whether descendants may abandon an ancestral return
- Whether the Shell-Ledger Houses deserve revival
- How fixed archives can preserve songs, currents, and moving law

## VI. Testudinans in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemancian Testudinans live along river mouths, western shores, lakes, and old trade crossings.

They are archivists, ferry keepers, gardeners, jurists, and protectors of recurring shore use.

### Oria

Oric Testudinans are uncommon but present in cold ports, heated House waters, deep lakes, and archival institutions.

Leatherback communities are especially prominent in northern navigation and cold-water research.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Testudinans work in law, contracts, banking, shipping, insurance, archives, and relic provenance.

Their bodies and historical reputation attract institutions seeking “incorruptible” records. Testudinan advocates insist that credibility must never require bodily inscription.

### Arneria

Arneria contains ancient Shell-Ledger sites, wetland Houses, nesting coasts, and Salt-Ink traditions.

The region remains the major center of debate about inscription, Sealing, and archival inheritance.

### Al’Ar

Al’Ari Testudinans maintain island routes, reef archives, storm shelters, nesting shore councils, and ship-linked families.

Their long route memory is essential after storms erase visible landmarks.

---

## VII. Testudinans and Delving

Testudinans serve as defensive explorers, route keepers, archivists, salvage judges, swimmers, and relic custodians.

Their advantages include natural armor, long breath, navigation, and sublineage environmental traits.

Labyrinths exploit shell and return through inscriptions that appear on the body, false ancestral shores, rooms that claim prior occupancy, and routes that lead correctly to the wrong version of a place.

A common warning states:

> A perfect return may still arrive in another history.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

The following mechanics are an initial playtest version.

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Constitution score increases by 2.

### Size

Your size is Medium.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

You have a swimming speed of 35 feet.

### Long Breath

You can hold your breath for up to 4 hours.

### Natural Armor

When you aren’t wearing armor, your base AC equals 13 + your Dexterity modifier.

You can use a shield and still gain this benefit.

### Route Memory

You have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made to retrace a route you previously traveled.

You cannot become lost through nonmagical means while retracing such a route.

### Shell Brace

As a bonus action, you brace within your shell.

Until the start of your next turn, your speed becomes 0 and you gain a +2 bonus to AC and Strength saving throws.

You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Green Turtle

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Meadow Reader.** You have advantage on Nature and Survival checks concerning aquatic plants and shallow-water ecosystems.

You know the `druidcraft` cantrip.

### Loggerhead

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 1.

**Powerful Jaw.** Your beak is a natural weapon that deals `1d6 + your Strength modifier` slashing damage.

When you hit an object with it, you deal double damage.

### Leatherback

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 1.

**Cold Route.** You have resistance to cold damage and ignore the effects of extreme cold while immersed in water.

### Hawksbill

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Intelligence score increases by 1.

**Reef Passage.** Moving through nonmagical difficult terrain caused by coral, rock, wreckage, or aquatic vegetation costs you no extra movement.

You gain proficiency in Investigation.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

### Allemance — Shared Crossing

You gain proficiency in Persuasion.

When negotiating passage or temporary shared use, you may add `1d4` to one ability check. Once used, this trait refreshes after a short or long rest.

### Oria — Heated Archive

You gain proficiency with smith’s tools or calligrapher’s supplies.

You have advantage on checks made to protect records or delicate objects from cold and moisture.

### Vinyot — Provenance Shell

You gain proficiency in History.

After examining an object for 1 minute, you may determine whether it has been repaired, deliberately altered, or had identifying marks removed.

### Arneria — Salt-Ink Tradition

You gain proficiency with calligrapher’s supplies.

You may create writing that remains legible underwater and cannot be removed by nonmagical water, ordinary abrasion, or heat below an open flame.

### Al’Ar — Storm Return

When you fail a Wisdom (Survival) check to navigate, you may reroll it.

Once you use this trait, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

## XII. Playing a Testudinan

Consider:

- What route calls you back?
- How do you feel about shell inscription?
- What part of your life remains unwritten?
- Which shore failed to recognize your return?
- What does protection cost your movement?
- Do you distinguish shelter from home?
- Which archive do you trust?
- Would you abandon an ancestral route?
- Who may read your shell?
- What must continuity change in order to survive?

A Testudinan may be restless, forgetful, inland-born, uninscribed, impatient, or uninterested in ancestral return.

Their lineage carries shell and route.

It does not make the body a public archive.
