## I. Lineage Overview

Crustaceans are Aquaticfolk with articulated shells, multiple limbs or specialized appendages, and life histories shaped by molting, tidal structures, and the vulnerable intervals between old armor and new.

They include Crab, Lobster, Mantis Shrimp, and other related peoples. Their body plans vary extensively; public infrastructure designed around a two-handed upright default often excludes them even when submerged access exists.

---

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

Ancient shell middens once described as food waste contain inscribed molts, family marks, legal tallies, and records of passage. Their recognition transformed Aquaticfolk archaeology.

The **Soft-Shell Armistices** protected molting communities from predation and warfare during known vulnerable seasons. Modern law extends the principle to medical privacy and employment leave.

---

## III. Body and Environmental Needs

Crustaceans may possess carapaces, claws, antennae, stalked or compound eyes, swimmerets, powerful tails, and varied limb arrangements.

Molting is metabolically expensive and personally variable. A shed shell remains personal biological material. It may be memorialized, recycled, eaten ritually, destroyed, or retained; outsiders have no claim.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Households include molt circles, reef blocks, burrow towns, armored caravans, craft guilds, and tidal councils.

The **New Shell Silence** grants a person control over visitors and public duties after molting. Care is offered through prepared water, mineral food, shelter, and the absence of demands.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Crustacean cultures admire preparation, structural thinking, boundaries, patience, renewal, and strength that acknowledges vulnerability.

Their shadow is armor expectation. A person known for defense may be denied fear, softness, or the right to withdraw while recovering.

---

## VI. Crustacean Peoples in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Canal works and armor guilds host visible Crab communities and contested markets in shed shell.

### Oria

Cold-water Lobster towns maintain deep stone shelters and mineral stores.

### Vinyot

Mantis Shrimp athletes and broadcasters fight ownership claims over color perception and striking ability.

### Arneria

Salt basins and seasonal crustacean pools depend upon precise water-right agreements.

### Al'Ar

Reef Crustaceans shape architecture, salvage, food systems, and storm-hardening across Al'Ar.

---

## VII. Crustacean Delvers

Crustacean delvers are defenders, breachers, structural readers, color analysts, and confined-space specialists. Natural armor reduces some risks without replacing protective equipment.

Molting status is private operational health information. A crew plans around capacity without broadcasting why it changed.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits are an initial Aerathon playtest package and require DM approval until table testing is complete.

### Creature Type and Size

You are a Humanoid, Small or Medium.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 25 feet and your swimming speed is 30 feet.

### Amphibious

You can breathe air and water.

### Natural Armor

When not wearing armor, your Armor Class equals 13 + your Dexterity modifier. You can use a shield and retain this benefit.

### Grasping Claw

One natural claw deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage. After hitting, you may attempt to grapple the target as a bonus action proficiency bonus times per long rest.

### Tidal Brace

You have advantage on checks and saves against being moved while touching a stable surface.

---

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Crab

Crab peoples excel in lateral movement, burrow and reef architecture, and intertidal communities.

### Lobster

Lobster peoples are often cold-water adapted and maintain long-lived deep-shelter and mineral traditions.

### Mantis Shrimp

Mantis Shrimp peoples possess extraordinary color perception and rapid striking appendages requiring disciplined care.

### Cleaner Shrimp

Cleaner Shrimp communities are associated with medicine and mutual care but reject assumptions of compulsory healing service.

---

## XI. Playing a Crustacean

Consider:

- What happens to your shed shell?
- Who may visit during recovery?
- What does armor conceal?
- Which limb do strangers misunderstand?
- What boundary keeps care from becoming access?

A character may embrace, reinterpret, or reject every tradition described above. Environment and body create circumstances, not personality.
