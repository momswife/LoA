# Bat Reclassification Record

**Former Classification:** Mythicfolk — Astralborn Bat  
**Current Classification:** [[Chiropterid]] [[‎ Avianfolk|Avianfolk]]  
**Registry Status:** Superseded Record Retained for Link Continuity  
**Mechanical Status:** Use Current Chiropterid Lineage Rules  
**Date of Reclassification:** 2nd Cycle, 3388 A.D.

---

## I. Reclassification Notice

The people formerly recorded in this directory as **Bats** are now recognized as **[[Chiropterid|Chiropterids]]**, an Astralborn Avianfolk lineage.

The former entry described the same people, anatomy, Astral origin, Cheiropockets, gliding adaptation, Astral Echoes, and astralcraft traditions now documented in the current Chiropterid dossier.

This file remains in place so older links, character records, and campaign notes do not fail silently.

It is not a separate playable lineage.

---

## II. Reason for Transfer

Early registries classified Astralborn Bats as Mythicfolk because they were rare in Aerathon and originated beyond the Material Plane.

That classification emphasized anomaly over community.

Consultation with Astralborn households established that aerial anatomy, spatial culture, architecture, Astral flight, and shared social identity place the lineage more coherently within the expanded Avianfolk classification.

The name **Chiropterid** is used in formal records. **Bat**, **Astralwing**, and **Veilwing** remain common names when welcomed by the person addressed.

---

## III. Mechanical Migration

Characters created from the former Bat rules should migrate to the [[Chiropterid#X. D&D Lineage Traits|current Chiropterid traits]].

The following legacy features remain represented there:

- **Cheiropocket**
- **Wings**, including Material gliding and Astral flight
- **Astral Echoes**
- **Practiced Astralcrafter**, now maintained as an Astral Sea Homeland Imprint

The current dossier also supplies recognized sublineages, regional Homeland Imprints, cultural guidance, privacy law, and revised operational language.

Do not combine the legacy and current versions unless the DM explicitly approves a conversion.

---

## IV. Archive Advisory

References to “Mythicfolk Bats,” “Bat anomalies,” or “conditionally recognized Astralborn” should be understood as obsolete Ministry terminology.

Those phrases may remain appropriate inside historical documents whose prejudice or uncertainty is relevant to the record.

They should not be used as current classification.
