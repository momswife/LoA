## I. Lineage Overview

Centaurs are composite-bodied Mythicfolk with a Humanoid torso joined to an equine lower body. Related communities may show deerlike, antelope-like, zebra-like, or other ungulate traits while identifying within Centaur history.

They are distinct from [[Equine]] Beastfolk, whose bodies are primarily upright and whose lineages follow horse, donkey, or zebra kinship without the same composite form.

Centaurs are Mythicfolk because their origin compacts, body scale, and relationship to ancient road magic materially affect campaigns—not because they are less established as people.

---

## II. Origins and the Four Roads

Centaur histories trace descent through divine messengers, Convergence caravans, transformed riders and mounts, road spirits, and peoples who have always possessed their present form.

The **Four Roads Compact** ended the practice of classifying Centaurs as a rider and animal for taxes, law, and battlefield salvage. Its oldest surviving line reads: one will, one testimony, one person.

Origin stories remain plural. No Centaur must accept the claim that two beings were merged to make them.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Centaurs are long, heavy, and tall, with powerful lower bodies, sensitive flanks, hooves, tails, and large turning radii. They may sleep standing, kneeling, or reclining with appropriate support.

Stairs, ladders, narrow taverns, fragile floors, low ceilings, and humanoid furniture can exclude them. Ramps are not sufficient when a corridor offers no turning space.

Riding a Centaur is an intimate act requiring explicit consent. Tack is clothing or equipment, never evidence of availability.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Centaur households include road families, settled courts, orchard communities, courier companies, herding traditions, mixed kinships, and urban accessible districts.

The **pace promise** states that a group travels at a speed that allows everyone to arrive with dignity. It applies as readily to children and injured companions as to the fastest runner.

Many Centaur communities maintain public grooming courts where bodily care occurs socially without becoming public permission to touch.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Centaur cultures frequently value mobility, route knowledge, physical candor, hospitality, endurance, music carried at travel pace, and promises tied to arrival.

Modern debate concerns freedom and settlement. Romantic stories insist Centaurs must roam. Settled Centaurs answer that accessible homes and political rootedness can be freely chosen, while travelers note that borders often make movement look voluntary only to those permitted to cross.

---

## VI. Centaurs in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Processional avenues accommodate Centaurs better than housing, courts, and ordinary shops.

### Oria

High-road communities maintain switchbacks, snow routes, and House courier compacts.

### Vinyot

Freight and racing industries exploit Centaur speed while excluding them from many workplaces off the track.

### Arneria

Caravan, oasis, and open-road Centaurs hold extensive navigation and hospitality traditions.

### Al'Ar

Island Centaurs are fewer, with strong ferry-access law and coastal road communities.

---

## VII. Centaur Delvers

Centaur delvers are mobile defenders, couriers, rescuers, archers, explorers, and logistical specialists on open routes.

Labyrinths with ladders, squeezing corridors, or collapsing floors require alternate paths or magic. The party should never discover after entry that its only extraction plan assumes a Centaur can be carried like a Humanoid.

---

## VIII. Names, Titles, and Address

Examples include:

- Asha Apple Road

- Lio Four Hooves
- Mara Last Switchback

Terms such as mount, half-horse, beast of burden, or riderless are degrading when imposed upon Centaur people.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] DM Approval Required  
> Centaur bodies interact strongly with mounts, climbing, squeezing, vehicles, equipment, and dungeon architecture. Establish rulings before play.

### Creature Type, Size, and Speed

You are a Fey, Medium. Your walking speed is 40 feet.

### Equine Build

You count as one size larger when determining carrying capacity and the weight you can push or drag. A climb that requires hands and feet costs you 4 feet of movement for each foot climbed.

### Charge

If you move at least 30 feet straight toward a creature and then hit it with a melee attack on the same turn, you may deal an extra 1d6 damage. Uses equal your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Hooves

Your hooves are natural weapons dealing 1d6 bludgeoning damage.

### Roadwise

Gain proficiency in Animal Handling, Nature, or Survival.

---

## X. Recognized Traditions

### Road Centaurs

Road communities treat routes as maintained relationships, carrying news, supplies, names, and obligations between settlements.

### Court Centaurs

Court Centaurs build permanent accessible settlements around assembly spaces, orchards, broad halls, and civic grooming grounds.

### Highland Centaurs

Highland communities use compact bodies, strong lungs, and carefully engineered switchback routes in steep country.

---

## XI. Playing a Centaur

Consider:

- What spaces fail to accommodate you?
- Who may ride you, if anyone?
- Which road recognizes your household?
- Do you prefer travel or settlement?
- What pace do you keep for others?
- How do you handle ladders and narrow routes?
- Which assumptions do Equine Beastfolk make about you?

Speed, strength, and road history do not require restlessness or service.
