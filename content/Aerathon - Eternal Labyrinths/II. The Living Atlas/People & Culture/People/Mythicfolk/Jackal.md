## I. Lineage Overview

Jackals are extraordinarily rare Fey peoples associated with the Beast World, hidden cities, ancient knowledge, long life, and an intimacy with the Arcana unmatched by most mortal lineages.

For much of recorded history, they appeared only in fairy tales, disputed murals, sealed correspondence, and the memories of people nobody believed.

Then they returned.

Jackals are tall, enigmatic, and frequently burdened by the expectation that every sentence should contain forgotten wisdom. They may have lived through events others know only as myth. They may also be artisans, gardeners, physicians, soldiers, cooks, musicians, bored travelers, and people who simply do not want to discuss antiquity over breakfast.

Their secrecy is often treated as proof of manipulation.

Jackal traditions describe it as an adaptation to ages in which rulers, cults, and scholars repeatedly attempted to possess their cities, knowledge, bodies, and memory.

A Jackal’s presence can change a story.

That does not mean the story belongs to them.

## II. Origins and the Jackal Exodus

Jackal accounts place their awakening near the first self-aware Beasts of the Beast World. Whether “beside” means simultaneous creation, early discovery, Fey transformation, or deliberate companionship remains unclear.

They claim no single visible homeland.

Their cities are said to exist beneath oceans, within clouds, behind ritual doors, inside folded seasons, and in moments ordinary time passes without entering.

### The Jackal Exodus

The **Jackal Exodus** describes a period when hidden-city households left their sanctums to aid communities threatened by political collapse, magical predation, and changes in the Beast World’s boundaries.

Jackals served as teachers, healers, ward-makers, witnesses, and guides. They also learned regional practices from the peoples among whom they lived.

This history underlies their ability to adopt another people’s Homeland Trait.

It does not authorize Jackals to claim ownership of what hosts taught them.

### Return Without Explanation

Modern Jackals have not provided one public account of why they became more visible. Some say the Labyrinths are approaching roads they were built to conceal. Others deny any coordinated return.

The Ministry records the disagreement rather than declaring a secret plan.

## III. Body, Age, and Arcana

Jackals are tall, jackal-headed Fey, generally standing between 6'8" and 7'8". They possess expressive ears, fur, long muzzles, dexterous hands, keen senses, and bodies in which Arcana behaves as though recognized rather than learned.

Their lifespans extend deep into recorded history. The registry uses **ageless** cautiously: Jackals resist unnatural aging, but may still be injured, changed, exhausted, grieved, and perhaps killed.

Long life does not produce complete recall.

Jackal memory is shaped by attention, magic, trauma, secrecy, and deliberate release. Some preserve precise centuries. Others allow portions of the past to fade so the present can remain inhabitable.

Their bodies and possessions attract collectors. Fur, blood, dreams, teeth, tools, and personal effects have all been sold as arcane materials.

Possessing unusual resonance does not make a body a resource.

## IV. Hidden Cities and Social Life

The Ministry has verified the existence of Jackal communities without publishing their locations.

Hidden cities are not one civilization. They vary in architecture, governance, relationship to time, Seelie practice, and willingness to receive outsiders.

### Invitation Roads

Many cities can be reached only through an **invitation road**: a sequence of actions, relationships, and perceptions rather than a stable map.

Knowing a route once does not grant permanent entry.

### Life’s Passion

Jackals often cultivate a **Life’s Passion**, a craft or field pursued deeply enough to remain meaningful across ages. A passion may change. Ending one is not failure.

### Secrecy and Accountability

Jackal privacy is culturally protected, but secrecy does not excuse harm. Hidden-city councils increasingly maintain outside witnesses for disputes involving visitors, memory alteration, or events beyond the city’s boundaries.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Jackal communities commonly admire patience, discretion, mastery, hospitality, proportion, memory, and intervention performed without ownership.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Patience | Waiting until action can matter | Allowing preventable harm while seeking certainty |
| Secrecy | Protecting vulnerable people and places | Withholding information others need for consent |
| Mastery | Giving a life sustained direction | Treating beginners as temporary beings |
| Memory | Preserving what history erased | Controlling the present through privileged knowledge |
| Intervention | Offering help at a decisive moment | Leaving before consequences become ordinary work |
| Detachment | Releasing grudges across long time | Avoiding accountability by outliving conflict |

A recurring Jackal fear is that intimacy becomes a road someone else can follow home.

Some maintain strict separation between relationships. Some hide every attachment. Others reject ancestral secrecy and live publicly despite the danger.

## VI. Jackals in Modern Aerathon

Jackals do not possess conventional regional populations. The following records describe contact patterns rather than homelands.

### Allemance

Allemagnian courts seek Jackal witnesses, antiquarian knowledge, and fashionable association. Jackals who appear publicly must navigate hospitality designed to become obligation.

### Oria

Oric Houses preserve stories of tall ward-makers arriving before glacial disasters. Modern Jackals visit observatories and sealed mountain archives while refusing claims that old assistance created permanent service.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti broadcasters and collectors pursue proof of hidden cities aggressively. Jackals participate through masks, intermediaries, and contracts with unusually severe privacy clauses.

### Arneria

Arnerian road traditions leave conceptual space for travelers whose destination cannot be mapped. Jackals work as archive guests, physicians, tale-correctors, and witnesses to ruins that predate local states.

### Al'Ar

Al'Ari accounts place hidden roads beneath reefs and volcanic cloud. Jackals living openly among island communities often do so through reciprocal kinship rather than public registry.

## VII. Jackals and Delving

Jackal delvers are rare and exceptionally valuable as arcane analysts, ritualists, ruin witnesses, memory specialists, scouts, and negotiators with Fey or ancient intelligences.

Their strengths include:

- Recognizing old magical forms
- Ritual spell access
- Extraordinary learned expertise
- Long historical perspective
- Protecting identity through dream and memory magic
- Adapting to cultures encountered during the Exodus

Their presence can unbalance an expedition’s knowledge.

Before play, the DM and player should establish what the Jackal remembers, what they have forgotten, which mysteries remain inaccessible, and why other solutions cannot be supplied from ancient experience.

The most dangerous Jackal ability is not age.

It is the capacity to alter another person’s memory of an encounter.

Use of that power against allies or player characters requires explicit table consent.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] DM Approval Required  
> These are high-impact lineage mechanics. Review ability scores, _dream_, memory alteration, expertise, ritual access, and adopted Homeland Traits before play.

### Creature Type

You are a Fey.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Dexterity, Intelligence, and Wisdom scores each increase by 2.

### Size

You are Medium. Most Jackals stand between 6'8" and 7'8".

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Ageless

You are immune to any magical effect that would cause you to age unnaturally.

### Obscuring Dream

You can cast the _dream_ spell once per long rest without expending a spell slot. When using this trait, you may instead alter a target's memory of an encounter from the last 7 days, causing them to forget your presence (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + Intelligence modifier). If the target has an Intelligence score lower than yours, they automatically fail. You may cast _dream_ using spell slots as normal.

### Life’s Passion

You gain proficiency with one artisan’s tool and one skill from the following: Arcana, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Religion, or Survival. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for these checks. You also gain proficiency with one martial melee weapon without the heavy property; for you, it counts as finesse and thrown (range 20/60).

### Birthmates of the Arcana

You know the _eldritch blast_ cantrip. Choose two 1st-level ritual spells from the Wizard spell list. You can cast each once per long rest without material components. You can also cast them with spell slots as normal. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

## X. Homeland Traits

Jackals do not conform to traditional homelands. Their cities lie beneath oceans, within clouds, or folded between forgotten moments. A Jackal character selects one of the following options with DM approval.

### Wanderers of the Shrouded Roads

You can choose any homeland trait from another species as if you had grown up there, reflecting time spent aiding other homelands during the Jackal Exodus.

Record the people who taught the trait and what relationship grants your character the right to practice it.

### City of the Unseen

You have advantage on Intelligence (History) checks related to ancient ruins, lost civilizations, or Seelie worship. Additionally, you can speak, read, and write one exotic language, such as Sylvan, Deep Speech, or Primordial.

## XI. Playing a Jackal

Consider:

- Which hidden city shaped you?
- Why are you visible now?
- What is your Life’s Passion?
- What historical event did you witness incompletely?
- Which memory did you choose to release?
- Who may know the road home?
- What ordinary modern invention delights you?
- When has intervention become control?
- What prevents your age from solving the campaign’s mysteries?
- Under what circumstances would you alter a memory?

A Jackal may be impatient, inexperienced outside one field, openly emotional, politically naive, poor at riddles, hostile to secrecy, or young by Jackal standards.

Ancient is not omniscient.
