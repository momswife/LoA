## I. Lineage Overview

Constructfolk are sapient created beings whose bodies may be forged, carved, assembled, grown, inscribed, or continuously rebuilt from metal, wood, stone, ceramic, glass, cloth, bone substitute, living fibers, and arcane matrices.

Not every construct is a person. Not every Constructfolk began as a non-sapient machine. Recognition concerns the present self, not whether a maker expected one to emerge.

They are filed as Mythicfolk because creation methods can reveal lost technologies, divine bargains, forbidden laboratories, or active Labyrinth processes and because chassis traits require campaign review.

---

## II. Origins and the Unsigned Petition

Constructfolk origins include deliberate person-making, accidental awakening, inhabited vessels, copied cognition, divine animation, emancipated war engines, and bodies built by Constructfolk for future kin.

The **Unsigned Petition** was submitted without a maker’s certification. Its authors argued that requiring the creator’s signature made freedom depend upon the very party most likely to claim ownership.

Modern recognition accepts self-testimony, sustained agency, relationship, and independent preference. A serial plate is provenance, not a legal name or deed.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Constructfolk bodies range from nearly organic forms to visibly articulated chassis. They may experience touch through pressure runes, filament nerves, resonant plates, alchemical fluids, light sensors, or bound senses.

Repair can be medicine. Opening a maintenance panel, reading a core inscription, changing a component, copying a memory lattice, or disabling a safety mechanism requires consent.

Many can replace bodily parts without treating those parts as disposable. Some consider original material sacred; others understand continuity through pattern or relationship.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Constructfolk households include forge families, emancipation circles, maintenance cooperatives, maker-child relationships, copied siblings, temple workshops, veteran communities, and mixed homes.

The **Mutual Maintenance Compact** separates emergency repair from authority. A trusted mechanic may know how to open a person’s body without gaining a right to do so whenever they choose.

Constructfolk can create later generations, but doing so is reproduction or community formation—not manufacture of inventory.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Constructfolk philosophies ask whether selfhood lies in matter, memory, purpose, continuity, soul, choice, or the promises other people keep with us.

Modern debate concerns designed purpose. Some reclaim intended functions as vocation or heritage. Others regard every embedded directive as a surviving command to be removed. Both reject the claim that origin determines obedience.

Emotion may be expressed through voice, light, posture, heat, sound, script, or nothing visible to an organic observer.

## VI. Constructfolk in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Courts recognize Constructfolk while inheritance law still struggles with maker estates and replacement bodies.

### Oria

Artificer Houses sponsor sophisticated chassis work, creating both excellent care and dangerous patronage dependence.

### Vinyot

Industrial Constructfolk organize against planned obsolescence, proprietary repairs, broadcast dismantling, and corporate ownership of memory formats.

### Arneria

Solar, wind-driven, ceramic, and caravan-maintained bodies adapt to dust and limited workshop access.

### Al'Ar

Salt-resistant, volcanic-glass, and ship-integrated traditions blur the line between mobile citizen and inhabited structure without surrendering personhood.

---

## VII. Constructfolk Delvers

Constructfolk delvers are resilient scouts, artificers, translators of ancient machinery, defenders, and specialists in hostile atmospheres.

Labyrinths frequently issue false owner commands, identify bodies as keys, offer replacement parts carrying hidden directives, or attempt to restore a person to an earlier backup.

Crews should agree which commands have no authority, how emergency repair works, and whether memory restoration is acceptable before descent.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] DM Approval Required  
> Constructfolk bodies can interact strongly with healing, survival, and equipment rules. Approve the chassis, repair fiction, and campaign technology before play.

### Creature Type, Size, and Speed

You are a Construct. Choose Small or Medium. Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Constructed Resilience

You have advantage on saving throws against poison and disease, resistance to poison damage, and magic cannot put you to sleep. You do not need to breathe.

### Sentry Rest

You complete a long rest in 6 hours while remaining motionless but conscious. You retain normal hearing and vision.

### Integrated Tool

Choose one artisan’s tool. It is integrated into your body, and you gain proficiency with it. You may replace the chosen tool during downtime with appropriate materials and assistance.

### Repairable Form

The _mending_ cantrip can stabilize you when you are dying if cast within touch range, granting one successful death saving throw. Healing magic otherwise affects you normally.

---

## X. Recognized Chassis Traditions

### Forged Bodies

Metal, wood, ceramic, and stone chassis emphasize repairable structure, visible craft lineages, and debates over maker marks.

### Woven Bodies

Cloth, cord, paper, fiber, and flexible lattice bodies often use replaceable layers and portable inscription.

### Vessel-Born

Vessel-Born Constructfolk awakened when a spirit, copied self, or emergent mind inhabited a body originally built for another purpose.

---

## XI. Playing a Constructfolk Character

Consider:

- Who made your body, if anyone?
- What directive did you keep or remove?
- Which repairs feel intimate?
- What part of you has never been replaced?
- Who may access your memories or core?
- Do you want to make another person like yourself?
- What ordinary desire proves nothing except that it is yours?

Constructed origin is history. It is never a complete personality or binding instruction.
