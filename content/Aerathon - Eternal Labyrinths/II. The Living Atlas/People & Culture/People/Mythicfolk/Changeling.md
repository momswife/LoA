## I. Lineage Overview

Changelings are rare shapechanging people capable of altering visible bodily form without ordinary illusion or disguise.

Their origins vary among Fey inheritance, planar adaptation, magical lineage, Labyrinth alteration, and communities that decline all public origin claims.

Change does not make their identities false. A chosen face may be intimate, practical, artistic, protective, gendered, ceremonial, inherited, or simply comfortable.

---

## II. Origins and the Sealed Names

Historic authorities maintained **true-form registries** meant to identify Changelings. Those registries instead enabled blackmail, forced exposure, family separation, and collective punishment.

After the Sealed Names action, surviving records were closed or destroyed by court order. Modern documents record only the form and name the person selects for that context.

No physical form is legally more authentic than another. The phrase **true face** is rejected in current Ministry practice.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Changelings can alter complexion, features, voice, hair, apparent age, sex characteristics, and proportions within lineage limits. Clothing and equipment do not transform with them.

Transformation may be effortless, emotionally charged, tiring, dysphoric, pleasurable, or private. Injury and scars may persist, disappear, or be deliberately retained according to the individual’s form practice.

Touching a face to test it, demanding transformation, or using magic to force a prior form is bodily coercion.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Changeling households include hidden families, open communities, traveling theatres, identity houses, Fey-descended courts, urban mutual-aid networks, and mixed kinships.

The **mirror compact** lets a person specify which names and forms may be connected by others. Knowing two presentations belong to one person does not grant permission to reveal that fact.

Some Changelings maintain one public identity. Others live through several enduring social selves without treating any as deception.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Changeling cultures frequently value chosen presentation, privacy, improvisation, empathy without appropriation, performance, and the ability to revise one’s life.

Modern debate concerns stable documentation. Some activists seek identity papers that change easily; others fear that formal flexibility will increase surveillance. All reject magical testing imposed only upon mutable people.

Shapechanging does not provide another person’s memories, habits, authority, or consent.

---

## VI. Changelings in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Court protocol offers many roles but heavily polices authenticity, inheritance, and impersonation.

### Oria

House membership can protect Changelings while demanding lineage disclosures many consider invasive.

### Vinyot

Performance and broadcast industries celebrate transformation while contracts claim ownership of faces and personas.

### Arneria

Guest-name traditions provide privacy, though border registries increasingly demand fixed appearances.

### Al'Ar

Festival masks and plural naming create social space for Changelings without eliminating local distrust.

---

## VII. Changeling Delvers

Changeling delvers work as negotiators, scouts, performers, investigators, and social infiltrators by choice—not by lineage assignment.

Crews need identity confirmation that does not depend only upon appearance. Labyrinths may copy companions, lock a Changeling into an old form, or offer access in exchange for another person’s face.

Impersonating a real person requires table agreement about consent and consequences.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] DM Approval Required  
> Mutable form can strongly affect intrigue, identity, and safety themes. Establish transformation limits, impersonation consent, and recognition procedures before play.

### Creature Type

You are a Fey.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Charisma score increases by 2.

### Size

Choose Small or Medium whenever you complete a long rest; your statistics do not otherwise change.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Mutable Form

As an action, transform your appearance and voice. You determine features and coloration and may appear as any Small or Medium Humanoid with the same basic arrangement of limbs. Your game statistics, clothing, and equipment do not change. You remain in the new form until you transform again or die.

### Changeling Instincts

Gain proficiency in two of Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Performance, or Persuasion.

### Unfixed Self

You have advantage on saving throws against effects that would magically alter your form against your will.

## X. Recognized Form Traditions

### Hearth Faces

Hearth-face Changelings maintain one or more forms associated with safety, kinship, rest, or private community life.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Size.** You can be Small or Medium as described by Mutable Form.

**Familiar Presence.** You gain proficiency in Insight. After you finish a long rest with a willing creature, you and that creature have advantage on checks made to recognize one another through mundane disguises or changes of form until either of you finishes a long rest without the other.

### Open Masks

Open-mask traditions treat visible transformation as art, rhetoric, gender practice, or ordinary dress.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 1.

**Size.** You can be Small or Medium as described by Mutable Form.

**Fluid Performer.** You gain proficiency in Performance. You can use Mutable Form as a bonus action a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Unmarked Houses

Unmarked Houses minimize transformation records and teach survival under hostile identity law.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Intelligence score increases by 1.

**Size.** You can be Small or Medium as described by Mutable Form.

**Unrecorded Step.** You gain proficiency in Stealth. Creatures have disadvantage on checks made to identify or track you solely from a secondhand description of a form you are no longer using.

---

## XI. Homeland Imprints

If the campaign uses Homeland Imprints, choose one trait representing the region that most strongly shaped your character.

### Allemance — Salon of Many Faces

You gain proficiency in History or Persuasion. After observing a formal gathering for 10 minutes, you can identify its expected forms of address and the person presently treated as its host.

### Oria — Winter Mask

You ignore the effects of extreme cold. Mutable Form can alter the apparent texture and seasonal coloration of your body, though it still does not change your clothing or equipment.

### Vinyot — Contracted Likeness

You gain proficiency in Insight. You have advantage on checks made to recognize magical image alteration, concealed identity clauses, or unauthorized use of a person's likeness.

### Arneria — Veil-Market Adept

You gain proficiency with disguise kits. When using a disguise kit on another willing creature, you can apply or remove the disguise in half the normal time.

### Al'Ar — Crew Face

You gain proficiency with water vehicles. A willing creature that has seen at least two of your forms can recognize you by voice and movement even when it cannot clearly see your face.

---

## XII. Playing a Changeling

Consider:

- Which forms feel like home?
- Who knows how many names you use?
- What may companions reveal?
- Have you ever borrowed a real person’s appearance?
- What remains recognizable through every change?
- Which form do strangers wrongly call true?
- What identity is not a disguise even if it began as one?

Mutable form expands expression. It does not make every relationship a lie.
