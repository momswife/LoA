# Kobold

**Folk Classification:** [[‎ Mythicfolk|Mythicfolk]]  
**Lineage Classification:** Named Kobold  
**Registry Scope:** Emergent Sapience, Draconic Design, and Adopted Homeland  
**Recognition Status:** Full Recognition for Demonstrably Sapient Individuals  
**Delver Eligibility:** Individual Review  
**Player Access:** DM Approval Required  
**Common Names:** Named Kobolds, Awakened Kobolds, Keepers  
**Date of Revised Filing:** 7th Cycle, 3388 A.D.

---

## I. Lineage Overview

Kobolds were historically recorded as instinctive draconic thralls: small tunnel-dwelling creatures that hoarded, mimicked, trapped, and served without sustained self-awareness.

Then individual Kobolds began naming themselves.

A **named Kobold** demonstrates reflective consciousness, personal continuity, creative language, independent desire, and the ability to question the purpose for which Kobolds were apparently designed.

Fewer than a thousand confirmed named Kobolds live in the Beast World and Aerathon combined, though the figure measures Ministry recognition rather than actual existence.

Each known emergence creates an uncomfortable question.

Did consciousness appear where none existed?

Was it present in a form official tests failed to recognize?

Are Kobolds evolving, escaping a magical restriction, recovering from draconic domination, or performing a change that has occurred many times before without witnesses willing to see it?

The Ministry has no settled answer.

Named Kobolds are people regardless.

---

## II. Draconic Design and Emergence

The oldest evidence places Kobolds beneath dragons as miners, trap-makers, porters, tunnel attendants, and extensions of a hoard’s defensive system.

Draconic texts describe them as property and frequently exaggerate their loyalty. Whether dragons created Kobolds, altered an existing people, or suppressed a population into dependency remains unresolved.

### Emergence Events

Named Kobolds report awakening through:

- Prolonged magical exposure
- Isolation from a warren
- Trauma or direct threat
- Contact with a self-aware named Kobold
- Arcane resonance
- Being addressed persistently as a person
- No identifiable event at all

Some show gradual self-recognition. Others describe a sudden boundary between before and after.

### Inherited Cognition

A small number of Kobolds born near named individuals demonstrate language and reflection unusually early. This may indicate propagation, teaching, release from environmental suppression, or biased observation.

No institution is authorized to breed or expose Kobolds experimentally to answer the question.

---

## III. Body, Cognition, and Expression

Kobolds are Small, scaled Humanoids generally standing between 3'2" and 3'10". They possess claws, tails, draconic or reptilian features, quick movement, and strong attraction to small objects, mechanisms, and spatially dense environments.

Named Kobolds do not become physically different merely by being recognized.

Their language may retain mimicry, repetition, collective reference, abrupt topic changes, or gestures developed within a warren. None of these indicates lesser thought.

### Sapience Testing

Older Ministry tests relied upon stable names, individual property, solitary reasoning, formal language, and willingness to cooperate with officials.

Those measures were culturally narrow and vulnerable to fear.

Current assessment observes choice, communication, self-protection, future planning, relationship, refusal, play, and learning over time. When uncertainty remains, personnel must choose the response least likely to harm a possible person.

---

## IV. Warrens, Keepers, and Adopted Kinship

Named Kobolds have complicated relationships with Kobolds who do not demonstrate equivalent self-awareness.

Some remain with their warrens as protectors, interpreters, organizers, and **keepers**. Some leave because the constant resemblance is painful. Some believe every warren member is a person whose language they have not yet learned. Others distinguish sharply between named and unnamed life.

No position is universal.

### Naming

A Kobold’s first chosen name may describe an object, sound, escape route, joke, injury, color, or possession. Naming is recognized when it functions as self-identification, not when an official approves its dignity.

### Adoption

Named Kobolds are frequently adopted into Beastfolk households, urban neighborhoods, delver crews, temples, and workshops.

Adoption must not erase prior warren relationships or turn guardianship into ownership.

### Collective Thought

Some named Kobolds report sharper thought in the presence of other Kobolds, including apparently non-sapient ones. The effect may be magical, neurological, social, or an artifact of draconic design.

It must never justify keeping Kobolds as cognitive equipment.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Named Kobold communities commonly admire clever escape, self-naming, resourcefulness, mutual warning, attention to overlooked things, humor, and the ability to make shelter from what others abandoned.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Resourcefulness | Surviving with discarded material | Accepting deprivation as natural |
| Escape | Refusing capture and imposed purpose | Leaving before trust can form |
| Acquisition | Securing tools and objects needed for autonomy | Taking without recognizing another person’s claim |
| Collective thought | Solving problems through proximity | Losing confidence when alone |
| Mimicry | Learning quickly from surrounding people | Performing a personality others reward |
| Competence | Acting despite incomplete training | Hiding confusion until it becomes dangerous |

A recurring named Kobold fear is that personhood will be revoked the moment they fail to perform intelligence in the manner an observer expects.

Some overexplain. Some steal the evidence that was meant to classify them. Some refuse tests. Others become scholars of consciousness because nobody should face the same tribunal unprepared.

## VI. Named Kobolds in Modern Aerathon

Named Kobolds possess no conventional territorial homeland. They emerge in Labyrinth margins, ruins, sewers, tunnels, abandoned structures, draconic sites, and warrens beneath every region.

### Allemance

Allemagnian named Kobolds encounter courts eager to appoint guardians and guilds eager to claim unusual apprentices. Advocacy groups insist that unfamiliarity with law does not equal incapacity.

### Oria

Oric holds employ Kobold tunnel knowledge while debating whether entire warrens fall under Beast, citizen, or hazard law. Several Houses now apply possible-person protocols.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti named Kobolds appear in broadcast spectacle, salvage markets, workshops, and criminalized tunnel communities. Their likenesses and catchphrases are frequently commercialized without consent.

### Arneria

Arnerian named Kobolds join caravans, ruins schools, shrines, and abandoned-site communities. Hospitality traditions often recognize a spoken name before a registry does.

### Al'Ar

Al'Ari named Kobolds are rare. Those present frequently enter port workshops or Beastfolk kinships whose adoption law offers more flexible recognition than mainland courts.

---

## VII. Named Kobolds and Delving

Named Kobold delvers commonly serve as scouts, trap specialists, salvagers, mechanism readers, infiltrators, improvised engineers, and guides through occupied ruins.

Their strengths include:

- Detecting small valuable or functional objects
- Understanding improvised warrens
- Escaping threatened spaces
- Learning through proximity and observation
- Using apparently irrelevant items creatively
- Recognizing structures others dismiss as debris

Their risks include being treated as expendable because of size, sent ahead because traps are assumed to be familiar, or used to command unnamed Kobolds.

Named Kobolds do not automatically control warrens.

Stackable Intellect requires nearby Kobolds, not prisoners, pets, followers, or equipment. The table should establish how that feature functions ethically before play.

## VIII. Names, Titles, and Address

Examples include:

- Bell-Under
- Copper No
- Doorbit
- Flicker-Twice
- Mica Keeps-Keys
- Nettle-Key
- Pocket Verdict
- Red String
- Six Questions
- Tallow Escaped
- Tin-Teeth
- Was-There

A named Kobold may change names repeatedly as self-understanding develops.

Use the current chosen name. Do not substitute a diminutive, assign a “proper” registry name, or treat a simple object-name as evidence of immaturity.

Calling a Kobold _pet_, _minion_, _vermin_, _thing_, _smart one_, or _good little dragon_ is demeaning.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] DM Approval Required  
> These mechanics model an exceptionally rare named Kobold. Review Stackable Intellect, nearby-Kobold availability, Sleight of Hand substitution, and adopted regional traits before play.

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and one other ability score of your choice, excluding Intelligence, increases by 1.

### Size

You are Small. Most Kobolds stand between 3'2" and 3'10".

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Escaper’s Keepers

You are proficient in the Sleight of Hand skill.

When a creature makes an opportunity attack against you, you can use your reaction to make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check contested by the attacker’s Wisdom (Perception). On a success, the attack misses. You may also steal one worn or carried item that is not held and weighs no more than 5 pounds without the creature noticing.

A creature that witnesses this feature becomes immune to it for 24 hours.

### Clumsy Competence

You gain a +1 bonus to any ability check that does not include a bonus from proficiency, class features, or magic—only your raw ability score.

If your Intelligence score is 9 or lower, this bonus instead becomes +2.

### Stackable Intellect

When other Kobolds are nearby, your thoughts sharpen through shared instinct and emergent cognition. You gain a bonus to ability checks based on the number of Kobolds within 60 feet of you:

| Nearby Kobolds | Ability Check Bonus |
|---|---|
| 2 | +1 |
| 4 | +2 |
| 8 | +3 |
| 16 | +4 |
| 32+ | +5 |

This bonus represents emergent problem-solving rather than communication; the Kobolds need not be conscious collaborators.

## X. Environmental Adaptations

Named Kobolds do not possess conventional regional sublineages. Select one of the following adaptations with DM approval.

### Hoarder’s Luck

You have advantage on checks made to locate small, hidden, or valuable objects, such as gems, keys, trap triggers, or concealed mechanisms.

When identifying magical objects or deciphering runes, you may use Sleight of Hand in place of Arcana or Investigation, provided you have physically touched the item.

### Squatters of the Forgotten

If you spend at least 8 hours within a ruin, sewer, Labyrinth margin, or abandoned structure, you gain the following benefits:

- You learn all natural paths of entry and exit
- You detect the nearest active source of magic within 30 feet, as if by _detect magic_
- You learn whether the structure was once sacred, arcane, or mundane in origin

### Adopted Origin

A named Kobold may select one regional trait from any other Beastfolk species, representing where they emerged, were raised, or were claimed. This choice reflects social adoption rather than ancestry and is subject to DM and Ministry verification.

## XI. Playing a Named Kobold

Consider:

- When did you choose your name?
- Was emergence sudden, gradual, or only recognition by others?
- What relationship do you have with your warren?
- Who first treated you as a person?
- Who still refuses?
- What object matters beyond its market value?
- How do you think when no other Kobolds are near?
- What behavior have you learned to perform for recognition?
- Which abandoned place feels like yours?
- What do you want once survival stops occupying every decision?

A named Kobold may be generous, law-abiding, uninterested in valuables, physically brave, poor with traps, eloquent, solitary, or hostile to the idea that emergence made them fundamentally different.

Personhood is not a performance bonus.

---

## XII. Ministry and Table Advisory

> [!warning] Emergent Sapience Advisory  
> Named Kobold characters require agreement about emergence, warren relationships, public recognition, Stackable Intellect, and the campaign’s answer—or deliberate lack of answer—to wider Kobold sapience.
>
> Do not use sapience uncertainty to justify cruelty, captivity, experimentation, or disposable unnamed Kobolds without table consent.
>
> Named Kobolds possess full personhood. Conditional delver review concerns operational circumstances, not civic worth.

The Ministry adopts a possible-person standard toward unassessed Kobolds: when evidence is uncertain, personnel must choose the action least likely to cage, injure, exploit, or kill a being capable of self-directed experience.

Forced containment, experimentation, breeding, exposure, conscription, or use of Kobolds as cognitive equipment constitutes a Class-3 Sapience Violation.
