## I. Lineage Overview

Gryphonids are sapient composite peoples combining raptor-like forequarters, wings, and senses with leonine or other great-feline hindquarters and power. Some move primarily as winged quadrupeds; others possess upright shoulders and grasping forelimbs while retaining a long feline lower body.

The Ministry uses **Gryphonid** in formal records because **Gryphonfolk** and **Gryffinkind** vary by region. Communities use all three.

Gryphonids are distinct from Avianfolk and Feline Beastfolk. Their composite anatomy, high eyrie histories, and inherited sky-oaths place them within Mythicfolk.

---

## II. Origins and the Broken Bridle

Gryphonid histories describe descent from divine heralds, guardians who outlived vanished kingdoms, unions of Wind and Crown, Convergence populations, and wild eyries that were never created to serve anyone.

The **Broken Bridle Compact** ended a royal practice of binding sapient Gryphonids as hereditary mounts. Its signatories shattered ceremonial tack and retained the metal as gate hinges: what once confined them would open their own doors.

No claim of ancient guardianship establishes a modern obligation to defend a ruin, bloodline, treasure, or throne.

Gate-hinge metal from the Compact is still divided among several eyries. None claims an original throne. The fragments are displayed at ordinary doors—schools, kitchens, clinics, and council halls—to emphasize that liberation was not escape into wilderness but the right to build institutions of their own.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Gryphonids are large-framed but mechanically Medium, with wings, hooked beaks, foretalons or grasping hands, feline hindquarters, tails, feathers, fur, and exceptionally sharp distance vision.

Launching requires space, airflow, and healthy wings. Armor and packs must distribute weight without restricting the chest, shoulders, or wing roots.

Feather preening and fur grooming can be social or intimate. Touching wings, climbing onto the back, or taking shed feathers requires permission.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Gryphonid households include eyries, prides, gate circles, cliff cities, adopted broods, courier houses, temple flights, and mixed aerial communities.

The **held height** custom treats safe altitude as shared infrastructure. Strong fliers maintain launch ledges and rescue lines for the young, injured, terrestrial guests, and anyone whose wings cannot carry them that day.

Kinship does not follow one side of composite anatomy. No family is more properly eagle or lion.

Gate circles often unite those who maintain a difficult threshold: a cliff landing, storm refuge, mountain pass, or aerial approach. Membership is work and covenant rather than descent. A terrestrial relative who keeps the rescue lines may hold as much authority there as the strongest flier.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Gryphonid traditions often admire vigilance, hospitality at difficult thresholds, physical courage, visual art meant for distant viewing, strong boundary law, and promises made in open sky.

Their most contested inheritance is guardianship. Some reclaim the role as chosen civic service. Others consider every expectation that Gryphonids hoard, watch, or defend a surviving relic a continuation of domestication.

Collecting beautiful or historic objects is common in some eyries and absent in others. It is culture, never instinctive proof of greed.

Modern eyries debate contracts inherited from vanished states. Some guardianships now protect villages that genuinely depend on them; others preserve royal claims no living person chose. Review councils ask who benefits, who may end the duty, and whether the protected object matters more than the people living beside it.

## VI. Gryphonids in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Palaces employ Gryphonid heraldry while living citizens challenge mount imagery, feather fashion, and inherited gate contracts.

### Oria

High eyries exchange rescue practice and weather knowledge with Raptorids, Goliaths, and mountain Houses.

### Vinyot

Aerial racing and illegal broadcasts make Gryphonids famous while encouraging lethal flight expectations.

### Arneria

Mesa and desert Gryphonids travel by dawn and maintain long-distance cliff refuge.

### Al'Ar

Storm eyries and island gate circles specialize in sea-cliff rescue and volcanic updrafts.

---

## VII. Gryphonid Delvers

Gryphonid delvers are aerial scouts, rescuers, mobile defenders, route observers, and threshold specialists.

Labyrinth storms can create irresistible false updrafts, reproduce royal bridle commands, or mark a Gryphonid as the “guardian” required to remain behind.

Crews must distinguish emergency carrying from riding and plan for routes where wings cannot open.

## VIII. Names, Titles, and Address

Examples include:

- Asha Gold Eyrie
- Brann Breaks-Bridles
- Iri Held Height
- Kael Above-the-Gate
- Nema Cloud-Talon
- Oren No Hoard
- Sava Storm Mane
- Veyra Open Sky

Terms such as mount, beast, pet, royal stock, or treasure animal are degrading. “Gryffinfolk” appears in some Vinyoti transliterations but is not the only accepted spelling.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] DM Approval Required  
> Flight and composite body shape strongly affect maps, hazards, equipment, rescue, and encounter balance. Establish the campaign’s flight progression before play.

### Creature Type

You are a Monstrosity.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Strength score increases by 2.

### Size

Your size is Medium.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Keen Sight

Gain proficiency in Perception. You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on sight at distances greater than 30 feet.

### Talons

Your talons are natural weapons dealing 1d6 slashing damage.

### Powerful Frame

You count as one size larger when determining carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.

### Wings

At 1st level, you may glide: when falling, use your reaction to move 2 feet horizontally for every 1 foot descended and reduce falling damage by five times your proficiency bonus. At 5th level, with DM approval, you gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed while not wearing heavy armor.

## X. Recognized Eyrie Traditions

### Crown Gryphonids

Crown traditions descend from palace and gate communities and lead many efforts to end inherited service compacts.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Chosen Guardian.** When a creature within 5 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to grant it a +2 bonus to AC against that attack, potentially causing it to miss. You may use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Storm Gryphonids

Storm eyries cultivate powerful-weather flight, sea-cliff rescue, and kinship with modern Avianfolk [[Modern Stormriders|Stormriders]].

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Stormwise.** You have resistance to thunder damage and advantage on saving throws made to resist being moved by wind.

### Dust Gryphonids

Dust Gryphonids inhabit mesas, desert ruins, and warm thermals, maintaining wide territories through refuge rather than ownership.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Thermal Reader.** You gain proficiency in Survival. You ignore the effects of extreme heat and can identify the direction of the strongest natural rising air within 300 feet.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

If the campaign uses Homeland Imprints, choose one trait representing the region that most strongly shaped your character.

### Allemance — Gatehouse Citizen

You gain proficiency in Insight or Persuasion. After observing an entrance for 1 minute, you can identify its ordinary traffic pattern and any visible accessibility problem for a winged or quadrupedal body.

### Oria — Aurora Eyrie

You ignore the effects of extreme cold. Snow and ice do not prevent you from using Wings to glide, though magical weather can still do so.

### Vinyot — Skyline Rescue

You gain proficiency in Acrobatics. When using Wings to glide, you can carry a willing creature of your size or smaller, but both of you descend 2 feet for every 1 foot moved horizontally.

### Arneria — Mesa Refuge

You gain proficiency in Survival. You have advantage on checks made to find shelter from heat, sand, or aerial observation.

### Al'Ar — Sea-Cliff Stormrider

You gain proficiency with water vehicles. When falling over water, Wings reduce your effective falling distance by an additional 10 feet.

---

## XII. Playing a Gryphonid

Consider:

- What height feels like home?
- Who may ride or be carried by you?
- What were your ancestors expected to guard?
- What object do you keep, and why?
- Which parts of your body need separate care?
- How do you behave when a room is too small to open your wings?
- What oath did you freely choose?

Composite form creates no split loyalty, and wings create no duty to fly.
