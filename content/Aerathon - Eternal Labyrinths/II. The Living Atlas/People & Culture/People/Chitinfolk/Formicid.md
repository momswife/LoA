## I. Lineage Overview

Formicids are antlike Chitinfolk recognized by antennae, strong chemical senses, segmented bodies, and traditions of engineered collective life.

Their coordination is cultural, linguistic, architectural, and learned. It is not proof of command pheromones or instinctive submission. A Formicid who leaves a colony remains a complete person.

---

## II. Origins and the Unnumbered Names

The oldest Formicid petitions are called the **Unnumbered Names** because officials originally replaced thousands of personal names with labor tallies. Surviving colonies recite these lists during civic initiations.

The lesson is not that every Formicid belongs to an ancient super-colony. It is that bureaucracy can erase individuals most efficiently when their cooperation already looks uniform from outside.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Formicids are usually Small or Medium, with two or four arms, powerful mandibles, antennae, compound eyes, and scent glands. Body forms vary more than occupational labels suggest.

Antennae combine touch, smell, balance, and social signal. Grabbing them is disorienting and intimate. Chemical masking can be protective equipment, formal dress, spiritual practice, or enforced assimilation.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Colonies may be cities, extended families, cooperatives, nations, housing structures, or political federations. Membership is not universal or permanent.

Many Formicids practice **load declaration**: before work begins, every burden is named as physical, emotional, legal, or hidden. Efficient coordination is judged by whether nobody disappears inside the task.

Terms translated as queen and worker require local explanation and should not be entered as gender or profession by default.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Formicid cultures often admire reliability, spatial reasoning, preparation, redundancy, mutual aid, and the dignity of necessary work.

Modern debate concerns whether celebrating labor reinforces external exploitation. Younger movements insist that rest, art, idleness, privacy, and unproductive joy belong equally within Formicid tradition.

---

## VI. Formicids in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Formicid civil engineers and archivists navigate fascination with colony government and hostility toward migrant nests.

### Oria

Deep-road colonies collaborate with Dwarven holds while defending independent tunnel law.

### Vinyot

Freight, manufacturing, and communications unions are major centers of Formicid political life.

### Arneria

Subterranean settlements use cool galleries and scent-marked routes beneath the desert.

### Al'Ar

Island colonies specialize in storm-braced earthworks and distributed food storage.

---

## VII. Formicid Delvers

Formicid delvers excel at route marking, load management, tight-space rescue, chemical hazard detection, and coordinated movement.

Labyrinths can forge colony scent or generate overwhelming alarm signals. Crews should pair chemical recognition with passwords and visible confirmation.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits are an initial Aerathon playtest package and require DM approval until table testing is complete.

### Creature Type, Size, and Speed

You are a Humanoid. Choose Small or Medium. Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Darkvision

You have darkvision out to 60 feet.

### Powerful Build

You count as one size larger when determining carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.

### Chemical Sense

You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) checks made to identify creatures, poisons, or environmental changes by scent.

### Coordinated Labor

When you take the Help action, the creature you aid may add your proficiency bonus to one damage roll or to the total of one Strength or Dexterity ability check made before the start of your next turn. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

---

## X. Recognized Colony Traditions

### Gallery Colonies

Gallery communities construct layered underground cities around airflow, scent routes, storage, and collective assembly.

### Caravan Colonies

Caravan Formicids carry modular homes and treat the moving column as civic space rather than mere transport.

### Solitary Houses

Solitary Houses were established by Formicids who prefer individual households while retaining language and ritual ties to larger communities.

---

## XI. Playing a Formicid

Consider:

- What does your colony mean to you?
- How do you mark a route?
- Which burdens do you refuse?
- What does rest look like outside a work schedule?
- Which scents carry social meaning?
- Who knows your personal name rather than your role?

Cooperation can be a value without being an instinct or obligation.
