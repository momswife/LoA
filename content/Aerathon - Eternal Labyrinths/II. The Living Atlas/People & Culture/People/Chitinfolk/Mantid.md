## I. Lineage Overview

Mantids are Chitinfolk distinguished by triangular mobile heads, wide fields of vision, grasping forelimbs, rapid striking motion, and long periods of comfortable stillness.

Popular entertainment depicts them almost exclusively as assassins or serene duelists. Most Mantids live ordinary lives as growers, artisans, teachers, civil servants, parents, engineers, performers, and tradespeople.

---

## II. Origins and the Patient Witnesses

During the Court of Green Glass, Mantid witnesses remained motionless through three days of procedural delay and then separately described a massacre officials claimed nobody had clearly seen.

The **Patient Witnesses** became a tradition of attentive civic testimony. Modern schools stress that observation carries duties: accuracy, humility, and refusal to turn another person’s private life into spectacle.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Mantids are usually Medium, with four or six limbs, flexible necks, compound eyes, antennae, and grasping forearms. Some possess decorative or functional wings without sustained flight.

Their apparent stillness may be rest, focus, overload management, politeness, fear, or simply a comfortable posture. Sudden motion does not automatically signal aggression.

Forelimb edges can be blunt, toothed, or sharp and require customized sleeves, tools, and restraint protocols.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Mantid households include garden courts, witness schools, molt cohorts, family homes, hunting traditions, temples, and urban professional circles.

Partners often practice **declared approach**, announcing when they enter another’s near field because Mantid vision notices movement quickly and crowded rooms can be exhausting.

Stories of mates inevitably consuming one another are a degrading simplification of rare historic ritual and non-sapient animal behavior.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Mantid cultures commonly value precision, restraint, decisive action, visual art, garden design, and the difference between seeing and understanding.

Modern debate concerns martial tradition. Some reclaim blade dances and hunting schools as arts of bodily control. Others reject a public culture eager to praise a Mantid only when their forelimbs become weapons.

---

## VI. Mantids in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Mantid witnesses, artists, and duelists face both prestige and constant assumptions about violence.

### Oria

Warm galleries support small craft communities specializing in fine visual inspection.

### Vinyot

Broadcast slow-duels and illegal reflex contests commodify Mantid reaction speed.

### Arneria

Nocturnal and heat-adapted Mantids maintain shade gardens and patient hunting traditions.

### Al'Ar

Leaf-mimicking island communities build suspended gardens and storm-fold shelters.

---

## VII. Mantid Delvers

Mantid delvers serve as sentries, scouts, skirmishers, investigators, and visual analysts. Their ability to hold position does not remove fatigue or eliminate the need for rotation.

Labyrinth illusions often exploit motion sensitivity. Crews should test suspicious movement from cover and avoid forcing the Mantid to verify every visual threat alone.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits are an initial Aerathon playtest package and require DM approval until table testing is complete.

### Creature Type, Size, and Speed

You are a Humanoid, Medium. Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Wide Field

You gain proficiency in Perception. You cannot be surprised while conscious unless your vision is fully obscured.

### Grasping Limbs

Your forelimbs are natural weapons dealing 1d6 slashing damage. They may also manipulate objects but cannot provide additional attacks, shields, or magic item attunement.

### Sudden Motion

When you roll initiative, you may move up to half your speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Once used, this trait cannot be used again until you finish a short or long rest.

---

## X. Recognized Traditions

### Leaf Mantids

Leaf traditions use color, silhouette, and garden craft as visual language rather than proof of secrecy.

### Orchid Mantids

Orchid communities are associated with ceremonial display, pollinator gardens, and the politics of being exoticized as beautiful.

### Dryland Mantids

Dryland Mantids maintain heat management, night travel, and spare-motion disciplines adapted to desert life.

---

## XI. Playing a Mantid

Consider:

- What do you notice first?
- When is stillness comforting?
- What relationship do you have with martial expectations?
- How do you mark the boundary of your near field?
- Which movement is part of your personal expression?
- What have you seen but not understood?

Observation provides information, never omniscience or moral certainty.
