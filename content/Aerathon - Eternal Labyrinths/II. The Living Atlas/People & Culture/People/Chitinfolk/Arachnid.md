## I. Lineage Overview

Arachnids are spider-, scorpion-, and related Chitinfolk with enormous variation in limb arrangement, silk, venom, eyes, sensory hairs, body segmentation, and preferred environment.

They are united more by shared administrative needs and histories of persecution than by one culture. Web-building, venom, nocturnal life, and solitary behavior are each common in some communities and absent in others.

---

## II. Origins and the Burned Webs

Many Arachnid archives are woven rather than written. During historic **Burned Web** campaigns, authorities destroyed homes, genealogies, contracts, and artworks together, then cited the missing records as proof that Arachnids had no civic history.

Modern silk conservatories preserve what survived and teach duplicate weaving so no single confiscation can erase a community again.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Arachnids may be upright, tailed, broad-bodied, or possess a horizontal lower body. They may have two to eight functional limbs and two to many eyes.

Fine hairs and webs detect vibration with great precision. Crowded floors and careless contact can become sensory overload. Webbing is sometimes bodily output and sometimes crafted property; neither may be cut or collected casually.

Venom is a medical and bodily matter. Possession does not imply danger, and lack of venom does not make a person less Arachnid.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Arachnid households include web circles, burrow families, silk houses, solitary residences linked by correspondence, venom clinics, caravan tents, and mixed urban homes.

The **guest thread** marks which strands a visitor may touch. Crossing other web lines can be equivalent to opening drawers, reading mail, or entering a locked room.

Solitude often functions as privacy and sensory care rather than hostility.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Arachnid cultures frequently value patience, craft, boundary clarity, indirect communication, vibration music, preparedness, and self-sufficiency within chosen networks.

Modern debate concerns fear as public policy. Some advocates favor visible venom declarations to reduce panic; others argue that no comparable burden is placed upon citizens carrying weapons, spells, or alchemical tools.

---

## VI. Arachnids in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Silk houses receive elite patronage while Arachnid tenants still face discriminatory housing restrictions.

### Oria

Cavern webs, heat pockets, and stone burrows support craft and mining communities.

### Vinyot

Industrial silk and venom pharmaceuticals generate wealth alongside severe bodily-rights disputes.

### Arneria

Scorpion and burrowing traditions maintain night markets, cool dens, and desert route signals.

### Al'Ar

Canopy web settlements and volcanic-stone burrows specialize in storm lines and suspended bridges.

---

## VII. Arachnid Delvers

Arachnid delvers are climbers, trap analysts, silk engineers, scouts, medics, and vibration sentries.

Labyrinths may animate abandoned webs, counterfeit familiar steps, or demand venom as a key. The crew should determine before entry whether collection is acceptable and how accidental envenomation is treated without blame or spectacle.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits are an initial Aerathon playtest package and require DM approval until table testing is complete.

### Creature Type, Size, and Speed

You are a Humanoid. Choose Small or Medium. Your walking speed is 30 feet, and you have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed.

### Darkvision

You have darkvision out to 60 feet.

### Webcraft

You know the _mage hand_ cantrip; the hand appears as visible silk. At 3rd level, you may cast _snare_ once per long rest without material components. Wisdom, Intelligence, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability, chosen when you select this lineage.

### Vibration Sense

While touching a web, rope, floor, or wall, you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to detect movement across the same connected surface within 30 feet.

---

## X. Recognized Body Traditions

### Web Arachnids

Web traditions center silk architecture, suspended travel, vibration language, and strict boundary customs.

### Hunting Arachnids

Hunting communities favor mobile observation and rapid pursuit; many produce little or no structural silk.

### Scorpion Kin

Scorpion traditions commonly include grasping limbs, tails, desert burrows, venom medicine, and night-route law.

---

## XI. Playing an Arachnid

Consider:

- What does a web mean in your life?
- Which vibration do you recognize instantly?
- How do you communicate a boundary?
- Does your body produce silk or venom?
- What kind of home accommodates you?
- How do you respond when someone fears your appearance?

An Arachnid character need not be eerie, secretive, predatory, or solitary.
