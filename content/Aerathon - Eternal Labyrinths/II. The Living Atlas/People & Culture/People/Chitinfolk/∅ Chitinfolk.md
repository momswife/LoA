## I. Classification Overview

**Chitinfolk** is the modern collective classification for terrestrial sapient peoples whose bodies follow insectoid, arachnid, or myriapod patterns.

The category includes Formicids, Mantids, Arachnids, and Myriapodids. It does not imply a shared empire, hive mind, diet, reproductive system, or degree of individuality.

Chitin is body tissue, not armor issued at birth. Additional limbs are anatomy, not public tools. Familiar resemblance to small animals does not reduce a person’s scale of thought.

---

## II. Origins and the Many-Voiced Census

Older officials counted colonies, nests, and warrens instead of people. A settlement might appear as one citizen when taxed and ten thousand enemies when cleared.

The **Many-Voiced Census** began when Chitinfolk communities demanded that every consenting adult be recorded by name while collective institutions remained legible in law. It established that strong social coordination is evidence of society, not absence of self.

Chitinfolk origins include Convergence migrations, deep-earth emergence, divine alteration, long-settled Labyrinth ecologies, and lineages with no surviving origin story.

---

## III. Boundary with Other Folk

[[Pterygotid|Pterygotids]] remain [[∅ Avianfolk|Avianfolk]]. Though many possess chitinous anatomy, their central histories concern aerial migration, wing law, sky architecture, and the Four Winds.

Aquatic arthropod peoples whose lives are structured by sea or river may be recognized as [[Crustacean]] Aquaticfolk. Classification follows embodied culture and collective preference rather than exterior material alone.

No person must abandon kin in one category to receive recognition in another.

---

## IV. Body, Molting, and Sensory Life

Chitinfolk bodies may include segmented exoskeletons, antennae, palps, compound or clustered eyes, multiple limbs, spinnerets, stingers, mandibles, chemical senses, fine hairs, and periodic molts.

Molting can be routine, vulnerable, painful, celebratory, private, or medically dangerous. Discarded exuviae remain protected bodily material.

Buildings may require varied door widths, low controls, ceiling routes, resting hooks, horizontal sleeping surfaces, humidity chambers, vibration buffering, and signage readable without color assumptions.

---

## V. Society and Kinship

Chitinfolk society includes colonies, broods, houses, clutch families, guild nests, solitary households, traveling companies, webs of correspondence, religious orders, and mixed neighborhoods.

Words such as **queen**, **worker**, **drone**, and **hive** have distinct local meanings and should never be inferred from body shape. A queen may be a parent, elected coordinator, ritual figure, reproductive role, historic office, or a mistranslation a community rejects.

Pheromonal communication can convey identity, alarm, health, mood, direction, or social convention. It is not involuntary truth.

---

## VI. Living Lineages

### [[Formicid]]

Antlike peoples known for chemical language, load-sharing, engineered settlements, and constant struggle against the stereotype that cooperation erases choice.

### [[Mantid]]

Mantid peoples with grasping forelimbs, exceptional stillness, rapid motion, and traditions that distinguish observation from predation.

### [[Arachnid]]

Spider, Scorpion, and related peoples whose silk, venom, vibration sense, and varied body forms support extensive craft and kinship traditions.

### [[Myriapodid]]

Centipede- and Millipede-like peoples whose many-limbed motion, subterranean architecture, and segmental body knowledge challenge humanoid assumptions about access.

---

## VII. Chitinfolk in the Greater Regions

### Allemance

Court fashion imitates carapace and silk while real Chitinfolk contest museum collecting, molt trafficking, and exoticized etiquette.

### Oria

Deep workshops and geothermal galleries support cold-sensitive communities and precision engineering.

### Vinyot

Factories exploit fine manipulation and coordinated labor, while Chitinfolk unions turn those same strengths toward collective bargaining.

### Arneria

Desert, burrowing, nocturnal, and oasis lineages maintain heat-smart cities and chemical route markers.

### Al'Ar

Island communities build suspended dwellings, storm anchors, and volcanic-soil tunnels in close exchange with Avianfolk and Aquaticfolk.

---

## VIII. Chitinfolk and Delving

Chitinfolk delvers contribute climbing, vibration reading, chemical analysis, narrow-route exploration, load coordination, silk engineering, and unusual fields of view.

Labyrinths may counterfeit pheromones, exploit molt cycles, produce giant vermin that resemble people without being people, or create geometry intended to trap segmented bodies.

Crews must establish how alarm signals work, who may handle shed carapace or venom, and what extraction looks like for a body that cannot simply be dragged upright.

---

## IX. Religion and Philosophy

Chitinfolk participate in every major faith. Molt, metamorphosis, colony, patience, venom, silk, and decomposition carry meanings that differ sharply between communities.

Many traditions ask how an individual remains accountable within a collective. Others reject the premise that Chitinfolk are uniquely collective at all.

Coordination is not obedience. Solitude is not exile. Metamorphosis is not moral purification.

---

## X. Common Misconceptions

- Chitinfolk are not automatically hive-minded.
- Multiple limbs do not imply greater labor capacity or consent to work.
- Pheromones do not reveal every private feeling.
- Molting does not make old carapace ownerless.
- Venom and silk are not universal.
- A hard exoskeleton is not immunity to pain or injury.
- “Bug” is not a neutral collective term unless a person uses it for themselves.

---

## XI. Playing a Chitinfolk Character

Consider:

- Which signals can your companions perceive?
- What does a molt mean in your household?
- How does architecture usually fail your body?
- Are you part of a collective, and what can it ask of you?
- Which limb or sense do outsiders misunderstand?
- What material does your body produce, and who may receive it?
- What stereotype about instinct most often erases your choices?

Body plan creates possibilities and access needs, not personality.
