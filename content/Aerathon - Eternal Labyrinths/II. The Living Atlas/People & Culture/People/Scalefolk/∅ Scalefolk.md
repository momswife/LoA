## I. Classification Overview

**Scalefolk** is the modern collective classification for sapient reptilian peoples whose bodies and cultures are shaped primarily by terrestrial, arboreal, riverbank, marsh, desert, or littoral reptile kinships.

The classification addresses a major absence in older Ministry law. Reptilian people were previously scattered among Beastfolk, Aquaticfolk, Mythicfolk, monster registries, and draconic appendices.

Scalefolk are not lesser dragons.

They are not cold-blooded in the moral sense.

They are not one ancient empire waiting to return.

---

## II. Origins and the Broken Scale Registers

Old records repeatedly confused biological resemblance with draconic descent. Saurians were described as failed Dragonborn, Serpentines as incomplete Naga, and Crocodilians as speaking monsters.

The **Broken Scale Registers** are the surviving administrative volumes in which communities crossed out those imposed labels and wrote household names over them.

Modern classification follows self-identification, collective history, and body form rather than presumed proximity to dragons.

---

## III. Boundary with Other Folk

Testudinan turtle peoples remain [[∅ Aquaticfolk|Aquaticfolk]] because water routes, nesting shore law, and shell-ledger identity are central to their classification.

[[Dragonborn]] and [[Kobold|named Kobolds]] remain [[∅ Mythicfolk|Mythicfolk]] because their records concern draconic pact and emergent sapience.

Serpentine Scalefolk are distinct from the mythic [[Gorgonite|Gorgonites]], whose living crown and petrifying resonance arise through curse, divine inheritance, or transformation.

A person may participate in more than one community without becoming an administrative contradiction.

---

## IV. Body, Heat, and Molting

Scalefolk bodies may include scales, scutes, tails, claws, specialized teeth, sensory pits, crests, dewlaps, and periodic shedding.

Most regulate temperature behaviorally and through clothing, architecture, heated surfaces, shade, water, or magic. This does not mean they are emotionless, sluggish, or helpless in cold conditions.

Shed skin is personal biological material. Communities decide whether it is destroyed, composted, archived, crafted, or used ritually.

---

## V. Society and Kinship

Scalefolk households include clutches, families, sun courts, marsh blocks, river settlements, desert burrows, craft houses, temples, and chosen kin.

The word **clutch** may describe siblings, an age cohort, a political circle, or people who completed early care together. It does not imply that parents or biology own adult membership.

Heat and shade are common public responsibilities. A seat in the sun can be an accessibility provision rather than a privilege.

---

## VI. Living Lineages

### [[Saurian]]

Gecko, Chameleon, Monitor, and Iguana peoples associated with climbing, color, sun architecture, and varied arboreal or terrestrial traditions.

### [[Crocodilian]]

Crocodile, Alligator, Caiman, and Gharial peoples shaped by river margins, patient motion, powerful bodies, and nesting-water law.

### [[Serpentine]]

Viper, Cobra, Boa, Python, and Colubrid peoples with limbless or partially limbed bodies, chemical senses, and strong traditions concerning gaze, venom, and bodily fear.

---

## VII. Scalefolk in the Greater Regions

### Allemance

Heated arcades, glass gardens, canal districts, and court fascination create both access and spectacle.

### Oria

Thermal springs and artificed heat support small communities whose infrastructure demonstrates that climate compatibility is designed, not inherited fate.

### Vinyot

Scale fashion, shed-skin markets, industrial heat, and broadcast combat generate major consent disputes.

### Arneria

Desert Saurian and Serpentine communities maintain extensive sun, shade, burrow, and night-route traditions.

### Al'Ar

Crocodilian river mouths and island Saurian communities participate deeply in littoral trade and storm refuge.

---

## VIII. Scalefolk and Delving

Scalefolk delvers contribute climbing, heat sensing, amphibious movement, patience, scent analysis, and navigation through narrow or unstable terrain.

Labyrinths frequently exploit reptilian stereotypes by presenting false basking chambers, draconic commands, or prey-response enchantments.

No member should be assigned a hazard solely because scales appear protective.

---

## IX. Religion and Philosophy

Scalefolk participate in every major religion. Solar imagery is common without implying Dawn Court allegiance. River and shed-skin traditions often treat renewal as continuity through changed form.

A molt is not automatically rebirth.

A sun is not automatically a god.

A dragon is not automatically an ancestor.

---

## X. Common Misconceptions

- Scalefolk do experience complex emotion.
- Temperature needs do not determine personality.
- Venom is not universal among Serpentines.
- Crocodilian stillness is not laziness.
- Color change is not permission to read a Saurian’s private state.
- Scales are not armor equivalent in every lineage.
- Dragonborn are not the nobility of all scaled peoples.

---

## XI. Playing a Scalefolk Character

Consider:

- What environmental provision does your body need?
- What happens to shed skin?
- Which collective name do you use?
- Has anyone mistaken you for a dragon or monster?
- What form of stillness restores you?
- Which climate is home because of community rather than biology?
- What body signal remains private?

Scalefolk identity offers embodied histories, not assigned temperament.

---
