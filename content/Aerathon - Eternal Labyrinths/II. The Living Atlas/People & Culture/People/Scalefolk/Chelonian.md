## I. Lineage Overview

Chelonians are terrestrial Tortoise Scalefolk with protective shells, powerful limbs, long life spans in many communities, and extensive traditions of dryland engineering and deliberate civic process.

They are distinct from [[Testudinan]] Aquaticfolk, whose sea-turtle bodies and cultures center swimming, nesting shores, and oceanic return. Chelonians may enjoy water, but their principal histories are terrestrial.

They are common in Arnerian drylands, Al'Ari islands, warm grasslands, and old walled cities across Allemance.

---

## II. Origins and the Moving Walls

Early observers mistook resting Chelonians for carved guardians, boundary stones, or animate fortifications. Several settlements were mapped as abandoned while their citizens were present and refusing to answer trespassers.

The **Moving Walls Declaration** established that a protective body does not become architecture and that stillness cannot be treated as civic absence.

Chelonian oral histories preserve roads measured by shade, water, edible scrub, and the time required for every household member to arrive.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Chelonians are Medium, with shells integrated into the spine and ribs, beaked mouths, scaled limbs, and varied neck and tail length.

A shell feels pressure, temperature, injury, vibration, and touch. Painting, tapping, drilling, polishing, climbing, or attaching equipment requires consent.

Shell size complicates narrow doors, seating, sleeping, armor, falls, and extraction. The body’s protection does not eliminate internal injury.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Chelonian households include shell circles, road families, courtyard blocks, orchard communities, archive houses, caravan rests, and mixed Scalefolk neighborhoods.

The **long table** custom keeps deliberation open long enough for slow speakers, distant members, and people requiring translation or rest to participate.

This tradition values complete participation, not delay for its own sake. Emergency councils use named authority and mandatory later review.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Chelonian cultures often value preparedness, continuity, dry humor, careful consent, durable public works, hospitality with clear boundaries, and decisions that remain usable after their authors leave.

Their shadow is compulsory patience. Anger, urgency, speed, risk, and changing one’s mind are as Chelonian as deliberation.

Modern youth movements reject the expectation that every long-lived person should become an archive or elder.

---

## VI. Chelonian Peoples in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Old courtyard districts and civil archives host large communities challenging inaccessible doors and ceremonial shell tapping.

### Oria

Geothermal halls support modest populations with insulated shell coverings and winter commons.

### Vinyot

Chelonians work in planning, freight, construction, law, performance, and industry while broadcasts exaggerate their pace for comedy.

### Arneria

The largest populations maintain dryland roads, water courts, orchard walls, and slow-season caravans.

### Al'Ar

Island Chelonians build volcanic courtyards, storm shelters, and long-lived civic gardens.

---

## VII. Chelonian Delvers

Chelonian delvers are defenders, structural readers, logisticians, archivists, rescuers, and patient hazard analysts.

Labyrinths may identify shells as doors, inscribe commands onto them, create passages too narrow to turn within, or mistake defensive withdrawal for consent to transport.

Crews must plan route width, fall recovery, fitted packs, and extraction before entry.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits are an initial Aerathon playtest package and require DM approval until table testing is complete.

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Constitution score increases by 2.

### Size

Your size is Medium.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Integrated Shell

When you are not wearing armor, your base Armor Class is 13 + your Constitution modifier, to a maximum bonus of +2. You may use a shield and still gain this benefit.

### Shell Brace

When you are hit by an attack, use your reaction to gain a +2 bonus to AC against that attack, potentially causing it to miss. You may use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Steady Frame

You have advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to resist being knocked prone or moved against your will.

### Long Memory, Chosen Subject

Gain proficiency in History or Nature.

## X. Recognized Chelonian Traditions

### Dryland Chelonians

Dryland communities maintain water law, heat routes, burrow courtyards, and seasonal economies across arid regions.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Dryland Reserve.** You ignore the effects of extreme heat and require half as much water as normal.

### Forest Chelonians

Forest peoples preserve low-route agriculture, leaf-litter craft, orchard settlements, and rain calendars.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Underbrush Path.** Nonmagical difficult terrain caused by plants does not cost you extra movement. You gain proficiency in Nature.

### Giant Chelonians

Giant Tortoise communities are associated with island histories, long civic memory, and body-scale access distinct from combat strength.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Powerful Build.** You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

If the campaign uses Homeland Imprints, choose one trait representing the region that most strongly shaped your character.

### Allemance — Long-Table Citizen

You gain proficiency in Persuasion. If a conversation lasts at least 10 minutes, you can add your Wisdom modifier to one Persuasion check made during it.

### Oria — Snow-Shell Traveler

You ignore the effects of extreme cold. You have advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to keep your footing on ice.

### Vinyot — Crowd Shelter Protocol

You gain proficiency in Insight. A willing creature of your size or smaller can treat you as half cover while within 5 feet of you and while you are not incapacitated.

### Arneria — Cistern Witness

You gain proficiency in Survival. After examining a cistern, well, or reservoir for 1 minute, you can determine whether its water level changed significantly within the last 24 hours.

### Al'Ar — Tide-Shell

You gain a swimming speed of 25 feet and can hold your breath for up to 30 minutes.

---

## XII. Playing a Chelonian

Consider:

- Who may touch or decorate your shell?
- What decision deserves a long table?
- Which road accommodates your body?
- How do you express urgency?
- What memory do others expect you to preserve?
- When have you chosen speed over certainty?

A shell offers protection and embodiment, not a personality built from patience.
