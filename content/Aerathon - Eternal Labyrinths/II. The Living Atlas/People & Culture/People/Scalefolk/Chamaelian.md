## I. Lineage Overview

Chamaelians are Chameleon Scalefolk distinguished by independently mobile eyes, prehensile bodies, specialized tongues, grasping hands and feet, and skin capable of complex color change.

Their color is influenced by communication, temperature, health, light, stress, display, and deliberate choice. It is neither a perfect mood ring nor a public confession.

Chamaelian communities are common in warm forests, garden cities, island interiors, and carefully heated urban districts.

---

## II. Origins and the Untranslated Court

Early officials employed painters to “translate” Chamaelian testimony by recording skin color. Those records routinely replaced spoken statements with outsider guesses.

The **Untranslated Court** refused all such evidence and required interpreters to distinguish observed color, speaker-provided meaning, and uncertainty.

Modern color lexicons remain community-specific. Even a trained interpreter asks what a display means to the individual using it.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Chamaelians are usually Small or Medium, with grasping digits, independently mobile eyes, prehensile tails, compressed bodies, crests or horns in some peoples, and long projectile tongues.

Their visual field is wide but depth judgment often sharpens when both eyes focus together. Visually chaotic rooms can be tiring despite their ability to observe several directions.

Color change may be difficult under illness, cold, injury, exhaustion, scar tissue, or magical interference. A muted display is not an emotional absence.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Chamaelian households include garden courts, branch families, color schools, orchard blocks, observatory circles, market houses, and mixed urban homes.

The **declared palette** lets a person identify which colors or patterns carry deliberate public meaning. Everything else remains ambiguous unless they explain it.

Formal meetings often combine speech, gesture, written color, texture, and light so no single channel controls participation.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Chamaelian cultures often value contextual speech, visual artistry, careful observation, patience before interpretation, thermal hospitality, and the freedom to become conspicuous.

Their recurring debate concerns concealment. Camouflage can provide safety and beauty, yet public institutions often reward Chamaelians for disappearing rather than altering hostile surroundings.

Visibility may be celebration, protest, warning, fashion, or simply comfort.

---

## VI. Chamaelian Peoples in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Garden courts and fashion houses celebrate color while courts continue to overread bodily display.

### Oria

Heated observatories and glass galleries host small but established cold-climate communities.

### Vinyot

Broadcast color grading, advertising, and surveillance create major image-rights disputes.

### Arneria

Oasis and night-active Chamaelians use shade courts, reflective cloth, and deliberate cooling displays.

### Al'Ar

Forest and island Chamaelian communities are widespread in canopy agriculture, storm observation, and festival arts.

---

## VII. Chamaelian Delvers

Chamaelian delvers are sentries, scouts, climbers, illusion analysts, negotiators, and readers of visually unstable environments.

Labyrinths may counterfeit companion colors, impose visible emotions, split the eyes between separate illusions, or create backgrounds that compel involuntary matching.

Crews should privilege explicit words and agreed signals over interpretations of skin.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits are an initial Aerathon playtest package and require DM approval until table testing is complete.

### Creature Type, Size, and Speed

You are a Humanoid. Choose Small or Medium. Your walking speed is 30 feet, and you have a climbing speed of 20 feet.

### Panoramic Sight

Gain proficiency in Perception. You have advantage on saving throws against being surprised while conscious and able to see.

### Color Veil

As a bonus action, alter your skin to match your surroundings. You gain advantage on the next Dexterity (Stealth) check you make before the end of the turn. You may use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Prehensile Balance

You have advantage on Strength (Athletics) and Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks made to climb, balance, or retain your grip.

## X. Recognized Chamaelian Traditions

### Forest Chamaelians

Forest traditions use layered greens, branch routes, rain timing, and color speech suited to changing canopy light.

### Horned Chamaelians

Horned peoples cultivate crests and horns through personal decoration, challenge display, lineage history, and ceremonial art.

### Garden Chamaelians

Garden communities are strongly urban, using built plantings, mirror law, controlled light, and public color performances.

---

## XI. Playing a Chamaelian

Consider:

- Which colors do you use deliberately?
- Who may interpret your skin?
- When do your eyes focus together?
- What environment makes camouflage impossible?
- Do you enjoy being visible?
- Which signal does your crew use instead of guessing your mood?

Color language offers expression and misunderstanding, not emotional transparency.
