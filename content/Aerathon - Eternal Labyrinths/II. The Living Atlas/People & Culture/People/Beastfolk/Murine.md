## I. Lineage Overview

Murines are Mouse and Rat Beastfolk known for route memory, small-bodied navigation, communal adaptation, and distinct relationships to inherited magic.

They live throughout Aerathon, especially in Arneria and the great southern cities.

Murine communities understand what it means to inhabit systems scaled for someone else.

Steps are too tall.

Counters obscure the speaker.

Crowds become walls.

Gaps become roads.

The same condition shapes politics. Institutions overlook Murines physically and then describe the resulting hidden networks as suspicious.

Mouse communities preserve **Voiceless Speech**, a facial and micro-gestural language invisible to most untrained observers. Rat communities lost access to that language in a poorly understood historical break and developed other forms of mutual aid, public speech, magical adaptation, and political organizing.

The distinction matters.

It does not dissolve shared lineage.

Murines ask:

> What can a community build inside a structure that was never designed to see it?

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

In the **Account of the First Maze**, the Old Court creates a path with walls too high to see over.

Larger peoples break through.

The first Murine walks it carefully, touching each turn and remembering not only where they went but how the choice felt.

When the gods move the walls, the Murine retraces the route that had existed.

The Old Court asks why they followed a vanished path.

The Murine answers:

> Because someone may still be waiting inside the version I remember.

The story grounds Murine route memory in responsibility rather than mere navigation.

### The Broken Twitch

Mouse oral traditions state that all early Murines once shared Voiceless Speech.

Rat access disappeared during an event called the **Broken Twitch**.

Accounts disagree about the cause:

- A magical plague
- A sacrificed language used to protect survivors
- Deliberate Crown experimentation
- A divine punishment
- Gradual divergence later remembered as catastrophe

No explanation has been verified.

The subject is politically sensitive because investigators have repeatedly treated living Rats as damaged Mice rather than a people with their own complete culture.

### Hidden Publics

Murine neighborhoods developed message walls, crawl routes, face-to-face councils, miniature markets, mutual-aid kitchens, and emergency paths within larger cities.

Authorities labeled these networks secret.

Murines answer that a public built too large for them does not become inclusive merely because its doors are technically open.

## III. Body and Small-Scale Form

Murines commonly possess:

- Mouse or Rat facial inheritance
- Mobile ears and whiskers
- Long tails
- Small or moderate frames
- Strong balance
- Low-light awareness
- Precise route memory
- Rapid hands and expressive facial movement

Mice are Small.

Rats may be Small or Medium.

### Scale

Small bodies interact differently with danger.

A shallow flood becomes a current. A normal crowd becomes difficult terrain. A falling tool becomes a major hazard. A larger ally becomes cover.

Small scale also creates access to routes, concealment, riding, gliding, and spaces unavailable to others.

Neither reality should be reduced to weakness or advantage alone.

### Mazeproof

Murines can retrace a consciously traveled route for seven days and possess an unusual relationship with maze magic.

The ability depends upon conscious travel. Being carried unconscious, teleported, or moved through altered memory may not establish a route.

### Voiceless Speech

Mouse Voiceless Speech uses facial tension, whisker position, breathing, mouth shape, eye focus, and other micro-gesture.

It is language, not a universal expression of emotion.

Secretly recording or training automated systems to decode it without community consent has become a major privacy concern.

## IV. Society and Kinship

Murine communities include warrens, blocks, extended families, mutual-aid circles, mounted companies, newspapers, gliding clubs, temple households, and political networks.

### The Second Route

Murine planning often requires a **Second Route**.

It is not merely an emergency exit. It must remain usable by the smallest, slowest, injured, burdened, or publicly recognizable member of the group.

A route that works only for the scout who found it is information, not access.

### Two Conversations

Mouse gatherings may carry spoken and Voiceless conversations simultaneously.

This can allow subtle coordination, affection, correction, or commentary.

It can also exclude non-Mouse family members.

Arnerian Twitch Literacy traditions developed partly to bring chosen outsiders into the second conversation.

### Rat Mutual Aid

Rat communities are strongly associated with rapid response where formal institutions fail.

Food, medicine, shelter, rumor verification, legal assistance, and transport may move through networks with no central leader.

The absence of central authority creates resilience.

It can also make accountability difficult when harm passes through the same network.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Murine communities commonly admire:

- Collective effort
- Route memory
- Resourcefulness
- Subtle communication
- Mutual aid
- Political vigilance
- Adaptation under neglect
- Refusal to equate size with importance

These values cast long shadows.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Community | Ensuring nobody survives alone | Treating privacy as disloyalty |
| Subtlety | Communicating under pressure | Excluding those who cannot read the signs |
| Survival | Adapting when systems fail | Expecting crisis forever |
| Route memory | Preserving access and return | Refusing changed paths |
| Mutual aid | Meeting need directly | Avoiding accountability |
| Political vigilance | Recognizing institutional harm | Assuming every institution acts in bad faith |

A recurring Murine fear is disappearance without record: being lost in a crowd, buried inside a system, or remembered only as a population rather than a person.

### Internal Divisions

Modern Murines debate:

- Whether the Broken Twitch should remain a shared Mouse-Rat project
- Whether teaching Voiceless Speech protects or dilutes it
- How invisible communication should be treated in court
- Whether Allemancian mounted traditions preserve skill or romanticize youth conscription
- Whether mutual-aid networks need formal oversight
- How Small and Medium Rats experience the same institutions differently
- Whether mazeproof route memory should be used in state mapping

## VI. Murines in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemancian Murines have long served as mounted messengers and lancers.

Many take pride in the skill.

The history of baronial youth conscription remains a source of anger. Modern companies increasingly require adult voluntary entry and independent mounts rather than treating larger people as convenient transport.

### Oria

Oric Murines use larger allies, machinery, snow structures, and House architecture as mobile cover.

They work in inspection, courier routes, records, repair, kitchens, emergency signaling, and small-scale defense.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Murines are journalists, organizers, gliders, merchants, printers, performers, informants, and mutual-aid coordinators.

Their Drifting Drop methods transformed city movement and were quickly commercialized by firms that did not credit the neighborhoods that developed them.

### Arneria

Arneria is the spiritual heartland of many Mouse communities and the principal center of Voiceless Speech teaching.

Rats are equally influential in public organizing, bazaar politics, route aid, and independent media.

### Al’Ar

Al’Ari Murines adapted gliding descent to ship boarding, rigging, rescue, and performance.

Mixed Feline-Murine communities have produced both strong alliance and enduring tension concerning surveillance, pursuit, and who controls hidden spaces.

## VII. Murines and Delving

Murines serve as scouts, route keepers, spell supporters, infiltrators, gliders, mounted combatants, journalists, and recovery specialists.

Their advantages include:

- Route retracing
- Low-light vision
- Small-scale access
- Silent coordination
- Unusual interaction with nearby magic
- Strong homeland movement techniques

Labyrinths exploit Murine scale through passages that narrow gradually, false familiar routes, maze effects with altered memory, and party members large enough to become involuntary terrain.

A common warning states:

> If the route remembers you back, stop calling it a map.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Dexterity score increases by 2.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Mazeproof

You never become lost while retracing any path you have consciously traveled within the last 7 days.

Additionally, you are treated as a minotaur for the purposes of the `maze` spell.

### Low-Light Vision

You treat dim light as bright light.

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Mouse

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Small.

**Voiceless Speech.** You can silently communicate with any Mouse who can see your face, and vice versa. These conversations are undetectable to other species without special training or magic.

**Collective Magic.** As a reaction, when a creature within 60 feet loses concentration on a spell, you may expend a spell slot equal to or higher than its level to sustain it. The spell continues for its full remaining duration.

Mouse Murines are Small-bodied people with fine facial expression, precise movement, and inherited access to Voiceless Speech. Their silent conversations sustain intimacy and coordination in places built to overlook them, but do not make Mouse communities secretive by nature or grant outsiders permission to decode them.

### Rat

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Small or Medium, your choice.

**Leftover Magic.** When a creature within 60 feet casts a spell of a level less than or equal to your level divided by 3, minimum 1st level, you can use your reaction to gain its benefits as if you were also targeted.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

**Survivable.** You can survive twice as long without food or water.

Rat Murines range from compact Small bodies to sturdier Medium frames and inhabit rural, urban, maritime, and subterranean communities throughout Aerathon. Their ability to endure scarcity and repurpose lingering magic reflects generations of adaptation; it does not excuse institutions that expect Rats to survive neglect.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

### Allemance — Versatile Rider

As a bonus action while mounted, prepare for an emergency dismount. If hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to dismount into a nearby space.

Mounting a bipedal creature costs only 5 feet of movement.

**Royal Lancer.** You are proficient with the lance. While mounted, you ignore the lance’s disadvantage against targets within 5 feet.

### Oria — Versatile Rider

You gain Versatile Rider as described above.

**Mouse Among Giants.** If a creature at least one size larger than you would provide half cover, you instead gain three-quarters cover.

### Vinyot — Drifting Drop

With 1 minute of preparation, tie a sail or 10-by-10-foot cloth to your ankles and grip the corners.

While falling, you descend at 60 feet per round and take no fall damage. You can release the cloth as an action.

**Gliding Descent.** While using Drifting Drop in moderate or strong wind, you can move up to your speed in the wind’s direction without spending movement. In strong wind, choose a fall speed between 0 and 60 feet per round.

### Arneria — Twitch Literacy

You can teach a creature who shares a spoken language to understand Voiceless Speech after 30 days of 2-hour daily practice.

If linked via `telepathic bond`, the training takes only 2 hours.

**Collective Effort.** You can use the Help action to assist any creature who understands your Voiceless Speech and can see your face.

### Al’Ar — Drifting Drop

You gain Drifting Drop as described above.

**Pirate Drop.** While using Drifting Drop, you can wield a weapon in one hand. After falling at least 10 feet, make a melee attack against a creature you pass.

## XII. Playing a Murine

Consider:

- Which route can you retrace perfectly?
- Who belongs in your second conversation?
- How do you feel about the Broken Twitch?
- What structure was not built for your body?
- When does mutual aid become obligation?
- Have you ever disappeared inside a crowd?
- What larger ally makes you feel safe?
- What does public speech cost you?
- Which institution have you learned to use from underneath?
- Who would notice if you did not arrive?

A Murine may be solitary, loud, poor with directions, politically trusting, physically conspicuous, or uninterested in hidden routes.

Their lineage offers memory and adaptive community.

It does not make them small in consequence.
