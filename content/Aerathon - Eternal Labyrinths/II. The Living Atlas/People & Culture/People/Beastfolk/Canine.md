# Canine

**Folk Classification:** [[‎ Beastfolk|Beastfolk]]  
**Lineage Classification:** Canine  
**Registry Scope:** Sapient Lineage and Homeland Variance  
**Recognition Status:** Full Sapient Recognition  
**Delver Eligibility:** Unrestricted  
**Common Names:** Packfolk, Road-Hounds, Keen-Noses  
**Date of Revised Filing:** 7th Cycle, 3388 A.D.

---

## I. Lineage Overview

Canines are Wolf, Dog, and Hound Beastfolk known for scent memory, expressive communication, and a remarkable ability to coordinate with people whose habits differ from their own.

They are found in royal courts, shepherd camps, caravans, docks, neighborhood watches, theater companies, broadcast crews, temples, and delver parties.

This adaptability is often reduced to one word:

**Loyalty.**

Canines value loyalty, but the word has been used against them.

Employers describe obedience as instinct. Armies describe service as natural. Families dismiss departure as betrayal. Political leaders praise Canine devotion when they mean that a Canine should remain after everyone else has been permitted to reconsider.

Canine cultures therefore distinguish between:

- Loyalty and compliance
- Belonging and ownership
- Coordination and hierarchy
- Protection and control
- Being needed and being loved

The lineage’s defining social gift is not obedience.

It is the capacity to create momentum among people who have decided to move together.

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

Old Court traditions place early Canines among the Predatory and Herd Kinships: peoples taught to recognize both individual pursuit and collective survival.

In the **Tale of the Two Trails**, the first Canine receives one scent leading toward prey and another leading toward an injured companion.

The Old Court refuses to say which trail is correct.

The Canine circles both, returns to the group, and makes the choice public.

Most tellings focus less upon the decision than upon the return. Knowledge carried alone belongs only to the knower. Knowledge brought back becomes something a community can answer together.

### The Mourn-Howl Inheritance

Canine and Vulpine peoples formed the heart of the ancient [[Mourn-Howl Circles]], communities that treated grief as a shared transformation rather than a private weakness.

Their Four Moons of Mourning moved from silence, to naming, to howling, to remembrance.

Modern Canine culture retains fragments of those rites:

- A delver crew’s shout before entry
- A family naming a loss aloud before eating
- A neighborhood howl after a missing person is found
- The reverse telling of a life at funerals
- The belief that grief concealed too long becomes dangerous to the group

The Circles were persecuted as “wolf-witchcraft.” Their descendants learned that expressive emotion could create community and invite punishment at the same time.

That contradiction remains.

### Roads and Service

Canines became prominent along roads because scent, stamina, and social flexibility made them effective messengers, scouts, guards, herders, and mediators.

Some service was honored and freely chosen.

Some was enforced through breeding houses, military registries, debt contracts, and the assumption that a Canine separated from command would become unstable.

Modern Canine identity contains both histories. Pride in service cannot be understood honestly without acknowledging the systems that attempted to own it.

## III. Body and Sensory Form

Canines commonly possess:

- Fur of varied length and density
- Mobile ears
- Strong scent perception
- Muzzles and powerful dentition
- Digitigrade or semi-digitigrade legs
- Expressive tails
- Broad vocal ranges
- Bodies adapted for steady travel

Most are Medium, though Dog sublineages display considerable variation.

### Scent

Canine scent perception can identify recent contact, familiar individuals, emotional stress, food, smoke, blood, weather, and environmental change.

It is not mind-reading.

Scent evidence is affected by age, contamination, magic, crowding, wind, water, illness, and deliberate masking. Ministry courts treat it as skilled sensory testimony rather than infallible proof.

Strong industrial odors, alchemical clouds, perfume, and densely inhabited spaces can produce sensory fatigue.

### Ears and Tail

Canine body language carries meaning, but no gesture has one universal translation.

A lowered tail may indicate caution, relaxation, pain, concentration, cultural etiquette, or nothing more than available space. Ear position varies with anatomy and habit.

Reading a Canine body as though consulting an animal-handling manual is both unreliable and offensive.

### Food

Canines possess strong resistance to many ingested toxins.

This does not make spoiled food, poison, or unsafe expedition rations harmless. The belief that Canines can “eat anything” has caused preventable injury and unequal food provision.

## IV. Society and Kinship

Canine households include packs, families, road companies, shepherd circles, neighborhood groups, military households, broadcast teams, temples, and solitary adults whose kinship is maintained across distance.

The word **pack** may describe family, team, political affiliation, or chosen intimacy.

It does not automatically imply a leader.

### The Check-In

Many Canine groups practice a brief **check-in** before difficult action.

Each member states:

- Where they are
- What they understand the goal to be
- What danger they notice
- Whether they can continue

The practice may take seconds.

Its purpose is to replace assumed unity with confirmed unity.

Authoritarian groups imitate the form while punishing honest answers. Canine reformers argue that a check-in without the right to say no is merely roll call.

### Chosen Loyalty

Canine adoption often involves a witnessed act of return.

The prospective kin leaves the gathering, then chooses to cross the threshold again after being told what membership asks of them. The ritual emphasizes that belonging exists because departure was possible.

### Isolation

Canines are stereotyped as incapable of solitude.

Many enjoy it. Hounds in particular may spend long periods tracking, studying, or traveling alone.

The significant distinction is often between solitude chosen and exclusion imposed.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Canine communities commonly admire:

- Reliability
- Social courage
- Emotional honesty
- Coordination
- Protective attention
- Follow-through
- The willingness to return
- Celebration shared openly

These values cast long shadows.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Loyalty | Remaining through meaningful difficulty | Staying where one is exploited |
| Attunement | Recognizing another person’s needs | Taking responsibility for every emotion |
| Coordination | Helping a group act together | Suppressing disagreement |
| Protection | Responding quickly to danger | Controlling those being protected |
| Expressiveness | Making joy and grief shareable | Feeling required to perform emotion |
| Persistence | Completing difficult work | Pursuing what should be released |

A recurring Canine fear is that belonging can be revoked the moment they stop being useful, cheerful, or agreeable.

Some become indispensable. Some test relationships continually. Some leave first. Others refuse groups entirely because they have never experienced membership without command.

### Internal Divisions

Modern Canines debate:

- Whether pack language empowers or stereotypes them
- Whether the Lupine Throne preserves Canine history or privileges Wolf hierarchy
- How military and service traditions can be honored without romanticizing coercion
- Whether scent evidence belongs in court
- Whether public emotional culture leaves enough room for privacy
- When protective intervention becomes paternalism
- Whether inherited road obligations should survive modern borders

## VI. Canines in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemance is often called the **Lupine Throne**, though Dog and Hound communities are equally woven into Hearthland life.

Canines serve as magistrates, couriers, shepherds, brewers, performers, caravan wardens, and keepers of local roads.

Belonging is highly valued. The difficulty comes when familiarity becomes a political qualification and outsiders must earn protections locals receive automatically.

### Oria

Oric Canines work in remote Houses, rescue teams, messenger routes, herd management, machinery patrol, and winter watch.

Old conflicts between Canine and Cervine communities still shape some House relationships. Younger Canines increasingly reject inherited suspicion while insisting that reconciliation cannot require forgetting.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Canines dominate portions of public performance, broadcast delving, trade representation, sport, private security, and commercial storytelling.

Their social responsiveness is profitable. Contracts may require enthusiasm, audience access, or a public “pack identity” long after a crew’s relationships have changed.

Canine performers have become leaders in likeness and emotional-labor law.

### Arneria

Arnerian Canines travel pilgrimage roads, trade routes, temple circuits, caravan paths, and the living routes of Bat’yan.

Road Dogs and Hounds are renowned for persistence. Wolf communities often serve as oath witnesses and collective defenders.

Arnerian Canines debate whether a vowed road must be completed when conditions make the original purpose impossible.

### Al’Ar

Al’Ari Canines are coordinators, ship officers, storm messengers, performers, harbor mediators, and neighborhood organizers.

Island life rewards the ability to form working trust quickly.

It also produces temporary groups whose intense closeness may disappear after crisis. Canines raised in this tradition often become skilled at loving a crew without pretending it will last forever.

## VII. Canines and Delving

Canines commonly serve as trackers, leaders, scouts, morale keepers, guards, and broadcast personalities.

Their advantages include:

- Scent tracking
- Initiative and surprise resilience
- Coordinated combat
- Persistence across long routes
- Social awareness
- Rapid formation of team routines

Their greatest risk is over-identification with party success.

A Canine may internalize every injury, missed clue, or retreat as personal failure. Responsible crews rotate leadership, name responsibility clearly, and do not use loyalty to override consent.

Labyrinths can manipulate scent through false trails, preserved clothing, emotional residue, and creatures that imitate familiar people chemically.

A common warning states:

> A scent proves contact. It does not prove the person you want is waiting at its end.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

Your Canine character has the following traits.

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Charisma score increases by 2.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Scent Tracking

You have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track a creature if you have something it carried or wore within the past seven days.

### Hardy Stomach

You have advantage on saving throws to resist ingested poisons.

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Wolf

Wolf Canines commonly possess tall frames, strong group awareness, and traditions of coordinated defense.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Unit Tactics.** You have advantage on an attack roll if at least two allies are within 5 feet of your target and aren’t incapacitated.

**Better With an Audience.** If three or more allies you can see are within 30 feet, you gain a +2 bonus to Charisma checks.

### Dog

Dog Canines contain extraordinary physiological diversity and equally varied household histories.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Herding Tactics.** You have advantage on attack rolls against a creature if one of its allies is within 5 feet of it and isn’t incapacitated.

**Ears Toward Danger.** You have advantage on saving throws to resist magical traps or spells cast on you while surprised. You also add `1d4` to initiative rolls.

### Hound

Hound Canines possess strong scent inheritance and bodies adapted for sustained pursuit.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Relentless Pursuit.** When you hit a creature you are tracking using Scent Tracking, your movement does not provoke opportunity attacks from that creature until the end of your turn.

**Long Haul.** When you succeed on a Constitution saving throw to resist exhaustion, you may treat the result as if you had rolled a natural 20 for determining additional effects.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

### Allemance — Insistent Strike

Once per round when you miss with a melee weapon attack, you deal `1d4` damage of the weapon’s type to the target.

You can’t use this against the same target again until you hit it with a melee attack.

### Oria — Charm with Outsiders

You have advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks made with non-Cervine people from Oria, and with Cervines not native to Oria.

### Vinyot — Sniffing Out Deals

You have advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks to find the best price on nonmagical goods in any city with more than 10,000 people.

### Arneria — Strafing Strike

When you hit with a light weapon, increase the weapon’s damage die to the next size, up to `d12`.

You can’t use this again until you move at least 10 feet.

### Al’Ar — Like Herding Cats

If two allies use the Help action to assist you on the same skill check, you can reroll one of the d20s used to make that check.

## XII. Playing a Canine

Consider:

- Who receives your loyalty, and why?
- What group expectation do you refuse?
- How do you behave when nobody needs you?
- Which scent means home?
- Do you enjoy being emotionally legible?
- What pursuit should you have ended?
- Who taught you that departure can be honest?
- Is leadership a desire, skill, burden, or performance?
- What kind of solitude restores you?
- When did you last return by choice?

A Canine may be solitary, private, disloyal, poor at teamwork, physically unexpressive, or uninterested in leadership.

Their lineage offers social and sensory capacities.

It does not appoint a master.
