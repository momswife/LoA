# Avian Reclassification Record

**Former Classification:** Beastfolk — Avian Lineage  
**Current Classification:** [[‎ Avianfolk|Avianfolk]]  
**Registry Status:** Obsolete Filing Retained for Link Continuity  
**Mechanical Authority:** Superseded  
**Date of Reclassification:** 4th Cycle, 3387 A.D.  
**Date of Archival Notice:** 7th Cycle, 3388 A.D.

---

## I. Archival Notice

This file formerly classified Aarakocra, Raptors, Corvids, and Songbirds as one Beastfolk lineage.

That classification is obsolete.

The Aerial Peoples Review established that the old record:

- Collapsed several distinct peoples into one lineage
- Treated feathered physiology as more important than community identity
- Omitted non-avian flyers
- Failed to represent the Sky-Reeve inheritance
- Confused homeland with ancestry
- Presented cultural stereotypes as biological quirks
- Assigned one mechanical chassis to peoples whose modern records require separate treatment

The Ministry now recognizes [[‎ Avianfolk|Avianfolk]] as an independent folk classification.

The filename remains in place because older character records, campaign notes, and Lorevault links may still refer to it.

It must not be cited as current ethnography or used to create a new character.

## III. Mechanical Migration

Characters created under the legacy record should migrate as follows:

1. Select the current lineage that best represents the character.
2. Retain narrative history, homeland, relationships, and earned identity.
3. Replace legacy core, variant, and regional mechanics with the current lineage traits.
4. Resolve changed ability scores, equipment, and derived statistics with the Game Master.
5. Do not treat reclassification as an in-world bodily transformation.

Raptor characters ordinarily migrate to [[Raptorid]].

Corvid characters migrate to [[Corvids|Corvid]].

Songbird characters ordinarily migrate to [[Passerid]].

Characters who used the old page to represent another winged people should select the closest current lineage through consultation rather than being forced into one of those three.

The legacy abilities **Flight**, **Talons**, **Skyward Pulse**, **Predator’s Dive**, **Keen Opportunist**, and **Resonant Call** survive in revised form within the relevant modern entries.

---

## IV. Why the File Was Not Deleted

Deleting the record would create broken links and conceal the Ministry’s role in producing the original error.

Retention serves three purposes:

- It guides readers toward current material.
- It preserves mechanical migration information.
- It makes the history of classification visible.

An obsolete record should not disappear merely because the institution that issued it became embarrassed.

It should become unmistakably obsolete.
