## I. Lineage Overview

Celerines are a convergent Beastfolk lineage composed principally of Rabbit, Squirrel, and Hare peoples.

They are joined by more than speed.

Celerine history developed in settlements where survival depended upon noticing change early and making that notice socially useful. A warning unseen by others could sound like panic. A trend recognized too soon could look foolish. A danger escaped before it became visible could be dismissed as cowardice.

Celerine cultures learned to make attention difficult to ignore.

Color, fashion, gesture, spellcraft, performance, alarm, and rapid movement became tools for turning private perception into public response.

This history explains the lineage’s association with visibility.

It does not mean every Celerine wants an audience.

Many value anonymity precisely because their bodies and reactions are noticed before their words. Others embrace spectacle as control over attention that would exist regardless.

The central Celerine question is:

> If you notice the future first, how loudly are you allowed to warn the present?

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

In the **Story of the First Startle**, the Old Court gathers several small kinships at the edge of an unfinished forest.

The gods create wind.

The Celerines move.

Nothing dangerous happens.

The other peoples laugh.

The gods create wind again. The Celerines remain still to avoid embarrassment. This time a dead branch falls where they had been standing.

The Old Court asks which response was correct.

The Celerines answer:

> The first saved us from nothing. The second taught us that shame can kill.

The story is often told to children who have been accused of overreacting.

Its second lesson is told less often: a life spent responding to every possible branch leaves no time to stand beneath a living tree.

### The Bright Warrens

Early Celerine settlements frequently combined ground warrens, tree routes, warning platforms, communal stores, and open performance spaces.

Color patterns identified safe doors, changed routes, seasonal hazards, food reserves, and the emotional condition of a household. Beauty and emergency design were not separate.

A beautiful warning was easier to remember.

A decorated refuge was easier to find.

A public costume could show what aid the wearer knew how to provide.

Modern Celerine fashion still carries traces of this practical language, though many motifs have become purely aesthetic or deliberately subversive.

### The First-Away Accusation

During the Age of Chains, Celerine communities that evacuated before official orders were frequently accused of cowardice, theft, panic, or abandoning civic duty.

When disaster arrived, authorities relied upon the routes and stores those same communities had prepared.

The phrase **first away** was originally an insult.

Modern emergency workers have reclaimed it as a title for the person who moves early enough to create an exit for everyone else.

## III. Body and Rapid Form

Celerines commonly possess:

- Long or highly mobile ears
- Strong hind limbs
- Fast reflexes
- Sensitive peripheral awareness
- Tails adapted to balance or signaling
- Light to moderate frames
- Rapid heart and breathing response
- Magical resistance expressed through motion

Most are Medium.

### Alertness

Celerine awareness is not perpetual fear.

Their senses are highly responsive to changes in sound, air, motion, magic, and group behavior. This makes surprise difficult and crowded environments exhausting.

Some Celerines use ear wraps, tinted lenses, weighted clothing, scent blockers, or quiet rooms to reduce sensory input.

### Arcane Alacrity

Celerine bodies respond rapidly to spell effects.

Researchers disagree whether this is ancestral magic, refined nervous response, or a remnant of communities that survived magical disasters by moving before an effect completed.

The ability does not indicate magical training.

### Rest

The stereotype that Celerines never sleep is false.

Many sleep lightly. Some require several short rest periods. Others learn deep sleep only in spaces guarded by trusted people.

Sleep difficulty is not a charming lineage quirk when it causes illness.

## IV. Society and Kinship

Celerine communities include warrens, canopy households, fashion houses, neighborhood alarms, theater families, courier circles, shared workshops, and dispersed networks coordinated through message and trend.

### Warning Has a Shape

Many communities distinguish warning levels through color, gesture, rhythm, or magical flourish.

The practice prevents every alert from carrying equal emotional force.

A household may signal:

- Notice this
- Prepare quietly
- Gather
- Leave soon
- Leave now
- Do not follow

Young Celerines are taught that making a warning clear includes saying what response is actually needed.

### Presentation

Self-presentation can be art, play, defense, politics, or a way of choosing what strangers notice first.

Traditional Celerine etiquette often asks before commenting upon ears, tail, clothing, magical color, or apparent nervousness.

An elaborate appearance does not necessarily invite public access.

### Stores and Projects

Squirrel and mixed communities often maintain distributed stores rather than one central reserve.

Food, tools, documents, spells, costumes, and emergency supplies are placed where they may be needed rather than where ownership is easiest to display.

This habit can produce astonishing preparedness.

It can also produce unfinished projects, duplicated effort, and homes in which nothing may be discarded because every object still contains a possible future.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Celerine communities commonly admire:

- Timely action
- Alertness
- Expressive courage
- Adaptability
- Preparation
- Inventive self-presentation
- The ability to create exits
- Curiosity about what is changing

These values cast long shadows.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Alertness | Recognizing danger early | Living as though danger never ends |
| Speed | Acting while action matters | Moving before understanding |
| Visibility | Controlling one’s public image | Feeling unreal without attention |
| Preparation | Making survival possible | Hoarding every possible solution |
| Adaptability | Changing without losing purpose | Never committing long enough to build |
| Escape | Preserving life and choice | Leaving every conflict unresolved |

A recurring Celerine fear is not danger itself, but being told they imagined it until response becomes impossible.

Some become theatrical. Some gather evidence obsessively. Some apologize for every instinct. Others cultivate stillness as an act of defiance.

### Internal Divisions

Modern Celerines debate:

- Whether the convergent lineage unfairly joins Rabbit, Squirrel, and Hare peoples
- Whether public spectacle empowers or commodifies Celerine bodies
- Whether alarm traditions worsen communal anxiety
- Whether magical fashion should be treated as protected speech
- Whether First-Away evacuations require official approval
- Whether large distributed stores constitute private property or community infrastructure
- How to distinguish intuition from fear without shaming either

## VI. Celerines in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemancian Celerines are artists, couriers, mill workers, brewers, teachers, gardeners, emergency organizers, and keepers of festival color.

Hearthland communities appreciate visible participation. Celerines who prefer privacy may be treated as withholding warmth rather than simply living differently.

### Oria

Oric Celerines work in rapid inspection, messenger service, magical hazard response, vertical construction, workshop logistics, and contrast-rich public design.

Their speed is valued when machinery fails. Oria can be less comfortable with Celerine improvisation when procedure remains technically intact.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Celerines shape fashion, advertising, theater, arcane entertainment, courier markets, nightlife, and public reputation.

Visibility creates wealth and surveillance together. Image contracts, copied magical effects, and compulsory public appearances are major political concerns.

### Arneria

Arnerian Celerines are prominent in bazaars, arenas, pilgrimage logistics, message routes, ritual performance, and disaster evacuation.

Kinetic display often carries sacred meaning. A burst of movement may mark vow, warning, celebration, or refusal.

### Al’Ar

Al’Ari Celerines thrive among ship rigging, storm stages, floating markets, island couriers, and architecture meant to move.

They are admired for improvisation. The cultural pressure to treat every disruption as exciting can leave little room for those exhausted by continual change.

## VII. Celerines and Delving

Celerines serve as scouts, arcane responders, mobile combatants, trap spotters, couriers, and party organizers.

Their advantages include:

- Immunity to ordinary surprise while conscious
- Speed
- Magical reflexes
- Vertical or evasive sublineage movement
- Homeland bursts capable of reshaping a short encounter

Their greatest danger is acting before the party understands what drew their attention.

Labyrinths exploit alertness with repeated false movement, delayed hazards, spell effects that reward premature dodging, and exits designed to separate the first person who reaches them.

A common warning states:

> The first opening is still part of the room.

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Rabbit

Rabbit Celerines often possess expressive ears, strong hind limbs, and a long history of social and magical display.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Extraordinary Panache.** You know the `prestidigitation` cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it.

### Squirrel

Squirrel Celerines possess strong balance, mobile tails, and bodies adapted to climbing and abrupt changes of direction.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Intelligence score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Vaulting Poise.** You can move up vertical surfaces equal to your speed without falling during the movement.

### Hare

Hare Celerines are commonly long-limbed and capable of extraordinary sustained bursts.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Longstride Escape.** When you take the Dash action, opportunity attacks against you are made with disadvantage until the start of your next turn.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

### Allemance — Burst of Color

As an action, emit a dazzling strobe of light for 1 minute.

Creatures within 60 feet that can see you must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks against other creatures until the start of their next turn. Creatures immune to charm automatically succeed.

Once you use this trait, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Oria — Burst of Darkness

As an action, summon black tendrils in a 5-foot radius around you for 1 minute. The area becomes difficult terrain.

Creatures in it must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take `1d6` bludgeoning damage and become restrained. They may escape with a DC 13 Strength or Dexterity check.

The effect ends early if you move or dismiss it.

Once you use this trait, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Vinyot — Burst of Silence

As an action, generate a 5-foot-radius sphere of magical silence centered on you for 1 minute. All sound and thunder damage are blocked. Creatures inside are deafened.

Once you use this trait, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Arneria — Burst of Speed

As an action, you and allies within 5 feet gain 10 feet of movement and increased long-jump distance for 1 minute.

Once per turn, you may choose which allies benefit.

Once you use this trait, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Al’Ar — Burst of Air

As an action, conjure a transparent air bubble around yourself for 1 minute. It floats on water, provides breathable air, and can move at half your speed.

You may submerge or surface with a bonus action. The bubble ends if you deal or take damage.

Once you use this trait, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

## XII. Playing a Celerine

Consider:

- What change do you notice first?
- When has early action saved someone?
- When did it carry you away too soon?
- Do you enjoy being noticed?
- What do you keep for a possible future?
- Where can you sleep deeply?
- How do you tell intuition from anxiety?
- Which part of your appearance is entirely for you?
- What unfinished project follows you?
- What would make you stand still beneath the branch?

A Celerine may be slow, anonymous, calm, unfashionable, physically cautious, or uninterested in magic.

Their lineage gives them rapid perception and movement.

It does not require them to live in alarm.
