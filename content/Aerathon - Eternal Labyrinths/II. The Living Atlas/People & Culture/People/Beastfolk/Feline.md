## I. Lineage Overview

Felines are Beastfolk divided broadly into **Chikitu**, the smaller-cat peoples, and **Grandi**, the great-cat peoples.

Al’Ar is their most influential cultural heartland, but Feline communities live throughout Aerathon.

They are known for privacy, precise movement, strong boundaries, watchful rest, and resistance to low-order divination.

Outsiders often summarize these traits as independence.

The word can be useful.

It can also conceal the relationships that make independence possible.

Felines frequently maintain networks of quiet aid, shared space, exchanged craft, and permission-based intimacy. Their communities may look loose because obligation is not always performed publicly.

The stereotype of the solitary Feline has justified abandonment:

- They prefer to handle it alone.
- They did not ask.
- They disappeared, so they must be fine.
- They will return if it matters.

Feline cultures ask how privacy can remain a right without becoming an excuse for others to stop paying attention.

Their central question is:

> What may remain yours even inside a relationship?

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

In the **Story of the Closed Eye**, the Old Court creates a creature that can be seen from anywhere.

The creature is never alone. Gods, spirits, neighbors, and enemies observe every meal, mistake, grief, and moment of sleep.

The first Feline closes one eye.

Half the watchers disappear.

They close the other.

For the first time, the creature has an inner life.

The Old Court grants Felines a path between observation and concealment, warning that privacy can protect the self but cannot excuse the refusal to witness harm.

### The Pride of Seven Roads

The ancient [[Pride of Seven Roads]] was a network of Feline-led caravans that joined trade, performance, militia, and law.

Its Coin-Law used ritual contests and Sandglass Coins to prevent disputes from becoming endless vendetta. Authority was temporary, public, and challengeable.

Modern Feline culture retains:

- The open-paw gesture ending formal conflict
- Decorative trade attire
- Road duels under supervised law
- Public negotiation as performance
- Suspicion of authority that cannot be challenged directly
- The idea that elegance is most meaningful when it remains functional in motion

The Prides were not universally just. Their law privileged people capable of public contest and could turn survival into spectacle.

Feline descendants debate whether the tradition democratized power or merely made power entertaining.

### Al’Ari Continuity

Felines shaped Al’Ar before its modern mixed populations and remain central to its maritime identity.

This does not make Al’Ar Feline property.

It does mean island architecture, privacy customs, ship craft, movement arts, and political performance cannot be understood without Feline history.

## III. Body and Prowling Form

Felines commonly possess:

- Fur of varied length, color, and pattern
- Mobile ears
- Tails used for balance and expression
- Retractable or fixed claws
- Powerful legs
- Acute hearing
- Flexible spines
- Strong low-light awareness

Most are Medium. Chikitu may stand near four feet, while Grandi commonly approach seven.

### Divinatory Privacy

Felines resist magical scrying and divination of moderate power.

The origin of this trait is disputed. Al’Ari traditions describe it as the Closed Eye’s gift. Researchers propose inherited planar occlusion or a lineage-wide echo of ancient concealment magic.

Whatever its source, the ability has political consequences.

Felines have served as couriers, dissidents, spies, mediators, and private citizens precisely because common magical surveillance cannot reach them easily.

Some governments treat that privacy as suspicious.

Current Ministry law does not.

### Alert Sleep

Felines remain aware of sound during unconsciousness.

This does not mean their rest is optional or that they can perform a watch while sleeping. Continual exposure to noise can degrade sleep and health even when it produces remembered sound.

### Touch

Ear, tail, fur, and whisker contact carries individual meaning. Permission should be obtained rather than inferred from familiarity.

## IV. Society and Kinship

Feline households may be called prides, houses, decks, courts, alleys, circles, crews, or simply families.

### The Closed Room

Many Feline homes preserve a space into which no one enters without invitation.

It may be a private room, rooftop, workshop, hammock, marked corner, or scheduled hour.

The practice teaches that intimacy does not dissolve boundaries.

In crowded or poor communities, the Closed Room may be symbolic: a curtain, a seat left undisturbed, a phrase meaning the person is present but unavailable.

### Quiet Aid

Feline support is often practical and indirect.

Food appears. A broken clasp is repaired. A dangerous person is watched. An exit is left open. A public argument ends because someone changed the subject at the correct moment.

This style can protect dignity.

It can also make care difficult to recognize or request. Modern Feline mutual-aid groups increasingly pair quiet provision with explicit check-in.

### Challenge

The legacy of the Seven Roads favors authority that can be questioned without destroying the relationship.

A person may challenge the plan, the price, the leader, or the boundary.

The right to challenge does not guarantee victory.

It establishes that disagreement is not treason.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Feline communities commonly admire:

- Self-possession
- Precision
- Privacy
- Controlled movement
- Observation
- Decisive action
- Elegance with purpose
- Respect for chosen boundaries

These values cast long shadows.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Independence | Preserving agency | Refusing needed help |
| Privacy | Protecting inner life | Concealing harm |
| Observation | Acting with good information | Watching without participating |
| Precision | Avoiding waste and error | Punishing imperfection |
| Decisiveness | Ending danger quickly | Acting without explanation |
| Poise | Remaining grounded under attention | Treating visible emotion as failure |

A recurring Feline fear is that being known will become being possessed.

Some curate every detail others see. Some leave before intimacy gains weight. Some choose loud, public lives because privacy should be voluntary rather than expected. Others build relationships through carefully repeated permission.

### Internal Divisions

Modern Felines debate:

- Whether Al’Ar remains a Feline homeland or a shared society shaped by Felines
- Whether Coin-Law duels preserve accountability or commercialize conflict
- Whether divinatory resistance creates special civic duties
- How Chikitu and Grandi power differs within shared institutions
- Whether quiet aid allows communities to ignore explicit need
- Whether piracy is history, stereotype, living trade, or political theater
- How much solitude employers and households must accommodate

## VI. Felines in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemancian Felines inhabit dense cities, farms, breweries, coastal towns, and old trade routes.

They adapt well to layered architecture and informal neighborhood movement. Their privacy can conflict with Hearthland expectations of visible participation and open doors.

### Oria

Oric Felines are artificers, spellbreakers, lodge specialists, inspectors, and ambitious migrants.

Their ability to disrupt magic and resist observation makes them valuable in House security. It also exposes them to suspicion from institutions built around traceable procedure.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Felines work in trade, shipping, security, finance, fashion, salvage, and private negotiation.

They have transformed old Pride performance into commercial sophistication. The danger is a market that treats every boundary as the opening position of a negotiation.

### Arneria

Arnerian Felines are navigators, spies, climbers, shrine wardens, performers, and practitioners of pursuit games such as **Kolaçan**.

Ritualized pursuit teaches attention and consent when practiced well. Reformers oppose versions that reproduce surveillance or coercive courtship.

### Al’Ar

Al’Ari Felines remain central to maritime craft, storm politics, piracy, performance, shipbuilding, and island identity.

Their cultural prominence carries responsibility. Younger Felines increasingly distinguish ancestral influence from entitlement to dominate newer communities.

## VII. Felines and Delving

Felines serve as scouts, mobile combatants, anti-divination couriers, climbers, infiltrators, and relic hunters.

Their advantages include:

- Divinatory privacy
- Powerful jumps
- Alert sleep
- Rapid movement
- Chikitu evasion or Grandi follow-through
- Strong environmental navigation

Their confidence in private movement can separate them from rescue.

Labyrinths exploit Feline curiosity and boundaries with hidden compartments, rooms visible only to one party member, scrying effects that deliberately fail, and paths that reward entering alone.

A common warning states:

> The room that cannot watch you may still be waiting.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Dexterity score increases by 2.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 35 feet.

### Prowling Between the Lines

You can’t be targeted by divination spells or magical scrying of 5th level or lower, unless the spell has a range of Self.

### Legs for Pouncing

Your Strength score is treated as 6 higher when calculating jump distance.

### Alert Sleeper

You remain aware of sounds while unconscious.

You can awaken from nonmagical sleep when hearing something. If rendered unconscious, you remember what you heard.

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Chikitu

Chikitu include shorthair, ocelot, wildcat, and other smaller-cat inheritances.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Bounding Grace.** When you take the Dash action, you also gain the benefits of the Dodge action.

### Grandi

Grandi include tiger, panther, lion, and other great-cat inheritances.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Hunter’s Cleave.** When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a melee weapon attack, you may immediately make another melee weapon attack.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

### Allemance — Alley Cat

You have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed if you can brace against two parallel walls.

**Spiced Brews.** You gain proficiency with brewer’s supplies.

### Oria — Cat’s Got Your Tongue

As a reaction to a spell cast within 60 feet that you can hear, you may speak a disrupting word.

The caster must make a saving throw using their spellcasting ability. The DC equals `8 + your proficiency bonus`.

On a failed save, the spell fails.

Once you use this trait, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Vinyot — Corked Armor

You can convert armor you are proficient with into corked armor over 24 hours.

While wearing it, water up to chest height isn’t difficult terrain, and the armor does not reduce swim speed or impose disadvantage on Athletics checks made to swim.

### Arneria — Uncanny Navigator

When climbing to a higher surface, you may use Strength (Athletics) instead of Dexterity (Stealth) to Hide.

**Kolaçan.** You have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks to follow an unaware target you can see.

### Al’Ar — Deadman’s Drop

You gain proficiency with carpenter’s tools.

You may spend 8 hours to build a secret wooden compartment measuring a 6-inch cube using a Dexterity (carpenter’s tools) check. Discovering it requires an Investigation check equal to your result.

You have advantage on Investigation checks to find hidden doors or compartments.

**Piratical Boldness.** If you are hit while moving, such as by an opportunity attack, you gain advantage on your next attack roll before the end of your next turn.

## XII. Playing a Feline

Consider:

- What remains private even from people you love?
- How do you ask for solitude?
- What quiet aid have you offered?
- Which authority are you willing to challenge?
- Does poise feel natural or performed?
- What does Al’Ar mean to you?
- When has independence become isolation?
- What can ordinary divination never learn about you?
- Who may enter your Closed Room?
- What makes you act without further observation?

A Feline may be dependent, clumsy, public, trusting, noisy, physically weak, or uninterested in Al’Ar.

Their lineage protects movement and privacy.

It does not require them to live alone.
