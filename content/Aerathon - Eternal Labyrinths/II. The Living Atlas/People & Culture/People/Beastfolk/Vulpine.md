## I. Lineage Overview

Vulpines are Tradewind and Desert Fox Beastfolk known for directional awareness, sustained magical concentration, and the ability to draw useful conclusions from incomplete evidence.

They do not see the future.

They infer.

A shifted object, broken routine, pause in speech, wrong shadow, changed price, absent animal, or damaged path becomes part of a pattern. Vulpine deduction is magical in speed and usefulness, but it remains deduction.

The distinction matters.

Governments, caravans, delver companies, and families routinely ask Vulpines to guarantee outcomes no evidence can guarantee. When the prediction is correct, others call it obvious. When it is wrong, the Vulpine is accused of withholding information or manipulating the result.

The fox stereotype creates another problem.

Caution becomes cunning. Privacy becomes deception. Contingency becomes disloyalty. A person who prepares several exits is presumed to have planned betrayal.

Vulpine cultures ask:

> How much certainty does another person deserve before choosing to act?

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

In the **Threads Before Footfall**, the Old Court places the first Vulpine at a fork.

One path holds a broken branch.

The other holds an undisturbed stone.

The gods ask which is safe.

The Vulpine refuses to answer until learning who passed before, what weather occurred, what creature made the branch break, and whether the stone had been placed deliberately.

The Old Court laughs and grants no prophecy.

It grants the patience to keep asking after a simple answer becomes suspicious.

### Polar Sense

Vulpines orient instinctively toward magnetic north.

Many sleep with the head northward and arrange tools, maps, or important objects along a north-south axis.

The practice is sometimes ritual, sometimes comfort, and sometimes unconscious.

Magical regions where north becomes unstable can cause disorientation and distress.

### The Mourn-Howl Inheritance

Vulpine communities participated in the ancient [[Mourn-Howl Circles]] alongside Canines and other adherents.

They became keepers of sequence: naming the event, the loss, the warning signs, and the point after which grief changed direction.

Modern Vulpine memorial traditions often reconstruct not only what happened but what people reasonably knew at each moment.

This protects the dead from hindsight judgments.

It also teaches that warning signs visible afterward may not have formed a clear pattern before the loss.

### Map and Blame

Vulpines have been employed as navigators for millennia.

When expeditions vanished, rulers often punished the navigator regardless of weather, war, sabotage, or impossible orders.

Modern route contracts distinguish guidance from command. A navigator describes risk. The person with authority chooses whether the party proceeds.

## III. Body and Predictive Form

Vulpines commonly possess:

- Foxlike muzzles
- Large mobile ears
- Fur adapted to varied climate
- Long tails
- Acute hearing
- Strong night and peripheral awareness
- Magnetic orientation
- Patient stillness or light movement

Most are Medium.

### Deduction

Vulpine Deduction provides a useful warning concerning a closed-ended question.

It does not compel truth from the world.

The answer may be limited by available evidence, magical concealment, the framing of the question, and events that change after the insight.

### Prolonging Focus

Vulpines can sometimes extend long-duration concentration magic.

The practice requires stillness, safety, and repeated effort. It ends under injury.

Employers may not assume a Vulpine will prolong a spell indefinitely merely because the possibility exists.

### Ears and Heat

Desert Fox ears are especially important for temperature regulation. Ear coverings, helmets, and magical disguises require appropriate ventilation.

## IV. Society and Kinship

Vulpine communities include route houses, trading families, desert watches, dock circles, magical schools, investigative offices, caravan networks, and solitary households connected by favors.

### The Stated Confidence

Traditional advice distinguishes:

- What is known
- What is strongly inferred
- What is possible
- What is feared
- What cannot presently be determined

This is the **Stated Confidence**.

Vulpines use it in route planning, family disputes, medicine, trade, and delving.

The practice prevents intuition from gaining false authority.

It can also become defensive overqualification when a simple personal opinion would be enough.

### Contingency

Many households maintain spare routes, hidden funds, alternative contacts, and conditional plans.

Contingency is not necessarily expectation of betrayal.

It is a cultural response to histories in which one blocked road could destroy a community.

Relationships become strained when planning for failure communicates lack of trust.

### Favors

Tradewind communities often record favors as social paths rather than debt.

A favor creates a possible connection, not automatic ownership of a future action.

Vinyoti markets have repeatedly attempted to convert these networks into transferable obligations. Vulpine councils largely reject the practice.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Vulpine communities commonly admire:

- Careful inference
- Preparation
- Navigational competence
- Contingency
- Sustained focus
- Dry humor
- Intellectual humility
- The ability to warn without controlling

These values cast long shadows.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation | Reducing avoidable risk | Never feeling ready |
| Inference | Seeing meaningful pattern | Treating suspicion as evidence |
| Contingency | Preserving choice | Undermining trust |
| Focus | Sustaining difficult magic | Refusing interruption |
| Caution | Acting with proportionate risk | Missing opportunity |
| Confidence language | Communicating uncertainty honestly | Avoiding responsibility through qualification |

A recurring Vulpine fear is not being wrong, but being held responsible for certainty they never claimed.

Some refuse advice. Some make every warning absolute so others will listen. Some become controlling planners. Others embrace improvisation because no model deserves their whole life.

### Internal Divisions

Modern Vulpines debate:

- Whether Vulpine Deduction should be admissible evidence
- Whether route specialists may refuse dangerous commands
- Whether Tradewind favor networks have become hidden class systems
- How the Mourn-Howl legacy should be shared with Canines
- Whether Polar Sense has been over-romanticized
- Whether predictive labor requires special contract protection
- When contingency becomes emotional distance

## VI. Vulpines in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemancian Vulpines are mediators, route keepers, investigators, traders, brewers, and protectors of household trust.

Their attention to hostile motive can support community safety. It can also make them unofficial judges without the safeguards of a court.

### Oria

Oric Vulpines study spell schools, aurora magic, machine behavior, and northern navigation.

The Aurora Tradition treats classification as a practical tool rather than an immutable truth, allowing a spell’s apparent school to be shifted strategically.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Vulpines are brokers, navigators, auditors, performers, merchants, insurers, and keepers of hidden reserves.

Their ability to negotiate through unexpected skills reflects the League’s respect for leverage. The same culture constantly tests whether every relationship has a price.

### Arneria

Arnerian Vulpines guide caravans, study desert signs, serve as spell-duelists, and maintain long watches.

Desert Fox communities value the action taken after patient observation. Their Dune Dagger tradition makes spell and blade one continuous decision.

### Al’Ar

Al’Ari Vulpines live among Feline families, island fleets, docks, swimmers, and storm routes.

The joking phrase **Cats Stuck in the Family Tree** reflects deep intermarriage and shared maritime adaptation. It should not be used to deny distinct Vulpine identity.

## VII. Vulpines and Delving

Vulpines serve as navigators, trap analysts, investigators, concentration casters, illusion testers, and contingency planners.

Their advantages include:

- Magnetic orientation
- Extended concentration
- Closed-question deduction
- Illusion awareness
- Vigilant watches
- Strong homeland spell and trade techniques

Labyrinths exploit inference by arranging evidence into persuasive false patterns. A room may contain every sign of danger except danger, or conceal one contradiction within an otherwise perfect explanation.

A common warning states:

> A pattern can be real and still have been placed for you.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Intelligence score increases by 2.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet.

### Polar Sense

You always know magnetic north.

You unconsciously arrange objects north–south and sleep with your head oriented north unless prevented.

### Prolonging Focus

When you concentrate on a spell with a duration of 1 hour or longer, you may attempt to prolong it.

At the end of the spell’s normal duration, make a DC 10 Intelligence saving throw. On a success, the spell continues.

Every 10 minutes thereafter, repeat the save with the DC increasing by 2.

The spell ends if you take damage or fail a save.

### Vulpine Deduction

As an action, make a DC 10 Intelligence check related to a closed-ended question, such as which path is safer, which object is cursed, or who is lying.

On a success, the DM provides a useful warning or insight.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest.

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Tradewind

Tradewind Vulpines are associated with maritime routes, trade networks, illusion awareness, and courtly planning.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Preternatural Sense.** As a bonus action, you may inspect an object for illusions.

You have advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks to detect illusions.

Once per long rest, you may substitute an Intelligence saving throw for a Dexterity saving throw.

### Desert Fox

Desert Fox Vulpines possess heat-adapted bodies, large ears, and traditions of patient environmental watch.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Vigilant Senses.** If you remain in place for 1 hour, you become aware of any creature that enters a 90-foot radius around you and learn its size.

This sense awakens you if asleep and ends when you move.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

### Allemance — Sense for Betrayers

You gain proficiency in Deception.

You have advantage on Insight checks to detect hostile motives.

After spending 8 hours with a creature, you gain advantage on Insight checks to detect hostile intent against that creature for the next 24 hours.

### Oria — The Aurora Tradition

You automatically learn the school of any spell cast within 60 feet if you can see or hear its casting.

Once per long rest, when you cast a spell, you may treat it as if it belonged to a different school of magic.

### Vinyot — Stashed in a Foxhole

If separated from your wealth through robbery, imprisonment, or similar circumstances, you can recover coins or valuables equal to twice your level.

This amount is deducted from your total wealth.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you regain access to your assets.

**Position of Strength.** You may negotiate costs using any skill check you can reasonably justify.

### Arneria — Dune Dagger

After casting a spell of 1st level or higher on your turn, you may immediately make a dagger attack as a bonus action.

### Al’Ar — Cats Stuck in the Family Tree

You have advantage on Athletics checks to swim and are proficient with the trident.

When attacking with a trident against a creature you cannot see, you do not suffer disadvantage.

## XII. Playing a Vulpine

Consider:

- What confidence do you attach to your own plans?
- Which contingency hurt someone’s trust?
- What pattern do others refuse to see?
- What prediction are you tired of being asked to make?
- How do you respond when magnetic north fails?
- Which route did you advise against?
- What favor would you never trade?
- When do you stop gathering evidence?
- What grief have you reconstructed without hindsight?
- What part of your life is deliberately unplanned?

A Vulpine may be impulsive, directionless, trusting, magically unfocused, poor at inference, or uninterested in navigation.

Their lineage offers pattern and orientation.

It does not reveal the future.
