# Equine

**Folk Classification:** [[‎ Beastfolk|Beastfolk]]  
**Lineage Classification:** Equine  
**Registry Scope:** Sapient Lineage and Homeland Variance  
**Recognition Status:** Full Sapient Recognition  
**Delver Eligibility:** Unrestricted  
**Common Names:** Roadfolk, Longstriders, Horizon-Kin  
**Date of Revised Filing:** 7th Cycle, 3388 A.D.

---

## I. Lineage Overview

Equines are Horse, Donkey, and Mule Beastfolk shaped by long travel, powerful builds, horizon awareness, and cultures that treat routes as living public works.

Roads occupy a central place in Equine history.

A road is not simply ground made easy to cross. It is a chain of water, shelter, warning, repair, memory, and people willing to keep the next section open.

Equines built and carried many such chains.

They were also forced along them.

Empires praised Equine endurance while binding families to courier service, military supply, freight contracts, pilgrimage labor, and state breeding registries. The statement that Equines are “born to move” has excused both romantic restlessness and coerced displacement.

Many Equines love travel.

Many do not.

Their central question is:

> When does carrying the road become the right to choose where it leads?

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

In the **Horizon Account**, the Old Court shows the first Equine a distant line and asks what lies beyond it.

The Equine walks until the line moves.

They continue.

Eventually they return and answer:

> The horizon is not a place. It is how the world keeps asking.

The gods grant them long stride and endurance, then warn that movement without return can become another way of forgetting.

### Route Houses

Early Equine communities maintained route houses at intervals determined by water, weather, and ordinary fatigue rather than political border.

A route house offered:

- Rest
- News
- Hoof care
- Medical aid
- Repair
- Message exchange
- Neutral mediation
- A record of who had not arrived

The last duty mattered most.

Equine routes were networks of accountability. A traveler was not simply expected at a destination. They were expected at each place capable of noticing their absence.

### The Harness Centuries

During several Crown periods, Equine service shifted from reciprocal route duty to inherited labor.

Families were assigned roads. Mule children inherited freight debts. Horse households were requisitioned during war. Donkey refusal was described as stubbornness rather than judgment.

Modern Equine politics remain shaped by the **right of unharnessed labor**: strength and endurance do not create a public claim upon the body carrying them.

Some households preserve ceremonial harnesses as evidence of skill. Others will not keep one inside the home.

## III. Body and Road Form

Equines commonly possess:

- Hooved feet
- Powerful lower bodies
- Long limbs
- Mobile ears
- Tails used for balance and expression
- Wide fields of vision
- Strong carrying capacity
- Efficient sustained movement

Most are Medium despite substantial variation in height and weight.

### Hoof Care

Hoof condition affects balance, pain, speed, and long-term health.

Shoes, wraps, enchanted plates, soft-road boots, and bare-hoof traditions vary by region. Removing or fitting hoof equipment without consent is bodily interference, not ordinary tack work.

### Horizon Awareness

Equines frequently notice the beginning of movement before others: a crowd shifting, a route opening, an opponent committing weight, weather crossing open land.

This awareness is represented mechanically through initiative.

It is not prophecy.

### Carrying Capacity

Powerful Build has made Equines targets of exploitative labor systems.

Equipment standards require balanced loads, body-fitted frames, rest, and independent weight limits. “Capable of carrying” does not mean “safe to load.”

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Equine households include route families, postal circles, caravans, steadings, urban couriers, freight cooperatives, shrine roads, and stationary communities that maintain the movement of others.

### The Turnaround

Before a major journey, many Equines name a **turnaround condition**.

It may be:

- A date
- A weather sign
- A level of injury
- A lost supply
- A failed contact
- A companion’s refusal

The practice recognizes that persistence needs an ending chosen before exhaustion makes every decision feel like defeat.

Ignoring a declared turnaround condition carries shame in some communities because it risks everyone for the pride of one traveler.

### Messages

Equine message traditions emphasize custody.

The carrier is responsible for:

- Preserving the message
- Distinguishing exact words from interpretation
- Naming delays
- Refusing impossible secrecy
- Confirming delivery

Vinyoti Equines developed sophisticated hidden-text methods. Other communities rely upon memorized cadence, token chains, or witnesses along the route.

### Staying

Equines who prefer stationary life often face questions about what made them stop.

Many answer that staying is not failed movement. A route house, workshop, farm, archive, or family can become the place that makes everyone else’s travel possible.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Equine communities commonly admire:

- Endurance
- Foresight
- Momentum
- Reliable delivery
- Balance under burden
- Respect for routes
- Honest persistence
- Knowing when to turn back

These values cast long shadows.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Endurance | Continuing through real difficulty | Accepting exploitation |
| Momentum | Preventing paralysis | Moving without reflection |
| Reliability | Becoming a trusted carrier | Measuring worth by completed tasks |
| Independence | Traveling capably | Refusing support |
| Foresight | Seeing the route ahead | Living only for the next destination |
| Balance | Carrying complex demands | Hiding strain until collapse |

A recurring Equine fear is becoming stationary without having chosen to stop.

Some travel compulsively. Some collect routes while avoiding relationships. Some build permanent homes with fierce intention. Others reject movement because it was once compulsory.

### Internal Divisions

Modern Equines debate:

- Whether ceremonial harness can be reclaimed
- Whether old route rights override modern borders
- Whether courier secrecy protects people or powerful employers
- Whether Mule is a sublineage, mixed inheritance, or imposed registry
- How much migration should define Equine education
- Whether a declared turnaround can be overruled by group need
- How to compensate stationary keepers of mobile systems

## VI. Equines in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemancian Equines maintain rural patrols, migration roads, inns, farms, messenger circuits, and seasonal gatherings.

Hearthland culture honors return. Equines who choose open-ended travel may be treated as unfinished members of communities they still love.

### Oria

Oric Equines carry supplies, cross frozen passes, maintain javelin traditions, inspect roads, and connect isolated Houses.

The north respects durability. It can also normalize dangerous loads because the route “has always been done.”

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Equines are couriers, traders, information brokers, clergy, transport owners, racers, and logistics experts.

Messages have value. So do delays. Equine labor organizations have become central to disputes over impossible delivery contracts and magical acceleration.

### Arneria

Arnerian Equines are legendary mail runners, navigators, caravan leaders, and keepers of oath roads.

Some messages must arrive before a ritual hour. Arnerian tradition increasingly distinguishes sacred urgency from institutional failure to plan.

### Al’Ar

Al’Ar offers few open roads, so Equine motion enters shipyards, performance, martial craft, dock systems, and island paths.

Warhammer traditions convert full-body momentum into precise structural force.

---

## VII. Equines and Delving

Equines serve as scouts, carriers, mobile defenders, couriers, navigators, and expedition anchors.

Their advantages include speed, initiative, forced-march endurance, carrying capacity, and strong balance.

Their common failure is momentum after the route has changed.

Labyrinths create horizons that recede, roads that reward uninterrupted movement, loads that grow unnoticed, and destinations that appear only to whoever is farthest ahead.

A common warning states:

> The road wanting you to continue is not the same as the road being safe.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Wisdom score increases by 2.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 35 feet.

### March on Hooves

You have advantage on saving throws made to avoid exhaustion from forced marches.

### Powerful Build

You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.

### Eyes on the Horizon

You add your Wisdom modifier to initiative rolls.

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Horse

Horse Equines commonly possess tall, athletic frames adapted for rapid acceleration and open movement.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Out of the Gate.** Your speed is doubled during the first round of combat.

**Sure-Footed Stomp.** You can move through the space of a creature smaller than you or prone without it counting as difficult terrain.

When you do, you may make an unarmed strike dealing `1d4 + your Strength modifier` bludgeoning damage. On a hit, that creature cannot make opportunity attacks against you until the end of your turn.

You can’t use this feature on the same creature again until your next turn.

### Donkey

Donkey Equines are compact, resilient, and strongly associated with considered refusal.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Unbroken Focus.** You have advantage on Constitution saving throws made to maintain concentration on spells.

**Sleepwalker.** You require only 4 hours of sleep to gain the benefits of a long rest.

While sleeping, you may move up to your speed in a half-conscious state. During this movement you have disadvantage on Perception checks, and your speed becomes 0 if you are carrying more than half your carrying capacity.

### Mule

Mule Equines carry mixed Horse and Donkey inheritance and a distinct history of being classified through labor rather than community.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength or Constitution score increases by 1, your choice.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Hybrid Endurance.** When you fail a saving throw against exhaustion, you can choose to succeed instead. Once you use this feature, you can’t do so again until you finish a long rest.

**Load-Bearer’s Balance.** You have advantage on Dexterity saving throws and Acrobatics checks made to keep your footing or avoid being knocked prone.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

### Allemance — Hunter Legacy

You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed.

### Oria — Horseshoes in the Drifts

You have advantage on Survival checks in arctic terrain. Snow and ice do not count as difficult terrain for you.

You gain proficiency with javelins, and you have advantage on attacks with them against creatures smaller than you.

### Vinyot — Carriers of the Good Word

You gain proficiency in Religion.

You may encode hidden messages within mundane text. Teaching an ally to decode them takes 1 hour.

Discovering encoded text requires 1 hour and a DC 15 Investigation check. Decoding it without instruction requires a DC 25 check.

### Arneria — Mail Runner

When you make a Dexterity saving throw or Acrobatics check to maintain balance or avoid being knocked prone, treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10.

You also have advantage on Wisdom checks related to navigation.

### Al’Ar — Hammer Masters

You gain proficiency with warhammers.

When you attack objects with a warhammer, you deal double damage.

---

## XII. Playing an Equine

Consider:

- Which route shaped you?
- What is your turnaround condition?
- Do you travel because you love movement or fear stopping?
- What burden have others assumed you will carry?
- Which message did you fail to deliver?
- What does an unharnessed life mean to you?
- Who notices when you do not arrive?
- What permanent place have you chosen?
- Which horizon no longer interests you?
- When does persistence become pride?

An Equine may be sedentary, directionless, physically weak, unreliable, impatient, or uninterested in roads.

Their lineage offers motion and endurance.

It does not determine the destination.
