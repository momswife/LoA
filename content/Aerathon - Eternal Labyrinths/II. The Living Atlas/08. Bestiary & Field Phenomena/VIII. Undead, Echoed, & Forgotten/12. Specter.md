# Specter

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A specter is an incorporeal undead manifestation capable of passing through creatures and solid objects. It must be distinguished from an Echo, a Ghost, projected imagery, and other spiritlike phenomena before any theory of origin is accepted.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Reports commonly describe a partial figure that crosses walls, leaves no physical track, and produces sudden weakness or necrotic injury. Sunlight impairs the standard form. Sealed northern ruins and Labyrinth chambers are not secure merely because every door remains barred.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Use overlapping observation and avoid assuming a wall protects the rear of the formation. Radiant or magical capability should remain distributed across the party. Life-drained casualties require immediate assessment because ordinary healing may not restore the lost maximum vitality.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> A specter may understand language without speaking. Silence is not proof of mindlessness; soul-resonance and novel-response tests remain necessary.

>
> ### Traits
>
> ***Incorporeal Movement.*** The specter can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.
>
> ***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the specter has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Life Drain.*** Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, its Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the damage taken.
