# Reliquary Eidolon

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Labyrinth Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A reliquary eidolon is a powerful spirit bound to a vessel, monument, or protected interior. It may be a consenting guardian, imprisoned soul, or Labyrinth-generated custodian patterned after communal remembrance.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Guardian figures appearing in several materials, inscriptions responding to vows, localized suppression of hostile magic, and architecture reorienting around one vessel indicate an eidolon. Most warn intruders before force unless their binding has deteriorated.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Identify the protected charge and authority behind the binding. Do not remove the vessel. Present credentials and seek negotiated passage. If coercive binding requires intervention, separate spirit and reliquary without destroying either.

> [!warning] Identity and personhood
> A bound spirit remains a person. Consent, terms of service, capacity to renegotiate, and possible release must be investigated. Protective violence alone does not make it evil.

>
> ### Traits
>
> ***Bound to the Reliquary.*** The eidolon can't willingly move more than 300 feet from its vessel. While the intact vessel is in range, it regains 15 Hit Points at the start of its turn. Radiant damage suppresses this restoration for one turn.
>
> ***Magic Resistance.*** The eidolon has Advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The eidolon makes three Reliquary Strike attacks.
>
> ***Reliquary Strike.*** Melee or Ranged Attack Roll: +10, reach 10 ft. or range 90 ft. Hit: 15 (2d8 + 6) Force damage plus 7 (2d6) Radiant or Necrotic damage.
>
> ***Expulsion Decree (Recharge 5–6).*** Charisma Saving Throw: DC 17, each hostile creature in a 30-foot Emanation. Failure: 27 (6d8) Force damage, pushed 30 feet, and Prone. Success: Half damage only.
>
> ### Reactions
>
> ***Guardian Intercession.*** When a protected creature or object within 60 feet takes damage, reduce it by 18 (4d8), and the eidolon may teleport beside the target.
