# Shadow

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A shadow is an undead manifestation resembling a mobile patch or silhouette of darkness. It is distinguished from an ordinary cast shadow by independent movement, persistence when the light source changes, and active response to living creatures.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Reliable signs include light behaving inconsistently around a humanlike silhouette, victims weakened without proportionate wounds, and darkness moving through gaps too narrow for a body. Northern ruins and light-poor Labyrinth floors provide favorable concealment. A confirmed shadow may create another from a slain humanoid, allowing a local incident to expand rapidly.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Establish bright, overlapping illumination and prevent the entity from isolating a delver. Radiant attacks are strongly preferred. Withdraw before accumulated Strength loss makes armor, climbing, or casualty extraction impossible. A slain companion must be guarded until the risk of a new manifestation has passed.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> The standard shadow profile shows no speech and limited behavior, but origin must still be investigated. Do not assume that appearance alone proves the deceased's soul is present.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Amorphous.*** The shadow can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.
>
> ***Sunlight Weakness.*** While in sunlight, the shadow has Disadvantage on D20 Tests.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Draining Swipe.*** Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) Necrotic damage, and the target's Strength score decreases by 1d4. The target dies if this reduces that score to 0. If a Humanoid is slain by this attack, a Shadow rises from the corpse 1d4 hours later.
>
> ### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Shadow Stealth.*** While in Dim Light or Darkness, the shadow takes the Hide action.
