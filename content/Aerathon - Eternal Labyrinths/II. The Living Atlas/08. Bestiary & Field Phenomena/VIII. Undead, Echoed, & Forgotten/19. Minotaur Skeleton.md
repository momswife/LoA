# Minotaur Skeleton

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A minotaur skeleton is a large, horned skeletal undead profile built for direct pursuit and impact. The field name describes anatomy and behavior; it does not establish the remains' people, origin, or creator.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Deep hoof-like impacts, horn scoring at shoulder height, and shattered doors or shield lines indicate this profile. It is most dangerous where long corridors permit a straight charge, but its reach remains limited in cramped chambers.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Break line of sight before it gains distance, avoid forming a single rank across a corridor, and force it through turns or broken terrain. Bludgeoning weapons remain preferred. After neutralization, preserve marks, bindings, and burial goods for identification.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> Apparent silence is not proof that no soul or imposed will is present. Secure the scene, then assess the remains under MDO mortuary and soul-resonance procedure.
