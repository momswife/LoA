# Ogre Zombie

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

An ogre zombie is a large animated corpse with the reach, strength, and mass of an ogre. The term identifies a physical profile; it does not establish the body's former identity or the source of animation.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Heavy dragging tracks, broken doors at shoulder height, and impacts that continue despite severe bodily damage are reliable warnings. These undead require broad passages or chambers and are most often associated with battlefield remains, breached fortifications, or Labyrinth reconstructions capable of accommodating their size.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Do not meet the creature in a narrow doorway where its mass controls the route. Use distance and terrain, protect any fallen delver from trampling or removal, and expect it to remain active after injuries that would stop a living body. Radiant damage and critical strikes bypass the standard form's persistence.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> The standard profile is a low-cognition animated body, but soul status must be tested rather than inferred from size, damage, or silence.
