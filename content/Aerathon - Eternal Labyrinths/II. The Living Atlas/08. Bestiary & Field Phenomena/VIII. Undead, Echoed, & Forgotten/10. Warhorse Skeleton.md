# Warhorse Skeleton

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A warhorse skeleton is a large set of equine remains animated as a single mobile undead body. Surviving tack, barding marks, or military fittings may indicate former use but do not identify the animating force.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

The entity is most dangerous where a long route permits a straight charge. Hoof scoring without living tracks, empty tack moving under tension, and patrol circuits through ruined roads or stable-like chambers are common signs. Northern battlefield reconstructions and broad frozen passages can preserve the distance it needs to build speed.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Break the charge lane with corners, obstacles, or changes in elevation. Do not stand in a doorway that leaves a straight approach. Bludgeoning attacks are effective against exposed bone. After neutralization, preserve tack and markings for identification rather than treating them as ordinary salvage.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> The standard profile is a soulless animated body. That status must be confirmed; the former animal's remains retain funerary and property protections.
