# Revenant

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Labyrinth Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A revenant is a continuing soul returned to mortal influence through one dominant unresolved purpose. It may inhabit its former body, another corpse, a temporary shell, a constructed form, or a visible spiritual presence.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Revenants retain memory, language, and planning but orient persistently toward a person, place, object, or promised act. Any Labyrinth preserving an unresolved death, failed expedition, abandoned charge, or broken oath may admit this phenomenon.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Open communication before containment. Determine the sustaining purpose and whether it can be completed lawfully. If violence occurs, defend proportionately while preserving routes to resolution; making the purpose impossible can accelerate deterioration.

> [!warning] Identity and personhood
> A revenant is a continuing soul and therefore a person. Narrowed judgment may affect particular decisions, but undeath does not erase its rights.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Purpose-Bound Return.*** If destroyed before completing its purpose, the revenant forms a new body within 1 mile of a person or place tied to that purpose after 24 hours.
>
> ***Relentless Restoration.*** The revenant regains 10 Hit Points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 Hit Point. Fire or Radiant damage suppresses this trait until the start of its next turn.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The revenant makes two Purposeful Strike attacks.
>
> ***Purposeful Strike.*** Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Radiant or Necrotic damage.
>
> ***Compelling Recognition (Recharge 5–6).*** Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 14, one creature within 30 feet connected to the revenant's purpose. Failure: The target has the Frightened condition and can't take Reactions until the end of the revenant's next turn.
