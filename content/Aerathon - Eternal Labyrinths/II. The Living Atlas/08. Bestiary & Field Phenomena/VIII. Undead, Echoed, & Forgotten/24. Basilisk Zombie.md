# Basilisk Zombie

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Labyrinth Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A basilisk zombie is an animated basilisk corpse or a Labyrinth reconstruction matching that anatomy. Animation preserves a degraded petrifying organ-response even when memory and the original soul are absent.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Heavy reptilian drag marks, clawed prints, chipped statuary, and localized calcification are common warnings. Specimens often remain motionless among broken stone until living movement enters range.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Use indirect observation, deny mutual gaze, and fight through sight-blocking obstacles. Nearby statues may be casualties, Labyrinth copies, or ordinary sculpture; examine them before salvage or demolition.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> Most authenticated specimens are soulless animations, but that is a site finding rather than a universal rule. Responsive or self-directed specimens require reassessment.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Degraded Petrifying Gaze.*** A creature that starts its turn within 30 feet while it and the zombie can see each other makes a DC 11 Constitution saving throw. Failure: Its Speed is halved until the start of its next turn; failure by 5+ also gives it the Restrained condition for that duration. A creature can avert its eyes, becoming immune until its next turn but gaining Disadvantage on attacks against the zombie.
>
> ***Undead Fortitude.*** When reduced to 0 Hit Points, the zombie makes a Constitution saving throw (DC 5 plus the damage taken) unless the damage is Radiant or from a Critical Hit. Success: It drops to 1 Hit Point instead.
>
