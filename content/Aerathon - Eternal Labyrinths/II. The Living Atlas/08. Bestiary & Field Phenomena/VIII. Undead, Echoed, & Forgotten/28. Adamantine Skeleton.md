# Adamantine Skeleton

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Labyrinth Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

An adamantine skeleton has major bones and joints plated, replaced, or infused with adamantine alloy. Some are crafted guardians; others are altered when a forge-oriented Labyrinth reconstructs ordinary remains.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Metallic footfalls, sparks at the joints, deep impacts, and weapons blunted against exposed bone identify the profile. The frame resists ordinary weapons but conducts disruptive resonance.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Use thunder, forced movement, joint entanglement, or environmental hazards instead of repeated edged strikes. Recovered alloy remains attached mortuary evidence until lawful disposition.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> Reinforcement says nothing about soul status. The guardian may be a soulless tool, bound spirit, or emergent construct-person.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Adamantine Frame.*** Critical Hits against the skeleton become normal hits.
>
> ***Immutable Construction.*** The skeleton has Advantage on saves against effects that alter its form.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The skeleton makes two Alloy Blade attacks.
>
> ***Alloy Blade.*** Melee Attack Roll: +8, reach 5 ft. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Necrotic damage.
>
> ***Driving Blow.*** Melee Attack Roll: +8, reach 5 ft. Hit: 18 (3d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. A Large or smaller target must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be pushed 15 feet and knocked Prone.
