# Vampire Spawn

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

Vampire spawn is the operational profile for an agile vampiric undead capable of restraining a victim and draining vitality. The term does not establish voluntary allegiance, the identity of a creator, or whether coercive control persists.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Entry routes across walls or ceilings, repeated blood loss, weakened survivors, and avoidance of sunlight or running water indicate this profile. A spawn can speak, plan, and exploit invitations or occupied shelter; it should never be treated as a mindless ambusher.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Watch vertical surfaces, deny isolated grapples, and establish who can lawfully invite entry before approaching a suspected refuge. Sunlight and running water create strong exclusion zones. A stake is decisive only while the spawn is incapacitated, so it is not a substitute for controlled containment.

> [!warning] Continuing intelligence and coercion
> This profile retains language and tactical judgment. Hostile action may require immediate defense, but responders must assess continuing identity, enslavement, magical compulsion, and capacity before assigning culpability.

>

>

> **HP** 90 (12d8 + 36)\
> **Speed** 30 ft.
>

>
> **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +6\
> **Resistances** Necrotic\
> **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 13\
> **Languages** Common plus one other language\
> **CR** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>
> ### Traits
>
> ***Spider Climb.*** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.
>
> ***Vampire Weakness.*** The vampire has these weaknesses:
>
> ***Forbiddance.*** The vampire can't enter a residence without an invitation from an occupant.
>
> ***Running Water.*** The vampire takes 20 Acid damage if it ends its turn in running water.
>
> ***Stake to the Heart.*** The vampire is destroyed if a weapon that deals Piercing damage is driven into the vampire's heart while the vampire has the Incapacitated condition.
>
> ***Sunlight.*** The vampire takes 20 Radiant damage if it starts its turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The vampire makes two Claw attacks and uses Bite.
>
> ***Claw.*** Melee Attack Roll: +6, reach 5 ft. Hit: 8 (2d4 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13) from one of two claws.
>
> ***Bite.*** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 14, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or that has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. Failure: 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. The target's Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the vampire regains Hit Points equal to that amount.
>
> ### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Deathless Agility.*** The vampire takes the Dash or Disengage action.
>
