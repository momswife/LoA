# Bone Ballista

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Labyrinth Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A bone ballista is an undead siege assembly built from many articulated remains around a torsion frame. Some are deliberate weapons; others appear to be Labyrinth attempts to reconstruct an artillery crew and its engine as one body.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Floor grooves, long bone splinters embedded in distant walls, missing large bones, and coordinated clicking behind barricades mark an emplacement. The assembly moves slowly but selects firing lanes with mechanical patience.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Never advance along its straight gallery. Use hard cover and intersecting passages, then disable its firing arm. Every incorporated remain is mortuary evidence after neutralization.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> One bound operator or an emergent construct-person may direct the assembly. Test for communication before dismantling a contained specimen.
