# Skeleton

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\

**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

This filing concerns articulated remains animated without living tissue. The Ministry classifies such entities as undead. The record does not assign a single cause: deliberate necromancy, persistent command, an active site, and Labyrinth reconstitution remain separate possibilities until evidence distinguishes them.

## II. Field Signs & Observed Behavior

Loose bones arranged around weapons, scrape marks without ordinary footprints, repeated patrol paths, and remains that retain unnatural articulation are warning signs. Confirmed examples often repeat a narrow task—guarding, pursuing, or attacking intruders—but apparent simplicity should not be mistaken for harmlessness.

Skeletons have been encountered in tomb-like floors, ruins, ossuaries, and reconstructed battle sites within Labyrinths. Their presence does not prove that the chamber reproduces an actual burial place.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Expect both melee and ranged weapons where equipment survives. Bludgeoning force is particularly effective against exposed bone. After an encounter, confirm that animation has ended before disturbing weapons or remains, and record any sigils, command objects, or environmental changes before removal.

> [!warning] Operational Warning
> Do not treat recognizable remains as ownerless salvage. Secure the site for funerary, forensic, and cultural review whenever conditions permit.
