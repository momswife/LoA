# Will-o'-Wisp

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A will-o'-wisp is a small luminous undead manifestation capable of flight, concealment, and electrical attack. It must be distinguished from ordinary flame, reflected spell-light, Echo phenomena, and benign guides before pursuit or engagement.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

A light that maintains distance, crosses barriers, disappears without smoke, or draws travelers away from marked routes requires suspicion. Wisps favor marshes, ruins, storm-dark northern ground, and Labyrinth routes where visibility and direction are already unreliable. The standard form can consume the life of a creature at the edge of death.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Never follow a solitary light off an authenticated route. Keep the wounded inside the formation, use fixed physical markers, and verify direction independently of the manifestation. Its high evasion and ability to vanish make concentrated magical attacks more reliable than scattered pursuit.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> Language and tactical behavior warrant caution. A luminous undead presence may also be a Ghost, an Echo, or another spirit; classification requires more than appearance.

>

>
> **AC** 19 **Initiative** +9 (19)\
> **HP** 27 (11d4)\
> **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 50 ft. (hover)
>
> | Ability | Score | Modifier | Save |
> | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
> | Str | 1 | −5 | −5 |
> | Dex | 28 | +9 | +9 |
> | Con | 10 | +0 | +0 |
> | Int | 13 | +1 | +1 |
> | Wis | 14 | +2 | +2 |
> | Cha | 11 | +0 | +0 |
>
> **Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Necrotic, Piercing, Slashing\
> **Immunities** Lightning, Poison; Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious\
> **Senses** Darkvision 120 ft.; Passive Perception 12\
> **Languages** Common plus one other language\
> **CR** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>
> ### Traits
>
> ***Ephemeral.*** The wisp can't wear or carry anything.
>
> ***Illumination.*** The wisp sheds Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet.
>
> ***Incorporeal Movement.*** The wisp can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Shock.*** Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 11 (2d8 + 2) Lightning damage.
>
> ### Bonus Actions
>
> ***Consume Life.*** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 10, one living creature the wisp can see within 5 feet that has 0 Hit Points. Failure: The target dies, and the wisp regains 10 (3d6) Hit Points.
>
> ***Vanish.*** The wisp and its light have the Invisible condition until the wisp's Concentration ends on this effect, which ends early immediately after the wisp makes an attack roll or uses Consume Life.
>
