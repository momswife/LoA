# Swarm of Crawling Claws

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A swarm of crawling claws is a coordinated mass of severed, animated hands. A single movement pattern across many mismatched remains usually indicates imposed direction, but the source of that direction must be established separately.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Parallel finger tracks, scratches beneath doors, disturbed hand bones, and objects dragged at floor level are common warnings. The swarm passes through small openings, spreads through occupied ground, and pulls victims down before concentrating its attack.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Seal cracks before resting, elevate packs and wounded personnel, and avoid narrow positions where the swarm can occupy the whole formation. Ordinary weapon cuts are inefficient; area effects and damage types outside its resistances are preferable. Do not handle separated remains until animation has ceased across the entire site.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> Fragmentation and swarm behavior strongly suggest imposed animation, but do not prove absence of a soul. Preserve samples and identifying marks once immediate danger is contained.
