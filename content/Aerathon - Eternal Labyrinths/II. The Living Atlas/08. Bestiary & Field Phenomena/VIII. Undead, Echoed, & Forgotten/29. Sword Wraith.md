# Sword Wraith

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Labyrinth Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

Sword wraith is the field designation for a martial manifestation sustained by an unfinished battle, abandoned command, or repeated Labyrinth record of organized violence. Warriors preserve formations; commanders impose those patterns on nearby undead.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Spectral drill calls, synchronized weapon strikes, footprints ending at old battle lines, and the dead reforming into ranks indicate this family. Some accept formal parley.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Identify the defended objective before crossing it. Proof that an order was superseded or lawful completion of a watch may avert combat. If engagement is unavoidable, separate commanders from warriors.

> [!warning] Identity and personhood
> A sword wraith may be a Ghost, Revenant, Echo-derived pattern, or bound spirit. Responsive entities retain the right to parley and individual assessment.
