# Zombie

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Current Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

This filing concerns corpses animated while retaining substantial soft tissue. The Ministry classifies them as undead and distinguishes them from living victims affected by disease, poison, possession, or impaired consciousness. A source of animation must be investigated rather than presumed.

## II. Field Signs & Observed Behavior

Uneven dragging steps, impact marks against barriers, disturbed remains, and movement despite injuries incompatible with life are common signs. Zombies usually display limited judgment and direct pursuit, but groups can still block passages or overwhelm a fallen delver.

Confirmed Labyrinth encounters include burial-like chambers, ruined settlements, and floors that reconstruct battle remains. A corpse resembling a known person is not proof that the Labyrinth contains that person's original remains.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Maintain distance, use obstacles, and avoid becoming fixed in a narrow route where numbers can accumulate. Ordinary injuries may not immediately stop an animated corpse. Radiant damage and decisive strikes are more reliable under the standard rules profile below, but field teams must still verify the actual behavior of unusual specimens.

> [!warning] Operational Warning
> Treat remains with dignity after the immediate danger ends. Record identifying features and preserve the site for funerary and forensic review whenever conditions permit.
