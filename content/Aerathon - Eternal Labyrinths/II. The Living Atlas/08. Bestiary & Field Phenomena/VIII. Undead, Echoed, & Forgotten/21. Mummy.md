# Mummy

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

Mummy is the MDO field term for a deliberately preserved body animated through a binding, curse, or comparable post-mortal force. Preservation practices alone are not evidence of undeath, and no culture's lawful funerary customs should be treated as suspect without observable animation.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Sealed funerary spaces, preserved wrappings or resins, disturbed wards, and victims unable to recover from a wasting injury mark likely encounters. The profile advances slowly but combines repeated close strikes with a gaze that can break an unprepared formation.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Do not disturb sealed remains merely to test this classification. When animation is confirmed, keep distance, use fire with strict regard for the site and trapped personnel, and rotate anyone affected by the creature's gaze. A cursed casualty needs immediate curse-removal capability; ordinary rest does not arrest the decline.

> [!note] Identity, culture, and personhood
> A speaking preserved dead may be a continuing person, a bound soul, or an imposed pattern. Treat funerary objects as evidence and cultural property, not salvage, and conduct personhood assessment whenever communication is possible.

> ### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The mummy makes two Rotting Fist attacks and uses Dreadful Glare.
>
> ***Rotting Fist.*** Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 8 (1d10 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, it is cursed. While cursed, the target can't regain Hit Points, its Hit Point maximum doesn't return to normal when finishing a Long Rest, and its Hit Point maximum decreases by 10 (3d6) every 24 hours that elapse. A creature dies and turns to dust if reduced to 0 Hit Points by this attack.
>
> ***Dreadful Glare.*** Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 11, one creature the mummy can see within 60 feet. Failure: The target has the Frightened condition until the end of the mummy's next turn. Success: The target is immune to this mummy's Dreadful Glare for 24 hours.
