# Fractured Testament

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Labyrinth Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A fractured testament forms when a death, a dangerous realization, and the compulsion to communicate become entangled. It is neither an ordinary Ghost nor a passive Echo: it reacts, pursues listeners, and rearranges remembered statements.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Overlapping whispers, writing repeated in unfamiliar hands, headaches near sealed texts, and witnesses recalling incompatible confessions indicate a manifestation.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Use written signals and hearing protection, separate affected witnesses, and never repeat the complete message aloud. Determine whether the phenomenon contains a continuing speaker or only copied memory.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> It is usually an active pattern rather than the original soul. Self-recognition, new choice, or revision of purpose requires assessment for emergent personhood under false-return doctrine.
