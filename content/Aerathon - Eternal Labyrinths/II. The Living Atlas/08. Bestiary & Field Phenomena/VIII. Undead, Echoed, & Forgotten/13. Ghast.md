# Ghast

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A ghast is a stronger corpse-feeding undead form distinguished operationally by necrotic resilience, a disabling stench, and paralytic claws. The classification describes observed capabilities rather than a proven universal stage in another undead creature's development.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

The odor of advanced decay without a corresponding visible source, clustered feeding remains, and victims showing brief paralysis are primary warnings. Ghasts can function in enclosed northern ruins and Labyrinth passages where their stench affects an entire formation.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Do not bunch at a doorway or stair. Approach from range, preserve airflow where possible, and rotate any delver showing poison symptoms away from the front. Keep a clear extraction route for paralyzed companions.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> The standard profile understands and speaks Common and shows greater judgment than a ghoul. Containment must include an assessment of will, identity, and the possibility of communication.
