# Vampiric Mist

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Labyrinth Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A vampiric mist is an undead field-form sustained by drawing blood and vitality into a mobile cloud. It may originate from a dispersed vampiric body, a failed transformation, or a Labyrinth copy of death by blood loss.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Crimson condensation, bloodless animals, droplets moving against gravity, and mist gathering around open wounds are characteristic. It travels through narrow gaps and hunts wounded targets.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Seal wounds, cover exposed skin, and prevent the mist from surrounding an isolated casualty. Wind can break its approach; sunlight and radiant effects remain reliable. Check vents before declaring a chamber clear.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Mist Form.*** The mist can occupy another creature's space and pass through a 1-inch opening without squeezing.
>
> ***Scent of Blood.*** The mist has Advantage on attacks against a creature missing any Hit Points.
>
> ***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** In sunlight, the mist has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Blood Siphon.*** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 13, one creature in the mist's space. Failure: 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage, and the mist regains half that many Hit Points. Success: Half damage only.
>
> ### Reactions
>
> ***Scatter.*** Trigger: The mist takes Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage. Response: It moves up to 10 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
