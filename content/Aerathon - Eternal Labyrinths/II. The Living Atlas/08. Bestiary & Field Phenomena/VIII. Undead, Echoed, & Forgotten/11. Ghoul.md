# Ghoul

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A ghoul is a mobile undead body marked by active corpse-feeding, coordinated pursuit, and paralytic claws. Hunger and appearance do not establish whether the original soul remains, whether another will is present, or whether a Labyrinth has produced an imitation.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Gnawed remains, low claw marks, bodies dragged into confined spaces, and victims left rigid after shallow wounds indicate possible activity. Ghouls favor routes with access to remains and cover from open light. Northern casualty sites and burial-like Labyrinth floors can sustain groups when remains are repeatedly exposed.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Do not allow the formation to collapse around a paralyzed delver. Use reach, barriers, and controlled withdrawal; protect corpses from removal. Paralysis may end quickly under the standard profile, but the affected person remains vulnerable during that interval.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> Ghouls understand and speak Common under the standard profile. Field teams must test for novel thought and continuing identity once immediate danger is controlled.
