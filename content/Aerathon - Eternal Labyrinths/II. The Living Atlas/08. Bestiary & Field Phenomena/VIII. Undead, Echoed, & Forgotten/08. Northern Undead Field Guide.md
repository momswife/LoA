# Northern Undead Field Guide

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Operational Bestiary Reference / Northern Deployment_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Scope

Historical surveys, containment records, and Labyrinth reports establish undead manifestations as a recurring and numerous northern hazard. The Ministry therefore maintains a broad reference set rather than treating every animated body or incorporeal presence as the same phenomenon.

This filing does not claim a current census inside the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/4. Nattefrost/∅ Nattefrost|Nattefrost]]. Its plague cordon remains closed, and post-cordon signals are unauthenticated. The operational conclusion is narrower: northern deployments must be equipped to distinguish and respond to multiple undead forms.

### Rules ceiling

This public compendium accepts undead encounter profiles through **Challenge Rating 12**. Openly licensed standard creatures are supplemented by original Aerathon profiles where recurring Labyrinth phenomena require higher-tier guidance. A creature's challenge rating measures table-facing encounter pressure, not authority, intelligence, or rights.

## III. Northern Deployment Doctrine

Before engagement, determine whether the manifestation is an animated corpse, an Echo, a continuing soul, a Labyrinth copy, or another phenomenon. Protect fallen companions from removal or reanimation, preserve radiant capability, and maintain overlapping light sources. Teams should carry bludgeoning tools, restraint-breaking equipment, and a means of marking passages when incorporeal movement makes ordinary perimeter control unreliable.

> [!warning] Nattefrost limitation
> No entry in this guide authenticates a current population, route, settlement, or command structure beyond the northern plague cordon. Automated signals and apparent survivor reports remain subject to temporal-echo and mimicry review.

## IV. Rights, Remains & Identification

A continuing soul remains a person. An undead body may also retain a soul, contain another spirit, or hold no soul at all. Hostile conduct can require immediate defense, but classification as undead does not erase legal or moral consideration. When the scene is secure, preserve identifying evidence and refer remains or responsive entities for mortuary and soul-resonance review.
