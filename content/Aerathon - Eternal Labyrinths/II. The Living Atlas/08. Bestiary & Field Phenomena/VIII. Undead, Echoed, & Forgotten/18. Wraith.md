# Wraith

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Undead, Echoed & Forgotten)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Northern Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

A wraith is a powerful incorporeal undead presence capable of draining vitality and raising a recently dead humanoid's spirit as a controlled specter. The term identifies a dangerous operational profile and does not by itself determine the entity's original identity.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Multiple specters acting under coordinated direction, passage through sealed barriers, and victims suffering severe loss of vitality indicate possible wraith command. Wraiths favor darkness and can turn a fresh casualty into an additional threat within moments, making them especially dangerous during northern mass-casualty incidents.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Prevent access to the fallen, maintain sunlight or radiant capability where possible, and do not rely on walls as containment. Concentrate attacks on the controlling wraith when subordinate specters are present. Life-drained survivors require assessment for reduced maximum vitality before further deployment.

> [!note] Identity and personhood
> A wraith speaks and acts with continuing intelligence under the standard profile. Determine whether a soul, imposed pattern, or other will is present; containment does not remove the duty to assess personhood.

> ### Wraith
>
> _Medium or Small Undead, Neutral Evil_
>
> **AC** 13 **Initiative** +3 (13)\
> **HP** 67 (9d8 + 27)\
> **Speed** 5 ft., Fly 60 ft. (hover)
>
> | Ability | Score | Modifier | Save |
> | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
> | Str | 6 | −2 | −2 |
> | Dex | 16 | +3 | +3 |
> | Con | 16 | +3 | +3 |
> | Int | 12 | +1 | +1 |
> | Wis | 14 | +2 | +2 |
> | Cha | 15 | +2 | +2 |
>
> **Resistances** Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Piercing, Slashing\
> **Immunities** Necrotic, Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious\
> **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 12\
> **Languages** Common plus two other languages\
> **CR** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
>
> ### Traits
>
> ***Incorporeal Movement.*** The wraith can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.
>
> ***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the wraith has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Life Drain.*** Melee Attack Roll: +6, reach 5 ft. Hit: 21 (4d8 + 3) Necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, its Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the damage taken.
>
> ***Create Specter.*** The wraith targets a Humanoid corpse within 10 feet of itself that has been dead for no longer than 1 minute. The target's spirit rises as a Specter in the space of its corpse or in the nearest unoccupied space. The specter is under the wraith's control. The wraith can have no more than seven specters under its control at a time.
>
