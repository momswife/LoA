# Djinn, Bargains, and Bound Intelligences

## A name broader than a species

Across Aerathon, *djinn* and *genie* are used for several kinds of articulate elemental intelligence. Traders favor the words when a being negotiates, remembers an obligation, or inhabits a vessel. These usages describe encounters more reliably than ancestry. A shared name does not establish a shared origin, temperament, or power to grant wishes.

This comparative record distinguishes documented entities from local interpretations. Bestiary filing describes how an intelligence is encountered; it does not settle whether it is a person, a hazard, a captive, or several of these at once.

## Distinct records

[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/08. Bestiary & Field Phenomena/II. Elementals & Weather-Born/02. Glassfire Djinn|Glassfire Djinn]] arise from heat, reflection, and expressed desire around the Dunes of the Sun Bull and Porta Strega's mirrored wastes. Their gifts carry the destructive mirror clause documented in their dedicated record. They are not true efreet from the Plane of Fire. Malarthain's recorded pair, called Sire and Daughter, remain a separate containment case in its furnace depths.

The genie bound within [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/06. Magic, Technology & Infrastructure/I. Everyday Magic & Artifice/04. Stormhowler|Stormhowler]] is associated with air, thunder, and a persistent will toward battle. Its behavior establishes an intelligence within a weapon, not a common origin with Glassfire. Silence that renders a wish-born phenomenon inert cannot be assumed to quiet a storm genie; mirrored containment cannot be borrowed merely because both records use the word djinn.

## Wind agreements along the coast

Among Vinyot's coastal crews, a **wind agreement** is a narrow bargain with an air intelligence encountered along a working route. Reliable agreements name a task: carry a warning across a harbor, keep one sail drawing until a headland is rounded, or clear smoke from a specified landing. These are limited services, not purchases of the season's weather.

The being needs air through which to act and cannot promise safe passage through every competing storm. A sealed hold, damaged vessel, or blocked intake can make a promised service impossible. Experienced crews therefore record what ends an agreement as carefully as what begins it. A captain may promise a berth or a song; dockworkers have reason to object when the captain promises their labor instead.

The common custom of leaving a clear lee-side space on a returning vessel gives a visiting intelligence room to manifest without passing through cabins. Whether the guest values the space, the courtesy, or the sailors' expectation differs from encounter to encounter. It is not a universal summoning formula.

## Vessels, names, and consent

A lamp, weapon, kiln, or speaking tube may be a dwelling, a conduit, a prison, or a device imitating a voice. The shape alone distinguishes none of these. Repeated answers, memory across interruptions, and the ability to refuse a novel request provide stronger evidence of agency than a single uncanny phrase.

Likewise, a name offered in conversation need not be a binding name. Scholars distinguish a chosen address from a designation imposed by an earlier keeper. Paying the owner of a vessel does not prove the occupant consented to the sale. [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/06. Magic, Technology & Infrastructure/I. Everyday Magic & Artifice/05. Bound Intelligences and Relic Custody|Custody records]] should preserve both claims without requiring an immediate answer to every question of personhood.

## Where conflicts arise

The most consequential disputes often begin with useful work. A harbor wants dependable warning winds; an intelligence wants freedom to depart. A furnace crew needs heat; a contained being's preferred fuel would endanger the neighborhood. A repairer can restore a vessel but cannot reconstruct the promise that once made it safe.

Such conflicts reward witnesses, bounded commitments, and a practical alternative when cooperation ends. They also leave room for kindness: a maintained refuge or an honestly limited agreement can outlast an elaborate coercive seal. No practice in this record guarantees benevolence, and no single hostile encounter establishes an entire kind as evil.
