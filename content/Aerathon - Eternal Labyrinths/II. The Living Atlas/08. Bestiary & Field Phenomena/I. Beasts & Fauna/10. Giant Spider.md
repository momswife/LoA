# Giant Spider

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Beasts & Fauna)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Current Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

Giant spiders are large web-building predators filed as beasts unless a specimen displays confirmed anomalous traits. Webs are part of the animal's hunting environment and should not, without further evidence, be interpreted as writing, ritual, or deliberate architecture.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Layered webbing, wrapped remains, shed outer shells, and strands crossing open routes are the clearest signs. Giant spiders use walls and ceilings as readily as floors and can detect contact with their connected webs. Suitable Labyrinth habitats include caves, vertical shafts, overgrown ruins, and chambers with stable anchor points.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Check above eye level and test suspicious strands from a distance. Fire can clear webbing but may also consume air, reveal the party, or spread through an enclosed floor. A restrained delver should be freed before the group pursues the spider. Antivenom planning is advised where prior reports establish venomous populations.

> ### Traits
>
> ***Spider Climb.*** The spider can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.
>
> ***Web Walker.*** The spider ignores movement restrictions caused by webs, and it knows the location of any other creature in contact with the same web.
>
> ### Actions
>
> ***Bite.*** Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) Poison damage.
>
> ***Web (Recharge 5–6).*** Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 13, one creature the spider can see within 60 feet. Failure: The target has the Restrained condition until the web is destroyed (AC 10; HP 5; Vulnerability to Fire damage; Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage).
