# Boar

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Beasts & Fauna)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Current Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

Boars are sturdy, tusked foragers filed as natural fauna. They are distinct from the altered Iron-Bough Boar and should not be assumed to share that creature's mineralized defenses or anomalous origin.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Rooted soil, churned leaf litter, mud rubs, coarse bristles, and paired hoofprints are reliable signs. Boars occur in wooded and overgrown places, including Labyrinth chambers capable of sustaining dense vegetation. They generally avoid a clear escape route but may charge when cornered, startled at close range, or defending young.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Do not block the animal's retreat. Use elevation, a broad obstacle, or steady withdrawal to break a charge line. A wounded boar remains capable of sudden aggression; do not approach merely because it has stopped moving toward the party.
