**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Beasts & Fauna)\

---

## I. Identification & Classification

Giant rats are oversized scavenging rodents filed as natural fauna unless examination identifies an anomalous cause. Size alone is insufficient evidence of relic exposure, deliberate alteration, or Labyrinth manufacture.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Gnawed wood and leather, greasy rub marks along walls, shredded nesting material, and clustered droppings indicate occupation. Giant rats favor confined routes with food, water, and multiple exits. In Labyrinths they are especially common around abandoned camps, drains, middens, and passages too narrow for larger predators.

They retreat readily when escape remains possible but become dangerous in numbers or when defending a nest.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

Inspect provisions before rest, elevate sealed food, and watch low openings. A swarm should be met in a defensible passage without allowing animals behind the party. Bites require prompt cleaning and ordinary medical assessment; this record does not presume that every population carries the same disease.
