# Wolf

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Beasts & Fauna)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Current Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

Wolves are social hunting beasts recognized by their long muzzle, erect ears, heavy tail, and coordinated movement. The Ministry files ordinary wolves as natural fauna even when a pack inhabits a Labyrinth environment; residence within a Labyrinth is not itself evidence of magical alteration.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Tracks resemble those of large dogs but tend to follow purposeful routes rather than wandering paths. Scat, shed fur, repeated scent marks, and several lines of tracks commonly indicate a pack. Confirmed Labyrinth encounters are most frequent in floors that reproduce forest, scrub, upland, or ruined-settlement conditions.

Wolves usually test distance, footing, and group cohesion before closing. A lone sighting does not prove a lone animal: other pack members may be downwind or outside the light.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

The principal threat is coordinated pursuit. Maintain formation, protect anyone knocked down, and do not allow the pack to divide the group. Give an unprovoked pack room to withdraw. Food should be sealed and waste carried away from camp rather than left as an attractant.
