# Brown Bear

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — _The Living Atlas_ (Beasts & Fauna)\
**Document Class:** _Standard Bestiary Record / Current Field Reference_\
**Access Designation:** _Public_

---

## I. Identification & Classification

Brown bears are large omnivorous beasts recognized by their heavy build, pronounced shoulder mass, broad paws, and long foreclaws. The filing covers ordinary animals, including individuals that enter or become established within suitable Labyrinth environments.

## II. Field Signs, Habitat & Behavior

Large five-toed tracks, clawed tree trunks, overturned logs, disturbed stores, and coarse fur are common signs. Bears may den in natural caves or cave-like chambers. Most encounters arise from surprise, access to food, or the defense of young rather than sustained predation upon delvers.

## III. Threat Assessment & Delver Guidance

A bear can close distance quickly and can climb. Make the party's presence known before entering dense cover or a suspected den, secure food, and avoid placing a bear between the group and its retreat. Do not approach cubs. If contact becomes unavoidable, preserve an open withdrawal path and avoid scattering.
