# Forbidden Archives

> _“A false record may still be evidence. It is not thereby truth.”_  
> — Archive Integrity Inspectorate

The **Forbidden Archives** are not a recognized directorate, access grade, or secret branch of the Ministry of Delving Operations.

The name is an inherited curatorial label for records too consequential to destroy and too disputed, compromised, dangerous, or politically volatile to place among ordinary public references. The collection includes exposed forgeries, withdrawn allegations, contradictory sealed testimony, obsolete emergency instruments, and documents whose claimed issuing offices cannot be reconciled with the current Ministry registry.

The MDO preserves such material for evidentiary comparison. Preservation does not constitute authentication.

> [!caution] Reader Advisory  
> Individual records in this collection may disclose protected identities, covert operations, or unresolved events omitted from ordinary public filings. Those records remain concealed until the reader deliberately opens them.
