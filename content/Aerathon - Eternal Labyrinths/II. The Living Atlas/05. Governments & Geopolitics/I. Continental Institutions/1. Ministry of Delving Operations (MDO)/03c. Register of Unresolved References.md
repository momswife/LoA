# Register of Unresolved References

## The Missing Reference

A reader comparing successive copies of a Ministry record may find a citation whose destination has vanished from the current catalogue. Retain the older citation. Removal from a directory does not establish that the cited material was destroyed.

The discrepancy desk keeps three possible explanations on the same slip: a corrected title, a withdrawn finding, or a file retained outside ordinary circulation. The first two should have an amendment. An absent amendment is a reason to keep looking.
