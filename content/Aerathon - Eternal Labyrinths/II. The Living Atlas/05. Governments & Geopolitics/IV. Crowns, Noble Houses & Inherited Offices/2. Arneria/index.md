# Arneria — Crowns, Houses & Inherited Offices

Modern Arneria's Beylik remains an active, centralized state along the Causeway. This branch therefore distinguishes the present authority of the Bey and recognized offices from predecessor dynasties, displaced claims, ceremonial survivals, and other regal inheritances that may no longer govern.

Future records should identify whether a subject is a functioning state office, a recognized house, a surviving court institution, a claimant without authority, or a historical continuity claim. Ancient origins belong in Annals & Antiquities rather than being inferred from modern titles.
