# Crowns, Noble Houses & Inherited Offices

This shelf records living remnants of regal government: ceremonial crowns, noble houses, dynastic claimants, hereditary offices, and successor bodies whose inherited status still shapes present-day law, diplomacy, landholding, crisis response, or public legitimacy.

## Filing Boundaries

- File the historical dynasty, former kingdom, succession, and evidentiary question in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/I. Annals & Antiquities/05. Figures, Institutions, Houses & Lineages/index|Figures, Institutions, Houses & Lineages]].
- File the current crown, house, claimant, court office, legal privilege, and recognized public role here.
- File a restorationist league, heritage society, or political movement without recognized inherited standing in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/09. Organizations, Companies & Associations/index|Organizations, Companies & Associations]].
- File changing alliances, public incidents, and emerging succession disputes in the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/III. Monthly Ledger/03. Faction Activity Reports/index|Monthly Ledger]], linking back to the stable record.

Dynastic descent, institutional continuity, and lawful present-day authority are separate claims. A record may establish one without establishing the others.
