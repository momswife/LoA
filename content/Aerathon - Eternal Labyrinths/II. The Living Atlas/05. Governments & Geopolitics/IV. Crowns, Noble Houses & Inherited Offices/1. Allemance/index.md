# Allemance — Crowns, Houses & Inherited Offices

Modern Allemance is not an absolute monarchy. Its crown survives as a unifying and mediating institution, while baronial houses retain localized titles, estates, privileges, and social leverage within a decentralized political order.

This branch is the present-day registry for the crown, individual noble houses, dynastic claimants, baronial lines, and inherited offices. Their ancient origins and disputed continuity remain in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/I. Annals & Antiquities/05. Figures, Institutions, Houses & Lineages/III. Houses, Dynasties & Inherited Offices/01. The Bell-Born Dynasty|The Bell-Born Dynasty]] and related Annals records.
