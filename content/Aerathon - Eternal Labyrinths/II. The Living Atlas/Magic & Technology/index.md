# Magic & Technology

Modern Aerathon does not divide magic neatly from technology. A public lamp may be powered by a crafted rune, a lift by steam and relic metal, a prosthetic by joints and aetheric calibration, and a warning network by mirrors, scribes, and resonance channels working together.

These records focus on what those systems mean in ordinary life: who can use them, who maintains them, what happens when they fail, and which forms of access remain uneven.

## Core Records

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/Magic & Technology/01. Everyday Magic & Domestic Craft|Everyday Magic & Domestic Craft]] — household enchantment, trained practice, maintenance, and unequal access.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/Magic & Technology/02. Relics, Resonance, & Modern Artifice|Relics, Resonance, & Modern Artifice]] — how recovered wonders become research, tools, industry, and public risk.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/Magic & Technology/03. Scrytech & Public Communication|Scrytech & Public Communication]] — the Grand Scrying Network, Arcanet, public displays, and the limits of connected communication.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/Magic & Technology/04. Accessible Design & Civic Infrastructure|Accessible Design & Civic Infrastructure]] — mobility, sensory access, prosthetics, public works, and emergency planning for many kinds of bodies.
