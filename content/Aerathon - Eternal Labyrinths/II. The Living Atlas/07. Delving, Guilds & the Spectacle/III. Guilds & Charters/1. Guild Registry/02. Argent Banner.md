# The Argent Banner

![[ea378bd0-bc1c-44a0-9f7d-85f5bbf0b7c6.webp]]

## I. Overview

The Argent Banner operates from Silverspire Hall in Oria under Ser Victor Kordane. Its expeditions combine dangerous fieldwork with the demands of a public production: sponsor schedules, live audiences, publicity agreements, and promised relic sales. Its silver helmet beneath the sun promises that courage will be seen.

The Banner has the resources to equip outstanding teams and the incentive to keep them visible. Those strengths can conflict when the safest decision is to withdraw before the promised climax. A delver’s public persona belongs in a contract; their life does not become the audience’s property.

Its motto is “For Gold, Glory, and the Grand Story.” The emblem depicts a silver knight’s helmet coated by the sun.

## III. Ideology & Reputation

The Argent Banner’s founding creed asserts that **“the story is as vital as the relic.”**  
They believe delvers are modern demigods—symbols of inspiration in an age that worships spectacle.  
Every delve is treated as a **scripted epic**: conflicts rehearsed, lighting curated through rune arrays, and triumphs immortalized in crystal broadcast.

While the Ministry officially condemns “manufactured heroics,” it nonetheless licenses the Banner’s shows, citing “public morale value.”

**Public Perception:** Glamorous, heroic, shallow.  
**Ministry Assessment:** Economically vital, politically volatile.

## V. Membership & Requirements

Admission into the Argent Banner is invitation-only.  
Applicants are assessed not just for talent, but **marketability**.

**Ideal Candidates Include:**

- Scions of noble bloodlines or proven military heroes.

- Delvers with public charisma and audience appeal.

- Duelists or tacticians with verifiable spectacle value.

- Artisans or mages capable of theatrical precision.

Recruits undergo the **Silver Trial**, a televised labyrinth performance scored by combat efficiency, composure, and aesthetics.  
Failure earns polite dismissal; success earns a contract gilded in platinum and politics.

---

## VI. Operations & Practices

### Expedition Format

Each delve follows the **Tri-Stage Production Model**:

1. **Pre-Narrative Design** – Storyboards, relic forecasts, and sponsorship placement.

2. **Live Delve Execution** – Multiple scrying angles, staged heroics, minimal actual risk.

3. **Post-Production Mythcraft** – Edited broadcasts, dramatized voiceovers, and relic auction teasers.

### Relic Handling

Recovered relics are sold to collectors or nobles through the **Silverspire Auction Circuit**, yielding a 25% tax tithe to the Ministry under §2-B (Reliquary Safety).  
The Banner claims “curation rights” over cultural relics, often resulting in prolonged legal disputes with the Free Company.

---

## VII. Assets & Holdings

- **Silverspire Hall:** A gothic tower complex housing relic archives, broadcast studios, and the **Hall of Mirrors**, an auditorium for viewing recorded delves.

- **The Argent Armory:** Custom weaponsmiths producing stylized relic-grade gear aligned with guild color codes.

- **The Museum of the Grand Story:** Private gallery displaying unused relics; accessible to donors and nobles only.

- **The Silver Wings:** A fleet of rune-bound skiffs for aerial transport and camera relay.

---

## VIII. Relationship with the Ministry

The Argent Banner is licensed under the **Private Guild Charter (Type-S, Oria District)** but operates outside MDO sponsorship.  
Its independence allows faster action, but obliges strict financial transparency and quarterly audits.

Ministry stance: _“Tolerated, essential, regrettable.”_  
Without the Banner’s broadcasts, delving would lose its largest revenue source—yet its dramatization often undermines Ministry authority.

> _“They turn danger into theatre, and theatre back into danger.”_ — Lt. Salvia Korrin

---

## IX. Relationships & Rivalries

### The Free Company of Louvain _(Adversarial Respect)_

The Free Company views the Banner as **soulless performers**, while the Banner dismisses the Company as **pious amateurs**.  
Public duels between the two are common and lucrative; both sides privately arrange them for spectacle ratings.

### The Crimson Reavers _(Uneasy Alliance)_

The Reavers provide the Banner with real combat footage for use in “authenticating” their own performances.  
In exchange, the Reavers gain financial patronage and access to noble clients.

### The Grand Scrying Network (GSN) _(Partnership)_

The Banner’s exclusive broadcast partner. GSN dictates narrative pacing and character focus, blurring the line between journalism and fiction.

---

## X. Cultural Influence

The Argent Banner has reshaped Aerathon’s definition of heroism.  
Children reenact their duels; nobles host “relic banquets” themed after their delves.  
Critics call it moral decay; the Ministry calls it economic salvation.

The Banner’s guild motto, now a common proverb, adorns half the delvers’ crests in Aerathon:

> _“For Gold, Glory, and the Grand Story.”_

## Working With the Guild

Applicants negotiate both field duties and broadcast terms. Safety staff retain responsibility for an exit even when a sponsor demands continuity. Inflato’s reinstated wardenship gives rescue crews an influential advocate, without making every production safe by association.

The immediate argument concerns who pays when a rescue interrupts a contracted livecast. Some producers want the loss charged to the rescued crew. Wardens want the contingency funded before departure, where it can be discussed without someone hanging from a collapsing ledge.
