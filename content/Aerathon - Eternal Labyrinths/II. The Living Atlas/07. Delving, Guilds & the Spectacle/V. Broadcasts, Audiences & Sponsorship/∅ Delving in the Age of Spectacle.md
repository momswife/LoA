# Delving in the Age of Spectacle

Modern delving is at once regulated fieldwork, guild enterprise, and public performance. Registration and classification determine who may descend; guild charters organize labor and claims; scrytech carries expeditions to distant audiences; and tipping, betting, sponsorship, and broadcast law turn each descent into a civic and commercial event.

This page is an orientation to those connected systems rather than a history of how they arose. Begin with:
