# N-Class Protocol

**Filed Division:** Ministry of Delving Operations — Living Atlas Division  
**Responsible Offices:** Arcane Infrastructure Registry; Structural Cartography Office; Guild Relations Bureau; Charter Counsel Office  
**Current Revision:** Late Redfall, 3388 A.D.  
**Document Class:** _Operational Advisory / Grade-I Ministerial Record_  
**Access Designation:** _Public with Advisory_

## I. Designation and Scope

An **N-Series Activation** is a rare registration event in which the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/I. Orientation, Licensing & Classification/03. Arcanet Core|Arcanet Core]] forms or assigns a team rather than assessing applicants only as individuals. The resulting group is recorded as an **N-Class Team** and receives a numbered unit designation.

The designation records an observed Core action and a convergence-relevant resonance pattern. It does not prove predestination, moral authority, divine approval, or inevitable success. It is not a UDMI rank, delver license, guild class, labyrinth tier, mission rank, or document classification.

Ministerial surveys have historically described activations as occurring in fewer than one of every 2,500 known registration ceremonies. That figure is an administrative estimate derived from surviving records, not a fixed probability governing future activations.

## II. Recognition of an Activation

The registration office opens an N-Series review when the Ceremony produces several of the following together:

- a shared resonance flare across the selected delvers;
- unscheduled audible, visual, or inscribed Core output;
- automatic creation of a Team Ledger and numbered unit designation;
- immediate notification of the responsible Ministry offices; and
- compulsory review by a Divine Auditor.

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/I. Orientation, Licensing & Classification/02. Arcanet Ceremony|Arcanet Ceremony]] record governs recognition of the event. A team is not N-Class merely because its members demonstrate unusual cohesion or attract public speculation.

## III. Legal Position

N-Class status creates a supervised exception framework. It does not place a team above the Canon Charter.

The Ministry may waive an ordinary procedure only when the waiver is connected to a documented convergence risk, Core directive, emergency response, or specific writ. All other licensing, ethical, reporting, relic, and public-safety duties remain in force. An exception granted for one operation does not become a permanent privilege in another.

## IV. Shared Duties

Every N-Class Team must:

- report anomalous resonance, new Core output, and unplanned changes in labyrinth behavior;
- preserve its Team Ledger and cooperate with assigned Oculi and auditors;
- avoid deliberately provoking a breach, relic awakening, or convergence event;
- comply with operation-specific limits recorded in its writ; and
- submit to Charter Counsel review when an exception departs from ordinary law.

## V. Domain-Specific Exceptions

### Labyrinth Entry and Team Formation

An approved N-Class Team may enter a labyrinth up to two tiers above its members' ordinary license eligibility. Access beyond that limit requires a separate High Ministry writ and, where applicable, Divine Oculi consent. The Ministry may waive ordinary party-size, role-assignment, formation, scheduling, and waiting-period rules when the Core-formed composition is material to the operation.

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/I. Orientation, Licensing & Classification/05. Labyrinth Classifications|Labyrinth Classifications]] record controls site eligibility and modifiers.

### Cartography and Core Routing

Assigned Oculi may provide enhanced path forecasts, early deviation warnings, and direct Core-linked hazard notices. Maps created in response to an N-Class presence receive restricted verification and permanent archival preservation. Because a team's presence may also generate new routes, rearrange floors, or intensify convergence anomalies, a predictive map remains an observation rather than a guarantee.

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/II. Field Operations & Safety/01. Labyrinth Cartography|Labyrinth Cartography]] record controls map production and field use.

### Breach Response

An N-Class Team may receive expedited authorization for Tier 4 or Tier 5 breach response, priority Oculi and Grand Scrying Network support, and direct routing to Core-linked hazard data. The team must not deliberately evolve the breach and must not remove a relic unless the operation's writ, the Ministry, or a recorded Core directive authorizes it.

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/II. Field Operations & Safety/02. Breach Containment|Breach Containment]] record controls the response operation.

### Relic Handling

Within an approved operation, N-Class personnel may handle Category C relics without an escort and Category D relics under reduced but recorded supervision. Category X handling always requires Oculi supervision and the applicable restricted authorization. All such interactions are Core-observed, sealed for permanent retention, and subject to enhanced containment when resonance rises.

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/II. Field Operations & Safety/03. Relic Handling & Regulation|Relic Handling & Regulation]] record controls custody, ownership, and containment.

### Audience Interaction and Broadcast Integrity

High-impact tips may be admitted when assigned Oculi determine that the team can receive them without unacceptable distortion. Every tip to an N-Class Team is logged as convergence-relevant activity. Manipulation of an N-Class broadcast is treated as a Class-1 broadcast offense; its Oculi record receives three-seal restricted review, and no guild, sponsor, or political office may alter the official narrative summary for public-relations purposes.

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/V. Broadcasts, Audiences & Sponsorship/02. Audience Tipping|Audience Tipping]] and [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/V. Broadcasts, Audiences & Sponsorship/04. Broadcast Manipulation Laws|Broadcast Manipulation Laws]] records control their respective systems.

### External Missions

An N-Class Team may accept Rank B through Rank S external missions without ordinary approval delays. The mission may be reclassified if the team's presence alters the threat profile. Core observation does not replace political authorization where a mission affects sovereign or diplomatic interests, and N-Class external work does not receive the ordinary Fame Engine bonus.

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/III. Monthly Ledger/01. Bounties & Mission Board/01. Mission & Bounty Ranking System|Mission & Bounty Ranking System]] controls external assignments.

## VI. Oversight and Risk

N-Class operations normally receive two or more Divine Oculi, a dedicated Arcanet auditor, Structural Cartography review, and Charter Counsel review where legal deviations occur. Relevant map, relic, broadcast, and Team Ledger records are retained permanently under the access designation appropriate to their contents.

N-Class resonance may stabilize some loops, hazards, or tipped effects while worsening others. Recorded risks include intensified reality overlap, attraction of additional anomalies, new route formation, and fate-resonance cascades. The designation therefore supports closer oversight and limited operational flexibility; it is not a blanket claim that the team is safer than an ordinary party.

## VII. Unit N-7

**Unit N-7**, publicly called **Overkill**, remains the best-known N-Series case. Its registration produced a shared synchronization pulse and forced multiple Oculi to recalibrate, prompting continent-wide observation. Later references to N-7 illustrate the protocol but do not create rules unique to that team unless a record states otherwise.

## VIII. Authority of This Record

This page controls the shared meaning, duties, and limits of N-Class status. Domain records control how those rules are implemented during registration, labyrinth entry, cartography, breach response, relic handling, audience interaction, broadcast review, and external missions. Where an older summary conflicts with this current protocol, the current protocol governs.
