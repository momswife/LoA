### _Ministerial Standards for Tiering, Threat Assessment & Descent Eligibility_

Every known Labyrinth—ancient, emergent, or anomalous—is classified by the Ministry of Delving Operations according to a comprehensive **Tier System**. This system determines who may enter, how delves are sanctioned, what protections are required, and how closely the Ministry must monitor the site.

Labyrinth classifications are based not on size or age alone, but on:

- Magical destabilization
    
- Monster density
    
- Environmental threat
    
- Curse saturation
    
- Relic volatility
    
- Known historical collapses
    
- Divine or planar resonance
    
- Probability of catastrophic breach
    

These tier designations are legally binding across all registered guilds, nations, and delver coalitions.

---

## I. The Labyrinth Tier System

The Ministry recognizes **seven active classifications**, from harmless youth-training labyrinths to reality-threatening catastrophes.

---

### Tier 0 — Pedagogical Constructs

**Threat Level:** _Negligible_  
**Authorized Delvers:** Trainees, provisional permit holders  
**Typical Features:**

- Simulated hazards
    
- Illusory monsters
    
- Non-lethal spellfields
    

Tier 0 sites exist purely for training and education.

---

### Tier 1 — Stable Labyrinths

**Threat Level:** _Low_  
**Authorized Delvers:** C-Rank and above  
**Typical Features:**

- Predictable floors
    
- Minimal curse activity
    
- Stable monster populations
    

Used for routine contracts, practice runs, and low-risk expeditions.

---

### Tier 2 — Transitional Labyrinths

**Threat Level:** _Moderate_  
**Authorized Delvers:** C-Rank (team only) or B-Rank solo  
**Common Hazards:**

- Occasional distortions
    
- Reactive monsters
    
- Relic nodes requiring stabilization
    

Often recommended for first serious delves.

---

### Tier 3 — Unstable Labyrinths

**Threat Level:** _High_  
**Authorized Delvers:** B-Rank teams, A-Rank solo  
**Common Hazards:**

- Shifting architecture
    
- Ambient magical bleed
    
- Increased curse density
    

These labyrinths are infamous for trapping or disorienting unprepared parties.

---

### Tier 4 — Volatile Labyrinths

**Threat Level:** _Severe_  
**Authorized Delvers:** A-Rank teams, S-Rank operatives  
**Common Hazards:**

- Severe distortion
    
- Planar resonance
    
- Monster lords & elite entities
    

Entry requires Ministry sign-off and Divine Oculi oversight.

---

### Tier 5 — Cataclysmic Labyrinths

**Threat Level:** _Extreme_  
**Authorized Delvers:** S-Rank teams only  
**Common Hazards:**

- World-affecting curses
    
- Dimensional seams
    
- Eldritch or divine involvement
    

Breaches from these labyrinths can devastate regions; many are sealed except in emergencies.

---

### Tier 6 — Forbidden Labyrinths

**Threat Level:** _Existential_  
**Authorized Delvers:** None (ministerially sealed)  
**Notes:**

- History-lost entities
    
- Convergence resonance
    
- High-risk temporal anomalies
    
- Divine prohibition in place
    

Tier 6 sites may only be opened with unanimous Divine Oculi consent and a special writ from the High Ministry.

## II. Secondary Classification Modifiers

Beyond Tier designation, Labyrinths can receive special modifiers based on environment, magical influence, or historical behavior.

#### • BLK — Black-Route Classification

Illicit labyrinth with no official registry. Entry is illegal.

#### • ANC — Ancestral Seal Condition

Labyrinth tied to beastfolk lineage or ancient clans.

#### • DIV — Divine Influence

Site under direct observation or historical influence of a deity.

#### • EXL — Extralinear Instability

Time, memory, or reality flows improperly inside.

#### • CRB — Corruption Bloom

Curses spread faster or strengthen over time.

#### • RCN — Relic-Concentrated Node

High relic density; extraction strictly regulated.

Modifiers may stack.  
A labyrinth labeled **4-DIV-EXL** is profoundly dangerous.

---

## III. Criteria for Tier Assignment

Tier classification is determined through a unified assessment of:

#### 1. Monster Density & Sentience

More sentient enemies dramatically increase tier consideration.

#### 2. Structural Stability

Includes:

- Corridor shifts
    
- Looping phenomena
    
- Pressure vortices
    
- Sudden spatial inversions
    

#### 3. Relic Volatility

Cursed or divine relics heavily influence tier.

#### 4. Environmental Hazard Saturation

Such as:

- Acid rains
    
- Ether pockets
    
- Whispering zones
    
- Time-dilated floors
    

#### 5. Historical Collapse Data

Previous fatalities, disappearances, or recorded distortions.

#### 6. Divine Oculi Reports

Live monitoring of hazard fluctuations.

## V. Reclassification Events

Labyrinth tiers may shift due to:

#### 1. Monster King Emergence

A sudden rise in local monster hierarchy.

#### 2. Relic Awakening

A dormant artifact activates unexpectedly.

#### 3. Spatial Rupture

Dimensional collapse or new floor manifestation.

#### 4. Convergence Signals

Rare resonance patterns aligned with prophetic anomalies.

When reclassification occurs, all delvers inside are immediately signaled with a **Crimson Recall Directive** and must evacuate.

## VII. N-Class Team Overrides

N-Class is a team designation, not a labyrinth tier. Within an approved operation, an N-Class Team may enter a labyrinth up to two tiers above its members' ordinary license eligibility and may receive expedited scheduling, direct Core-linked notices, and waivers from standard party-composition rules. Access beyond the two-tier allowance requires a separate High Ministry writ and any applicable Divine Oculi consent.

Site tier and modifiers remain unchanged by the team's presence.
