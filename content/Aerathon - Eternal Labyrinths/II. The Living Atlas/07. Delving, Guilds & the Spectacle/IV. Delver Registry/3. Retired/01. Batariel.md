# Batariel

## I. Overview

Batariel the Sealed Flame rose from a scribe’s vocation through the Celestial Bastion and founded Penumbra Sanctum. His campaigns sought to seal dangerous relics and impose an uncompromising order. Their record includes successes in containment and lethal judgments that the Ministry does not accept merely because he calls them holy.

He is retired from the active Delver Registry while retaining his role in the faithbound order. His deity remains unidentified; suspicions concerning Helm, Tyr, or a surviving divine echo are not settled doctrine.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** High Commander of the **Penumbra Sanctum**, recognized as a “Faithbound Private Order”

- **Religious Allegiance:** Devotion to an unnamed lawful deity of warding and conquest (suspected remnant of **Helm** or **Tyr**)

- **Known Titles:** _The Sealed Flame_, _Blade of the Ward_, _Batariel the Golden Sin_

- **Livecast Popularity Index:** **3.6 / 5** — Revered by the devout; distrusted by moderates; hated by relic cartels

---

## III. Ideals

- **Order Over Chaos** — Civilization must be reforged by holy discipline

- **Relics Are Not Meant for All** — Power belongs to shepherds, not sheep

- **Faith Without Doubt Is Strength** — Doubt is decay; conviction is purity

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Brand of Kaldrith

Batariel led a complete purge of the **Labyrinth of Kaldrith**, sealing all lower vaults with radiant scripture.  
The runes still burn—untouched, unbroken, and unapproved by the Ministry.

### 2. The Thousand-Chain Judgment

After a rogue crew stole a forbidden relic, Batariel hunted all members across five nations.  
Their trial—publicly broadcast—took place mid-delving.  
None survived sentencing.

### 3. The Silent Benediction

During a catastrophic delve collapse, Batariel emerged alone carrying relics and a broken priest-mask.  
The Celestial Bastion declared it a “holy reclamation.”  
The Ministry declared it “deeply suspicious.”

### 4. Storm-Harrowing of the Pale Vault

He single-handedly routed a labyrinth core emitting psychic corruption, though his companions reported that **the core whispered his name** before death.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### Gilded Vow

A radiant greatsword etched with divine prohibitions.

- Cannot be wielded by creatures bearing unresolved guilt

- Emits a 30-ft cone of **radiant suppression**, silencing active magic

- Known to scorch the hands of liars

### Aegis of Twelve Eyes

A living shield covered in twelve sealed eyes.

- Eyes open in the presence of grave sin

- When open, each eye fires a radiant beam once per long rest

- Some say the shield sleeps… others say it hungers

### Mantle of the First Doctrine

A gold-inlaid tabard enforcing divine honesty.

- Grants resistance to psychic and necrotic damage

- Prevents wearer from speaking deliberate falsehood

- Documented to “tighten” when heresy is spoken nearby

## VIII. Rumors & Anomalies

- His armor reportedly **tightens** when falsehoods are spoken nearby

- Some scholars believe **his god is dead**—and that Batariel’s faith sustains a fading divine echo

- The Aegis of Twelve Eyes may be a sealed celestial parasite

- The Penumbra Sanctum maintains a secret vault of “sanctified relics” that sometimes **whisper in dreams**

- More than one delver claims Batariel's shadow moves independently during moments of judgment

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Retirement removes Batariel from routine deployment, not from political influence. Visitors seeking access to a sealed site must establish the applicable authority independently of his approval. His followers’ assurances of purity do not replace a custody record or a civilian safety plan.
