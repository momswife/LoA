# Nix of the Whispertail

## I. Overview

Nix of the Whispertail is a Beastfolk delver of uncertain lineage. Their work is reconstructed from bypassed traps, diverted creatures, and expeditions that discovered only afterward how much danger had been removed. Repeated descriptions of a possum, ferret, or “animated absence” do not establish an ancestry.

The registry retains Nix’s S-Rank standing while acknowledging how little direct public footage exists. Accounts of divine adoption or Labyrinth birth remain folklore. Their loose association with the Cinderpaw Pact never became a recorded membership.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** Unaffiliated; loosely linked to the **Cinderpaw Pact**, though never formally

- **Religious Allegiance:** Rumored follower of **Mask** or **Selûne**, changing with the moon

- **Known Titles:** _Whispertail_, _The Hidden Saint_, _Nix the Never-Seen_, _Patron of the Silent Path_

- **Livecast Popularity Index:** **3.0 / 5** — All attempts to broadcast them result in static, flickers, or blank feeds

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Silent Route of Wyrmreach

Six guilds perished in a wyvern-packed floor.  
Nix slipped through unseen, disarmed the traps, soothed the beasts, and left the relic waiting on a pedestal by the exit.  
_They were never spotted entering._

### 2. The Clockless Chamber Inversion

A temporal recursion trapped an entire team in infinite loops.  
Nix infiltrated _backwards_, edited runes by moonlight, and escaped unseen.  
Found note: _“Time’s fixed. Don’t break it again.”_

### 3. The Rescue of Thistlekin Hollow

A shadow-plague cursed a village.  
At dawn, every home held a healing draught on the pillow—doors still locked.  
A child found a **soft tailprint** in ash and whispered thanks to the wind.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### The Echocloak

Woven from dusk-thread and mothwing vellum.

- Grants invisibility in dim light

- Creates a **10 ft sphere of muted sound**

- Once per rest, splits into **three shadow decoys**

### Thievesight Veil

A silk band worn over the eyes.

- Echo-sense in total darkness

- Highlights trap weak points & false walls

- Cannot be caught on scrying while worn

### The Hollow Dagger

Cold iron fused with shadowglass.

- Cuts silence into wounds instead of blood

- Passes through wards without triggering them

- Severed illusions collapse in a puff of cold air

## VIII. Rumors & Anomalies

- **All livecasts fail.** Static, blackout, or camera drift into empty space.

- **Nix may be a succession of individuals**, passing the mantle through unknown criteria.

- **Trackers attempting to follow them disappear**—only to reappear unharmed, confused, but neatly fed.

- **Trail rations left in shadowed corners vanish overnight** in delver camps, accompanied by tiny pawprints.

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Nix is missing. A cleared corridor is not proof of their present location, and a familiar mark is not permission to enter an unsafe route. Their five legacy S-rated operations remain attributed through indirect evidence; the older description “confirmed, unwitnessed” should not be read as direct observation.
