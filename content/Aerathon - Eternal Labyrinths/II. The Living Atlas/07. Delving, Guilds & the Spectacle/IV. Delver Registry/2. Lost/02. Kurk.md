# Kurk of the Loose Rope

## I. Overview

Kurk of the Loose Rope began as a street thief and became a devotee of Kelemvor. His irreverence survived the change: stolen candles, graveyard games, and jokes beside death-wards appear repeatedly in accounts of his work. He had no formal guild membership, though the Free Company repeatedly gave unofficial support.

His concern was for those whom a successful expedition might still leave behind. Recovering a dying stranger or releasing a bound soul mattered more to him than appearing on the departing roster. That habit leaves the archive with an unusually incomplete operational record.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** None formally; repeatedly “unofficially backed” by the **Free Company of Louvain** (who “never caught him sneaking in”)

- **Religious Allegiance:** **Kelemvor**, though his rites often involved _dice in graveyards_ and incense lit from **stolen candles**

- **Known Titles:** _The Loose Rope_, _Saint of Last Jokes_, _The Pale Coin_, _Whisperfeet_

- **Livecast Popularity Index:** **4.5 / 5** — Known mostly through fan-made highlight reels; scry-cameras rarely caught more than a **blur and a quip**

## III. Ideals

- **Laugh First, Die Last** — Humor is armor.

- **Death is Fair—Make Sure You Are Too** — No rigged scales, no forgotten names.

- **Steal Only What the World Owes** — Riches, relics, redemption… whatever Death left unclaimed.

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Silence of Bonebridge

An undead lord cursed a drowned catacomb to trap souls in unending whispers. Kurk dove alone and resurfaced three days later with the phylactery swapped for a **coin pouch**.  
Whispers stopped instantly.  
Asked how, he shrugged: _“He bet double or nothing.”_

### 2. The Wake of Hollow’s Gate

A delve plagued by vengeful spirits found peace after Kurk held vigil for **seven days**, without food, water, or words. He ferried spirits with prayer, jokes, and lullabies.  
A captured ghostly whisper: _“He made us feel… remembered.”_

### 3. The Pocket Pick of the Pale One

At a cursed noble gala, disguised as a jester, Kurk “borrowed” a soulstone from a pact-bound warlock mid-toast.  
It shattered in moonlight, freeing the bound soul.  
He left a receipt: _“One soul. No interest.”_

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### The Grey Knot Sash

A threadbare belt tied into **thirteen loops**, one for each soul he failed to save.  
Grants:

- Shadowstep

- Mistmeld dodge

- Brief **ghostwalk** when he “ties off” reality

### Monk’s Vow (Coinblade Tonfa)

Tonfa forged from melted **grave coins** and funeral bells.

- Necrotic + radiant blend

- Spin like prayer wheels to **redirect one death effect** per long rest

### The Last Laugh Mask

Porcelain half-mask with an eternal smirk.

- Kurk’s laughter disrupts charm & fear

- Those who die hearing it are said to reach the afterlife **smiling**

## VII. Known Sayings

_“The gods weigh souls. I just carry ’em for a bit.”_  
— Note left on a cleansed altar

_“Death’s not scary. Being forgotten? That’s the real joke.”_  
— During a vigil for an unnamed delver

_“I prayed. Then I picked the lock. Both worked.”_  
— After freeing an entombed cleric (only a blur recorded on livecast)

## VIII. Rumors & Anomalies

- **No one ever saw him sleep**, yet his cloak was always warm.

- A copper with his coin-mark has been found in the mouths of delvers pulled from the brink.

- Some say he died years ago and is **“finishing the list.”**

- Whispers claim Kelemvor offered him a post as psychopomp; he declined but **took the robes**.

---

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Kurk is deceased. His rites are remembered as personal practice, not a blanket religious requirement. Investigators should distinguish his four legacy S-rated operations from the claim that he appeared on their official rosters; the latter is not supported.
