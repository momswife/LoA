# Thalia Vess

## I. The Other Flame

Thalia Vess attained S-Rank alongside [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/IV. Delver Registry/3. Retired/02. Zippo Chumgis|Zippo Chumgis]]. Public retellings often introduce her only at the moment of her loss. Their surviving performance and expedition records show a partner with her own discipline: she shaped the rhythm of the working while Zippo guided its blaze. Their collaboration became **Blazewaltz**, still taught in Arnerian academies.

Her teaching used a plain exercise. A student had to end a flame at a partner’s signal, even when continuing would make the performance more impressive. She considered the ability to yield control part of mastery. The lesson proved harder to sell to an audience than a brilliant display, and she taught it anyway.

## II. Ashfall Spire

During the catastrophe, Thalia held back an entropic warden while Zippo sealed the core. She was lost; her body was never recovered. Zippo returned carrying her broken pendant, then resigned from the Argent Banner and ceased delving.

The registry files Thalia under **Lost**, with present status **Missing**. The presumption of death in memorial accounts does not supply a recovered body or a confirmed account of her fate. Neither an unextinguished shop hearth nor an uncatalogued pendant establishes that she survives.

## III. Working Legacy

Blazewaltz demands timing between people, not merely two simultaneous fires. Thalia’s surviving exercises separate signals for approach, release, and abandonment of the working. A partner who cannot acknowledge the final signal is not ready for the next sequence, however strong their flame.

The academies disagree about how much of the original method should be performed publicly. Instructors preserving her restraint face pressure from promoters who want the version remembered from the most spectacular livecasts. The disagreement offers a reason to seek old teaching notes without manufacturing a new sighting of Thalia.

## IV. Record Limits

Her last established rank corresponds to level 20. No present availability is implied. This profile does not invent a rescue route, an exact date of loss, a new relic power, or a final answer to Ashfall. Zippo’s account remains the principal linked record of the disaster.
