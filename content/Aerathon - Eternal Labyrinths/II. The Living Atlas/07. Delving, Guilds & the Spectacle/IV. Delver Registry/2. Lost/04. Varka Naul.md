# Varka Naul

## I. Overview

Varkha “Carrion-Laugh” Naul, commonly indexed as Varka Naul, is a hyenafolk devotee of Loviatar. He stores and spends pain through ritual tallies, using the resulting power to influence wards, traps, and adversaries. His reputation for completing destructive contracts coexists with disputed survivor reports and severe restrictions on Ministry employment.

The Free Company’s censure is part of the record, not a feud to be erased by his rank. His public audience includes followers and hostile spectators; neither establishes consent from the people subjected to his methods.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** S-Rank freelance; formally **censured** by the **Free Company of Louvain**; black-flagged for Ministry contracts except under **Clause 9 (Total Denial)**

- **Religious Allegiance:** Devoted to **Loviatar**, The Maiden of Pain; maintains a portable shrine in a flayed-leather case

- **Known Titles:** _Carrion-Laugh_, _The Maw of Mercy_, _Tallyman of Screams_, _The Hyena of Louvain_

- **Livecast Popularity Index:** **1.1 / 5** — Core-throttled, repeatedly reported; sustained mostly by hate-watchers, ritual cultists, and dueling fans with poor taste

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Denial of the Salt Gate

A flood-prison gate had resisted three separate companies. Varkha opened it by **bleeding a volunteer** across the threshold and laughing until the hinge “answered.”  
Spirits fled.  
Clients stopped asking questions.

### 2. The Choir of Broken Glass

Within a prism-maze, Varkha fashioned a wind-chime of **teeth and shattered lens-crystals**. Each scream tuned the labyrinth to the correct harmonic, shattering panes in sequence.  
He left the chimes behind **“so the maze remembers.”**

### 3. The Taker’s Truce

When negotiating with a ghoul prefect, Varkha offered **one living hour per delver**. He then “borrowed” those hours from enemies mid-battle, leaving them alive but hollow when delivered.  
Technically fulfilled.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### Tally-Torque of the Crimson Garden (very rare)

A braided sinew collar threaded with wolf-bones etched in tally marks.  
Each scream within **30 ft** adds a notch.

- **3 notches:** Quickens next action

- **5 notches:** Refresh a spent ability

- **9 notches:** Impose disadvantage on a creature’s next save against his effects  
    All notches fade at dawn with a faint, unmistakable chuckle.

### Laughing Sundial

A brass palm-dial that casts a **second shadow** when fear is present.

- Align the two shadows: **Delay** a trap or lair action by 1 round

- Once per short rest: **Advance noon** to force the next trap to trigger **immediately**

### Hyenalaughed Hook

A serrated chain-hook of blackened steel.

- **Reach:** 10 ft

- **Special:** Returns when released

- **Against frightened foes:** Deals +2d6 psychic and **drags 10 ft**  
    If dragged adjacent, Varkha gains **temp HP equal to his level**

### Pain-Priest’s Satchel

Ritual clamps, brine, pins, salts.  
Confers proficiency (or advantage if proficient) on **Intimidation** or **Medicine** checks related to coercion, battlefield triage under extreme duress, or preventing unconsciousness.

- Livecast audio **clips and distorts** when Varkha laughs; automated transcripts frequently insert **[applause]** even when no audience is present

- A Ministry auditor insists Varkha’s shadow **lags three steps** behind, laughing off-beat

- A “weeping cube” in a breach once learned to toll a bell Varkha gave it; the bell **still rings** in that wing

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Naul is missing and black-flagged for ordinary Ministry contracts. His exceptional Total Denial clause does not amount to an open commission. A report of his return should be treated as a protection and evidence problem before it becomes a recruitment opportunity.
