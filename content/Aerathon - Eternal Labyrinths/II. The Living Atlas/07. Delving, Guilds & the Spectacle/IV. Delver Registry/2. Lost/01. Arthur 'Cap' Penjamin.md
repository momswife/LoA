# Arthur 'Cap' Penjamin

## I. Overview

Arthur “Cap” Penjamin served as a senior Free Company commander and frontline protector. His tower shield, measured retreats, and mentorship made him a reassuring presence on dangerous expeditions. His career changed after he recovered Stormhowler from the Breakerspine Labyrinth.

The relic amplified aggression and eroded the restraint on which his reputation depended. His death did not close the case: Stormhowler shattered into seven fragments during his final delve, and no intact weapon was recovered. The Company honors his earlier service while teaching the failure as evidence that a trusted reputation cannot replace relic monitoring.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** Senior Commander, Free Company of Louvain (posthumously honored)

- **Religious Affiliation:** Informal devotee of **Tempus**; personal shield bore a hand-carved shrine to **Torm**

- **Historic Titles:** _Cap_, _The Iron Wall_, _Breaker of the Twelve-Handed Siege_, _Penjamin the Bulwark_

- **Spectacle Index Rating:** **4.8 / 5** — One of the most beloved frontline delvers of the early Spectacle Era; memorial livecast broke attendance records

## III. Personality & Ideals

### Before Stormhowler (Stable Period)

- **“The Shield Comes First.”** Protection is purpose.

- **“Glory Is Shared.”** No victory belongs to one.

- **“Steel Bends. I Don’t.”** Endure, even when it costs.

### After Stormhowler (Corruption Period)

- **“Strike Before You’re Struck.”** Fear is a weapon.

- **“They’ll Thank You When They Survive.”** Even if they won’t.

- **“The Storm Chooses Its Champion.”** And it does not wait.

Ministry analysis confirms high-grade **behavioral displacement** following exposure to Stormhowler’s sentience.

Cap could usually contain the bound genie's pressure during his earlier use of the weapon. That control was a continuing effort, not immunity. The later displacement records the failure of that restraint; it does not mean every use of Stormhowler immediately stripped him of judgment.

## IV. Notable Exploits

### The Stand at Granite Hollow

Cap held a collapsing breach gate for seven uninterrupted minutes—long enough for his party to evacuate civilians and seal a dimensional rift.  
**Recorded impacts:** 146  
**Confirmed threats:** two spectral dragons; one curse-lance  
A defining example of frontline integrity.

### The Warpath of Twelve

Led an under-ranked squad through a corrupted multi-floor dive with **zero casualties**.  
Carried his squire unconscious for two floors; later nominated the boy for Guild Honors himself.

### The Fall of Stormhowler’s Bastion

Cap’s final delve.  
He used Stormhowler to destroy a living labyrinth core feeding on delver fear.  
In the process, the axe overtook him.  
Scry-feeds captured fragmented footage before the broadcast was terminated.  
His party survived only by stopping him.

Stormhowler broke into seven fragments during the catastrophe. The fragments scattered beyond the Bastion; their precise paths and present keepers are not established in this public record. The surviving account does not resolve the exact moment of the shattering relative to the party's final intervention.

This incident remains categorized as a **Mirror-Class Psychogenic Corruption Event**.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### The Aegis of Cadenwall

A legendary tower shield etched with adaptive sigils and divine boons.

- Grants resistance to **force** and **psychic** damage

- Sigils shift when allies are harmed, attempting to redirect blows  
    Currently held in the Free Company’s Hall of Fallen Steel.

### Stormhowler _(Shattered Relic — Seven Fragments)_

A titanic greataxe containing a powerful storm genie. Earlier descriptions of a "semi-sentient lightning spirit" recorded its whispers and elemental manifestations without adequately describing the intelligence within.

The blade was storm-forged adamantine, edged with blue-white lightning and carved with runes that brightened as storms approached. Its petrified wooden haft was attributed to the **Worldroot**, a tree said to stand at the heart of the Elemental Plane of Air. That provenance remains a tradition attached to the weapon, not an authenticated survey of the plane.

- Carries lightning through its strikes and releases violent gusts and thunder

- Earlier field accounts describe heightened strength, speed, and aggression

- Shields an attuned wielder against lightning and thunder while exposing them to the genie's pressure toward battle

- Known to whisper combat strategies and test its wielder's restraint

The seven fragments remain the subject of recovery inquiries. Their existence does not establish that the genie was destroyed, freed, or divided into seven independent beings.

### Medallion of Fourfold Oaths

Symbol of his vows to his first party.  
Blackened upon his first recorded Stormhowler overreach.  
Remains warm to the touch—no magical source identified.

## VIII. Rumors, Echoes & Anomalies

_(All claims pending formal verification under Charter §6-F.)_

- **Stormhowler was never recovered intact** from the Bastion site; claims about individual fragments require separate authentication.

- Several Free Company delvers report seeing **Cap’s shield in dreams** during moments of near-death panic.

- An unmarked cliff-grave in Ashglen is struck by lightning every year on his death-date.

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Cap is deceased and cannot be recruited. His preserved equipment and case records support instruction and investigation; reports of Stormhowler require containment review. Honoring his memory does not require suppressing testimony from people harmed during his decline.
