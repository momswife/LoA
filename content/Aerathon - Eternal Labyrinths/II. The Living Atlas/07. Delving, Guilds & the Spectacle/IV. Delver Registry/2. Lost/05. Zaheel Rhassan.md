# Zaheel Rhassan

## I. Overview

Zaheel Rhassan, the Key-Thief, entered delving while fleeing creditors in Vinyot. He returned with lock-maps, relics, and property taken from guild vaults reached through Labyrinth routes. His ability made him a folk hero to some debtors and an adversary to institutions whose locks he treated as suggestions.

In the Black-Marrow Vault he took Soulbite and was killed as the blade drew in his soul. His body was not recovered. The relic is held under Thrice-Bond Containment; the archive distinguishes that custody from recovery of Zaheel himself.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** Unaffiliated; formally **blacklisted** by the Argent Banner, Crimson Reavers, Free Company of Louvain, Velvet Ledger, and the Emberlight Union

- **Criminal Allegiance:** Rumored ties to the Vinyot Shadow Choir (unconfirmed)

- **Known Titles:** _The Key-Thief_, _The Door’s Shadow_, _Lockwhisper_, _Prince of Padlocks_

- **Livecast Popularity Index:** 3.3 / 5 — Mixed reactions; adored in under-city forums, despised publicly by guild leadership

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Vinyot Debt Escape

Zaheel entered a novice labyrinth pursued by enforcers.  
He exited seven hours later with:

- A new relic dagger

- A sack of monster teeth worth 300g

- All three enforcers’ coin purses  
    This incident began his legend.

### 2. The Argent Banner Vault Humiliation

Through a labyrinth breach connection, Zaheel infiltrated the Argent Banner’s **Tier-2 secure vault**, stealing:

- 16 relic-laced keys

- A full ledger of illicit guild taxes

- The guildmaster’s wine stash  
    He left a note: _“Your lock was lonely.”_

### 3. The Hundred Locks Trial

In a challenge chamber mislabeled as a “Puzzle Floor,” Zaheel picked **100 locks in 10 minutes**, all while being chased by animated gauntlets.  
He kept one cuff as a trophy.

### 4. The Black-Marrow Tragedy

During a delve with three independent delvers, Zaheel seized **Soulbite**, a cursed blade that devours souls through physical contact.  
It consumed him instantly.  
His companions reported hearing his final whisper echo through the blade:  
_“Finally… a lock I can’t open.”_

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### Whisperlock Tools

A mastercrafted set of picks forged from mythril-thread steel. Said to vibrate when a trap is nearby. Often used by guild infiltrators today.

### Ring of Quiet Steps

A loop of silver that dampened sound around Zaheel’s feet. Allowed him to move silently even on broken glass. (Recovered from a goblin fence two years after his death.)

### Soulbite _(Cursed Artifact)_

Currently sealed in MDO custody.

- Contains Zaheel’s trapped soul

- Emits occasional “tumbler clicks” when approached

- Has attempted to unlock its own containment twice  
    Pending destruction—denied due to unknown metaphysical risk.

## VIII. Rumors & Anomalies

- Some claim Zaheel’s **shadow still appears** near locked doors inside certain labyrinths

- Soulbite occasionally rattles in its containment box in a **rhythm matching Zaheel’s heartbeat**, which was recorded during autopsy

- Several poor districts reported mysterious donations matching Zaheel’s known handwriting **after his death**

- A Vinyot charlatan claims Zaheel’s soul still picks locks **from inside the blade**

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Zaheel cannot be hired or treated as merely missing. Soulbite’s attempted interference with its own containment makes proposed contact or destruction a separate hazard. Reports of his tools changing hands do not establish that the purchaser has inherited his skill or authority.
