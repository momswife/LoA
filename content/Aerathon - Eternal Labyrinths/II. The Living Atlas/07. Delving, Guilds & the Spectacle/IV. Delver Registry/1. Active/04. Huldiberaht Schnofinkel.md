# Huldiberaht Schnofinkel

## I. Overview

Huldiberaht Schnofinkel is a human druid, former Argent Banner initiate, and practitioner of Earthen Meteorform. He combines wildshape, gravitational augmentation, and druidic channeling to strike with catastrophic force. His former research in Convergence Theory survives in his calculations even where his conduct has abandoned academic restraint.

His followers describe him as nature’s necessary correction. People responsible for damaged shelters and dead companions have less charitable names. S-Rank records his capability; it does not endorse his methods or cancel the broadcasting prohibitions recorded against him.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** Former Argent Banner initiate (expelled); independent and unaffiliated thereafter

- **Religious Allegiance:** Claims devotion to “The True Circle,” believed to reference a long-dead primal deity

- **Known Titles:** _The Sky Root_, _Huld the Flattening_, _Schnofinkel the Soilwrought_, _Grandmaster of the Vertical Hunt_

- **Livecast Popularity Index:** **3.2 / 5** — Banned in Oria, Arneria, and all educational broadcasts; cult following in unregulated arenas

## III. Ideals

- **Survival Is a Game of Weight and Timing** — If they can’t stand the landing, they weren’t meant to stand.

- **The Earth Accepts All—Broken Bones First** — Gravity is the great equalizer.

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Pummeling of Cragspire Bastion

During a three-guild contested delve, Huldiberaht launched himself nearly **200 ft** into the air with druidic momentum, transforming mid-arc into a stone-armored badger.  
The resulting impact flattened:

- two chambers

- a rival delver strike team

- the Bastion’s central staircase  
    The rival guilds begrudgingly named the spell after him.

### 2. The Root-Flood Trap at Oathgrove

By collapsing unstable earthen tunnels via seismic hops, Huldiberaht pinned a corrupted guardian beast. When a group of young delvers attempted to “save” it for research, he crushed them with the rebuttal:

> _“Sentiment is rot in bloom.”_

### 3. The Court of Flattened Blades

In a tribunal duel with a noble paladin, Huldiberaht remained still for the entire first round—then vanished skyward.  
He returned as a falling star.  
The arena required full reconstruction. The paladin survived; his reputation did not.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### Stonegut Totem

A druidic ironwood-oak hybrid implanted directly into his abdomen.  
**Functions:**

- Channels gravity-aligned primal magic

- Enhances falling-damage spells

- Emits seismic pulses on impact  
    Considered a Class-3 Risk Relic due to catastrophic vertical amplification.

### Mantle of Mossbone

A living cloak of ivy interwoven with petrified wolf-bark.  
**Properties:**

- Regenerates between delves

- Grants resistance to force damage

- Reduces self-impact trauma (but not collateral trauma)

### Sigil of Crushing Bloom

A cracked druidic tattoo glowing during Meteorform casting.  
Rumored to bind Huldiberaht to a **primordial of mass and momentum**, slowly consuming his soul with each “successful landing.”

## VIII. Rumors & Anomalies

- Leaves crater-shaped impressions in multiple planes; some fear these weaken planar boundaries

- His “spellbook” contains only the word **DOWN** in various fonts

- Beastfolk druids insist he communes with a primal force of weight itself

- Notably, he has **never harmed an animal companion**—only people, exclusively people

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Meteorform requires a landing decision as well as a target. Huldiberaht dismisses conservative exclusion zones as timid, while recovery crews must live with the debris. Contracts involving inhabited ground require a separate evacuation authority; his former affiliation supplies no present Argent Banner command rights.
