# Inflato the Clown Pervert

## I. Overview

Inflato adopted his notorious epithet after Huldiberaht Schnofinkel used it as an insult. The registry retains the public name, but most rescue crews call him the Gentle Jester. Reinstated as an Argent Banner warden after tribunal review, he also serves as a liaison to Ministry rapid response.

Pressure wards, buoyancy sigils, and careful timing allow him to redirect violence without matching its destructiveness. His greasepaint and theatrical gestures reassure frightened crowds while communicating instructions to crews. His rivalry with Huldiberaht concerns what happens to everyone standing around a powerful delver.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** S-Rank Warden of the **Argent Banner** (reinstated after tribunal review); liaison to MDO Rapid Response

- **Religious Allegiance:** Dual devotee of **Tyr** (justice) and **Yondalla** (community & hearth)

- **Known Titles:** _The Gentle Jester_, _Marshal of Soft Landings_, _The Pressure Saint_, _Banner-Bearer of the Big Top_

- **Livecast Popularity Index:** **4.8 / 5** — Praised by city watches, medics, engineers, and nervous parents; banned from two fight-pits for “excessive mercy”

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Featherfall Carnival at Oathgrove

During the caverns’ collapse triggered by Huldiberaht’s Root-Flood Trap, Inflato deployed a shimmering network of **buoyancy bladders**.  
He lowered **106 civilians** into shallow pools—wet, safe, and humiliated—but alive.

### 2. The Masquerade at Cragspire Bastion

Anticipating a Meteorform strike, he disguised pressure-runes as clown masks and ringed the arena.  
Huld’s impact dispersed into harmless wind; cotton candy and pennants spiraled skyward.  
When Huld snarled, _“Name that trick, harlequin,”_ Inflato replied:

> _“Compliance.”_

### 3. The Court of Softened Blades

In a tribunal duel against three zealot champions, Inflato delivered a complete, **nonlethal** victory:

- blades caught mid-swing in balloon-steel tethers

- hammers cushioned into bounce

- tempers dropped by pressure-slowing waltzes  
    His method became codified as the **Inflato Protocols** for nonlethal tribunal combat.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### Codpiece of Counterpressure

A heart-shaped kinetic bulwark anchoring his ward network.  
**Properties:**

- Absorbs catastrophic force

- Redistributes impact into harmless airbursts, streamers, and “comedic bounce”

- Prevents lethal concussive chain reactions

### Parade Standard of the Argent Banner

A sigil-threaded collapsible pennant-pole.  
**Functions:**

- Projects calming glyphwork into panicked crowds

- Marks safe evacuation lanes

- Conjures temporary “stepping balloons” for children & stretcher teams

- Finial contains a single-use **resilient sphere** for emergency triage

### Seltzer of Sanctity

A brass siphon of consecrated tonic.  
**Uses:**

- Extinguishes alchemical fires

- Neutralizes mundane acids

- Functions as **holy water**

- Cleans wounds, clears lungs, and leaves a sugared-citrus scent

## IX. Rivalry with Huldiberaht Schnofinkel

Inflato considers Huldiberaht his **“favorite challenge.”**  
He studies Huld’s trajectories, deploys marshals and medics in advance, and demonstrates repeatedly that **properly engineered mercy will outmaneuver brute force**.

Where Huld aims **DOWN**, Inflato is already there—  
**catching everyone else.**

---

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Inflato surveys where displaced force will go before cushioning its first impact. Holding a collapsing structure is useful only while someone clears the people beneath it. He prefers a sober route marshal beside him during public rescues; comedy draws attention, but clear hand signals move a crowd.
