# Togi

## I. Overview

Togi the Wagerblade comes from Al’Ar and has been expelled from the Crimson Reavers, Argent Banner, and Velvet Ledger. His losses in ordinary gaming preceded the survival wagers that made him famous. He now ties feats of swordsmanship to explicit stakes, turning limits he accepts into the terms of a contest.

Public accounts make his victories sound effortless. The oath-bound silences and disastrous finances in his record suggest a more complicated practice. The rumored duel-spirit and the patronage of Tymora remain interpretations, not a settled explanation.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** Freelance; previously expelled from the **Crimson Reavers**, **Argent Banner**, and **The Velvet Ledger**

- **Religious Allegiance:** None formally; foxfire trails and spectral parries suggest influence of **Tymora** or the Al’ar spirit **Laughing Blade**

- **Known Titles:** _The Wagerblade_, _Lucky Ghost_, _Dueler of Ten Thousand_, _Spirit-Touched Scatterhand_

- **Livecast Popularity Index:** **5.0 / 5** — Togi’s delves are wildly popular, often accompanied by audience-backed wagers and escalating relic bets

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Mirror Duel of Hollowglass Bastion

Faced with a labyrinth-forged mirror-double, Togi bet he could defeat it **blindfolded**. He waited—motionless—until the illusion erred.  
It erred once.

### 2. The Demon’s Dice of Crimson Hollow

Confronting a fiend who demanded wagers, Togi staked **his name** for a one-on-one duel. He slew the demon and claimed a cursed coin capable of rerolling death itself.  
He has never spent the roll.

### 3. The Hundred Hands Bout

In a summoning chamber that called forth one hundred challengers, Togi bet he could defeat all of them **without breaking his scabbard strap**.  
Two hours of carnage later, the strap held.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### Kishou no Kaze — _“Wind of the Last Bet”_

A pearl-white katana with an obsidian guard. The blade sings during wagered duels, and on rare occasions **parries before Togi himself reacts**.  
Suspected ancestral pact. Unverified.

### The Coin of Crimson Odds

A hellmarked copper coin earned from a demon duel. Once per delve, allows Togi (or his foe) to **reroll fate**—for better or worse.

### Dice of the Forgotten Game

Ivory dice carved with runes from a lost gambling rite. When cast before combat, they alter the battlefield theatrically—ghost applause, drifting fog, or subtle shifts of fortune.

Togi claims they are “for ambiance.”

## VIII. Rumors & Anomalies

- His shadow occasionally adopts **a second stance**.

- **Kishou no Kaze** has never rusted—even after full acid submersion.

- Togi once lost a cursed relic in a bet—only to find it embedded in an enemy on his next delve.

- He allegedly bet his final coin against a **god’s avatar** and survived.  
    The god has not yet collected.

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Togi may wager his own safety; a mission contract must state that he cannot stake a companion’s life, wages, or return passage. His presence attracts escalating spectator bets. A handler who can close a book and end a broadcast may be as valuable to the party as another blade.
