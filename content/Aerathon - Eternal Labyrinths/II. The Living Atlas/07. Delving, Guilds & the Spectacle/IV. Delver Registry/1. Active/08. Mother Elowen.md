# Mother Elowen

## I. Overview

Mother Elowen of the Pale Hour is a human priestess of Ilmater and an independent S-Rank delver. She has refused ecclesiastical advancement and repeated offers of sainthood. Her reputation rests on entering places where suffering has become routine and treating those abandoned there as people still owed care.

Her history also includes executions for cruelty, betrayal, and forbidden pacts. Admirers describe these as final mercy; the record does not turn admiration into a general license to kill. Her work with several major guilds has never made her subordinate to one.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** Unaligned; has operated with **all four major guilds** yet bound herself to none

- **Religious Allegiance:** Devotee of **Ilmater**, with mercy-pact ties to **Chauntea** and **Selûne**

- **Known Titles:** _The Pale Matron_, _White Flame of Griefspire_, _Shepherd of the Last Door_

- **Livecast Popularity Index:** **5.0 / 5** — Beloved across Aerathon; considered a miracle-worker and omen of hope

## III. Ideals

- **Endurance Is Holiness** — Power is bearing pain without passing it on.

- **Protect the Lightless** — The weak, cursed, and fallen belong under her shield.

- **Grace With a Blade** — Mercy demands resolve; redemption sometimes requires steel.

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Severing of Ashlock Chain

A soul-trap Labyrinth that forced the dying to relive agony in an endless loop.  
Elowen walked calmly to its core and performed an Ilmatari rite known to only **two living priests**.  
Hundreds of trapped souls whispered her name for days after death.

### 2. The Mercyblade Trial

A delver accused of murdering his team was judged by Elowen herself.  
She descended with him into the very Labyrinth he betrayed them in.  
Only she returned—blade unstained.  
_“He chose the last truth,”_ she said. _“I only witnessed it.”_

### 3. The Quieting of the Crimson Choir

A cursed chorus of undead bards held a village hostage with grief-song.  
Elowen knelt for an hour at dawn, sang a single line, and the Choir dissolved to dust—  
released from torment they no longer remembered.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### The Mercyblade

A modest longsword wrapped in prayer-cloth.

- Strikes incorporeal beings

- Unbinds magical shackles with a whisper

- Glows **pale blue** near the irredeemable

### The Shroud of Pale Hours

A shawl that constantly weeps soft, clean water.

- Cleanses wounds & corruption

- Awakens courage

- Blinds the damned when unfurled in judgment

### Prayerbeads of the Martyr’s Path

Each bead contains the final prayer of someone she saved—or failed to save.

- Grants their insight, memories, and final strength

- Never grows heavier, though it should

## VIII. Rumors & Anomalies

- Said to be immune to fire—yet **screams softly in her sleep** remembering it

- Divine scryers attempting to view her future see only a **wall of white petals**

- Believed to have died once, returning bearing a piece of **Ilmater’s burden**

- Once healed a dying Reaver with a touch—breaking every bone in her own arm in the process

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Elowen insists that recovered people receive names in the report before recovered objects receive prices. She accepts practical help with shelter, transport, and aftercare, even when she refuses an armed escort. Her certainty in a moment of judgment can put her at odds with investigators who still need testimony.

## Frostguarde Attribution

## Northern Response

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/III. Monthly Ledger/04. Headlines & Rumors/02. Gonlev Survivor Bulletin|Gonlev Survivor Bulletin]] records Elowen as still engaged with the northern crisis alongside Sister Judiith. Neither is counted among the fourteen people who departed Gonlev aboard the ghost train. The report supplies no exact position or condition, and does not establish her route or the interval between the Frostguarde attribution and the Gonlev incident.
