# Jon Delver

## I. Overview

Jonathan A. Delver is an S-Rank operations technician of the Free Company, seconded to the Ministry’s safety office. His Baseline Conformance Field is associated with extraordinary changes: hazardous passages acquire workable service routes, mechanisms become legible, and unsafe spaces develop features resembling ordinary workplace protections. Jon describes this as doing the job properly.

The archive records the effect without accepting his explanation as its cause. He is reliable, punctual, and uncomfortable with interviews that ask him to declare himself a miracle. Engineers value the alterations; they also need to know what remains after he leaves.

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Eight-Hour Clear of the Nameless Annex

The notoriously shifting Annex straightened into a uniform loop under Jon’s steady pace.  
He clocked in at **07:55**, labeled all hazards with chalk, escorted civilians out in single file, and clocked out at **16:01**.

Final report:

> _“Nothing unusual to note.”_

### 2. The Compliance of the Basilisk Foundry

A petrification-plagued forge resumed production after Jon posted a **laminated ‘Eye Protection Required’ sign**, handed out smoked goggles, and read the manual **aloud**.  
The basilisks queued. They followed angles. Output met quota by Thursday.

### 3. The Quieting of the Wanehollow Stair

A despair-eating haunted stairwell repainted itself in Ministry yellow once Jon opened a tin of approved color.  
Risers brightened. Moans softened to OSHA hum.  
A ghost foreman signed off the site.  
Jon swept the landings.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### Hi-Vis Tabard of Ordinary Authority

A reflective vest embroidered “Operations.”  
Hostile entities must succeed on a Wisdom save or obey a **reasonable, safe instruction** (_“Step behind the line,” “Single file,” “No running”_).

### Punchcard of Adherence

A brass-edged timecard that **stamps reality** into compliance.  
When Jon “punches in,” nearby hazards seek **posted procedure**:

- fires look for extinguishers

- puzzles surface plain-language instructions

- doors spontaneously locate hinges

### Thermos of Lukewarm

A battered metal thermos of perpetually tepid tea.  
Drinking grants:

- **immunity to panic, fear, and charm** for 10 minutes

- **advantage on concentration checks** to maintain mundane tasks  
    Thermos refills itself on first break.

### Tape of the True Line

A chalk-line reel and tape measure.  
Snapping it creates a **30-foot safe lane** that suppresses:

- illusion drift

- random floor movement

- slippery surfaces

Monsters find it **awkward** to cross.  
Supervisors find it **delightful**.

## VIII. Rumors & Anomalies

- The **Baseline Conformance Field** peaks at **08:00, 12:00, and 16:00**, during which bosses may lose legendary actions if they violate posted safety.

- Jon once asked a planar rift to “wait a moment.” The rift **waited**.

- Magical artifacts become **mundane tools** in his hands: sunblades as shop lights, portable holes labeled “Storage.”

- Cats and constructs both follow him briefly before wandering off—apparently “fixed.”

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Jon documents the route as found and the route as left. Teams must not assume a handrail or instruction sheet will remain after a floor shifts. He is currently seeking a maintenance crew willing to record failures as carefully as successes, a less attractive offer to sponsors than another effortless-looking clear.
