# Sable Rook

## I. Overview

Sable Rook, Warden of Borrowed Tomorrows, is a Free Company auditor and contracted Ministry counsel. His former work in Louvain’s courts gave him the habits of precise reading and careful attribution that now govern his temporal magic. He can defer an injury, place a hazard under a stay, or move a burden into a prepared magical account.

The word “defer” is essential. Rook’s work creates time in which another solution can be found; it is not proof that consequences disappear. His courtesy persists even when he is explaining to a patron why a promised rescue cannot be charged to someone who never consented.

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Stay of Execution at Portico Gate

A collapsing stair-helix threatened to pulverize a refugee column.  
Rook **escrowed the kinetic force** into a signed iron chit nailed to the wall.  
Later, he collected the debt by redirecting a marrow-wyrm into the marked masonry.  
One chit, two solutions.

### 2. The Betemere Annuity

A cult primed a slow-burn blight to blossom at first snow.  
Rook **amortized the entire curse** into a hundred harmless nuisances—a stubbed toe, a cracked plate, a jammed drawer.  
The blight dissolved.  
Then he found the cult’s treasurer and made them sign a confession in diluted self-blood.  
Legally binding, mildly embarrassing.

### 3. The Masque of Mirrors in Louvain

Rival delvers deployed lethal glamours.  
Rook **audited the illusions**, requiring each duplicate to “pay” a tithe in heartbeats and breath.  
The fakes collapsed under debt; offenders arrested without a single casualty.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### Ledger of Atonement

A black-bound codex that records **injuries, debts, and bargains** in luminous script.

- Defer damage into escrow

- Reassign wounds to the liable party

- Refinance curses into harmless installments  
    Burning a page results in a cough, a fine, and another entry.

### Gavel-Hourglass

A magistrate’s mallet wrapped around a double-ended sandglass.

- **Invert:** 6-second stay on any hazard just triggered

- **Strike:** finalize the stay—either nullify at cost, or **redirect** the effect to a pre-placed sigil  
    Makes “time management” terrifyingly literal.

### Basilisk-Skin Retainer’s Cloak

Courtly cut; stony weight.

- Advantage on concentration vs coercion or glamours

- Once per rest, **stone-liquidate** a nonliving object into an escrow chit, re-manifesting on command

## VIII. Rumors & Anomalies

- Carries a **silver quill** that writes only **events that will occur**—never lies.

- Claims to have once **refinanced a prophecy**, converting a foretold death into “severe inconvenience at dawn.”

- He and **Lt. Salvia Korrin** exchange sealed letters marked _“For When I’m Wrong.”_ Neither has ever opened theirs.

- Allegedly owes a favor to the **Wanehollow itself**; once a year, he disappears into a courthouse that isn’t there come morning.

---

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Every stay needs an ending and a named way to discharge its cost. Teams working with Rook must preserve his prepared sigils and report changes to the situation instead of assuming the original terms still apply. He takes particular interest in debts sold without the debtor’s knowledge.
