# Ivara Sen

## I. The Weightkeeper

Ivara Sen is a human S-Rank delver who began as a quarry surveyor. Her reputation was made through recoveries in which everyone could see the danger but no one could reach it: a chamber ceiling hanging over a trapped crew, a bridge folding toward a crowded landing, a relic lodged in the support that kept a refuge standing.

She speaks softly because her crews need to hear the stone. She keeps broken survey pencils in a tin and lends good ones without asking for them back. Her patience with apprentices is considerable; her patience with an employer who calls a worker replaceable is not.

## II. A Burden Must Go Somewhere

Her working links a load to prepared receiving stones. It can carry an extraordinary weight across that linkage, but it does not abolish the weight or create a sound foundation. Every receiving point needs survey, preparation, and room for failure. If the stone beneath a receiver gives way, the transferred burden returns along the connection.

Ivara works with a set of numbered ceramic gauges. Their cracks record unequal strain and tell her when to stop adding load. Replacing a broken gauge without correcting the foundation conceals danger; it does not repair the working. Her strength is greatest when an ordinary crew has had time to prepare the ground.

## III. Work and Responsibility

She contracts independently and advises the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/III. Guilds & Charters/1. Guild Registry/04. Verdant Collision|Verdant Collision]] on recoveries where excavation threatens a living site. She refuses a permanent office because she wants the freedom to reject the next job. The guild values that independence until it disrupts a promised schedule.

Her defining failure occurred during a warehouse recovery: she saved the people beneath a loaded roof but ruined adjoining foundations used as receivers. No one died. Several households lost their workshops. She still contributes to the repairs, resisting patrons who would prefer the story end with the rescue.

## IV. Present Commission

A salvage consortium offers to purchase her receiver pattern outright. Ivara wants independent inspection and compensation for surrounding households written into its use. The consortium wants to call those costs someone else’s problem. She needs a survey team willing to measure the neighboring structures before the negotiations are declared complete.

Her level-20 capability establishes S-Rank; it supplies neither ownership of a worksite nor authority to spend another community’s land.
