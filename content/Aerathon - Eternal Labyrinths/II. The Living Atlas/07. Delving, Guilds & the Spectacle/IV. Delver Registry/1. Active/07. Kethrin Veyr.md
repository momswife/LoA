# Kethrin Veyr

## I. Overview

Kethrin “Brookblade” Veyr serves the Free Company in reconnaissance and recovery and regularly assists Ministry containment teams. She reads water through seepage, condensation, pressure, and rhythm. That knowledge allows her to find passages and failure points that a dry map misses.

Her hydrokinetic work can turn a lethal chamber into a controlled watercourse. Crews admire the calm appearance of her interventions, but the water must still go somewhere. Her sea bargains with Umberlee and quieter devotion to Eldath reflect the difference between surviving a force and making peace with it.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** S-Rank Recon & Recovery, **Free Company of Louvain**; regularly seconded to **MDO — Arcane Breach (Containment)**

- **Religious Allegiance:** **Umberlee** (at sea, via bargains); **Eldath** (ashore, via mercy and quiet flow)

- **Known Titles:** _The Spillwright_, _Eel-Cloak_, _Lockmaster of Sluices_, _River’s Auditor_

- **Livecast Popularity Index:** **4.6 / 5** — Cult favorite among delvers, engineers, and catastrophe-response crews; widely described as “mesmerizing and oddly cozy”

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Cofferdam of Monster Meadows

A corridor that flooded at random stabilized into a calm trench under Kethrin’s hand. She impounded the surge into a hovering **water cube**, walked civilians through, then poured the mass back—**washing an entire mimic swarm** into a drain.  
Post-op note: “Needed a broom.”

### 2. The Compliance of the Ooze Choir

A pit of sentient slimes lured crews with mimicked screams. Kethrin salted the rim, set metronome taps using dripping sconces, and trained the choir to sing a **four-count work bell**.  
The pit now acts as a warning system and morale booster. Screams discontinued.

### 3. The Ferry at the Wanehollow Drop

Where a rope bridge failed nightly, she established a **rope-and-water funicular**—buckets rising as others sank, counterweighted by Labyrinth seepage. An entire expedition (and a stubborn mule) crossed safely.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### Spillway Gauntlet _(very rare)_

Clockwork bracer with bronze fins.

- Redirect liquids/fog/ooze in a **30-ft line or 15-ft cone**

- **3 charges/day:** Impound a **hovering 10-ft water cube** for 1 minute

- Creatures in redirected flow must save or **slide 10 ft**

### Reel-Sword of Brooksteel _(rare)_

A ribbon-blade that coils into a spool.

- **Finesse, reach 10–20 ft**

- On hit near liquids: target must save or be **pulled 5–10 ft** along the current

- Retracts with a satisfying engineering click

### Eel-Skin Half-Cloak

Water- and heat-shedding hide.

- Advantage vs. wet or cold exhaustion

- Whisper a rumor into the hem; if it **“holds water,”** she feels a tug toward a related clue during the next delve

### Tide-Log & Hygrometer

- Records **humidity gradients** as contour lines

- Advantage to locate hidden **sluices, drains, voids**

- Hygrometer clicks faster near damp-sealed doors

### Salt & Chalk Kit

Snapping a **salted chalk-line** creates a **30-ft dry lane** suppressing minor ooze adhesion and illusion drift for 1 minute.  
Slimes hate it. Quartermasters love it.

## VIII. Rumors & Anomalies

- She once **parted an acid river by breathing in**, holding a corridor clear for the length of a single song.

- Rival crews claim her gauntlet can **bottle fog into stairs**; another says she taught a gelatinous cube to act as an elevator… and it **tipped her after**.

- River-spirit **Mirael** records three unfulfilled favors in her debt ledger; one involves **turning a powder-barrel magazine into rain** without igniting a single fuse.

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

Kethrin marks receiving basins before opening a sluice. She disputes salvage agreements that protect a valuable relic while allowing polluted drainage to reach an inhabited bank. A good supporting crew can read her marks, secure the return route, and tell her when a supposedly empty basin is no longer empty.
