# Sister Judiith

## I. Overview

Sister Judiith is a former Crimson Reaver now operating autonomously. Her field record identifies the martial tradition of a zealot; her endurance, healing touch, and movement through cursed ground have produced competing religious interpretations. The archive does not identify her patron from those claims.

She does not speak. She communicates through gestures, marks, and decisions that companions learn to read. Silence is neither consent nor an absence of judgment. The survivors associated with her appearances explain her public standing more reliably than the competing accounts of her origin.

## II. Affiliations

- **Guild Status:** Former **Crimson Reaver**, now autonomous; answers only to the **Divine Oculi** when summoned

- **Religious Allegiance:** Believed to channel **Ilmater**, **Tempus**, or an unnamed martyr-god

- **Known Titles:** _The Silent Flame_, _Saint of Ash_, _Burning Sister_, _Voice of the Last Mercy_

- **Livecast Popularity Index:** **4.9 / 5** — Audiences fall silent when she appears; her broadcasts often end with candle vigils

---

## III. Ideals

- **Suffering Is a Promise** – Pain shared is a chain that binds the righteous.

- **Justice Is Swift, Not Loud** – Truth needs neither witnesses nor applause.

- **Let the Fire Speak** – Words decay; deeds endure.

## IV. Notable Accomplishments

### 1. The Ashen Benediction

When a Labyrinth manifested inside a political-prison monastery, Judiith entered alone.  
Thirty-six hostiles fell.  
Forty-three prisoners emerged alive, many carried in her arms.  
Once the last was free, she nodded once—and the monastery immolated behind her.

### 2. The Silence at Hollowmarch

A beastfolk village besieged by relic-thieves held the line until dawn… when Judiith arrived.  
She advanced without signal or speech.  
The attackers’ will broke.  
She marked each living thief with ash across the mouth—a reminder, not a sentence.

### 3. The Martyr’s Gate

A condemned delver, cursed and awaiting execution, knelt beneath the blade.  
Judiith stepped forward, placed her hand on his chest, and the curse transferred to her—setting her robes ablaze.  
She survived.  
He walked free.  
The executioner resigned that night.

## V. Equipment & Artifacts

### Brand of the Sainted Chain

An iron flail wrapped in oil-soaked cloth strips.

- Emits radiant fire when defending the innocent

- Glows faintly in the presence of lies

- Burns hotter the more wounded she becomes

### Veil of Unspoken Light

A scorched shawl woven from martyr-ash scripture.

- Immune to scrying or compulsion

- Obscures mind-reading and divine probing

- Soft luminescence follows her steps

### Votive Emberbrands

Ritual sigils carved into her arms and palms—one for every life saved.  
Some dim. Some blaze gold.  
A few, newly carved, smolder with unspent miracles.

## VII. Known Sayings

**No direct quotes exist.**

Her **nonverbal rites** are widely known:

- **Hand over heart before battle:** _“No one dies alone.”_

- **Kneeling beside a fallen foe:** _“Your sins end here.”_

- **Lighting a candle with no flame:** _“This road continues.”_

## VIII. Rumors & Anomalies

- No spell, prayer, or telepathy has ever detected **language** in her mind.

- Her blood reportedly ignites on contact with air—yet heals others it touches.

- Some monks believe she **died as a child** and was returned as a vessel for a voiceless god.

- She is said to require no food or water—only the light of a shrine to sustain her.

---

## IX. Present Assessment & Field Practice

A supporting crew agrees on signals before entering danger and appoints someone to relay them. Judiith dislikes having her actions turned into sermons for a doctrine she has not endorsed. Her former Reaver ties make some rescued people reluctant to trust her; she allows them to choose another escort.

## Northern Response

Gonlev Survivor Bulletin|Gonlev Survivor Bulletin]] records Judiith as still engaged with the northern crisis alongside Mother Elowen. Neither is counted among the fourteen people who departed Gonlev aboard the ghost train. The report supplies no exact position, condition, or completed outcome for their work.
