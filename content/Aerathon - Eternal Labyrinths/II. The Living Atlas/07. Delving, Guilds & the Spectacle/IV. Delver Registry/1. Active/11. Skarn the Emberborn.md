# Skarn the Emberborn

## I. The Breaker of Irons

Skarn is an ursine Beastfolk and an independent S-Rank delver. He accepts work involving captive people, coercive relics, and passages that cannot be opened without bringing the structure down on those inside. He does not offer allegiance as part of the contract. Nor does he accept ownership of the people he frees.

His public manner is deliberate rather than theatrical. Before a rescue he asks how many people are inside, who counted them, and where they can sleep afterward. That last question has ended more negotiations than disputes over payment. He knows what it means to open a door onto another kind of captivity.

## II. Chainscour and the Surviving Record

The earliest accounts place him in the final year of the Chainscour Revolts. Records of Varnel’s east arena describe an enslaved gladiator surviving thirty-nine bouts before disappearing on the night the arena burned. Whether every bout belongs to Skarn remains disputed; he confirms the captivity and refuses public reenactments of it.

The burning of Chainscour Hall released seventy indentured delvers. The tribunal called it destruction of a prison; those released called it rescue. Skarn has never treated the disagreement as settled by his strength. He will appear before an inquiry that admits the freed people as witnesses rather than property claimants.

The Crimson Reavers once hunted him. At Shale-Tooth Crag, surviving echo-crystal shows him defeating seven bounty-hunters without a recorded killing. The Brimvault account is less secure: its silence after his entry is recorded, while the core later found in a village well and its alleged word, “Freedom,” remain an incomplete chain of evidence.

## III. Ashmaul and the Cost of Release

**Ashmaul**, called the Flame-Twin Hammer, is made of firebone and smoke-tempered iron. Skarn uses it to expose the points at which a binding holds: an iron fastening, a ward’s anchor, or the prepared focus of a coercive spell. Its heat reveals those stresses; a strike can break them.

The release is not free. Skarn takes a share of the binding’s recoil through the **Emberbrand** on his chest. His scars darken and his hands lose steadiness after repeated work. He must choose an order of release, cool the hammer, and allow companions to finish tasks he could perform alone when rested. A bond he cannot identify cannot safely be broken by anger.

The **Mantle of the Burnt Howl** supports resistance to compulsion. Stories that it forces every slaver to confess have acquired a life of their own; its response is not accepted as testimony or an infallible test of guilt. Neither Skarn nor his equipment can decide what freedom must mean for someone else.

## IV. Faith, Companions, and Boundaries

Observers associate his endurance with Tempus and Ilmater. Skarn denies being any god’s chosen instrument. The claim that he is a fallen fire-god is folklore. His S-Rank recognizes level-20 mortal adventuring capability, not divine origin.

He prefers small crews who can carry injured people, secure unstable ground, and keep a return route open. He can be a patient instructor in those tasks. He is much less patient with benefactors who offer rescue in exchange for service, worship, or exclusive rights to a survivor’s story.

## V. Present Work

Skarn is seeking independent witnesses for recovered indenture records whose owners claim that their destruction would release the debtors. Some debtors want the documents preserved as evidence of abuse. He can break a seal; he cannot make that choice for them. A reliable courier or patient interviewer can therefore help him in ways that another powerful combatant cannot.

Older dossiers credited six S-rated expedition clears and more than 120 descents, many unscored because of flame interference. These remain legacy expedition totals, not site tiers. New assignments use the current mission standard.

> “A door is only freedom if you may choose where to go next.”
