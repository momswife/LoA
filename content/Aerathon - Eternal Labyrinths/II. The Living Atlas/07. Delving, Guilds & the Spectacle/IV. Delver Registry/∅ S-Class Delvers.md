# S-Rank Delvers

This registry gathers the Living Atlas profiles of notable delvers recognized at S-Rank. The folders beneath it record each delver's present registry status—**Active**, **Lost**, or **Retired**—rather than a separate rank or judgment of merit.

Classification and performance are maintained through the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/I. Orientation, Licensing & Classification/04. Unified Delving Merit Index|Unified Delving Merit Index]]. For the roster itself, return to the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/IV. Delver Registry/index|Delver Registry]].


S-Rank corresponds to level 20 in campaign terms. These are near god-like mortal adventurers, with individual limits and obligations. Most working delvers belong to D-, C-, and B-Rank; this registry deliberately concentrates on exceptional figures. Lost and retired records preserve the highest documented rank, without claiming present fitness or survival.
