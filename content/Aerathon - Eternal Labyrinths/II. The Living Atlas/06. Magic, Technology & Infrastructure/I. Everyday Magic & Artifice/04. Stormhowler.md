# Stormhowler

## The weapon and its bearer

Stormhowler is the titanic greataxe recovered by [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/IV. Delver Registry/2. Lost/01. Arthur 'Cap' Penjamin|Arthur 'Cap' Penjamin]] in the Breakerspine Labyrinth. Its fame rests on two inseparable records: the destructive reach it gave a celebrated protector, and the gradual failure of the restraint that had made him trusted. It remains missing following his final delve at Stormhowler's Bastion.

The surviving public account does not establish its present condition or custody. Claims of recovery require physical examination and a documented chain of possession; an impressive thunderclap is not sufficient authentication.

## Material and attributed origin

The blade is described as storm-forged adamantine, carrying blue-white lightning along its edge. Elemental runes brighten as storms approach. Its haft is petrified wood attributed to the Worldroot, a tree said to stand at the heart of the Elemental Plane of Air. That origin survives as a tradition attached to the weapon. No authenticated route to the tree accompanies the record.

Witnesses describe a low thunderous hum before a committed swing, followed by a gust strong enough to disturb clothing and loose grit beyond the blade's path. Such observations help distinguish the weapon from an ordinary lightning enchantment, but they do not identify its maker or establish a reproducible forging method.

## The intelligence within

Stormhowler held a powerful storm genie. Early descriptions of a semi-sentient lightning spirit underestimated the occupant: its whispers urged action, offered combat judgments, and pressed against its bearer's restraint. Its true name and the circumstances of its original binding remain unestablished in public scholarship.

The genie belongs in the comparative study of [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/08. Bestiary & Field Phenomena/II. Elementals & Weather-Born/06. Djinn, Bargains, and Bound Intelligences|djinn and bound intelligences]]. Similar weather effects do not make it a Stormwraith, nor do stories of bargains make it a Glassfire Djinn. The recorded weapon supplied formidable elemental power; it supplied no demonstrated general power to grant wishes.

## Power and price

Strikes carried lightning, concussive thunder, and violent gusts. The axe also shielded its attuned bearer against lightning and thunder. These protections did not extend to judgment. Cap could ordinarily contain the occupant's pressure during his earlier use, but that control required continuing effort and eventually failed.

The central danger was not a price paid once at attunement. Repeated success made accepting the weapon's counsel easier, while the urgency of battle made another test of restraint seem reasonable. The record supports behavioral displacement; it does not establish that every wielder would follow an identical course.

## The Bastion record

Cap used Stormhowler against a living Labyrinth core that fed on delver fear. He destroyed the core and was overtaken by the axe. His companions survived by stopping him; the interrupted scry-feed leaves parts of the final sequence unresolved. Neither his earlier rescues nor his last victory erases the harm of that loss of control.

The case informs [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/06. Magic, Technology & Infrastructure/I. Everyday Magic & Artifice/05. Bound Intelligences and Relic Custody|relic custody practice]]: a bearer's reputation cannot replace independent observation, and a contained intelligence cannot be understood solely by measuring the force it produces.
