# The Cryostudies Annex

> [!warning] Historical record
> Frostguarde and its approaches remain under the northern plague cordon. This account does not establish present services, safe access, or surviving inhabitants. See [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/4. Nattefrost/1. Settlements/01. Frostguarde City|the city’s records status]].

## I. Northern Research at Working Scale

The Cryostudies Annex belonged to Frostguarde’s research setting: a place where irregular cold, temporal behavior, ley currents, and electrical readings could be studied through equipment rather than explained entirely through rumor.

The associated workshop account describes copper coils, a suspended stormglass lattice, adjustable instruments, and grounding rods. Lightning was contained and measured; an escaped filament was taken into a grounding rod. These details establish practical means of working with dangerous effects, not an unlimited source of power.

## II. Readings and Interpretation

Pressure fluctuations and bent ley currents appeared beneath a clear northern sky. Stormglass responded to the city’s bells. Assistant **Kara** reported abnormal readings shortly before the Northcall, while **Boe the Stormbearer** worked with an instrumented suit of armor.

Boe’s language of storms being remembered belongs to a research interpretation. The record does not establish that all Nattefrost storms are stored events, that they can be awakened safely, or that the observed readings caused the assault. A correlation with a bell or warning horn is not yet an explanation.

No complete staff register, surviving instrument inventory, or present research program is established. The Annex’s place in Frostguarde’s history is the effort to make careful observations near conditions that resisted ordinary expectations.
