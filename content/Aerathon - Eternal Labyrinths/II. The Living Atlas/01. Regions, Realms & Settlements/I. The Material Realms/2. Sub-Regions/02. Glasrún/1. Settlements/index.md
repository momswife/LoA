# Settlements of Glasrún

Glasrún has no central settlement authority. Its named cities share the forest's traditions of local responsibility while serving different geographic and civic functions.

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/II. Allemance/1. Settlements/03. Barraille|Barraille]] — a cliffbound mining city where forest gives way to mountain stone.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/II. Allemance/1. Settlements/05. Daltiarna|Daltiarna]] — the largest Glasrúni settlement and the forest's unofficial civic center.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/II. Allemance/1. Settlements/06. Dermad|Dermad]] — a farming city on Glasrún's fertile coastal plains.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/II. Allemance/1. Settlements/07. Domeall|Domeall]] — the northern port and de facto center of Glasrún's foreign affairs.

The many smaller hearth-villages, route houses, and seasonal meeting clearings remain part of the subregion's settlement pattern without being assigned unsupported names.
