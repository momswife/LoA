# Settlements of the Beylik

Because “Beylik” is used both politically and geographically in current records, this shelf lists authenticated civic relationships without claiming that every settlement lies inside a single settled boundary.

## Confirmed Seats & State Cities

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Settlements/01. Tilkisan (Capital)|Tilkisan]] — the administrative heart and recognized capital of the Beylik.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Settlements/03. Far'soro|Far'soro]] — a Beylik State City at the meeting of Bat'yan, Causeway, harbor, and caravan routes.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Settlements/04. Fort Tawiran|Fort Tawiran]] — a Causeway entry fortress governed directly under Beylik authority.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Settlements/07. Kazmak ve Küzmek|Kazmak ve Küzmek]] — a caravan city governed directly under Beylik authority.
