# Geography & Landmarks of the Beylik

The map confirms Beylik as an Arnerian subregional name, while settlement records also use it for state authority. The archive has not yet authenticated one boundary that reconciles those two meanings.

## Confirmed Orientation

- Tilkisan anchors the administrative center.
- Far'soro stands where Bat'yan lowlands, inland caravans, harbor traffic, and Beylik authority meet.
- Fort Tawiran controls a Causeway approach.
- Kazmak ve Küzmek connects the southern Beylik Causeway to northern mining routes through the Kazmak Gem Road.
