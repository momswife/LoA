# Houses of Oria

### _“The House endures. The Seat must be earned.”_

The **Houses of Oria** are not one continuous geographic territory. They are the recognized lodge-cities through which [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/IV. Oria/∅ Oria|Oria]] organizes civic authority, defense, infrastructure, learning, and inter-city obligation.

## II. Civic Distribution

Seven Houses currently maintain dedicated entries: Edelstann, Frostguarde, Heltenlave, Jegervalt, Podgorod, Priozhevsky, and Severport. Their offices differ because their public functions differ; a naval House, tunnel House, forge House, and scholarly House are not expected to govern in the same manner.

## III. Confederation Without Capital

No House holds permanent supremacy. Passage rights, winter supply, defense, and hazardous transit are coordinated through temporary accords and functional necessity. When a crisis makes one House the necessary leader, rival Houses are expected to obey until the shared task is complete.

## IV. Authority & Present Conditions

The House is permanent, but stewardship can fail. Competence challenges, term limits, engineering review, emergency command, curatorial oversight, and winter naval authority are local expressions of the same Oric expectation: a Seat is carried only while it protects the city whose name it bears.

> [!warning] Frostguarde's structure is archival. The city remains inside the northern plague cordon, and no current civic authority there has been authenticated.
