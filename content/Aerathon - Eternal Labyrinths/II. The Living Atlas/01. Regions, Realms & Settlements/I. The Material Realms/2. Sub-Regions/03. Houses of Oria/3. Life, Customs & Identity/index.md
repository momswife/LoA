# Life, Customs & Identity among the Houses of Oria

The central distinction of Oric civic life is simple: **the House is permanent; the Seat is conditional**. A House is the lodge-city, chartered institution, public obligation, and continuity of service. A steward family may carry its Seat, but does not own the House.

## Authority in Practice

- Edelstann joins lodge authority to batko representation and naval command.
- Frostguarde's last verified system divided civic and containment authority between its House and the MDO, while limiting the Warden-Regent to ten years.
- Heltenlave permits formal public challenges to a leader's competence.
- Jegervalt centralizes emergency authority without appeal when the mountain passage is threatened.
- Podgorod judges leadership through systems mastery and failure analysis.
- Priozhevsky regulates scholarship, medicine, archives, and outside partnerships through curatorial stewardship.
- Severport expands the civic authority of fleet captains during winter emergencies.

Inter-House rivalry is expected, but recognized leadership during a shared crisis receives immediate obedience. Sabotage after such recognition is treated as dishonor rather than clever politics.
