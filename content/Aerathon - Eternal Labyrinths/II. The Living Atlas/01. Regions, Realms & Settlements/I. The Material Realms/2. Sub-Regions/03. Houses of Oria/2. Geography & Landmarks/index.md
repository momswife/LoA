# Geography & Landmarks of the Houses of Oria

The Houses do not occupy one continuous subregion. They are a distributed constellation of city-states whose authority is rooted in a lodge-city, its approaches, and the function that city must keep operational.

## Geographic Reading

- **Edelstann** and **Severport** derive authority from maritime defense and port survival.
- **Frostguarde** is defined by the northern cordon and its former containment duty.
- **Jegervalt** controls the critical mountain passage entrusted to its gatewardens.
- **Podgorod** is organized through subterranean layers and civic infrastructure.
- **Heltenlave** and **Priozhevsky** bind their surrounding districts through martial and scholastic service respectively.
