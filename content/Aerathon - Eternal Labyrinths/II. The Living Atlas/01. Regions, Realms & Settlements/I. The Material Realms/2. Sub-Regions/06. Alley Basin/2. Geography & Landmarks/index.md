# Geography & Landmarks of Alley Basin

Alley Basin is a confirmed Allemantic subregion whose detailed limits remain under survey.

## Authenticated Site

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/II. Allemance/2. Geography & Landmarks/13. Symphony Canyon|Symphony Canyon]] — the ravine formed where the Basin breaks open south of Mansque and Fort Kingsfang.

At the canyon, the Veyra, Salend, and Dourin rivers converge in a harmonic 3:2:1 flow. Their dedicated river records have not yet been established, so the names remain indexed here without speculative links.
