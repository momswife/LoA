# Settlements of Bat'yan

Bat'yan's communities follow forest, flood, and Causeway access rather than surveyed municipal borders. This register separates settlements clearly placed within the subregion from cities whose importance comes from standing at its edge.

## Within Bat'yan

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Settlements/02. Castaway Point|Castaway Point]] — a coastal settlement identified with the Bat'yan coast.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Settlements/08. Linang|Linang]] — a Causeway ring-region protecting a fertile Bat'yan basin.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Settlements/10. Kal'oro Grove|Kal'oro Grove]] — a mapped rainforest town whose fuller record remains preliminary.

## Threshold Settlement

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Settlements/03. Far'soro|Far'soro]] — the confluence city where Bat'yan lowlands, Causeway traffic, inland caravans, and Beylik authority meet.

Smaller barangays and seasonal clearings remain part of the authenticated settlement pattern, but they are not added as named records until a source establishes their names and locations.
