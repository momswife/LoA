# Geography & Landmarks of Bat'yan

Bat'yan occupies western Arneria, extending inland from the Causeway cities through humid rainforest whose routes shift with rainfall and canopy growth. Its archive is organized around environmental systems rather than fixed districts.

## Maintained Geographic Features

- **Upper Canopy** — interwoven treetops that create elevated shade paths and natural bridges.
- **Rain Veins** — seasonal streams and rivers that redraw travel routes from year to year.
- **Redsoil Clearings** — fertile but fragile ground on which villages can be established.
- **Deep Green Zones** — old-growth areas where little light reaches the forest floor.
- **Causeway Fringe** — the southern approach where road traffic, state authority, and rainforest settlement overlap.

## Sites & Conditions Under Review

Overgrown ruins, seasonally flooded or sealed labyrinth entrances, and places where sound behaves unpredictably are authenticated as a recurring Bat'yan pattern. Individual sites remain unfiled until their positions and public-access risks can be verified.
