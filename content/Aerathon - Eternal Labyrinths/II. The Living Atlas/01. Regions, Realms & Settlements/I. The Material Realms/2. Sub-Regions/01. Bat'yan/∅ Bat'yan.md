# Bat'yan

### _The Living Canopy — “Life Moves Where the Roots Allow.”_

**Bat'yan** is the rainforest subregion of western [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/∅ Arneria|Arneria]], extending inland from the Causeway cities through layered jungle where roads can vanish as quickly as they are cut. It is organized through growth, rainfall, village memory, and the local agreements that keep movement possible.

## II. Setting & Boundaries

Bat'yan is hot, humid, and cyclically wet. Canopy bridges, seasonal waterways, fertile redsoil clearings, and deep old-growth zones make fixed cartography difficult. Its eastern relationship to central Arneria and its southern relationship to the Causeway are understood functionally; precise surveyed borders are less stable than routes maintained by communities.

## III. Historical Character

Repeated attempts to impose permanent estates, roads, and garrisons failed as structures rotted, paths disappeared, and settlers either adapted or withdrew. The communities that endured learned to build for repair, move when the land required it, and treat flexibility as a form of order rather than a failure of government.

## IV. Authority & Present Conditions

Bat'yan has no single subregional government. MDO access, trade, and delving are negotiated community by community. Overgrown ruins, seasonally sealed entrances, and anomalous sound remain survey concerns, while central authorities continue to depend on Bat'yani food, medicines, labor, and route knowledge without exercising uniform control.
