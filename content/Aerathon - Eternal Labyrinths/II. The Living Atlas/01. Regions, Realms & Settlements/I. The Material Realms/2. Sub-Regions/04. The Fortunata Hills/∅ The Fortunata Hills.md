# The Fortunata Hills

### _The Old Stone Between — “What Endures Is What Was Chosen.”_

The **Fortunata Hills** are the rolling highlands of northwestern [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/V. Vinyot/∅ Vinyot|Vinyot]], lying between the low trade plains and the Betemere Mountains. Their ore, coal, stone, and mountain routes make them strategically important; their thin soil and hard climate keep settlement sparse.

## II. Setting & Boundaries

Wind-worn ridges, shallow basins, cold streams, and jagged footholds divide the low plains from the mountains. Summers are short, winters are long and dry, and sudden storms can reopen old workings or isolate routes with little warning.

## III. Historical Character

The Hills drew prospectors, forge communities, and beastfolk refuges because they were too resource-rich to abandon and too difficult to turn into comfortable estates. Their settlements grew by necessity and deliberate choice, producing a regional preference for restraint over expansion.

## IV. Authority & Present Conditions

Malarthain is the principal named city and strongest coordinating authority, but the wider Hills have no unified government. Local stewards, forge-wardens, and elders preserve order. Mine instability, sealed proto-delving sites, difficult winters, and pressure to expand extraction remain the subregion's central public concerns.
