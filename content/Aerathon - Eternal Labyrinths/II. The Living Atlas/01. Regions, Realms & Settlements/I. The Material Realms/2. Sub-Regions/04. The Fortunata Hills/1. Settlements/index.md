# Settlements of the Fortunata Hills

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/V. Vinyot/1. Settlements/07. Malarthain Stronghold|Malarthain Stronghold]] — the Hills' principal named city, forge center, defensive authority, and western Vinyoti gateway.

Beyond Malarthain, the authenticated pattern includes mining towns clustered around individual seams, livestock-focused hill farms, forge communities, and caravan waystations. Their existence is established, but no additional names should enter the register until a source fixes them to the map.
