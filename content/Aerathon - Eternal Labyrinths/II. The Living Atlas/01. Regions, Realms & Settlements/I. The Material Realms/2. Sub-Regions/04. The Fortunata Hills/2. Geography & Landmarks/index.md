# Geography & Landmarks of the Fortunata Hills

The Fortunata Hills form a northwestern Vinyoti bufferland between low trade plains and the Betemere Mountains. Exposed stone, thin soils, and sudden mountain weather make the terrain valuable without making it easy to occupy.

## Maintained Geographic Features

- **Rolling Stone Ridges** — wind-worn hills bearing coal seams and ironstone.
- **Highland Basins** — shallow valleys where limited seasonal farming is possible.
- **Betemere Footholds** — jagged mountain approaches containing old mines and abandoned tunnels.
- **Coldwater Streams** — narrow, fast channels that freeze early in winter.

## Sites & Conditions Under Review

Sealed mines, collapsed proto-delving sites, storm-opened passages, deep vibrations, and valleys associated with persistent sensations of “bad weight” remain active survey concerns. Delving is normally coordinated through Malarthain; independent excavation is discouraged.
