# Malduta Estuary

## I. Cartographic Record

The **Malduta Estuary** is a confirmed named estuarine feature on material Aerathon's map. Its river system, tidal reach, and administrative boundaries remain unverified.

## II. Current Assessment

Future work should record channels, wetlands, settlements, navigation, fisheries, seasonal flooding, and any relationship to the Malduta Bay named in the Tempestrise record.
