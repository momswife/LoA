# Causeway

## I. Cartographic Record

The **Causeway** is a confirmed cross-regional structure and travel corridor. Its western reach lies in Allemance and its eastern reach in Arneria. Current records connect the Arnerian section to [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Settlements/12. Land's End|Land's End]], but do not yet authenticate the structure's construction history or full course.
