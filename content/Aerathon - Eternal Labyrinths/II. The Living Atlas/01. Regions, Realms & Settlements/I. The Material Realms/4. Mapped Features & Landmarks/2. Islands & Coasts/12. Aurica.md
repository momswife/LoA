# Aurica

## I. Cartographic Record

**Aurica** is a confirmed island of Al'Ar and the island named by the current [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/I. Al'Ar/1. Settlements/02. Mjinbou|Mjinbou]] record. This is the only regional relationship presently authenticated here.

## II. Current Assessment

A full island record should establish coastline, interior terrain, other settlements, authority, resources, and the seasonal effects of Mjinbou's civic dispersal.
