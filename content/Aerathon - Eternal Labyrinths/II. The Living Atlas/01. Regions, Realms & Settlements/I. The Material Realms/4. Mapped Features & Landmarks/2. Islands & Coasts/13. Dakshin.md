# Dakshin

## I. Cartographic Record

**Dakshin** is a confirmed named island on the material map. Its jurisdiction, communities, and navigational character remain unverified.

## II. Current Assessment

No inherited description is treated as Aerathon canon. Survey priorities are coastal access, weather exposure, settlement, resource use, and regional affiliation.
