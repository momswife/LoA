# Luck's End

## I. Cartographic Record

**Luck's End** is a confirmed named forest or wildland on the material map of Aerathon. The evocative name is not, by itself, evidence of danger, history, or supernatural influence.

## II. Current Assessment

The Ministry has not authenticated an Aerathon-specific origin for the name. Survey work should establish its borders, local usage, safe approaches, ecology, and whether the designation refers to one forest or a broader tract.
