# Mapped Feature Register

This register tracks the named places shown on the [external reference map](https://app.kanka.io/w/192006/entities/4411181/map) and their treatment in the Aerathon archive.

> [!note] Canon boundary
> The reference map establishes the inventory of names and their visible cartographic classes. Its descriptive prose is not Aerathon canon. A Living Atlas record must rely on Aerathon material or remain explicitly preliminary.

## I. Coverage Audit

The map contains **126 markers representing 120 unique names**. Six repeated markers identify places shown in more than one position or class: Causeway, Glimmerpool, Jegervalt, Kyry, Muraya, and Tempestat.

> [!note] Audit scope
> These figures preserve the initial marker audit. A later civic-layer reconciliation supplied additional settlement names and authoritative subregional placements; those records are maintained through each Greater Region's settlement index rather than retroactively folded into the original marker count.

| Filing result | Unique names |
| --- | ---: |
| Directly matched existing Aerathon records | 56 |
| Matched through retained Aerathon names and new aliases | 10 |
| Added as preliminary Aerathon records | 54 |
| **Total** | **120** |

## III. Preliminary Records

Every settlement is now filed beneath a maintained subregion, with **Greater [Region]** used for records not assigned to a narrower territory. The later civic reconciliation added preliminary records for Granfield, Padian, Lantern Bay, Kavrama, Tarlamak, and Boughport; their names and placements are established while their Aerathon-specific civic lore remains open. Beylik remains in the subregion register, while the Gorki Peaks reside on Oria's regional geography shelf. The cross-regional [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/3. Mapped Features & Landmarks/4. Structures & Sites/05. Causeway|Causeway]] and [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/3. Mapped Features & Landmarks/5. Distinct Features/01. The Astral Shadows|Astral Shadows]] remain centrally filed. Every record remains cross-indexed here by physical class where that classification is still useful. “Preliminary” means the name and map presence are secured while lore-rich treatment still awaits Aerathon-specific evidence.
