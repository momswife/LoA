# Causeway

## I. Cartographic Record

The **Causeway** is a confirmed cross-regional structure and travel corridor. Its western interregional reach lies in Allemance before entering Arneria at [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Subregions/2. Bat'yan/1. Settlements/02. Fort Tawiran|Fort Tawiran]]. Within Arneria, **Bat'yan owns the western section** and **Beylik owns the eastern section**. Current records also connect the corridor to [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Subregions/4. Matarono Range/1. Settlements/01. Duyan Vale|Duyan Vale]] and [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/1. Subregions/6. Beylik/1. Settlements/03. Land's End|Land's End]], but the archive has not yet authenticated the exact ownership boundary, construction history, or full course.
