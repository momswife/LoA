# Greater Allemance

**Greater Allemance** is the archival name for Allemance territory and communities not assigned to a narrower maintained subregion. “Greater” describes filing scope only: it does not make this territory more authentic, important, or politically superior to the named subregions around it.

## II. Setting & Boundaries

The boundary of Greater Allemance is archival rather than a claim that every remaining place forms one uniform landscape. When evidence supports a more precise territorial identity, that place may receive its own subregional filing without changing its membership in Allemance.

## III. Historical or Local Character

This shelf gives settlements outside the narrower named subregions a shared navigational home while leaving their distinct histories and personalities intact. Common regional practices belong in Allemance's life register; genuinely local practice belongs here or in the relevant settlement article.

## IV. Authority & Present Conditions

No new government is created by this label. Jurisdiction follows the maintained Allemance political record and the authority documented for each settlement.
