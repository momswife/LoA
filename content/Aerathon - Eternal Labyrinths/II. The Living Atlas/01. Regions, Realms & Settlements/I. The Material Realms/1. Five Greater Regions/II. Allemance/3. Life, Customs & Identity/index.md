# Life, Customs & Identity of Allemance

This shelf records practices and identities shaped within Allemance. It replaces the misleading idea that Allemance itself is a single people or that every Allemagnian community shares one culture.

## Current Orientation

Allemagnian identity is often expressed through hearth, table, neighborhood, guild, and remembered place. Hospitality, feastcraft, household memory, and mutual aid recur across the region, but their meaning changes substantially between Louvain, the river baronies, border settlements, displaced communities, and Glasrún.

The Table Oath illustrates a recurring principle: provision creates responsibility. Its legal or sacred force depends upon the witnesses and jurisdiction involved. The same caution applies to customs of emergency shelter, which may be protected locally without creating a region-wide right of entry.

## Filing Boundaries

Future records belong here when they concern Allemance's regional and local customs: hearth and table practices, hospitality, household memory, dress, cuisine, festivals, language, and differences among communities. Lineage traditions remain with their peoples, government remains under Governments & Geopolitics, and historical institutions remain in Annals & Antiquities.

## Development Priorities

- Hearth, table, feastcraft, and mutual aid
- The Table Oath and jurisdiction-specific hospitality customs
- Differences among the Hearthlands, baronies, border districts, and Glasrún
- Local expressions of memory, dress, cuisine, and public celebration
