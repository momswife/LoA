# Life, Customs & Identity of Oria

This shelf records forms of belonging and daily practice shaped within Oria. It distinguishes regional patterns from the obligations imposed by a particular House, settlement, occupation, lineage, or faith.

## Current Orientation

House affiliation is often more immediate than Oric regional identity because it determines maintenance, defense, provision, testimony, and care. Movement between Houses is possible, but it entails obligations rather than a simple change of residence.

Residents may identify themselves through birthplace, present service, or House connection. Craft, repair, preparedness, endurance, and competent service are widely honored public standards, while rivalry between Houses is expected to yield to cooperation when a failure threatens more than one jurisdiction.

## Filing Boundaries

Future records belong here when they concern Oric belonging, household practice, service, hospitality, craft prestige, food, clothing, language, commemoration, and relations among Houses. House government remains under Governments & Geopolitics, technical systems remain under Magic, Technology & Infrastructure, and historical development remains in Annals & Antiquities.

## Development Priorities

- House affiliation, residence, birthplace, and service
- Craft, repair, preparedness, and communal trust
- Rivalry, cooperation, and obligations during shared danger
- Local differences among Seats, lodges, ports, and threshold communities
