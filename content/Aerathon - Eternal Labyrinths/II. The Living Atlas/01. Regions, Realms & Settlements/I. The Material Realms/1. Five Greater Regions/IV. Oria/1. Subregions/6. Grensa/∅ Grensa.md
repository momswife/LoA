# Grensa

## I. Cartographic Record

**Grensa** is a confirmed mountain subregion within [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/IV.
