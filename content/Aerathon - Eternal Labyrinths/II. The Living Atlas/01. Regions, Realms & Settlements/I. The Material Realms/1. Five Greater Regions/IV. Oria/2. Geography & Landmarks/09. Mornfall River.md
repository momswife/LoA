# Mornfall River

## I. Cartographic Record

The **Mornfall River** is a confirmed named Oric waterway. Its course and the meaning of its name remain unverified.

## II. Current Assessment

An Aerathon survey should establish source, mouth, crossings, settlements, flood cycle, navigability, jurisdiction, and any safely documented optical or temporal conditions.
