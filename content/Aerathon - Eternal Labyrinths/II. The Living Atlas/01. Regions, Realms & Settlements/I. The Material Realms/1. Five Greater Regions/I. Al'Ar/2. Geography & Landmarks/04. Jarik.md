# Jarik

## I. Cartographic Record

**Jarik** is a confirmed named island. Its northeastern cliffs contain [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/I. Al'Ar/2. Geography & Landmarks/05. The Kasho'ta Caves|the Kasho'ta Caves]], providing the island's first authenticated Aerathon connection.

## II. Current Assessment

The rest of Jarik remains underdescribed. Future work should establish settlements, coastline, governance, ecology, safe approaches, and how the Caves affect local travel.
