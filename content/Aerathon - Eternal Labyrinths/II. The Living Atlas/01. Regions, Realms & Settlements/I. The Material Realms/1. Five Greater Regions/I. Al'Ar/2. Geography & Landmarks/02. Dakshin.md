**Dakshin** is a confirmed named island of Al'Ar. Its political jurisdiction, communities, and navigational character remain unverified.

No inherited description is treated as Aerathon canon. Survey priorities are coastal access, weather exposure, settlement, resource use, and regional affiliation.
