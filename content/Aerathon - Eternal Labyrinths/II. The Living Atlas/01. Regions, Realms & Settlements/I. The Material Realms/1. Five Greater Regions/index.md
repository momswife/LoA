# Five Greater Regions

Each Greater Region has a conceptual overview and three maintained shelves: Settlements; Geography & Landmarks; and Life, Customs & Identity. Regional identity, citizenship, culture, lineage, and political allegiance may overlap, but none should be treated as interchangeable.
