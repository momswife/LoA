# Five Greater Regions

Each Greater Region has a conceptual overview and four maintained shelves: Subregions; Settlements; Geography & Landmarks; and Life, Customs & Identity. Subregions are nested with their parent region so local settlements and identity can be read in geographic context. Regional identity, citizenship, culture, lineage, and political allegiance may overlap, but none should be treated as interchangeable.
