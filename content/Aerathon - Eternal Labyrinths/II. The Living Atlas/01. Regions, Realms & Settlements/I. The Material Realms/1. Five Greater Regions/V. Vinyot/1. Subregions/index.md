# Subregions of Vinyot

Every settlement is filed beneath a subregion so civic records, local identity, and mapped context can be read together. **Greater Vinyot** comes first as the archival home for territory not assigned to a narrower named subregion; “Greater” is a filing scope, not a political rank.
