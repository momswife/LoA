# Life, Customs & Identity in the Fortunata Hills

The Hills favor patience, preparation, and work that can withstand inspection. Their settlements are sparse, their winters long, and their local identities stronger than any single authority outside Malarthain.

## Local Order

- Reputation-selected stewards, forge-wardens, and elders maintain order outside the stronghold.
- Custom and remembered judgment matter at least as much as written law.
- Authority earns respect through consistency, safe labor, and promises kept quietly.

## Daily Life & Worldview

Beastfolk and human households live through mining, stonework, forge labor, animal husbandry, preservation, and caravan service. Faith is ancestral and practical: work done correctly, land treated with restraint, and obligations honored are themselves acts of devotion.

## Livelihood

Coal, iron, mixed ore, defensive stone, fuel, and skilled labor travel outward. Grain and finished goods travel inward. Extraction is deliberately limited by climate, transport, and a widespread resistance to expansion that would exhaust the land or destabilize old workings.
