# Tresoli Islands

## I. Cartographic Record

The **Tresoli Islands** are a confirmed named island group. The shorter name **Tresoli** is retained as an alias, but the archive has not authenticated a regional government or shared island identity.

## II. Current Assessment

A fuller record should establish the chain's members, coastlines, settlements, jurisdiction, trade routes, and relationship to neighboring waters.
