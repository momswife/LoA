## II. Geographic Orientation

Confirmed records place Beylik territorial ownership along the eastern Arnerian Causeway and state authority at Tilkisan and Kazmak ve Küzmek. Beylik institutions also operate at Far'soro and in the Fort Tawiran customs system, but those operations do not make Malduta Bay or Bat'yan part of the Beylik subregion.

## III. Political Character

Current filings associate the Beylik with direct state cities, old rewarded families, entry law, disciplined patrols, and treasury-backed infrastructure. These are political and civic facts, not proof that every community under that authority shares one culture.

## IV. Current Filing Priorities

The next revision must reconcile boundaries, ruling offices, subject settlements, route control, and the term's relationship to Bat'yan and the wider Arnerian government. Until then, this folder separates authenticated relationships from open questions.

## V. Maintained Local Geography

The map confirms Beylik as an Arnerian subregional name, while settlement records also use it for state authority. The archive has not yet authenticated one boundary that reconciles those two meanings.

## Confirmed Orientation

- Tilkisan anchors the administrative center.
- Far'soro stands in Malduta Bay where Bat'yan lowlands, inland caravans, harbor traffic, and Beylik authority meet.
- Fort Tawiran stands on Bat'yan-owned Causeway territory while hosting documented Beylik customs operations.
- Kazmak ve Küzmek connects the eastern Beylik Causeway to northern mining routes through the Kazmak Gem Road.
