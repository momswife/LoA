# Bat'yan

### _The Living Canopy — “Life Moves Where the Roots Allow.”_

**Bat'yan** is the rainforest subregion of western [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. The Material Realms/1. Five Greater Regions/III. Arneria/∅ Arneria|Arneria]], extending inland from the western Causeway through layered jungle where roads can vanish as quickly as they are cut. Bat'yan owns the western Arnerian section of the Causeway, including Fort Tawiran, while its communities organize wider life through growth, rainfall, village memory, and local agreements.

## II. Setting & Boundaries

Bat'yan is hot, humid, and cyclically wet. Canopy bridges, seasonal waterways, fertile redsoil clearings, and deep old-growth zones make fixed cartography difficult. The western Causeway supplies a fixed infrastructural boundary and asset within Bat'yan, while the rainforest borders beyond it remain less stable than routes maintained by communities.

## III. Historical Character

Repeated attempts to impose permanent estates, roads, and garrisons failed as structures rotted, paths disappeared, and settlers either adapted or withdrew. The communities that endured learned to build for repair, move when the land required it, and treat flexibility as a form of order rather than a failure of government.

## IV. Authority & Present Conditions

Bat'yan has no single subregional government. Ownership of the western Causeway is therefore not evidence of one centralized Bat'yani administration: MDO access, trade, delving, and civil authority remain negotiated community by community. Beylik customs operations at Causeway facilities do not transfer title to the road or surrounding territory.

## V. Maintained Local Geography

Bat'yan occupies western Arneria, extending inland from the Causeway cities through humid rainforest whose routes shift with rainfall and canopy growth. Its archive is organized around environmental systems rather than fixed districts.

## Maintained Geographic Features

- **Upper Canopy** — interwoven treetops that create elevated shade paths and natural bridges.
- **Rain Veins** — seasonal streams and rivers that redraw travel routes from year to year.
- **Redsoil Clearings** — fertile but fragile ground on which villages can be established.
- **Deep Green Zones** — old-growth areas where little light reaches the forest floor.
- **Western Causeway** — the Bat'yan-owned approach where road traffic, local territorial authority, Beylik customs operations, and rainforest settlement overlap.

## Sites & Conditions Under Review

Overgrown ruins, seasonally flooded or sealed labyrinth entrances, and places where sound behaves unpredictably are authenticated as a recurring Bat'yan pattern. Individual sites remain unfiled until their positions and public-access risks can be verified.
