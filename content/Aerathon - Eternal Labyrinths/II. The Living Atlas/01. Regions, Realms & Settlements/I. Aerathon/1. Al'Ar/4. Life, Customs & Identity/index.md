# Life, Customs & Identity of Al'Ar

This shelf records ways of living shaped by participation in Al'Ar's maritime region. It does not define an Al'Ari personality, lineage, or uniform island culture.

## Current Records

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/1. Al'Ar/4. Life, Customs & Identity/01. Crew, Harbor & Safe Return|Crew, Harbor & Safe Return]]

## Current Orientation

Al'Ari belonging may be carried through crew, dock, neighborhood, household, and remembered voyage as readily as through permanent residence. Music, movement, body ornament, performance, and public craft are prominent means of memory and reputation, but none is a requirement for individual identity.

Hospitality is shaped by scarcity. Water, shelter, repair labor, and reliable weather knowledge create practical obligations because withholding them can endanger an entire crew. The customs governing those obligations vary by harbor and island.

## Filing Boundaries

Future records belong here when they describe regional or locally situated practices: crew and household belonging, harbor etiquette, public expression, hospitality, voyage customs, and differences among island communities. Lineage traditions remain with their peoples, formal worship remains under Religion & Worship, and historical origins remain in Annals & Antiquities.

## Development Priorities

- Crew, household, and dock affiliation
- Hospitality, scarcity, and obligations of safe return
- Music, movement, adornment, and public craft
- Variation between major harbors and outer islands
