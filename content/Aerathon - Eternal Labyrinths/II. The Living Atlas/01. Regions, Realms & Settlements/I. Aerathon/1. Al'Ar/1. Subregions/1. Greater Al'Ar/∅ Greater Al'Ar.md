# Greater Al'Ar

**Greater Al'Ar** is the archival name for Al'Ar territory and communities not assigned to a narrower maintained subregion. It may also hold a record provisionally when the archive has not resolved whether a place belongs to Al'Ar at all. “Greater” describes filing scope only: it does not make this territory more authentic, important, or politically superior to the named subregions around it.

## II. Setting & Boundaries

The boundary of Greater Al'Ar is archival rather than a claim that every remaining place forms one uniform landscape. When evidence supports a more precise territorial identity, that place may receive its own subregional filing. A provisional record may instead move to another regional register without implying that its earlier placement was once a political fact.

## III. Historical or Local Character

This shelf gives settlements outside the narrower named subregions a shared navigational home while leaving their distinct histories and personalities intact.

## IV. Authority & Present Conditions

No new government is created by this label. Jurisdiction follows the authority documented for each settlement: Thalassar's Tide Concord, Neraval's Refuge Compact, and Vitrassa's Balance Charter are separate civic systems rather than branches of a Greater Al'Ar administration.
