# Settlements of Greater Al'Ar

This register includes authenticated Al'Ari settlements in shared waters and clearly marked provisional records. **Thalassar's placement here is navigational, not a final determination of whether the city belongs to Al'Ar or Vinyot.** Neraval and Vitrassa are authenticated Al'Ari cities whose locations cross the practical reach of neighboring islands.
