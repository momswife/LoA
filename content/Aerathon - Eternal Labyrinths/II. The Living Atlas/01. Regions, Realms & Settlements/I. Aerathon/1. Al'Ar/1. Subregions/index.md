# Subregions of Al'Ar

Every settlement is filed beneath a subregion so civic records, local identity, and mapped context can be read together. **Greater Al'Ar** comes first as the archival home for territory not assigned to a narrower named subregion; “Greater” is a filing scope, not a political rank.
