# Kyry

## Civic Overview

Kyry is a settled town of boatyards, drying racks, lodging houses, and stores serving island households as well as passing crews. A voyage's departure is treated as a practical public fact: which boat left, who traveled aboard it, and when someone should begin asking questions are written on a board near the landing.

## Work & Local Institutions

Musicians and researchers bound for Jarik's resonant caves purchase ordinary supplies here. Their presence supports guides and lodging keepers, but the town's workers do not all earn their living from the Caves. Fishing, hull repair, domestic trade, and carrying people between familiar landings continue when no expedition is in port.

## Current Conditions

Boat owners have recently disagreed over a broker who sells combined passage and cave excursions without making the distinction clear. A berth to Jarik is not permission to enter a hazardous site. The town's landing keepers now ask carriers to separate transport costs from guided work and to identify who is responsible if a passenger fails to return. The broker calls this interference; the families who crew the boats call it overdue.
