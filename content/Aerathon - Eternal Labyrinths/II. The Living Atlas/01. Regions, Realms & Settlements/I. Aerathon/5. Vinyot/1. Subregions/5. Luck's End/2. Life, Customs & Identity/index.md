# Life, Customs & Identity in Luck's End

## Local Practice

Visitors dealing with Verglass are advised to ask how an obligation is remembered before offering coin to discharge it. An object returned, a name retained, or a duty witnessed may matter in a way an outsider’s receipt cannot express. This is a boundary of exchange, not permission to turn remembered hospitality into ownership of a guest.

These practices belong to the communities and occupations described; they are not inherited traits or a rule for every resident.
