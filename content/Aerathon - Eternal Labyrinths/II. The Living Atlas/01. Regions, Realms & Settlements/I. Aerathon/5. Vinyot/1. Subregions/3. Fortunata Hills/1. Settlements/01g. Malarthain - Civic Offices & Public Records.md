# Malarthain — Civic Offices & Public Records

## I. District Overview

These places handle records, representation, identification, and claims. Listing them together does not place the union or every repository under a single authority.

This guide gathers related places across [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/5. Vinyot/1. Subregions/3. Fortunata Hills/1. Each entry retains its local setting; map numbers refer to the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/Explore Locations|Malarthain Stronghold Map]].

## II. Places & Services

### Cardwell Archive

**Map reference:** Marker 2.

The Cardwell Archive occupies a quiet, closely watched wing near Blackcrest Keep. Its narrow halls, iron doors, and carefully controlled reading rooms give the area the subdued air of a place where every page matters.

### Miners’ Union Hall

**Map reference:** Marker 7.

The Miners’ Union Hall anchors a busy corner of Cinderward near several shift roads. Notices, memorial ribbons, wage postings, and family appeals cover its outer boards, making the building one of the district’s most active gathering places.

### The Unclaimed Hall

**Map reference:** Marker 75.

The Unclaimed Hall stands in a quieter civic quarter near Redwatch. Its guarded storerooms, registry desks, and identification chambers receive lost property, unclaimed estates, and the belongings of unknown citizens.

### The Red Thread Office

**Map reference:** Marker 82.

The Red Thread Office keeps its public counters near the civic courts, with deeper record rooms reserved for inheritance disputes and family claims. Petitioners often wait for hours beneath walls covered in lineage charts and legal notices.
