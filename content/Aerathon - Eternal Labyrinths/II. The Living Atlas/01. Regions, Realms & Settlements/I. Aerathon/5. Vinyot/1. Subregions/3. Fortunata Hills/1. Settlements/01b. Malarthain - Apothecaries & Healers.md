# Malarthain — Apothecaries & Healers

## I. District Overview

Care and remedies connect the work streets of Cinderward with the healing courts and gardens of the Green Run. This guide groups those services across their existing neighborhoods.

## II. Places & Services

### Stonebreath Infirmary

**Map reference:** Marker 8.

Stonebreath Infirmary stands between Cinderward and the quieter paths of the Green Run. Its open courtyards and broad intake doors are designed for injured work crews, while nearby streets remain busy with healers, relatives, and supply runners.

### Ashroot Remedies

**Map reference:** Marker 26.

Ashroot Remedies operates from a narrow but crowded shop in Cinderward. The street outside is lined with workers seeking burn salves, breathing draughts, pain tonics, and practical cures that can be purchased without ceremony.

### House of Red Water

**Map reference:** Marker 32.

The House of Red Water occupies a sheltered healing court near the Green Run’s religious paths. Its tiled pools, open treatment rooms, and prayer alcoves serve patients seeking both divine care and traditional medicine.

### Moss & Iron

**Map reference:** Marker 33.

Moss & Iron is tucked among garden walls and small workshops near the Green Run. Bundles of herbs hang beneath its awning beside hammered charms, protective nails, and simple warding pieces.

### The Foxglove Exchange

**Map reference:** Marker 78.

The Foxglove Exchange fills a covered market near the Green Run’s healing quarter. Herbalists, apothecaries, traveling physicians, and household buyers trade remedies beneath rows of painted medicinal signs.

### Stonemarrow Clinic

**Map reference:** Marker 81.

Stonemarrow Clinic occupies a plain charitable building between Cinderward and the Green Run. It serves workers who cannot afford private treatment and is usually crowded with families, apprentices, and recovering laborers.
