# Malarthain — Gardens, Shrines & Remembrance

## I. District Overview

Public greens, medicinal gardens, quiet shrines, and memorial spaces serve different needs across the city. This guide brings them together without treating them as one continuous neighborhood.

## II. Places & Services

### Children’s Green

**Map reference:** Marker 34.

Children’s Green is a broad public field between family neighborhoods and the Green Run. Climbing frames, marked practice circles, shade trees, and low stone walls keep the area lively throughout the day.

### The Black Ribbon

**Map reference:** Marker 37.

The Black Ribbon maintains an elegant mourning house on a quiet street between Goldclasp and the sacred roads. Its windows display tasteful memorial pieces, while private consultation rooms protect grieving families from public attention.

### Brindlewick Conservatory

**Map reference:** Marker 74.

Brindlewick Conservatory rises from the Green Run beneath a broad glass roof. Warm paths wind between medicinal gardens, research beds, public exhibits, and carefully controlled growing chambers.

### The Ivory Steps

**Map reference:** Marker 80.

The Ivory Steps climb a steep hillside path beyond the busiest sacred roads. Small landings, weathered carvings, and distant views of the city make the shrine a quiet destination for solitary pilgrims.

### Chimney Court

**Map reference:** Marker 84.

Chimney Court is a small memorial square in Cinderward surrounded by old worker housing. A soot-dark monument lists those lost in industrial fires, while nearby families still use the court for gatherings and remembrance.

### The Night Orchard

**Map reference:** Marker 85.

The Night Orchard is enclosed behind stone walls near the quieter edge of the Green Run. Its paths open only near dusk, when rare flowering trees and nocturnal fruiting plants are tended beneath low lanterns.
