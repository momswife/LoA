# Malarthain — Inns, Taverns & Social Houses

## I. District Overview

Gathering places range from crowded scrying-night rooms to private dining houses. Each serves a different part of the city’s social life.

## II. Places & Services

### The Daylight Tap

**Map reference:** Marker 14.

The Daylight Tap glows from a sloping street in Stonecrest Valley, its broad windows kept open whenever weather allows. The surrounding lane fills on scrying nights with stools, food carts, loud arguments, and patrons unable to fit inside.

### Whitecoal House

**Map reference:** Marker 15.

Whitecoal House sits along the quieter edge of Goldclasp, away from the district’s noisier shops. Private dining rooms, discreet entrances, and carefully screened balconies make the surrounding block popular with officials and wealthy patrons.

### The Velvet Fang

**Map reference:** Marker 69.

The Velvet Fang occupies a discreet compound near Mawside, where the High Hunt meets Coldroad. Its bathhouse, tavern rooms, guarded entrances, and private gardens make it both respectable and deliberately private.

### The Crooked Hearth

**Map reference:** Marker 79.

The Crooked Hearth is a low miners’ alehouse in Cinderward, built around a chimney that leans visibly over the street. Its back room, cheap meals, and long benches make it popular with crews between shifts.
