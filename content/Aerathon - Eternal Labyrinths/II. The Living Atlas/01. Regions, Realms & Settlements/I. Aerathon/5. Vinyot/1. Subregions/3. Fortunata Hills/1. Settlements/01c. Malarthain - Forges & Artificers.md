# Malarthain — Forges & Artificers

## I. District Overview

Malarthain’s workshops serve working crews, delvers, and households. The guide follows established shops from the older forge lanes to Anvilring, Goldclasp, and Underhearth.

This guide gathers related places across [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/5. Vinyot/1. Subregions/3. Fortunata Hills/1. ## II. Places & Services

### Balval Forge

**Map reference:** Marker 5.

Balval Forge rests just off the High Hunt, where temple traffic gives way to older family workshops. The surrounding lane is warm with coal smoke, hammering, and the smell of quenched iron.

### Twofold Anvil

**Map reference:** Marker 6.

The Twofold Anvil occupies a prominent workshop near Goldclasp and the upper forge streets. Its front windows display matched blades, mirrored armor pieces, and other paired commissions meant to be purchased together.

### Moonless Shelf

**Map reference:** Marker 46.

Moonless Shelf sits beneath a low stone arcade in Underhearth. Its deep rooms and narrow aisles attract scholars, apprentices, delvers, and travelers searching for technical books or obscure magical instruction.

### The Listening Stone

**Map reference:** Marker 50.

The Listening Stone keeps a modest storefront just beyond Goldclasp’s busiest streets. Objects brought for examination are received through private booths, and the nearby lane is known for unusually patient guards and nervous customers.

### The Brass Burrow

**Map reference:** Marker 51.

The Brass Burrow fills a compact Underhearth workshop with articulated limbs, gear housings, mining devices, and half-finished constructs. Small test yards and reinforced doors separate its experiments from the surrounding residences.

See [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/5. Vinyot/1. Subregions/3. Fortunata Hills/1. Settlements/01a. The Brass Burrow|The Brass Burrow]] for its workshop practices, prosthetic fitting, and relic custody.

### Emberglass House

**Map reference:** Marker 59.

Emberglass House occupies a bright workshop near Goldclasp’s arcane trades. Its furnace towers and mirrored windows illuminate a lane lined with lens grinders, alchemical suppliers, and specialist craftsmen.

### The Hollow Key

**Map reference:** Marker 66.

The Hollow Key occupies a narrow, heavily warded shop in Goldclasp. Its plain exterior conceals demonstration vaults, seal-testing rooms, and private workspaces for clients with unusual security needs.

### The Measured Plate

**Map reference:** Marker 68.

The Measured Plate keeps a spacious fitting hall near the finer edge of Anvilring. Adjustable frames, practice floors, and private testing rooms allow armorers to study each client’s movement before beginning a commission.
