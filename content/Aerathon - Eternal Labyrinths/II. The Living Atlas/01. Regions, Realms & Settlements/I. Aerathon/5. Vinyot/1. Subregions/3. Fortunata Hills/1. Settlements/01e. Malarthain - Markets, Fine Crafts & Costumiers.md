# Malarthain — Markets, Fine Crafts & Costumiers

## I. District Overview

These shops and halls bring together fitted adornment, theatrical craft, collecting, and specialist commissions. Their addresses remain in the neighborhoods recorded on the city map.

## II. Places & Services

### Brightstone & Vale

**Map reference:** Marker 48.

Brightstone & Vale occupies a refined shopfront in Goldclasp. Its guarded display windows show gem-set weapons, formal jewelry, and religious pieces beneath controlled magical light.

### Claw & Circlet

**Map reference:** Marker 60.

Claw & Circlet maintains an ornate Goldclasp showroom designed around beastfolk formalwear. Private fitting rooms accommodate horns, tusks, tails, manes, feathers, and other features poorly served by foreign jewelers.

### The Gilded Roost

**Map reference:** Marker 73.

The Gilded Roost occupies a guarded auction hall in Goldclasp. Merchants, collectors, couriers, and appraisers gather beneath its high galleries whenever foreign art or recovered antiquities are offered for sale.

### The House of Borrowed Faces

**Map reference:** Marker 77.

The House of Borrowed Faces occupies a theatrical lane not far from the Old Crown Theatre. Its workshop windows display masks, wigs, costumes, and fitted disguises for performers, festivals, private clients, and formal ceremonies.
