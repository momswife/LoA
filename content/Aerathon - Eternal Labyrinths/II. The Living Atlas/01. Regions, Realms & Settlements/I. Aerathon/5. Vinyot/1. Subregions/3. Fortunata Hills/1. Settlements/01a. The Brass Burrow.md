# The Brass Burrow

## A workshop in the Underhearth

The Brass Burrow occupies a compact workshop in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/5. Vinyot/1. Subregions/3. Fortunata Hills/1. Settlements/01. Malarthain Stronghold|Malarthain Stronghold]]'s Underhearth. It is marked **51** on the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/Explore Locations|interactive stronghold map]]. Articulated limbs, gear housings, mining devices, and unfinished constructs crowd its benches. Reinforced doors and small test yards separate experimental work from neighboring residences.

Its reputation rests on things that must work twice: a prosthetic hand after its wearer leaves the fitting bench, a rescue winch after the demonstration crowd goes home, and a mine mechanism after grit has entered every joint. The workshop accepts unusual delver commissions, but routine adjustment and repair keep its doors open.

## Work and customers

Miners bring worn hoists and damaged housings. Residents return for the small changes that make a prosthesis comfortable through a working day. Delvers arrive with broken equipment, incomplete descriptions, and salvage whose behavior has already changed since recovery.

The intake bench keeps these jobs separate. Ordinary repair receives an estimate and a description of the fault. Unfamiliar salvage receives a handling record before it receives a price. A device that performed one impressive act is not advertised as a repeatable invention until the shop understands how it can stop.

Fittings are collaborative work. A wearer demonstrates the tasks the device must support—gripping a cup, fastening a coat, steadying a chisel—rather than being asked to imitate a diagram's ideal movement. Return adjustments are recorded with the original fitting so the next repairer knows what the user actually needs.

## Beyond the reinforced door

The test yards provide clear routes away from moving parts, marked load positions, and a place to isolate an unreliable assembly. Experiments stop when pressure or movement crosses the agreed limit; the instruction to stop belongs to the assigned observer as well as the maker.

This discipline has civic value. People live beyond the workshop walls. A successful trial that cracks a neighboring water pipe is still a failed trial, and the Burrow's standing depends on making those repairs visible rather than burying them in a customer's bill.

## Salvage and disputed custody

The Burrow may accept custody of an object without claiming it as stock for sale. Its repair notes distinguish who delivered a device, who claims it, and who authorized each alteration. Apparent voices or remembered commands are recorded before anyone attempts to improve them.

The shop's procedures illustrate [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/06. Magic, Technology & Infrastructure/I. Everyday Magic & Artifice/05. Bound Intelligences and Relic Custody|the distinction between custody and permission to use]]. They are workshop practice, not an assertion that Malarthain has accepted every Ministry claim over local salvage. Difficult cases can remain isolated while an ordinary replacement keeps the customer's work moving.

## Visiting and commissions

Customers should bring prior repairs, operating instructions, and any known exposure to unusual magic into the conversation. The Burrow's practical questions are simple: what must this device do, who depends on it, and what happens if it fails? Those answers determine whether a commission belongs at a bench, in the yard, or behind a closed reinforced door.
