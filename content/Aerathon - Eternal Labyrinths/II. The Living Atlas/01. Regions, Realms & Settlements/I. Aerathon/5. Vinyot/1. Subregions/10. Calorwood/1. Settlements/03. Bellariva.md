# Bellariva

## I. Civic Overview

**Bellariva** stands on a raised bank of the Quest River, inland from [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/5. Vinyot/1. Subregions/10. Calorwood/1. Settlements/01. Porta Strega|Porta Strega]], where inhabited clearings meet the Calorwood. It is the seat of the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/09. Organizations, Companies & Associations/IV. Professional, Scholarly & Cultural Societies/01. College of Many Hearths|College of Many Hearths]], the leading university of the Returning Roads tradition of witchcraft.

River landings, gardens, workshops, and boarding households sustain the city through the intervals between academic gatherings. Bellariva supplies teachers and repairers as readily as finished enchantments. Its reputation rests on work that can survive its maker's departure.

The college occupies several connected courts, but much of the teaching happens in occupied homes. Students cross the city carrying bread, drying frames, borrowed tools, and unfinished spellwork. Residents distinguish an invitation to a lesson from permission to experiment upon their property.

## II. Place & Built Form

The **Lower Landing** holds freight sheds, boat repair yards, and the covered food market. River traffic connects Bellariva with Porta Strega; travelers bound for Arneria commonly arrange onward passage through [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/5. Vinyot/1. Subregions/10. Calorwood/1. Settlements/02. Porta Ventura|Porta Ventura]]. Neither connection guarantees passage during bad weather or regional restrictions.

The **Hearth Courts** climb above the landing in clusters of houses around shared ovens, washhouses, and gardens. Covered walks protect books and work from persistent damp. Ground-level teaching rooms and rented workshop space allow households without large properties to participate in college life.

The **Common Court** contains the university's library, assembly hall, infirmary, and examination workshops. Its central kitchen feeds students and visiting hosts. Nearby municipal rooms remain available to residents whose business has nothing to do with the college.

The **Maple Verge** is the inhabited forest edge: nurseries, screened workyards, and maintained paths ending at marked gathering places. University ownership of a building does not grant unrestricted use of the surrounding forest. Fieldwork requires the relevant land permission and any applicable ritual authorization.

## III. People, Work & Daily Life

Bellariva earns its living through teaching, boat work, bookbinding, ceramics, pigments, garden produce, boarding, and the maintenance of household enchantments. Optical glass is brought from Stregan workshops; grain, fuel, cloth, and other necessities arrive through ordinary trade. Magic assists the work but does not eliminate the need for supplies.

Shared ovens make bread and baked river fish familiar street food. Mushroom broths and preserved vegetables vary by household and season. Visiting cooks often exchange kitchen duties with students, and recipes travel with the same corrections and arguments as spells.

Residents place a small lamp in a window when a traveling household member is expected. The custom does not require enchantment. A lamp may remain unlit while the family waits for reliable news, and outsiders are discouraged from treating either state as an omen.

At the **Returning Table**, held during the college's seasonal gatherings, visitors demonstrate something they learned and name the person who taught them. Hosts can decline public demonstration of a technique. The busiest tables often concern ordinary repairs rather than spectacular effects.

## IV. Authority & Local Institutions

A civic council administers streets, markets, sanitation, river works, and public order. The college governs admission, teaching appointments, and its own premises. Its rector can discipline a student but cannot settle a resident's civil complaint by academic decree.

The **Landing and Hearth Office** registers student lodging and mediates disputes over rent, workshop damage, and promised services. Residents may bring complaints without approaching the teacher responsible for the work. Unresolved civil matters remain with the city's authorities.

A **Civic Works Bench** maintains public water fittings, drains, lamps, and access routes. It employs ordinary tradespeople as well as witches. Student placements are supervised and do not replace the city's permanent maintenance staff.

Bellariva exchanges information with Porta Strega's Veil Registry on reflective work and forest incidents. Stregan municipal codes are not automatically Bellarivan law, and the cooperation grants neither city authority over the entire Calorwood.

## V. The University and Its Neighbors

The college grew from reciprocal lodging and teaching arrangements among working households. Its common library and examinations brought those arrangements together without absorbing every hearth into one campus.

The university now attracts more visitors than the courts can comfortably house. Landlords benefit; longstanding tenants face rising costs. Paid commissions help fund bursaries but compete with inexpensive local repairs. Residents regularly dispute how much civic labor the college owes in exchange for the space and reputation the city provides.

Some households welcome manufactured enchantments that reduce daily work. Others worry that replacing local practitioners will leave Bellariva dependent upon distant suppliers. Both positions are represented among witches and their neighbors.

## VI. Magic, Forest Access & Current Conditions

Instruction follows the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/I. Annals & Antiquities/01. Fundamentals of Recorded Reality/II. Laws of a Changed World/02. Mortal Magic and the Three Weaves|Three Weaves]]; the city's location does not grant students unlimited power or make an untested spell safe. Calorwood field lessons emphasize observation, controlled effects, and withdrawal when conditions change.

Porta Strega's record describes nearby manifestations and persistent liminal instability. Those findings do not establish that every Bellarivan household is haunted, that the forest is a confirmed planar breach, or that ambient power is uniformly greater here.

The city has no confirmed Labyrinth entrance. Its connections to delving concern travelers, recovered objects, and the effects of imported artifice. The university's inspection workshops cannot turn an artifact of unknown origin into an approved domestic tool merely by displaying it.
