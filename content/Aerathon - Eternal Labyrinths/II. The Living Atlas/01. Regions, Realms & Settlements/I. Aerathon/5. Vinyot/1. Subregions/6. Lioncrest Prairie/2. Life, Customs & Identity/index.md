# Life, Customs & Identity in Lioncrest Prairie

## Local Practice

Traveling camps separate their common water and repair stores from goods held for sale. A guest can see what is available before bargaining, and an emergency contribution is entered as such rather than quietly converted into a purchase. These practices describe mobile companies; the register does not assign a single settled prairie government.

These practices belong to the communities and occupations described; they are not inherited traits or a rule for every resident.
