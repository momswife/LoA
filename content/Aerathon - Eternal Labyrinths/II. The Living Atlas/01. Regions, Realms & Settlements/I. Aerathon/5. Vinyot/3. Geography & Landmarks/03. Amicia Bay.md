# Amicia Bay

## I. Cartographic Record

**Amicia Bay**, retained as **Amica Bay** in an earlier cartographic filing, is a confirmed named bay of Vinyot and the water around which [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/5. Vinyot/1. Subregions/7. Vincitri Prairie/1. Settlements/01. Arloris|Arloris]] is settled. Its shoreline beyond those communities and its navigational conditions remain incompletely surveyed.

## II. Current Assessment

Future work should document currents, weather, anchorages, settlements, trade relevance, hazards, and the Aerathon history of its name.
