# Life, Customs & Identity of Vinyot

This shelf records the varied ways people live and identify themselves within Vinyot. It does not treat corporate administration, commercial reputation, or one settlement's customs as a universal Vinyoti culture.

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/5. Vinyot/4. Life, Customs & Identity/01. Contracts, Work & Public Reputation|Contracts, Work & Public Reputation]]

## Current Orientation

Contracts, schedules, receipts, sponsorship, guild standing, and visible administrative results carry unusual public weight. Citizenship may follow residence, employment, guild recognition, or local charter rather than one regional rule.

Those systems do not describe every household or distribute their benefits equally. Company housing, independent farms, fishing hamlets, sacred districts, fortified enclaves, and expedition camps produce markedly different forms of daily life. Settlement and institution are therefore more reliable units of description than an assumed regional personality.

## Filing Boundaries

Future records belong here when they concern Vinyoti household life, work culture, sponsorship, etiquette, food, clothing, public reputation, festivals, and differences among communities. Corporate law remains under Governments & Geopolitics, economic systems remain under Society, Economy & Everyday Life, and individual organizations retain their own filings.

## Development Priorities

- Citizenship, residence, employment, sponsorship, and guild standing
- Household life within company, rural, sacred, and expedition communities
- Public reputation, receipts, schedules, and administrative visibility
- Local identity beyond commercial stereotypes
