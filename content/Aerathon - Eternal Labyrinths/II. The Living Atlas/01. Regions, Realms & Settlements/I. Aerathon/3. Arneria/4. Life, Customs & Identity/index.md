# Life, Customs & Identity of Arneria

This shelf records practices shaped within Arneria while preserving the differences between Causeway cities, Beylik authority, Bat'yan communities, and other local jurisdictions.

## Current Orientation

Oaths, discipline, skilled labor, ritual participation, and responsible use of scarce resources are prominent public values. They are practices rather than inherent Arnerian traits, and an oath's authority changes according to its witnesses and jurisdiction.

Causeway communities frequently organize daily life around schedules, checkpoints, water, and public ceremony. Bat'yan communities may instead emphasize ecological knowledge, household and village responsibility, seasonal conditions, and locally maintained sacred boundaries. Both belong to Arneria without being interchangeable.

## Filing Boundaries

Future records belong here when they concern localized Arnerian life: oaths and witnessing, hospitality, work, water use, household practice, ceremony, dress, food, and differences between communities. Formal law remains under Governments & Geopolitics, faith doctrine remains under Religion & Worship, and historical reconstruction remains in Annals & Antiquities.

## Development Priorities

- Oaths, witnesses, and differences of jurisdiction
- Water, scarcity, schedules, and public responsibility
- Causeway and Bat'yan patterns of household and communal life
- Regional ceremony without assuming uniform observance
