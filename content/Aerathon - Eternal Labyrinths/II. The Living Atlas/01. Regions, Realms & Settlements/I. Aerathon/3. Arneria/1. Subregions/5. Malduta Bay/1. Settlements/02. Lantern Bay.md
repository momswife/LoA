# Lantern Bay

## Civic Overview

Lantern Bay serves the small craft and coastal households for whom Far'soro's great traffic is a market, but not a home. Fish is landed at the inner quay, damaged skiffs are hauled above the tide, and cargo too small to justify a larger vessel changes hands beneath a roof of mismatched tiles.

## Work & Local Institutions

Local pilots maintain a shore-light roster. Each light has an assigned keeper and a relief household; an unexplained dark lamp brings a knock at someone's door before it becomes a story about wreckers. Harbor warden Tovin Reed administers that roster and hears disputes over blocked moorings. Customs and territorial claims require the appropriate outside authority rather than his private judgment.

## Current Conditions

Recent silt has made one familiar approach unreliable for heavily loaded boats. Pilots disagree about whether it needs clearing or merely different tide instructions. Merchants favor reopening it quickly, while the families who would provide the boats and labor want a fresh sounding first. The trouble is real, but neither sabotage nor a new supernatural hazard has been established.
