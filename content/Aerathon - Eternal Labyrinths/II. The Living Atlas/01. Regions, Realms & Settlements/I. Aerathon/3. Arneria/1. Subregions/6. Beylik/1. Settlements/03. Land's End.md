# Land's End

---

## I. Cartographic Record

**Land's End** marks a named locality at the far reach of the Arnerian Causeway. The current map groups it with towns, though the Ministry has not confirmed that it presently contains a permanent settlement.

## II. Current Assessment

The record preserves that uncertainty deliberately. Legacy claims concerning memorial use, spirits, or nearby planar activity are not accepted without Aerathon-specific corroboration.

## III. Filing Priorities

- determine whether Land's End is inhabited, ceremonial, or purely geographic;
- verify the condition of the terminal Causeway approach;
- coordinate any planar findings with the Netherworld record rather than duplicating speculation.
