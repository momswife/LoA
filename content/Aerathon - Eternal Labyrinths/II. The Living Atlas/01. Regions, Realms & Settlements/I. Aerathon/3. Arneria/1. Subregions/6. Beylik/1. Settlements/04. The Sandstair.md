# The Sandstair

## I. Cartographic Record

**The Sandstair** is a confirmed settlement or inhabited locality at a causeway descent within **Beylik**, [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I.
