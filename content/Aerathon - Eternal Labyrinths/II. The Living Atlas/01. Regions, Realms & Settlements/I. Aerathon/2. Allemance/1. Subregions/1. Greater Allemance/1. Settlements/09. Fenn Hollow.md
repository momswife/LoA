# Fenn Hollow

## I. Civic Overview

**Fenn Hollow** is an agricultural hamlet on the inhabited approaches to [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/2. Allemance/3. Geography & Landmarks/10. Monster Meadows|Monster Meadows]]. Its households work unusually fertile ground while living with the possibility that a tremor season or dangerous surge will force them to leave it. It is filed under Greater Allemance because no narrower subregional assignment has been established.

The hamlet's ordinary concerns are recognizably rural: weather, stores, tools, labor, and getting a harvest in. The nearby Rift makes those concerns harder to separate from readiness. A household may have good reason to expect a rich yield and good reason to keep its transport serviceable on the same day.

## II. Place and Work

Fenn Hollow's working spaces follow the needs of cultivation. Produce is sorted, baskets repaired, tools cleaned, and loads prepared for movement. Those tasks give the settlement a different rhythm from the tournament grounds. A crowd may spend an afternoon watching a contest; someone still has to finish bringing in what was cut that morning.

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/V. Broadcasts, Audiences & Sponsorship/05. The Meadow Circuit|Meadow Circuit]] brings additional mouths to feed. Visiting teams, vendors, and spectators create demand for provisions, but that custom shares labor and transport with the harvest. Residents have reason to welcome a successful tournament and to resent a visitor who assumes the entire countryside has stopped working to celebrate it.

Fenn Hollow's generosity is often practical. Food put aside for a late watch, a place found on a wagon, or help finishing a neighbor's load can matter more than a public speech about courage. Reputation is built through those repeated obligations as well as through the conspicuous moments when an alarm sounds.

## III. Living With the Watch

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/09. Organizations, Companies & Associations/I. Civic Defense & Emergency Service/01. The Hearthward Compact|Hearthward Compact]] protects the inhabited approaches. Its wardens are known locally through patrols, escorts, and evacuation work as well as through famous performances. Anya and Tiberius's reputation has meaning here because the force they lead helps residents continue an ordinary livelihood beside an extraordinary hazard.

The Compact's protective mandate does not establish it as Fenn Hollow's civil government or landowner. The settlement's fuller civic arrangements and population are not specified by the Meadows record. Its dependence on the watch should not be mistaken for ownership by the organization supplying that protection.

Rift Watch warnings and the Compact's response take precedence over a day's work when evacuation is required. That can mean leaving a task at the point when finishing it feels easiest. A nearly loaded cart and a nearly gathered field are difficult things to abandon; readiness includes accepting that the last useful trip may have already been made.

## IV. Visitors and Present Conditions

Fenn Hollow stands outside the restricted inner Meadows. Lodging with a resident, buying local provisions, or traveling with tournament spectators gives no right to enter the quarantine zone. The pastoral view across the perimeter is not an assurance that the ground beyond it is safe.

For delvers, the hamlet offers a measure of the work that continues after a broadcast ends. An escort completed without an encounter can be more valuable to its residents than a dramatic fight. The fields do not become less important because there was nothing worth replaying on a scrying mirror.

Neighboring [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/2. Allemance/1. Subregions/1. Greater Allemance/1. Settlements/10. Rakebridge|Rakebridge]] shares the Meadows' fertile surroundings and evacuation pressures, with more of its public activity centered on transport, repairs, and the passage of visiting teams.
