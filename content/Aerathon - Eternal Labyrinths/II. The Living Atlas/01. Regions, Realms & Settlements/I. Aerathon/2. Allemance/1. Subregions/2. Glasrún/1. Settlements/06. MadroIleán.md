# MadroIleán

## Civic Overview

MadroIleán's households maintain the landing together. Boat places are assigned by use and upkeep, and the person who neglects a damaged mooring cannot recover standing merely by arriving with a more impressive family name. Disputes are heard by residents chosen for their familiarity with the work and the parties involved.

## Work & Local Institutions

Nets, baskets, repaired tools, preserved food, and small timberwork pass through the landing. The settlement does not claim the whole surrounding forest as its private store. Cutting and gathering are discussed with the neighboring hearths whose paths and seasonal work overlap its own.

## Current Conditions

The immediate concern is the landing's worn support timbers. Several families want to enlarge it while repairs are underway; others fear that a larger landing would bring demands for warehouses, outside guards, and permanent commercial privileges. Visitors willing to help inspect and replace the damaged work are welcome. They are not being invited to decide the island's future for its residents.
