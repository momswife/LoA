# Glasrún

### _The Greenwood Without Lords — “What Grows Untended Endures.”_

**Glasrún** is the old-growth forest of northeastern [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/2. Allemance/∅ Allemance|Allemance]], extending from the upper Queensriver toward the western slopes of the Mantle Mountains. Formally associated with Allemance but protected from noble rule by the Glasrún Pact, it is unruled rather than lawless.

## II. Setting & Boundaries

The Queensriver forms Glasrún's southern legal and symbolic boundary. Old-growth forest rises north and east toward mountain passes shared with Oric travel. Cool, damp weather, long autumns, heavy snow, and dense undergrowth make care of routes and clearings a communal duty.

## III. The Glasrún Pact

The Pact relinquished noble claim, levy, and administrative responsibility over the forest while preserving its association with Allemance. Surviving records disagree about the earliest date and personal attribution of the relinquishment; later Gocaire records describe a formal abandonment in 571 A.D. The durable canon is the settlement itself: no noble levy crosses the Queensriver, and no permanent lord or charter office rules Glasrún.

## IV. Authority & Present Conditions

Local precedent, mediation, restitution, and care for the forest sustain order. Ministry presence is observational and negotiated. The region remains low-risk in ordinary civic terms but contains unexcavated ruins, dense Loamlink growth, and routes whose reported instability keeps unsanctioned delving under close local scrutiny.

## V. Maintained Local Geography

Glasrún covers the northeastern reaches of Allemance, from the upper banks of the Queensriver toward the western slopes of the Mantle Mountains. Its damp old-growth forest is dense enough in places to keep sunlight from the loam.

## Maintained Geographic Features

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/2. Allemance/3. Geography & Landmarks/16. Queensriver|Queensriver]] — the fast, shallow river that forms the forest's southern legal and symbolic boundary.
- **Mantle Slopes** — rocky, forested approaches toward Oria, crossed by narrow passes maintained by local wardens.
- **Deepwood** — central old-growth zones without roads or logging.
- **Mossfall Clearings** — natural glades used for seasonal rites, meetings, and exchange.

## Sites & Conditions Under Review

Dense Loamlink growth, old ruins, and paths reported to rearrange themselves are recurring survey concerns. Glasrún has no sanctioned labyrinth entrance; delvers who enter are still bound by local prohibitions against excavation, territorial claims, and uninvited encampment.
