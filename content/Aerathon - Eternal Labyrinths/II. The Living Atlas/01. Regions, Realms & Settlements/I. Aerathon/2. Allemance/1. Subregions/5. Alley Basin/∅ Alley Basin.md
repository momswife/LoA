# Alley Basin

**Alley Basin** is a confirmed subregion of [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/2. Allemance/∅ Allemance|Allemance]]. Its most thoroughly authenticated location is Symphony Canyon, where the Basin breaks into a resonant ravine south of Mansque and Fort Kingsfang.

## II. Geographic Orientation

The authenticated record begins at the three-river confluence within Symphony Canyon. The precise watershed, ridge boundaries, roads, and relationship to neighboring Allemantic waterways remain under survey.

## III. Local Significance

The Basin's best-known public life is concentrated at the Canyon: Bardic Evermeet, resonance study, sacred music, annual performance, and tightly regulated magical acoustics. These practices should not yet be generalized into a basin-wide culture.

## IV. Current Filing Priorities

Future work should establish the Basin's boundary, confirm any resident communities, distinguish civic sites from specialist institutions, and connect its river system to Allemance's maintained geography without importing unsupported source-map lore.

## V. Maintained Local Geography

Alley Basin is a confirmed Allemantic subregion whose detailed limits remain under survey.

## Authenticated Site

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/2. Allemance/3. Geography & Landmarks/13. Symphony Canyon|Symphony Canyon]] — the ravine formed where the Basin breaks open south of Mansque and Fort Kingsfang.

At the canyon, the Veyra, Salend, and Dourin rivers converge in a harmonic 3:2:1 flow. Their dedicated river records have not yet been established, so the names remain indexed here without speculative links.
