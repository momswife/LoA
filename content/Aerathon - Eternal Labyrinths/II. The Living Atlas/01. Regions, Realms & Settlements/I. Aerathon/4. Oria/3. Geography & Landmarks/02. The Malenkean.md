# The Malenkean

## I. Cartographic Record

**The Malenkean** is a confirmed named sea within [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/∅ Oria|Oria]]. Its regional placement and feature class are authenticated by the current atlas; detailed boundaries and local conditions remain preliminary. Existing Oric records place Severport on its coast.
