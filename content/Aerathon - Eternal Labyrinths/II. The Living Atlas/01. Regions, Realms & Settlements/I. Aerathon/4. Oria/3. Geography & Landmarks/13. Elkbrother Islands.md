# Elkbrother Islands

## I. Cartographic Record

**Elkbrother Islands** are a confirmed named island chain within [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/∅ Oria|Oria]]. Their regional placement and feature class are authenticated by the current atlas; detailed boundaries and local conditions remain preliminary. Doloretsk is recorded on one of these islands, south of the Nattefrost.

## II. Current Assessment

Future work should establish its coastlines, approaches, settlements, resources, jurisdiction, weather, and maritime hazards.
