# The Northcall and Emberward Assembly

> [!warning] Historical record
> Frostguarde and its approaches remain under the northern plague cordon. This account does not establish present services, safe access, or surviving inhabitants. See [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/4. Nattefrost/1. Settlements/01. Frostguarde City|the city’s records status]].

## I. Warmth as a Gathering Place

The Emberward Plaza brought two necessities into the same space: warmth and the ability to find one another. Its stone pylons stood in a broad circle, carrying layered runes that released enough heat to melt the surrounding snow. Wet paving reflected the lanterns; steam marked the edge between the heated square and the colder streets.

On ordinary evenings that warmth was part of the city’s infrastructure. During danger, the plaza became an assembly point. A familiar place reduced the need to explain a destination while frightened people were trying to understand an order.

## II. The Northcall

The Northcall Horn summoned attention with a long, unmistakable note. In the northern-assault account, watchmen directed willing people with field experience toward the pylons while civilians were guided inward. Clergy were asked for people able to preserve calm and support the injured. Carrying a weapon was not the only useful qualification.

[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/I. Annals & Antiquities/05. Figures, Institutions, Houses & Lineages/I. Figures, Sovereigns & Founders/07. Halric Stonewatch|Halric Stonewatch]] divided the gathered volunteers among wall reinforcement, supply movement, and observation of the approach. His instructions emphasized remaining together and following assignments. The summons offered useful work rather than a promise of glory.

## III. Withdrawal and the Inner Wards

The same account describes three sharp horn blasts accompanying withdrawal from the wall. These two signals do not provide a complete reconstruction of Frostguarde’s horn code.

Sealing the inner districts protected fewer streets at the cost of abandoning others. The Emberward’s warmth did not extend equally everywhere, and moving a barricade also changed who could reach shelter. The choice of where to hold a line therefore concerned civilian access as well as military ground.

The archive preserves the assembly and retreat as parts of [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/I. Annals & Antiquities/04. Conflicts, Catastrophes, & Treaties/IV. Delving-Era Expeditions & Disasters/02. The Night the Northern Wall Broke|The Night the Northern Wall Broke]]. It does not establish how many people reached the inner wards or whether the final sealing was completed.
