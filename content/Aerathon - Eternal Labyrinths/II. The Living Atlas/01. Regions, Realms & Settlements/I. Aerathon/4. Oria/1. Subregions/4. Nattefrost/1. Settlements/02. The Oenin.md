# The Oenin

> [!warning] Closed under the Nattefrost cordon
> The following account describes the Oenin before the northern closure. It does not establish current habitation, safe passage, or surviving services. The Nattefrost cordon remains controlling.

## Before the Closure

The Oenin was a small service settlement organized around a sheltered storehouse and heated common rooms. Households repaired clothing and hauling gear, kept fuel dry, and provisioned workers who needed to remain north longer than a single journey. The name belonged to the settlement; older surveyors did not consistently distinguish it from the surrounding work grounds.

Fuel allotments were read aloud before winter stores were sealed. Families could dispute their share, and illness or damaged housing could change an allotment after the first tally. The arrangement produced arguments, but it also made a missing household harder to overlook.

No reliable update confirms what remains of those rooms, stores, or families. A recovered receipt or familiar voice on a relay would be evidence to examine, not permission to send visitors through the cordon.
