# Archives of Frostguarde

> [!warning] Historical record
> Frostguarde and its approaches remain under the northern plague cordon. This account does not establish present services, safe access, or surviving inhabitants. See [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/4. Nattefrost/1. Settlements/01. Frostguarde City|the city’s records status]].

## I. A Building Reused

The Archives of Frostguarde occupied a former prison. Thick walls, narrow barred windows set high above the floor, and traces of sealed cells survived the conversion. Guard walkways became galleries for shelving and ladders. Iron hooks were filed down rather than wholly removed; chains supported lanterns and pulley systems.

The adaptation turned a building designed to hold people into one intended to keep records. Its older purpose remained legible in the stone. Preservation did not erase the history of confinement.

## II. The Approach and the Work

The road to the archives rose gradually through an older part of the city. Rounded paving, closely placed buildings, and houses bearing faded script or wax seals suggested a district accustomed to storing and carrying written material. The archives themselves were low and substantial, with little exterior ornament.

Scrolls, ledgers, loose correspondence, shelves, and lifting equipment formed its working interior. The record does not identify a complete catalog or prove that any particular collection survived the northern assault.

## III. Damage in the Assault Account

[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/I. Annals & Antiquities/04. Conflicts, Catastrophes, & Treaties/IV. Delving-Era Expeditions & Disasters/02. The Night the Northern Wall Broke|The Night the Northern Wall Broke]] describes a forced entrance, overturned shelving, broken ink bottles, disturbed papers, and undead moving through the building. That damage belongs to an undated account, not a current inspection.

A descending passage is also mentioned, but its relationship to [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/06. Magic, Technology & Infrastructure/III. Arcane Research & Observation/01. The Cryostudies Annex|The Cryostudies Annex]] and the oldest bastion is unresolved. This entry supplies no verified underground route. Nor should the building’s former cells be mistaken for evidence of a Labyrinth entrance; Frostguarde’s city record establishes no known entrance within its bounds.

The surviving description leaves two losses difficult to separate: harm to the people who kept the records and harm to the knowledge in their care. Neither can be measured by counting the fallen shelves alone.
