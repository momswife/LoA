# Life, Customs & Identity in Zaros Mountains

## Local Practice

Crews carrying goods between mountain settlements exchange damaged packing and worn harness at the receiving yard, so the next departure begins with the last journey’s evidence. It is courteous to report a delay before offering an excuse. This working practice does not make every House or harbor share the same discipline.

These practices belong to the communities and occupations described; they are not inherited traits or a rule for every resident.
