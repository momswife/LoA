# Avianfolk Cultural Traditions

The records in this shelf document several connected but distinct traditions practiced among Avianfolk communities.

They do not define one universal Avianfolk culture. Avianfolk lineages possess no single faith, government, household form, homeland, or relationship with flight. Participation varies by family, region, migration history, adoption, marriage, profession, and individual choice.

## I. The Cardinal Wind Tradition

[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/II. Cultural Systems & Traditions/1. Avianfolk Traditions/01. The Four Cardinal Winds|The Four Cardinal Winds]] describes four cultural currents descended from the Sky-Reeve confederations. A Wind is a form of household belonging rather than a region, personality category, or biological inheritance.

Two connected records describe institutions and practices within that system:

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/II. Cultural Systems & Traditions/1. Avianfolk Traditions/02. The Wind-Mothers|The Wind-Mothers]] examines a living cultural institution associated with household memory and authority.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/II. Cultural Systems & Traditions/1. Avianfolk Traditions/03. Turning the Wing|Turning the Wing]] records the practice through which a person may enter another household Wind through marriage or adoption.

## II. Modern Interpretations

- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/II. Cultural Systems & Traditions/1. Avianfolk Traditions/04. The Fifth Current|The Fifth Current]] documents a modern cultural philosophy that responds to inherited Wind structures without replacing the four historical Winds.
- [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/II. Cultural Systems & Traditions/1. Avianfolk Traditions/05. Modern Stormriders|Modern Stormriders]] documents a contemporary, high-risk delver subculture shaped by older Sky-Reeve traditions and the conditions of the Age of Spectacle.

These movements are not interchangeable. One concerns philosophy and cultural interpretation; the other concerns a modern delver subculture.

## III. Lineage and Culture

The modern Folk classification is documented separately in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/2. Avianfolk/∅ Avianfolk|Avianfolk]]. Individual lineage records describe bodies, histories, modern communities, and playable traits. The cultural records here describe forms of belonging that may cross lineage boundaries and may include non-Avianfolk participants.
