## I. Lineage Overview

Gekkonians are Gecko Scalefolk whose adhesive digits, broad visual range, varied night vision, and comfort on vertical surfaces have shaped cities with more than one usable orientation.

They are a common Scalefolk lineage, especially in warm ports, island settlements, cliff districts, cavern cities, and urban neighborhoods built around walls, beams, and ceilings as public routes.

Not every Gekkonian can cling to every surface. Dust, oil, water, injury, temperature, footwear, and material texture all matter.

---

## II. Origins and the Six-Sided Street

Early Gekkonian settlements built doors, markets, beds, and assembly ledges across every stable face of a chamber. Surface archaeologists repeatedly described these cities as storage shafts because they searched only the floor.

The **Six-Sided Street** ruling established that a route remains civic infrastructure when it crosses a wall or ceiling. It also required public notices, emergency lighting, and safety rails to be readable from multiple orientations.

Modern communities preserve this history through turning maps whose “top” changes with the reader.

The maps are also political records. They show which ledges were widened for children, where a non-climbing route was added, and which ceiling market lost access after a landlord sealed its stair. Gekkonian historians read those changes as evidence of who a city expected to participate in public life.

---

## III. Body and Sensory Life

Gekkonians are usually Small or Medium, with scaled bodies, large eyes, flexible spines, broad adhesive pads, and tails used for balance, fat storage, expression, or defensive release.

Some possess eyelids; others clean exposed eyes through specialized membranes or the tongue. Bright light, dust, adhesive solvents, and dry air may require protective equipment.

Tail release is not universal and is never painless. A detached tail is protected bodily material, not bait, food, medicine, or a curiosity.

---

## IV. Society and Kinship

Gekkonian households include wall courts, ledge families, night markets, ceiling gardens, cliff houses, molt cohorts, and mixed-orientation neighborhoods.

The custom of **named footing** asks a host to explain which surfaces are load-bearing, private, sacred, hazardous, or unavailable for climbing. A structurally reachable space is not automatically public.

Homes often use texture and vibration alongside color so navigation remains legible in darkness or through varied vision.

Molt cohorts form another layer of kinship. Members share care during periods when skin, adhesive pads, vision, or balance are unusually sensitive. A cohort may last for a single season or for decades. Its duties are practical and confidential; helping with bodily transition does not grant authority over the person afterward.

---

## V. Inner Life and Modern Debate

Gekkonian cultures often value adaptable perspective, quiet observation, spatial play, practical hospitality, night music, and architecture that assumes bodies will approach from unexpected directions.

Modern debate concerns vertical access. Some communities favor universal climbing infrastructure; others argue that public life cannot depend upon an ability injured, elderly, disabled, or non-Gekkonian residents may not possess.

The strongest designs offer several equivalent routes.

The debate now extends to memory and status. Prestigious wall districts sometimes treat the stair route as a service entrance even when officials call it equal. Access councils therefore judge routes by destination, safety, travel time, and dignity—not mere existence. A city is not six-sided if only climbers reach the council chamber through the front.

## VI. Gekkonian Peoples in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Arcades and theatre rigging support dense wall neighborhoods, while landlords often charge extra for usable vertical space.

### Oria

Geothermal caverns and observatories host cold-protected communities using heated handholds and enclosed wall routes.

### Vinyot

Ports, signage towers, maintenance galleries, and illegal high-wall broadcasts make Gekkonians highly visible.

### Arneria

Nocturnal desert and oasis communities travel cool stone after sunset and shelter within shaded cracks by day.

### Al'Ar

Island Gekkonians are numerous in volcanic cliffs, canopy settlements, and storm-braced vertical towns.

---

## VII. Gekkonian Delvers

Gekkonian delvers are climbers, scouts, riggers, trap readers, rescue specialists, and surveyors of rooms whose apparent floor is misleading.

Labyrinths may reverse gravity, shed false surfaces, fill adhesive pads with dust, or present routes that can be entered but not safely exited.

Crews should provide light protection, cleaning supplies, redundant lines, and a plan that does not make one climber responsible for transporting everyone.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

> [!warning] Playtest Mechanics  
> These traits are an initial Aerathon playtest package and require DM approval until table testing is complete.

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Dexterity score increases by 2.

### Size

Your size is Small or Medium, chosen when you select your tradition.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 30 feet, and you have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed.

### Adhesive Grip

You can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without making an ability check, provided the surface is solid and free of heavy oil, loose debris, or similar interference. You still need free limbs appropriate to the movement.

### Night Eyes

You have darkvision out to 60 feet and gain proficiency in Perception.

### Tail Release

When you become grappled or restrained by a creature, you may use your reaction to end that condition and move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. Describe whether this involves a released tail, flexible escape, shed outer tissue, or another lineage-appropriate response. Once used, this trait cannot be used again until a long rest.

## X. Recognized Gekkonian Traditions

### Night-Eye

Night-Eye communities include Leopard, House, Cave, and other Gekkonians whose lives emphasize darkness, vibration, and low-light color.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Small or Medium, your choice.

**Deep Night Eyes.** Your darkvision increases to 120 feet. You have advantage on Perception checks made to detect movement in dim light or darkness.

### Day-Jewel

Day-Jewel Gekkonians include brightly colored island and forest peoples with active daytime markets, visual display, and sun-safe architecture.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Small or Medium, your choice.

**Jewel Display.** You gain proficiency in Performance or Persuasion and know the `prestidigitation` cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it.

### Sand-Toe

Sand-Toe communities use fringed digits, cool burrows, and carefully timed movement in desert regions where ordinary adhesion is unreliable.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Small.

**Sand-Toe Passage.** Nonmagical sand does not count as difficult terrain for you, and you ignore the effects of extreme heat. Your Adhesive Grip works normally on dry, sandy stone.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

If the campaign uses Homeland Imprints, choose one trait representing the region that most strongly shaped your character.

### Allemance — Eaves and Courtyards

You gain proficiency in Investigation. After observing a building's exterior for 1 minute, you can identify an ordinary route to its roof or upper floor if one is visible and publicly accessible.

### Oria — Icewall Pads

You ignore the effects of extreme cold. Your Adhesive Grip works normally on stable nonmagical ice.

### Vinyot — Signboard Runner

You gain proficiency in Performance. Moving across signs, awnings, scaffolds, and other urban climbing surfaces does not draw more attention than walking at street level unless you choose otherwise.

### Arneria — Cool-Stone Sleeper

You gain proficiency in Survival. After touching stone for 1 minute, you can determine whether a cooler sheltered space lies within 100 feet on the other side or below it.

### Al'Ar — Rainwall Climber

Rain and ordinary wetness do not interfere with Adhesive Grip. You have advantage on saving throws made to resist being moved by wind while climbing.

## XII. Playing a Gekkonian

Consider:

- Which surface feels most like a street?
- How do you protect and clean your adhesive pads?
- What does your tail communicate?
- Which direction does your home treat as up?
- How does bright light affect you?
- Who built a second route when you could no longer climb?

Vertical movement offers perspective and access, not universal agility or emotional detachment.
