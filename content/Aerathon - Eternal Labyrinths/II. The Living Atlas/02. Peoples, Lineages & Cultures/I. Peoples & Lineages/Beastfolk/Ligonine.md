## I. Lineage Overview

Ligonines are a convergent Beastfolk lineage composed principally of Mole, Armadillo, and Sloth peoples.

They share little outward resemblance.

Their traditional environments are separated vertically:

- Moles entered deep earth.
- Armadillos crossed hard, exposed ground and defended burrows.
- Sloths climbed into canopy worlds above ordinary streets.

For generations, some communities had no evidence the others existed.

Ligonine origin stories say that when their routes finally met beneath the world, recognition came before language.

Modern scholars call this mythic.

Modern Ligonines call it family history.

The lineage is organized around a shared refusal of the **middle as default**. Its peoples adapted to environments most builders, governments, and taxonomies treated as margins: below the foundation, above the roofline, inside the structure, beyond the planned route.

This perspective produces Ligonine thought that often escapes ordinary skill categories.

Their central question is:

> What becomes visible when the accepted point of view is abandoned?

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

The **Meeting Beneath No Map** is the foundational Ligonine account.

A Mole tunnel follows water upward.

An Armadillo refuge follows heat downward.

A fallen canopy root carries a Sloth community below the forest.

The three passages intersect.

No one shares language, food custom, posture, or idea of proper sky.

Each recognizes that the others built shelter by refusing the surface’s answer.

They remain together long enough to invent a sign:

One hand above.

One below.

One outside the line between.

The sign means **kin beyond resemblance**.

### The Loamlink

Ligonines maintain deep relationships with [[The Loamlink]], called the Listening Earth by many Beastfolk.

Mole communities map root vibration, water, settlement weight, and distant work. Armadillos maintain protected descents through unstable or heat-hardened ground. Sloth communities interpret how deep structures alter canopy life above them.

No one sublineage owns the Loamlink.

Ligonines often serve as translators between communities that encounter it through different senses.

### Taxonomic Exception

Early Ministry registrars attempted to split Ligonines into unrelated animal categories.

Community councils refused.

Their successful petition established a major registry principle: shared peoplehood may be historically and culturally real even when surface morphology does not support a simple natural classification.

The phrase **exception that proves the taxonomy** remains popular.

Some Ligonines dislike it. They argue that the lineage does not prove the system works; it proves people had to force the system to notice what already existed.

## III. Body and Vertical Form

Ligonine bodies differ significantly.

### Mole

Moles are compact, broad-handed, dark-adapted, and capable of limited burrowing. Their ordinary sight is poor without individually crafted spectacles.

### Armadillo

Armadillos possess protective plates, strong legs, rapid ground movement, and the ability to reduce injury by curling or presenting armored surfaces.

### Sloth

Sloths possess long arms, strong claws, climbing bodies, and deliberate resting motion followed by powerful committed action.

### Shared Health Concerns

No single environment suits all Ligonines.

Deep communities may require controlled air, light, and vibration. Armadillos may need heat management and armor care. Canopy Sloths require vertical access, broad handholds, and suspension-safe rest.

The claim that Ligonines are “adapted to extremes” is not a substitute for specifying which extreme.

## IV. Society and Kinship

Ligonine communities include tunnel cities, surface survey camps, canopy villages, research enclaves, structural guilds, and households whose members live at several elevations.

### The Third Answer

When two proposals appear to exhaust every option, traditional Ligonine councils reserve time for a **Third Answer**.

The answer need not be accepted.

Someone must be permitted to question:

- The scale of the problem
- The assumed boundary
- The need to solve it now
- Whether the two choices share a hidden premise
- Whether the person presenting the choice benefits from its limits

This practice produces important breakthroughs.

It can also frustrate communities facing immediate danger. Skilled Ligonine facilitators distinguish generative refusal from delay disguised as cleverness.

### Intersections

Ligonine kinship is often renewed through **intersection gatherings** where sublineages exchange knowledge across environments.

A Mole may teach foundation sense.

An Armadillo may teach refuge defense.

A Sloth may teach suspended construction.

The gathering is not meant to erase difference. Its purpose is to remember that lineage began through it.

### Privacy

Ligonines are stereotyped as boundary-blind because Surface communities often display intense curiosity.

Deep and Canopy traditions may be highly private.

Community etiquette emphasizes that unusual perspective does not grant access to another person’s space, body, research, or thoughts.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Ligonine communities commonly admire:

- Unconventional thought
- Structural awareness
- Environmental adaptation
- Patient focus
- Kinship across difference
- Self-directed study
- Respect for overlooked spaces
- The ability to change scale or viewpoint

These values cast long shadows.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Originality | Discovering better possibilities | Rejecting ordinary solutions for being ordinary |
| Focus | Developing deep expertise | Neglecting relationships and health |
| Adaptation | Building where others cannot | Romanticizing dangerous isolation |
| Curiosity | Connecting separate worlds | Violating boundaries |
| Patience | Waiting for complete structure | Missing the moment to act |
| Kinship beyond resemblance | Resisting shallow taxonomy | Ignoring real sublineage difference |

A recurring Ligonine fear is being forced into the middle: one environment, one skill category, one social role, one explanation that makes sense only from outside.

### Internal Divisions

Modern Ligonines debate:

- Whether one lineage can represent three radically different sublineages
- Whether Loamlink research should be public
- Whether deep tunnels should appear on surface maps
- Whether Surface communities romanticize boundary violation
- Whether Canopy settlements receive adequate political standing
- How controlled collapses should be regulated
- Whether lineage unity depends too heavily upon one origin story

## VI. Environmental Traditions

Ligonine culture is shaped more strongly by vertical environment than national border.

### Loamlink

Loamlink communities build research chambers, craft halls, quiet archives, water maps, and vibration-safe homes.

Identity often centers on work, but reformers resist treating obsessive study as moral superiority.

### Surface

Surface Ligonines are surveyors, builders, explorers, diplomats, and enthusiastic connectors.

They often become the visible representatives of the lineage, which can distort public understanding of more private Deep and Canopy communities.

### Canopy

Canopy Ligonines build suspended neighborhoods, leaf routes, long-view observatories, and slow-moving communities adapted to branches rather than ground.

Their calm is not inactivity. Maintenance above the ground requires continual awareness of growth, wind, weight, and living support.

---

## VII. Ligonines and Delving

Ligonines serve as structural analysts, scouts, climbers, burrowers, researchers, demolition specialists, and route surveyors.

Their greatest shared mechanical gift applies to ability checks without a named skill: the precise moments when ordinary training categories fail.

Labyrinth hazards include false structural intuition, tunnels that close behind burrowers, canopies without real anchors, and spaces whose shortest route to the surface exits onto another plane.

A common warning states:

> Knowing how the room is held together does not tell you why it wants to remain standing.

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Mole

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Intelligence score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Speed.** Your walking speed is 25 feet.

**Burrow.** You have a burrowing speed of 10 feet while using both hands.

**Darkvision.** You can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can’t discern color in darkness.

**Soil Sense.** As a bonus action, you gain tremorsense out to 15 feet for 1 minute. While this feature is active, you lose normal vision and darkvision. You regain the use of this feature after a short or long rest.

**Bottlebottoms.** You have disadvantage on sight-based Perception checks unless wearing spectacles crafted for you. While wearing them, you are proficient in such checks.

Crafting a pair requires 8 hours, 50 gp, and a DC 15 Dexterity (glassblower’s tools) check.

**Tunnel Sense.** While underground, you always know the dimensions of the surrounding space and the shortest path to the surface. You also know whether water lies within 60 feet above you.

**Quick Fix.** You know the `mending` cantrip. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability.

Mole Ligonines possess compact digging bodies, highly sensitive touch, and eyes whose needs vary from ordinary low-light accommodation to specialized lenses. Their underground communities have produced influential traditions of spatial study, repair, and soil stewardship, though no Mole is obligated to serve as an engineer or excavator.

### Armadillo

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Speed.** Your walking speed is 35 feet.

**Natural Armor.** When not wearing armor, your AC is 14 + your Dexterity modifier. You can use a shield normally.

**Defensive Ball.** When you take the Dash, Disengage, or Dodge action, reduce nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage you take by 3 until the start of your next turn.

When you Dash in a straight line, your speed is doubled for that movement.

Armadillo Ligonines possess layered protective plates and strong limbs suited to digging, running, and crossing exposed ground. Curling or bracing is a bodily defense rather than a sign of emotional withdrawal, and many Armadillos resent being treated as movable cover for other people.

### Sloth

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Speed.** Your walking speed is 25 feet.

**Climb.** You have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed.

**A Mean Right Hook.** Your claws are natural weapons that deal `1d6 + your Strength modifier` slashing damage.

**Long Arms.** Your unarmed strikes have a reach of 5 feet greater than normal.

**Slow and Steady.** If you didn’t move or use a bonus action or reaction since your last turn, the first Strength or Dexterity ability check or attack you make on your turn is made with advantage.

Sloth Ligonines commonly possess long arms, curved claws, and bodies adapted to secure movement through canopies and other vertical spaces. Their deliberate pace conserves effort and conceals considerable strength; it does not indicate laziness, dullness, or indifference to urgency.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

If the campaign uses Homeland Imprints, choose one trait representing the environment that most strongly shaped your character.

Ligonine homelands are traditionally recorded by vertical environment rather than national border. Your imprint does not need to match your birthplace, current residence, or sublineage.

### Loamlink — Ligonine Focus

You gain proficiency in one artisan’s tool and one skill of your choice from Arcana, History, Medicine, Nature, or Religion.

After 24 hours of uninterrupted study, roll two d20s and keep the higher.

Within the next 7 days, you may replace one check using that tool or skill with the saved roll. This feature can be used once per study period.

### Surface — Stonemovers and Surveyors

You can cast `floating disk` once per long rest.

The disk is 10 feet wide, holds up to 1,000 pounds, and can cross gaps up to 20 feet wide, but cannot change elevation except by slope.

While inside a building, you can sense the presence of any basement, tunnel, or substructure within 300 feet below you.

### Canopy — Dweller in Leaves

You can see clearly up to 1 mile away, discerning details as if viewing from 100 feet. You gain proficiency in Acrobatics.

If you fall at least 10 feet and there is a handhold within reach, you may make a Dexterity (Acrobatics) check with a DC of 12 plus 1 per 10 feet fallen.

On a success, you swing and convert the fall into horizontal movement instead of taking fall damage.

## XII. Playing a Ligonine

Consider:

- Where do you feel correctly placed: below, above, or outside?
- What Third Answer do you keep proposing?
- Which sublineage difference matters most to you?
- What private work should remain private?
- How do you recognize kin?
- What ordinary solution do you respect?
- Which environment do you understand least?
- When does focus become neglect?
- What boundary has curiosity tempted you to cross?
- What holds your world together?

A Ligonine may be conventional, surface-bound, socially predictable, impatient, uncurious, or uninterested in structure.

Their lineage legitimizes uncommon perspectives.

It does not require contrarianism.
