## I. Lineage Overview

Sirenians are Manatee and Dugong Aquaticfolk shaped by warm shallows, patient grazing, long breath, and stewardship of seagrass waters.

They are widely described as gentle.

Many are.

The description becomes harmful when gentleness is treated as incapacity:

- Sirenians will not defend themselves.
- Sirenians do not become angry.
- Sirenians can be moved without resistance.
- Sirenian waters need no enforcement.
- Sirenian patience means there is time to negotiate after damage begins.

Sirenian cultures distinguish gentleness from surrender.

They often prefer intervention that redirects harm before it becomes violence. Their great bodies, environmental knowledge, and communal patience make that possible.

When prevention fails, Sirenians can become formidable defenders.

Their central question is:

> How long may patience wait before it begins protecting the wrong thing?

## II. Origins and Collective Memory

In the **Garden Beneath the Tide**, the Old Court creates a meadow underwater.

Every god praises its beauty.

No god tends it.

The first Sirenian grazes carefully, removes choking growth, carries seed in their wake, and returns before the damage of one season becomes permanent.

The gods call them keeper of the garden.

The Sirenian answers:

> I am part of what keeps it. Do not confuse participation with ownership.

### Grazing Waters

Sirenian communities maintained seagrass meadows, warm estuaries, spring-fed rivers, and migration corridors through repeated use and care.

Surface maps often described these as undeveloped shallows.

Ports dredged them.

Farms redirected sediment.

Relic operations treated plant beds as expendable approach zones.

Sirenian political movements established that a cultivated ecology does not require fences, buildings, or exclusive harvest to constitute inhabited land.

### The Stillwater Delegations

When early harbor councils refused to recognize Sirenian territory, communities began entering ports in large silent groups.

They blocked no ship intentionally.

They simply occupied the water required for their bodies.

Commerce stopped.

These actions became the **Stillwater Delegations**, one of Aerathon’s most influential traditions of nonviolent obstruction.

Their success depended upon bodies difficult to ignore.

Their danger was that authorities could interpret any accidental collision as aggression.

## III. Body and Grazing Form

Sirenians commonly possess:

- Broad, dense bodies
- Paddle-like arms or fins
- Powerful tails
- Sensitive facial whiskers
- Air-breathing lungs
- Thick skin
- Strong plant digestion
- Exceptional carrying and pushing capacity

Most are Medium by game classification.

### Breath

Sirenians breathe air and may hold their breath for long periods.

They require surface access and can drown.

### Skin and Temperature

Sirenian skin requires clean water, moisture, and protection from abrasion.

Cold exposure is dangerous for many communities. The assumption that all Aquaticfolk tolerate cold water is particularly harmful to Sirenians.

### Grazing

Sirenian relationships with aquatic plants resemble stewardship, agriculture, foraging, and environmental engineering at once.

They know that cutting, eating, and shaping growth can preserve an ecosystem when performed within its cycles.

This knowledge should not be reduced to “natural instinct.” It is taught.

## IV. Society and Kinship

Sirenian communities include meadow houses, spring families, warmwater circles, river kin, harbor delegations, restoration crews, and long-distance teaching routes.

### The Slow Question

Councils may designate a **Slow Question** that cannot be decided at the meeting where it is first raised.

Participants must observe at least one relevant environmental or social cycle before final decision.

The practice protects against urgency manufactured by whoever benefits from speed.

It can become dangerous when delay allows ongoing harm. Emergency exceptions require naming who will bear the cost of waiting.

### Intercession

Sirenian mediation often uses physical positioning.

An intercessor places themselves where two paths of harm would meet and creates space for another choice.

This can be political, relational, or literal.

No person is obligated to place their body between others.

### Teaching by Return

Sirenian education frequently repeats the same site across seasons.

Students learn not one condition but change:

- Water temperature
- Plant recovery
- Sediment
- Animal return
- Storm damage
- Human use

Knowledge is judged partly by whether the learner notices what is different.

## V. Inner Life, Virtue, and Shadow

Sirenian communities commonly admire:

- Patience
- Gentle strength
- Ecological stewardship
- Intercession
- Long observation
- Refusal to confuse use with ownership
- Careful appetite
- Collective calm

These values cast long shadows.

| Admired Quality | Constructive Expression | Potential Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| Patience | Allowing durable solutions | Waiting while harm continues |
| Gentleness | Reducing violence | Suppressing necessary anger |
| Stewardship | Sustaining shared ecology | Assuming authority over every user |
| Intercession | Creating safer choices | Sacrificing oneself habitually |
| Calm | Preventing panic | Making visible emotion shameful |
| Long study | Seeing change across cycles | Resisting urgent innovation |

A recurring Sirenian fear is that the first forceful refusal will be used to erase every prior act of patience.

### Internal Divisions

Modern Sirenians debate:

- When Stillwater Delegations become coercive
- Whether grazing waters should be public commons or community territory
- How cold-water migration should be regulated
- Whether Sirenian children are pressured into mediation
- Whether restoration knowledge should be licensed
- When a Slow Question requires emergency action
- How Manatee and Dugong traditions differ under one lineage

## VI. Sirenians in Modern Aerathon

### Allemance

Allemancian Sirenians live in rivers, warm springs, estuaries, and sheltered coasts.

They maintain flood gardens, public bathing waters, plant nurseries, and shared food systems.

### Oria

Sirenians are uncommon in Oria outside heated Houses, thermal waters, and protected industrial habitats.

Oric Sirenians are prominent in thermal engineering and campaigns against treating environmental accommodation as inefficiency.

### Vinyot

Vinyoti Sirenians work in restoration finance, harbor mediation, agriculture, shipping law, public advocacy, and water infrastructure.

Companies market Sirenian calm while resisting their strongest environmental demands.

### Arneria

Arnerian Sirenians inhabit warm coasts, river mouths, monsoon wetlands, and sacred springs.

They frequently mediate between temple claims, farming needs, and living aquatic communities.

### Al’Ar

Al’Ari Sirenians maintain reef meadows, storm-restoration teams, harbor commons, and inter-island teaching routes.

They are among the strongest advocates for rebuilding ecosystems rather than only buildings after storms.

---

## VII. Sirenians and Delving

Sirenians serve as environmental readers, defenders, rescue swimmers, plant interpreters, and stabilizing party members.

Their advantages include long breath, powerful build, swimming, plant knowledge, and protective intercession.

Labyrinths exploit patience through slow poison, environments that improve just enough to delay retreat, and plant systems that imitate recovery while consuming the party’s time.

A common warning states:

> A change happening slowly can still be an emergency.

## IX. D&D Lineage Traits

The following mechanics are an initial playtest version.

### Creature Type

You are a Humanoid.

### Ability Score Increase

Your Constitution score increases by 2.

### Size

Your size is Medium.

### Speed

Your walking speed is 25 feet.

You have a swimming speed of 40 feet.

### Long Breath

You can hold your breath for up to 1 hour.

### Powerful Build

You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.

### Grazing Counsel

As an action, you can touch an aquatic plant to learn whether the surrounding water has experienced poison, unusual heat or cold, violent disturbance, or large-creature passage during the past 24 hours.

The information is sensory and does not identify a creature by name.

### Gentle Intercession

When a creature within 5 feet of you takes damage, you can use your reaction to reduce that damage by an amount equal to your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier.

You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

## X. Recognized Sublineages

### Manatee

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Wisdom score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Freshwater Ease.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made in rivers, estuaries, springs, and wetlands.

When you regain hit points by spending Hit Dice during a short rest while immersed in clean fresh water, add your Wisdom modifier to one die.

Manatee Sirenians are most strongly associated with warm rivers, springs, estuaries, and sheltered coastal waters. Their detailed knowledge of freshwater change often supports stewardship and navigation, but their unhurried movement should not be mistaken for passivity or consent to displacement.

### Dugong

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 1.

**Size.** Your size is Medium.

**Seagrass Route.** You always know the direction of the nearest natural coast or healthy aquatic plant bed within 5 miles, provided one exists.

You gain proficiency in Persuasion.

Dugong Sirenians commonly inhabit coasts, lagoons, and broad seagrass routes, with bodies suited to sustained travel between scattered feeding grounds. Many households preserve maps of plant beds and shoreline change across generations, though route knowledge does not determine profession or temperament.

## XI. Homeland Imprints

### Allemance — Flood Garden

You learn the `druidcraft` cantrip.

You have advantage on checks made to restore or maintain a communal food garden after flooding.

### Oria — Thermal Refuge

You have resistance to cold damage while within 10 feet of a source of significant heat, such as a forge, thermal vent, or large fire.

You gain proficiency with tinker’s tools.

### Vinyot — Stillwater Negotiator

You gain proficiency in Insight.

When you take no movement on your turn, you may add `1d4` to one Charisma check made before the start of your next turn.

### Arneria — Sacred Spring

You know the `spare the dying` cantrip.

Once per long rest, you may cast `purify food and drink` without a spell slot.

### Al’Ar — Storm Meadow

When you use Gentle Intercession, the protected creature may also move 5 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

## XII. Playing a Sirenian

Consider:

- What has patience allowed you to understand?
- What harm has patience allowed to continue?
- Which living water do you tend?
- How do you express anger?
- Who assumes you will mediate?
- What appetite do you practice carefully?
- When would you join a Stillwater Delegation?
- What does cold mean to your body?
- Which Slow Question can no longer wait?
- When do you refuse to place yourself between others?

A Sirenian may be impatient, aggressive, cold-water adapted, urban, uninterested in plants, or unwilling to mediate.

Their lineage offers gentle strength.

It does not require surrender.
