## I. Classification Overview

**Verdantfolk** is the modern collective classification for sapient peoples whose living bodies arise primarily through plant, fungal, or arboreal processes.

They are not animated scenery. They are not automatically spirits of a place, servants of a druid, extensions of a forest, or communal minds without private selves.

The category includes cultivated and spontaneously emerged Leshials, network-forming Mycelials, and long-lived Arboreals. It describes related civic needs without claiming that leaf, fungus, and tree are one ancestry.

Verdantfolk are among Aerathon’s rarest recognized peoples. Most live in small groves, beds, garden households, or isolated root communities, and many Aerathenes will never knowingly meet one. A settlement containing several dozen Verdantfolk is considered a major population center.

---

## II. Origins and the Green Recognition

Older Aerathene law recognized Verdantfolk only when a divine court, mage, or landowner claimed to have created them. Unclaimed individuals could be logged as produce, summoned growth, dangerous flora, or part of the land beneath them.

The **Green Recognition** began when seven mobile groves jointly testified that no creator can own the person who grows from their work. Modern law bases personhood upon demonstrated selfhood and relationship, not upon whoever planted a seed, prepared a substrate, or awakened a tree.

No single origin explains all Verdantfolk. Some descend through seed, cutting, spore, graft, fruit, or root division. Others emerge after planar weather, sacred cultivation, Labyrinth leakage, or ecological restoration.

Verified emergence is infrequent and difficult to reproduce. This rarity has made every newly recognized community a target for collectors, courts, researchers, and institutions eager to claim a discovery.

---

## III. Body, Growth, and Care

Verdant bodies may contain sap, resin, pulp, mycelium, wood, leaves, caps, flowers, fruiting structures, mobile roots, or mineral-rich tissues. Their needs vary dramatically.

Sunlight is not food for every Verdantfolk. Water is not harmless in every quantity. Pruning can be medicine, grooming, ritual, injury, or assault depending upon context and consent.

Public provision may include full-spectrum light, clean substrate, humidity control, rooting courts, fungal-safe ventilation, broad thresholds, and protected dormancy.

---

## IV. Individuality and Networked Life

Verdantfolk personhood does not map neatly onto a single body.

A Leshial may replace most of their tissues while preserving one continuous self. A Mycelial may exchange sensations with a consenting network while remaining an individual. An Arboreal may root for decades, propagate a mobile child, or carry several grafted family memorials.

The Ministry records the person or collective named by the people involved. It does not decide that physical connection proves shared consciousness, or that division creates a lesser copy.

---

## V. Society and Kinship

Verdant households include gardens, rings, groves, beds, orchards, traveling pots, urban conservatories, restoration crews, temples, guilds, and mixed-family homes.

Words such as **gardener**, **tender**, and **cultivator** may describe beloved kin or abusive ownership. The relevant question is whether care is reciprocal and whether the Verdant person can refuse, leave, inherit, and speak for themselves.

Seasonal gatherings often coordinate pollination, spore release, graft exchange, seed guardianship, and dormancy care without making reproduction a public obligation.

Because populations are small, gatherings may draw participants across several Greater Regions. Distance makes chosen kin and mixed-folk households central to Verdant continuity.

## VI. Living Lineages

### [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/7. Verdantfolk/02. Leshial|Leshial]]

Small and medium plant-bodied peoples grown from seed, cutting, fruit, tuber, vine, or assembled vegetation. Leshial traditions center portable gardens, chosen cultivation, renewal, and the right to outgrow an intended purpose.

### [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/7. Verdantfolk/03. Mycelial|Mycelial]]

Fungal peoples whose bodies may form fruiting forms above a larger mycelial substrate. They maintain sophisticated laws of network consent, memory exchange, decomposition, and environmental health.

### [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/7. Verdantfolk/01. Arboreal|Arboreal]]

Tree-bodied peoples ranging from sapling-sized travelers to ancient rooted citizens. Arboreal law distinguishes residence from land ownership and stewardship from territorial possession.

## VII. Verdantfolk in the Greater Regions

### Allemance

Several protected conservatories shelter small populations while subjecting their residents to horticultural spectacle and patronage claims.

### Oria

Isolated highland glasshouses, lichen gardens, and geothermal root chambers support a handful of communities adapted to cold and mineral-rich soils.

### Vinyot

Vertical farms and reclamation works employ a small but highly visible Verdant population; disputes concern seed patents, image rights, and whether corporate substrate can become a form of tenancy control.

### Arneria

Rare night-blooming Leshials, oasis Arboreals, and deep-soil Mycelials maintain careful water law and seasonal movement.

### Al'Ar

Scattered Mangrove Arboreals, salt-tolerant gardens, and volcanic Mycelial beds participate in reef protection and storm recovery.

---

## VIII. Verdantfolk and Delving

Verdant delvers contribute environmental sensing, patient observation, alchemical knowledge, climbing growth, subterranean mapping, and recovery of poisoned terrain.

Labyrinth hazards include false sunlight, compulsive rooting, parasitic grafts, identity-altering spores, accelerated seasons, and rooms that classify Verdant bodies as terrain.

Crews must carry lineage-appropriate water, light, nutrients, air handling, and emergency substrate. A companion is not an expendable source of medicine, food, spores, wood, or rope.

---

## IX. Religion and Philosophy

Verdantfolk participate in every major faith. Growth, rot, fire, harvest, and season possess different meanings across communities.

Common philosophies ask whether continuity resides in matter, pattern, memory, relationship, or promise. None produces a single Verdant answer.

Decomposition is not necessarily death. Regrowth is not necessarily resurrection. Rooting is not necessarily worship. Cultivation is not necessarily creation.

---

## X. Common Misconceptions

- Verdantfolk are not automatically peaceful, patient, wise, or nature-bound.
- Fungal connection does not erase individual privacy.
- A planted origin does not create ownership.
- Fruit, flowers, seeds, sap, spores, and cuttings remain protected bodily material.
- Fire is neither universally terrifying nor universally fatal.
- Arboreals are not public parks or natural landmarks.
- Leshials do not owe gratitude to whoever first tended them.

---

## XI. Playing a Verdantfolk Character

Consider:

- What sustains your body, and who taught you that care?
- Which parts of your growth are private?
- What does dormancy mean to you?
- Did you emerge, descend, awaken, or get cultivated?
- What relationship do you have with the place that nourished you?
- Who may receive a seed, spore, cutting, graft, or memory from you?
- What do outsiders call natural that your community knows was carefully built?

Verdant identity offers unusual embodiments and histories, not assigned temperament.
