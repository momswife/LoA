# Playable Lineage Quick Reference

This register summarizes the mechanics actually present in each lineage record. It does not replace the full page, assign culture from ancestry, or silently complete missing rules.

- **ASI** reproduces the primary Ability Score Increase filed in the record; “Choice / special” means the rule cannot be reduced to one ordinary +2 entry.
- **Movement** uses W for walking speed and names additional movement modes.
- **Options** counts mechanically headed sublineages or lineage traditions in the recognized-options section. A dash means that no distinct option is currently filed there.
- **Imprints** counts mechanically headed Homeland Imprints. Most records provide five regional choices, while some subjects use a different model.
- **Record note** reports only what the source states. “No caution stated” is not an editorial declaration that the mechanics are final.

> [!warning] Character creation
> Confirm the current campaign rules with the DM. This page intentionally exposes missing fields and approval notices rather than inventing a complete option.

## Current Filing Gaps

The comparison makes several inherited record gaps visible:

- Fourteen older Beastfolk records do not state Size in their mechanics section.
- Kobold currently files no Homeland Imprint section options.
- Jackal uses two origin options instead of the five-region pattern.
- Chiropterid includes an Astral Sea option in addition to five regional Imprints.
- Several Brethren records delegate innate abilities to the campaign's approved core ancestry traits.

These are mechanics-review needs, not cultural conclusions. Until their source records are revised, the table leaves them visible.
