## I. Classification Overview

**Scalefolk** is the modern collective classification for sapient reptilian peoples whose bodies and cultures are shaped primarily by terrestrial, arboreal, riverbank, marsh, desert, or littoral reptile kinships.

The classification addresses a major absence in older Ministry law. Reptilian people were previously scattered among Beastfolk, Aquaticfolk, Mythicfolk, monster registries, and draconic appendices.

Scalefolk are not lesser dragons.

They are not cold-blooded in the moral sense.

They are not one ancient empire waiting to return.

They are a common presence across warm cities, deserts, river systems, marshes, islands, forests, and engineered cold-climate settlements. Their apparent rarity in older archives comes from being divided among monster ledgers, draconic appendices, Beastfolk registries, and local names rather than from small population.

---

## II. Origins and the Broken Scale Registers

Old records repeatedly confused biological resemblance with draconic descent. Saurians were described as failed Dragonborn, Ophidians as incomplete Naga, and Crocodilians as speaking monsters. Gekkonians and Chamaelians were filed as magical familiars, Varanians as war beasts, and Chelonians as immobile landscape spirits.

The **Broken Scale Registers** are the surviving administrative volumes in which communities crossed out those imposed labels and wrote household names over them.

Modern classification follows self-identification, collective history, and body form rather than presumed proximity to dragons.

## III. Boundary with Other Folk

Testudinan sea-turtle peoples remain [[∅ Aquaticfolk|Aquaticfolk]] because water routes, nesting shore law, and shell-ledger identity are central to their classification. Terrestrial Tortoise peoples are recognized as [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/6. Scalefolk/02. Chelonian|Chelonian]] Scalefolk.

[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/1. Aquaticfolk/12. Urodelan|Urodelans]] are permeable-skinned amphibian peoples whose bodies and histories are water-dependent; they remain Aquaticfolk rather than Scalefolk. Visual resemblance, tails, and an ability to live on land do not override that distinction.

[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/6. Scalefolk/03. Crocodilian|Crocodilians]] remain Scalefolk because their inherited lives join submerged water with indispensable banks, basking grounds, nests, and terrestrial civic space. Amphibious capability alone does not establish Aquaticfolk classification.

Hereditary reptilian gliders whose histories are organized around aerial-canopy movement are [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/2. Avianfolk/05. Limnid|Limnid]] Avianfolk. Scalefolk registration excludes those branches, and no hereditary branch may be entered beneath two Folk classifications.

[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/5. Mythicfolk/04. Dragonborn|Dragonborn]] and [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/5. Mythicfolk/08. Kobold|named Kobolds]] remain [[∅ Mythicfolk|Mythicfolk]] because their records concern draconic pact and emergent sapience.

Ophidian Scalefolk are distinct from the mythic [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/5. Mythicfolk/05. Gorgonite|Gorgonites]], whose living crown and petrifying resonance arise through curse, divine inheritance, or transformation.

A person may participate in more than one community without becoming an administrative contradiction.

## IV. Body, Heat, and Molting

Scalefolk bodies may include scales, scutes, tails, claws, specialized teeth, sensory pits, crests, dewlaps, and periodic shedding.

Most regulate temperature behaviorally and through clothing, architecture, heated surfaces, shade, water, or magic. This does not mean they are emotionless, sluggish, or helpless in cold conditions.

Shed skin is personal biological material. Communities decide whether it is destroyed, composted, archived, crafted, or used ritually.

---

## V. Society and Kinship

Scalefolk households include clutches, families, sun courts, marsh blocks, river settlements, desert burrows, craft houses, temples, and chosen kin.

The word **clutch** may describe siblings, an age cohort, a political circle, or people who completed early care together. It does not imply that parents or biology own adult membership.

Heat and shade are common public responsibilities. A seat in the sun can be an accessibility provision rather than a privilege.

## VI. Living Lineages

### [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/6. Scalefolk/06. Saurian|Saurian]]

Iguana, Skink, Anole, Agamid, and related lizard peoples associated with sun architecture, crested display, rapid movement, and varied arboreal or terrestrial traditions.

### [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/6. Scalefolk/03. Crocodilian|Crocodilian]]

Crocodile, Alligator, Caiman, and Gharial peoples shaped by river margins, patient motion, powerful bodies, and nesting-water law.

### [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/6. Scalefolk/05. Ophidian|Ophidian]]

Viper, Cobra, Boa, Python, and Colubrid peoples with limbless or partially limbed bodies, chemical senses, and strong traditions concerning gaze, venom, and bodily fear.

### [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/6. Scalefolk/04. Gekkonian|Gekkonian]]

Gecko peoples whose adhesive movement, nocturnal or jewel-bright bodies, vertical neighborhoods, and tail customs shape distinct access and privacy law.

### [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/6. Scalefolk/01. Chamaelian|Chamaelian]]

Chameleon peoples with independently mobile eyes, prehensile bodies, specialized tongues, and color languages that cannot be translated without context and consent.

### [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/6. Scalefolk/07. Varanian|Varanian]]

Monitor and Komodo peoples known for strong chemical senses, distance endurance, heat-route traditions, and resistance to the assumption that visible power establishes predatory intent.

### [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/02. Peoples, Lineages & Cultures/I. Peoples & Lineages/6. Scalefolk/02. Chelonian|Chelonian]]

Terrestrial Tortoise peoples whose shells, long household memory, dryland engineering, and deliberate public life differ from aquatic Testudinan traditions.

## VII. Scalefolk in the Greater Regions

### Allemance

Heated arcades, glass gardens, canal districts, and court fascination create both access and spectacle.

### Oria

Thermal springs and artificed heat support small communities whose infrastructure demonstrates that climate compatibility is designed, not inherited fate.

### Vinyot

Scale fashion, shed-skin markets, industrial heat, and broadcast combat generate major consent disputes.

### Arneria

Desert Saurian, Ophidian, Varanian, and Chelonian communities maintain extensive sun, shade, burrow, and night-route traditions.

### Al'Ar

Crocodilian river mouths and island Saurian, Gekkonian, and Chamaelian communities participate deeply in littoral trade and storm refuge.

---

## VIII. Scalefolk and Delving

Scalefolk delvers contribute climbing, heat sensing, amphibious movement, patience, scent analysis, and navigation through narrow or unstable terrain.

Labyrinths frequently exploit reptilian stereotypes by presenting false basking chambers, draconic commands, or prey-response enchantments.

No member should be assigned a hazard solely because scales appear protective.

---

## IX. Religion and Philosophy

Scalefolk participate in every major religion. Solar imagery is common without implying Dawn Court allegiance. River and shed-skin traditions often treat renewal as continuity through changed form.

A molt is not automatically rebirth.

A sun is not automatically a god.

A dragon is not automatically an ancestor.

---

## X. Common Misconceptions

- Scalefolk do experience complex emotion.
- Temperature needs do not determine personality.
- Venom is not universal among Ophidians.
- Crocodilian stillness is not laziness.
- Color change is not permission to read a Chamaelian’s private state.
- A Chelonian shell does not make its owner slow-minded or available as shelter.
- Scales are not armor equivalent in every lineage.
- Dragonborn are not the nobility of all scaled peoples.

---

## XI. Playing a Scalefolk Character

Consider:

- What environmental provision does your body need?
- What happens to shed skin?
- Which collective name do you use?
- Has anyone mistaken you for a dragon or monster?
- What form of stillness restores you?
- Which climate is home because of community rather than biology?
- What body signal remains private?

Scalefolk identity offers embodied histories, not assigned temperament.
