# Reading the Monthly Ledger

The Monthly Ledger records events while their meaning and outcome remain unsettled. Every entry is a dated claim made by an identifiable source within a stated jurisdiction. It may be useful without being complete, neutral, or permanently correct.

## I. Required Lifecycle Fields

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Posted** | When this version first entered the public Ledger |
| **Issuer** | The office, organization, community, or named source responsible for the notice |
| **Jurisdiction** | Where the filing applies or which authority may act upon it |
| **Status** | Whether the entry is active, amended, resolved, withdrawn, superseded, expired, or unverified |
| **Effective Through** | The stated expiry, review date, event boundary, or “until withdrawn” condition |
| **Last Updated** | The date of the most recent visible amendment |
| **Supersedes** | The earlier filing this version replaces, if any |
| **Outcome** | The confirmed resolution, or “not yet filed” while unresolved |

Unknown information should be marked as unknown, not omitted or reconstructed. An undated legacy notice cannot be presumed active merely because no withdrawal survives.

## II. Status Vocabulary

- **Active:** presently in force or still seeking action within its stated effective period.
- **Amended:** still operative, but changed by the visible update log.
- **Resolved:** the reported event or request has concluded and an outcome is filed.
- **Withdrawn:** the issuer ended the notice without replacing it.
- **Superseded:** a later linked filing now controls.
- **Expired:** the stated effective period ended without renewal.
- **Status Unverified:** the surviving copy lacks enough lifecycle evidence to establish current force.

Status describes the filing, not the truth of every claim it contains.

## III. Reliability and Updates

Confirmed facts, attributed reports, inference, rumor, and disputed claims should remain visibly distinct. Corrections belong in a dated update log. Do not silently rewrite an earlier version in a way that hides what readers or officials previously knew.

When a correction changes the recommended action, affected area, named party, or legal force of a notice, update **Last Updated**, identify what changed, and link any superseding entry.

## IV. Resolution and Promotion

Resolved Ledger entries remain useful as records of developing knowledge. Their outcomes should be stated rather than deleting the original notice.

- Stable present-day facts established by the outcome belong in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/Overview|The Living Atlas]].
- Events whose consequences have become historical belong in [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/I. Annals & Antiquities/Overview|Annals & Antiquities]].
- The Ledger retains the dated notice and links to the promoted record.

Promotion does not erase uncertainty that existed at the time of filing.
