# Gonlev Survivor Bulletin

## Notice

Fourteen people escaped [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/7. Lisvenn/1. Settlements/01. Gonlev|Gonlev]] following an undead assault from the north. The Ministry of Delving Operations records the escape as a new development in the northern plague emergency. The incident date has not been entered in this filing.

The recorded group comprises:

- **[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/10. Notable Figures/II. Military & Civic Defense/03. Gutsy|Gutsy]]**, a murine MDO agent.
- **Sickle Grymes**, a vulpine survivor.
- **Twelve grandi felines** from Gonlev.

The group departed aboard a [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/III. Monthly Ledger/04. Headlines & Rumors/03. Ghost Train at Gonlev|ghost train]]. Its destination and present whereabouts are not established. This count identifies the escape party; it does not establish a complete death toll or prove that every other person at Gonlev died.

## Northern Response

Gutsy leads the effort to locate **A-Rank or S-Rank operatives** to assist with the northern events. This assignment concerns recruitment; it does not establish unrestricted command of the entire response.

**[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/IV. Delver Registry/1. Active/10. Sister Judiith|Sister Judiith]]** and **[[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/IV. Delver Registry/1. Active/08. Mother Elowen|Mother Elowen of the Pale Hour]]** remain engaged with the northern crisis. They are recorded separately from the fourteen people who departed aboard the train. Their exact positions and condition are not supplied by this bulletin.

Qualified operatives should make their availability known through the Ministry for referral to Gutsy's recruitment effort. The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/III. Monthly Ledger/08. Travel Advisories/01. Northern Approaches Remain Closed|northern travel restrictions]] remain in force; the escape report grants no public right of entry.

## Limits of the Report

“Plague” remains the emergency's working description. This filing establishes no transmission mechanism, cure, confirmed rate of spread, or identity for the force directing the dead. It does not settle the fate of Gonlev's individual defenders, buildings, or relics.

The earlier contact-lost notices remain evidence of what was known when they were issued. This bulletin adds a recorded escape from Gonlev; it does not authenticate every message attributed to the north or establish survivor contact from Frostguarde.

## Update Log
