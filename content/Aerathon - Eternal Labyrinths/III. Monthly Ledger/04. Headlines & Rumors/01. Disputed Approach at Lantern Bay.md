# Disputed Approach at Lantern Bay

## Notice

Several working crews report unexpected grounding in a familiar approach. Shore-light keepers agree that fresh soundings are needed; they do not agree that the channel has permanently shifted. Claims that a rival carrier moved a marker have not been substantiated.

Loaded craft should obtain current local guidance rather than relying on an older account of the channel. Anyone who observed a marker being moved is asked to provide the time, conditions, and original account. Repetition of the rumor is not a second witness.

- **26 Redfall, 3388 A.D.:** Initial filing. Outcome not yet filed.
