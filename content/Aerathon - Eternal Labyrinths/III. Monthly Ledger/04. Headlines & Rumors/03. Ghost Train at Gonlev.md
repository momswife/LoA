# Ghost Train at Gonlev

## Recorded Departure

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/III. Monthly Ledger/04. Headlines & Rumors/02. Gonlev Survivor Bulletin|Gonlev Survivor Bulletin]] records fourteen people leaving the besieged monastery aboard a **ghost train**: [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/10. Notable Figures/II. Military & Civic Defense/03. Gutsy|Gutsy]], Sickle Grymes, and twelve grandi felines. Their escape is the basis for this entry.

“Ghost train” is the description attached to that departure. It does not by itself identify a vessel, an operator, a kind of undead, or a repeatable means of travel. No timetable, boarding place, or return service has been established.

## Open Questions

The record does not establish where the train went, what carried it across the mountain terrain, or whether it followed physical rails. Its crew, purpose, and relationship to the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/4. Oria/1. Subregions/4. Nattefrost/∅ Nattefrost|Nattefrost]] remain unknown.

The northern region's temporal phenomena make careful comparison necessary, but they do not prove that this train travels through time. The rescue also provides no basis for identifying it as the source of the plague or as a safe service available to other travelers.

## Reporting a Sighting

Anyone reporting a similar train should preserve the original account and distinguish what they saw from what they later heard. Useful details include the location, the observer's recorded time, visible passengers, direction of travel, and whether rails were present. Separate accounts should remain separate until their relationship can be established.

A reported departure is not a route map. The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/III. Monthly Ledger/08. Travel Advisories/01. Northern Approaches Remain Closed|northern cordon]] remains active.
