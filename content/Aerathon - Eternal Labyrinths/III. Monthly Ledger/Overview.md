> _The Ledger does not describe the world as it is understood. It records the world as it is moving._

The Monthly Ledger contains the archive's most immediate and least settled material. Reports may be incomplete, contradictory, politically shaped, or overtaken by events before they can be formally revised.

Use these records to understand what is happening now—not what history will eventually decide occurred.

> [!danger] Provisional Record
> Ledger entries may be amended, sealed, reclassified, or superseded without notice. Verify critical information against the most recent filing.
