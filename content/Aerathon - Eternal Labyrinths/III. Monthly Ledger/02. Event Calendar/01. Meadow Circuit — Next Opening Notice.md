# Meadow Circuit — Next Opening Notice

> [!info] Advance program
> This notice describes the proposed program for the next monitored opening. Its issue date, event date, entry deadline, and final roster have not been recorded. Confirm the current card with the Ring Office before treating it as an active booking or traveling to compete.

## I. The Next Card

The [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/09. Organizations, Companies & Associations/I. Civic Defense & Emergency Service/01. The Hearthward Compact|Hearthward Compact's]] Ring Office is preparing a [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/07. Delving, Guilds & the Spectacle/V. Broadcasts, Audiences & Sponsorship/05. The Meadow Circuit|Meadow Circuit]] program around the next monitored active interval of the [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/II. The Living Atlas/01. Regions, Realms & Settlements/I. Aerathon/2. Allemance/3. Geography & Landmarks/10. Monster Meadows|Monster Meadows Rift]]. The proposed bill combines the **Fence Trials**, an **Old Blades Open** holo challenge, and a **Lanternwrights' Showing**.

Live engagements help manage the creatures emerging from the Rift. Their composition depends on the actual surge and the wardens' assessment. A projected encounter may replace a live bout when no suitable opponent is available; the change will be identified before competition.

## II. Proposed Program

| Session | Program | Confirmation needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fence Trials** | Teams hold a defended passage against eligible intercepted monsters. | Live opponents, team clearance, and arena readiness. |

No named champion, guest appearance, prize amount, or particular live creature is confirmed by this advance notice. The advertised program is not an entrance examination and cannot award a delving license.

## III. Entrants and Visiting Crews

Prospective competitors should contact the Ring Office about team places and equipment declarations. Training appointments belong with the Trial Office and Lantern Works; entering a tournament does not reserve a practice session or an MDO examination slot.

Visiting crews can also ask about support work around the event: provisions, equipment handling, and transport. Operational watch assignments require the Compact's own assessment and qualification. Being available to help or wearing a famous guild's colors does not place a visitor in command of a patrol.

Rakebridge|Rakebridge]] remain working settlements throughout the program. Keep carts and equipment clear of the approaches needed by residents and wardens.

## IV. The Watch Comes First

The grounds are outside the restricted inner Meadows. Attendance does not authorize access through the quarantine perimeter. Anya Lykoi and Tiberius Lykoi retain the authority to stop the Compact's events when field or ward conditions require it.

If the Retreat Call sounds, the program ends and marshals direct the crowd toward the homeward routes. The broadcast carries the instructions. Competitors follow the wardens' directions; an interrupted round creates no obligation to finish a fight for the audience.

## V. Scheduling and Outcome

The Ring Office's dated confirmation must establish the event's current status, booking arrangements, and final card. A later amendment should retain this proposed program and explain any changes. Results, cancellations, or a superseding notice belong here once they are filed.

**Outcome:** Not yet filed. This advance program records no completed bouts, winners, or casualties.
