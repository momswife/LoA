# Northern Approaches Remain Closed

## Notice

Travelers must not treat an old lodging entry, a recovered supply list, or an unauthenticated relay message as evidence that the northern routes have reopened. Frostguarde, Gonlev, and the approaches to the Nattefrost remain restricted under the existing cordon. Historical settlement records describe earlier life; they do not authorize entry.

Anyone holding a purported recent message should preserve its original form and record how it was received. This notice announces no rescue expedition and confirms no survivors or losses.

## Survivor-Report Amendment

The later [[Aerathon - Eternal Labyrinths/III. Monthly Ledger/04. Headlines & Rumors/02. Gonlev Survivor Bulletin|Gonlev Survivor Bulletin]] records fourteen people escaping Gonlev and identifies Gutsy's effort to recruit A-Rank or S-Rank assistance. It updates the earlier absence of a survivor account. The original notice above is retained as issued; its statement that it confirms no survivors describes that earlier filing.

The cordon remains active. The train's departure establishes no authorized route through the exclusion zone, and recruitment is not a general entry permit. The amendment's date and the incident's date remain unrecorded.
