# Granfield Storehouse Repair Work

## Notice

Granfield seeks roofers, sack handlers, and carters for repairs to the older shared storehouse. Applicants should agree their rate and duties with the steward before beginning. Community service owed by residents is recorded separately and cannot be used to deny agreed pay to hired workers.

Work includes moving stored grain under cover and replacing damaged roof tiles. No abnormal contamination has been reported. This notice does not promise delver credit, relic rights, or Ministry reimbursement.
