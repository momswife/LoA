- **Port Ventura / Porta Ventura**, **Amica Bay / Amicia Bay**, and **Kashòtta Caves / Kasho’ta Caves** are existing spelling reconciliations.
- Duplicate pins are a map maintenance issue, not evidence for separate settlements. Glimmerpool’s City/Town labels do not establish two populations.
