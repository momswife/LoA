# Continuity Check

Determine what the repository supports, where evidence differs, and which records a change would affect.

## Investigate

1. Extract the material claims: names, aliases, dates, sequence, relationships, locations, institutions, rules, classifications, and current status.
2. Search those terms and meaningful variants across the published vault. Follow relevant wikilinks and inspect the subject's dedicated page, parent indexes, newer revisions, and connected records.
3. Apply the archive layers correctly:
   - Annals may preserve competing historical reconstructions.
   - The Living Atlas states maintained present-day consensus.
   - The Monthly Ledger is provisional and may be superseded.
4. Distinguish setting truth from an institution's interpretation, witness testimony, rumor, propaganda, historical uncertainty, and DM-only truth.

Avoid treating different scopes, dates, narrators, or public and restricted perspectives as contradictions merely because their wording differs.

## Report

Classify each material finding as:

- Supported by current canon
- Compatible but perspective-dependent
- Stale or superseded
- Direct contradiction
- Unresolved because evidence is insufficient

Cite the relevant repository files and headings. For proposed changes, list the records, indexes, terminology, mechanics, and timelines that would require review. Offer reconciliation options without silently choosing or inventing canon.

Do not edit files during a review-only request. If edits are requested, make only evidence-supported changes, preserve intentional disagreement, and place a consequential unresolved decision in `WIKI_REVIEW.md`.
